# A Turn, on One Page

<!-- print:skip-start -->
*Draft, 2026-09-28. A summary for the table, not a new rule: every line here restates `rules/combat.md` or `rules/resolution.md`, and **where this page and those files disagree, those files win and this page is the thing to fix.** Written to be revised from what players actually ask at the first session.*

## Starting a fight

**Everyone rolls 1d6 + Soul**; highest goes first, ties to higher Soul, then players before enemies. **Everyone draws to hand size** — your Mind, never fewer than 2.

## Your turn

**Draw** back to hand size (empty deck: shuffle your discard in). Then take **one Action**:

- **Play a card** — attack (below)
- **Move** — Frontline ↔ Backline, or from the Frontline **Rushdown** onto a Backline enemy
- **Use an item**, **Take Cover** (Backline), or **Interact** with anything the scene allows
- **Flee** — 2d10 + Soul against 10 + the highest enemy Soul

**Plus one free action** — eat, drink, use a consumable, spend a Quick to move, switch on your gear. **One per turn.**

## Attacking

1. **You** put a card face down.
2. **They** may defend with one face down, legal for the range. No defence, you win.
3. **Anyone holding Blind or Evade** rolls a d2.
4. **Both flip together — Blue beats Red, Red beats Green, Green beats Blue.**

- **You win:** damage is **your stat + the card's die**, then your Effect.
- **They win:** no damage, and their Defense Effect.
- **Same colour:** no damage — your Effect, then theirs.

**Range:** Melee needs you both in the Frontline; Ranged needs you not to be; Both works anywhere. **Damage** goes Immunity → Armour → Resist (half) or Vulnerable (×1.5); **unpreventable** skips it all. **Initiative shifts** move your turn sooner or later — never take it away.

**At 0 HP you're Down:** no attacking or moving, but you still defend and keep your free action. Healing lifts you above 0; **standing costs your Action**. At **minus half your max HP**, you die.

## Outside a fight

**Roll 2d10 + a stat** against 11 / 13 / 16 / 19 (Easy to Extreme) — argue for the stat that fits. **A fitting Skill adds +2.** **Discard a card whose name fits your aim** to roll 3d10 and drop the lowest.
