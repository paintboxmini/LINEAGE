# Locations

Name, Price, and Distance (`mythology/creation-myth-the-three-cuts.md`) applied to designing a location. This file doesn't redefine any of the three — it points at where each one actually lives and shows the mechanic locations already use to express it. For the cosmology of what makes a place *Resonant* specifically, see `mythology/resonant-locations.md`.

---

## Name

What a location is — its nature, its geography, what happens there. No separate mechanic: a location's Name is established the same way `locations/` overviews already establish it, through ordinary world-building. Every location has a Name. Not every location is Resonant (`mythology/resonant-locations.md`) — Resonance is a further alignment with a Seat's domain, not a requirement for existing.

---

## Price — The Pressure Track

**A location's Price is paid, or accrued, through its pressure track.** This isn't a new mechanic invented for this file — it's the one already built and running across `quests/turnroot-weald-adventure.md`, `locations/the-coil.md`, `quests/hollow-below-briarwatch.md`, `quests/sour-tomatoes.md`, `quests/settling-stones.md`, and `quests/the-wallows-descent.md`. What's new is naming what it's always been: the Second Cut, expressed at a place's scale. A place can't spend vitality or degrade an object the way a person or item pays — what it can do is escalate against whoever's standing in it, and that escalation *is* the debt (`mythology/resonant-locations.md`, Price at a Place's Scale).

**The shared shape, across every existing implementation:**

- **A numeric track**, starting at 0 (or the party's current level, on return visits).
- **Named states or levels** the track passes through — not a bare number, a legible escalation (Turnroot's Unsettling → Watched → Predation → Lethal Forest → Root Heart; the Coil's Silence → a sound → closer → moving toward you).
- **What raises it** — always tied to failing to move through the place the way its domain demands: a failed navigation check, a broken custom, noise, a rule violated. This is the failure to pay.
- **What holds or lowers it** — real compliance. Some tracks only hold (Turnroot: the DM may hold for strong RP or compliance, never actively lowers). Some actively pay it back down (the Coil: a full passage in silence costs −1). Both are legitimate; which one fits is a property of the specific place, not a rule this file should flatten into one shape.
- **An early, voluntary payment** is optional, not required — Turnroot's Seeker's Price lets a party skip straight to the Root Heart for a gold piece, at whatever Pressure they're currently carrying. Not every location needs this, but it's worth asking whether one should exist: a location that offers no way to buy down or bypass its own escalation is a harder, more punishing kind of place, and that should be a choice, not an oversight.

**Not every location needs a pressure track.** Most places a party passes through cost nothing to move through — that's not a gap, that's the default. Build one when the place itself is meant to push back.

## Distance — What a Place Can Never Be

See `agent-tools/design-principles.md`, Distance — What Can Never Be, for the actual principle and worked test. In short: a resonant place can't fully become some other kind of place, permanently — Turnroot Weald can never become safe ground, because the pressure that makes it what it is has no off state, only a held one. `mythology/resonant-locations.md`'s "No Confirmed Ceiling" section is the related, still-open question of whether a place can reach an equivalent to Archon at all — a different question from Distance, not to be conflated with it.
