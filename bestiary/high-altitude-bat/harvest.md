# High Altitude Bat — Harvest

## Harvesting

Harvested at roost during daylight hours when the colony is inactive. A single trained collector with the right approach can take one specimen without waking the colony. Rushing or using light wakes the entire roost.

Senshi's supplier works the cliffs above Glasslight Reach. He does not share the contact.
