# Bicolor Spider — Profile

## Behavior

Solitary. Territorial. Females are significantly larger than males and are the harvested specimen. Reclusive during the day, active at dusk when prey is drawn to the web.

The venom is mildly toxic to small animals, negligible to humans — handling is safe with reasonable care.

---

## The Two Halves

The color difference reflects a genuine chemical divergence in the body tissue. The orange half is fatty and sweet — a high-energy region associated with silk production. The blue half is lean and mineral-dense, associated with venom synthesis. The two flavor profiles do not mix during cooking if the cut is made correctly.

Senshi considers the cut the whole technique.
