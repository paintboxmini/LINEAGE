# Bicolor Spider — Harvest

## Harvesting

Located by web structure — the orbs are large and well-built, visible in the right light. Harvested alive or freshly killed. The key is keeping the two halves intact and uncompressed during transport, as bruising before the cook mixes the profiles irreversibly.

## Culinary Note

See `places/capital/gilded-tusk.md` — Bicolor Spider Tempura. Orange: sweet, nutty. Blue: savory. Tempura batter preserves both. Do not ask Senshi to remove the legs. The legs are the point.
