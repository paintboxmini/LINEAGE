# Future Lock Wasp — Mechanics

---

## Dispersal

The swarm has counters. Characters who find them don't get stung again.

**Fire:** Any open flame presented toward the swarm disperses it immediately. No further saves required. Fire does not need to hit the wasps — the light and heat drives them off.

**Full submersion in water:** Wasps will not follow into water. Submerged characters take no further saves. The swarm hovers at the surface for 1 round then disperses if no other targets are present.

**Leaving the area:** Move out of the nest zone. One final save on the round of departure, then the swarm returns to the nest.

---

## Future-Lock Venom

The venom interrupts what a body was about to do next.

**Future-Lock X.** Reduces the stung character's effective hand size by X. Stacks additively into a single running total — the same shape as Armour and Thorns, never queued. **No floor at 2.** This is not a Mind loss (`rules/card-glossary.md`, Stat Change, which never drops hand size under 2) — Future-Lock is a direct hand-size reduction, the same mechanism CLIMB's own bonus uses, just negative, and it can take you to 0. At 0, you draw no cards and cannot act. Another combatant can clear it: spending their action removes 1 stack of Future-Lock from you. Not a Debuff — Ward and Deflect do not prevent it. Clears at the end of the combat it was active in; stacks earned outside combat carry into the next fight, or clear on a long rest if no fight comes first.

*The rule lives here rather than in `rules/card-glossary.md` because no card prints it — this wasp is its only source in the game, and the glossary is meant to be printed and handed to players. (2026-08-17, Drew.) If cards are ever designed for it, it moves back.*

Outside a fight the same venom makes the hands slow and the next action arrive a half-beat late — enough that a navigation check or a careful harvest can fail for no obvious reason. The mechanical weight is in combat; the fiction is continuous.

## Swarm Trigger

Wasps do not attack unless a nest is disturbed. Nests are found in root cavities, beneath bark overhang, and occasionally anchored to the underside of fallen logs. They do not announce themselves.

On disturbance, a swarm lifts. Each character in the area makes a **2d10 + Body save**.

| Result | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| 15+ | No sting — evaded cleanly |
| 10–14 | Future-Lock 1 |
| 5–9 | Future-Lock 2 |
| Below 5 | Future-Lock 3 |

The swarm persists for up to 3 rounds in the disturbed area. Each round spent in the area requires a new save; applications stack. Moving out of the nest zone ends further checks.

If the party is not in combat when stung, track the stacks and apply them at the start of the next combat (hand size reduced before the first draw of that fight). A long rest clears unspent stacks that never reached a fight.
