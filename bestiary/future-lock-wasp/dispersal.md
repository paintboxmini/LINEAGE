# Future Lock Wasp — Dispersal

The swarm has counters. Characters who find them don't get stung again.

**Fire:** Any open flame presented toward the swarm disperses it immediately. No further saves required. Fire does not need to hit the wasps — the light and heat drives them off.

**Full submersion in water:** Wasps will not follow into water. Submerged characters take no further saves. The swarm hovers at the surface for 1 round then disperses if no other targets are present.

**Leaving the area:** Move out of the nest zone. One final save on the round of departure, then the swarm returns to the nest.
