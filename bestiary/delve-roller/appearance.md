# Delve Roller — Appearance

Low to the ground. Segmented shell the color of wet limestone, each plate slightly overlapping the next. Wider than it is tall. When threatened it pulls its legs in and the seams lock — a smooth oval of stone-hard chitin.

The claws don't match. Disproportionately large, jointed differently from the legs. Built for breaking soil, not walking. They drag when it moves.

Two antennae sweep constantly ahead of it. Long, thin, whip-fast. They sense vibration before the body needs to respond.
