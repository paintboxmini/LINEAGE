# Briar Scratcher

*Road encounter. Teaches Wounds as deck pressure. Run before Briarwatch on day 2 of travel.*

**Mind 1 / Body 1 / Soul 2 — HP 9**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 4


**Deck (4 — 1 Blue / 1 Red / 2 Green):** SKITTER AWAY *(blue)* · RAKING CUT *(red)* · NIP AND TEAR, BRISTLE *(green)*
**Cards:** `cards/briar-scratcher.md`

---

## Appearance

Something like a large squirrel but wrong. Too many joints. Eyes that catch light at the wrong angle. Briar thorns work into their fur until the line between animal and undergrowth blurs.

They move in short bursts. Still, still, still — then gone.

---

## Behavior

Territorial, not predatory. Displaced when the farmland was cut into the Weald, they never went far — they claim the hedgerows and fencelines between fields now, and the road that cuts through them. Movement and noise draw them. They dart in, cut, and leave. They're not trying to win. They're marking.

Run 2–3 Scratchers. They don't coordinate — they happen simultaneously.

---

## Combat Behavior

After each attack a Scratcher repositions. They don't hold ground. Their goal is to apply Wounds and disengage before the party organizes.

**Cycles 1–2:** Light damage. Wounds enter the deck.
**Cycle 3+:** Players start drawing Wounds. Hand quality degrades. The lesson lands.

Keep this short. 2–3 cycles. The point is the marked decks going into Briarwatch, not the Scratchers themselves.

---

## Tactical Purpose

First encounter of the session. Introduces Wounds as a deck pressure system before players face anything that can exploit them. The Briar Scratcher doesn't need to threaten HP — it threatens future hands.
