# Phantom-Tail Slug

A bioluminescent slug, six to eight inches long, found in damp forest undergrowth and cave margins. The body produces a steady blue-white glow through the skin — bright enough to read by at close range, impossible to miss in darkness.

The tail section is structurally distinct from the body: softer tissue, brighter luminescence, connected by a narrow isthmus. When threatened, the slug detaches the tail. The tail continues glowing for up to an hour after separation, twitching occasionally. The slug does not glow without its tail — it goes dark and moves away while the predator investigates the light.

The tail regrows over several weeks.

---

## Behavior

Slow. Reclusive. Feeds on fungal growth and decaying plant matter. Nocturnal. Found individually, not in groups.

The detachment response is involuntary — any significant physical stress to the tail region triggers it. Slugs that have recently detached will be darker, smaller at the rear, and faster-moving than intact specimens.

---

## Harvesting

Hand collection at night. A lit lantern draws them — they're attracted to competing light sources, possibly as a territorial response to the glow of rival specimens.

Harvesting the tail separately is possible but Senshi considers it wasteful. He takes the whole animal.
