# Elder Tower Creature

Not a combat encounter. An environmental presence.

Grows fifteen to twenty-five feet tall from the floor of The Trench. At distance it looks like a pale architectural spire — could be mistaken for a ruin column. Up close: the surface is warm. The texture is irregular. The structure breathes in a slow four-second cycle that takes a moment to notice.

Along the body: pores, each a few inches across, ringed with darker coloration. At irregular intervals a fleshy tube emerges, extends a few inches, holds, then slowly retracts.

They do not attack. They respond to touch, proximity, and vibration by retracting all exposed tubes immediately. They extend again once the stimulus passes.

---

## Classification

Unknown. They predate the ruins. They may predate the civilization that built the ruins. The runes on the surrounding walls do not reference them. Something built those walls around these things and didn't leave notes.

No current taxonomy applies. Scholars who have examined them cannot agree on whether they are animal, fungal, or something that predates the distinction.

---

## Interaction — Harvesting Tubes

A tube emerges approximately once per minute from each active pore. It extends fully, holds for a few seconds, then begins to retract.

**Harvesting:**
- DC 12 Body/Senses to cut cleanly before retraction.
- **Success:** 1 tube harvested. Tower is undisturbed.
- **Failure:** Tube retracts faster than the cut. The Tower retracts all visible tubes and does not extend again for 1 minute.
- **Touching the Tower body:** Triggers full retraction for 1 minute. Any contact — even incidental — counts.

**Climbing:**
The surface is slick. DC 14 Body to ascend (standard tree is DC 10). Higher pores produce more tubes — up to 3 harvestable per successful check near the crown. Falls from the upper section carry real risk.

---

## GM Notes

Three to five Tower Creatures grow at the deepest point of The Trench. Patience is the skill being tested — not combat, not navigation. A party that rushes will harvest nothing. A patient party with solid Body checks can collect 5–8 tubes in a single visit.

The towers have been growing here longer than anyone can establish. Some have ruins growing around their base — the architecture adapted to them.

The pulse sound from the towers is regular. Its absence is the tell. If the towers go silent, something else has entered The Trench.
