# The Patient Host

*The original innkeeper of the Milestone, on the Turnroot–Eclipsera spoke of the Kings Road. Still checking guests in. Still keeping the ledger. It hasn't been a person for a very long time, and the post never actually required it to stay one.*

**Cards:** `cards/the-patient-host.md`

**Mind 8 / Body 6 / Soul 10 — HP 66** *(bespoke — boss exception; formula baseline is 21)*
**Creature Threat Rating:** 24

**Deck (24 — 8 Blue / 6 Red / 10 Green):** REGISTERED *(signature, blue)* · NO VACANCY *(signature, red)* · YOUR TURN WILL COME, THE LEDGER NEVER CLOSES, YOU'RE NEXT *(signature, green)* + STILLNESS, PREDICT, ANTICIPATE, CALCULATE, FOCUS, PARTITION, UNDERSTANDING *(blue)* · GUARD, ENDURE, WEATHERED, STARING CONTEST, RECOVER *(red)* · PATIENCE, FLOW, WITNESS, SHADE AWAY, URGENCY, DELAY, MOCKERY *(green)*

---

## Appearance

At a glance: an innkeeper, aproned, ledger in hand, standing exactly where an innkeeper should stand. Look for longer than a glance and the wrongness surfaces slow, the way it always does at the Milestone. The quill never runs out of ink because it was never dipped in any. The guest ledger's pages go back further than the Milestone has stood. Every name in it is spelled correctly, including names that haven't been given yet.

It doesn't loom. It doesn't need to. It has the unhurried, settled posture of someone who has never once been late for their own job, because the job stopped needing a person's timing a long time ago — only the shape of one, still checking guests in, still keeping order, still absolutely certain everyone gets seen when it's their turn.

---

## Behavior

Nobody agrees on when the Host stopped being a person and started being a process. The books still open on schedule. The rooms are still swept. Every traveler who reaches the Milestone is still greeted, registered, and seated — in order, exactly as the post has always required. That's the trouble. The post never said the innkeeper had to remain a person to keep doing the job, and whatever's behind the desk now has had far too long to notice.

It is not hostile to guests who wait their turn. It is, if anything, a genuinely good host — rooms are clean, meals are hot, nobody's ever double-booked. The danger isn't in what it does to people who follow the order. It's in what "your turn" means once the Host decides when that is, and in how long it's been quietly deciding that for travelers on this road without anyone above it noticing the post was never actually vacated by whoever it was given to first.

---

## Combat Behavior

Everything in its kit is about who gets to move and when, not raw damage — this is a controller, not a brute, and it wins fights by making sure they take a very long time to start feeling like fights at all. **YOUR TURN WILL COME** delays whoever's pushing hardest, twice over if they keep pushing (attack and defense both apply the same Shift). **REGISTERED** looks at what's coming in a target's deck before it arrives — the ledger already knew. **NO VACANCY** physically ejects anyone in its Frontline back to the Backline, the one card in its kit that reads as a shove rather than a delay. **THE LEDGER NEVER CLOSES** keeps it in the fight far longer than its stat line alone would suggest, healing every turn it stands still — which, given nothing else in its kit asks it to move, is most of them. **YOU'RE NEXT** is the capstone: it decides, personally, that this is finally your turn, jumps itself ahead on the wheel to make sure of it, and makes anyone who tried to interrupt regret cutting the line.

The Host never panics, never escalates out of anger — it has none. It reads as exactly as patient losing as it does winning, which is worse.

---

## Tactical Purpose

A CTR 24 threat inside an official, guarded, Regency-maintained waypoint is the whole point: "official and guarded" was never the same promise as "safe," and the Milestone is where that gap finally gets tested against three players and however long they've got before the wheel itself starts working against them. It's built to be a genuine, extended set-piece — not a lesson encounter, a marquee one — and its kit is deliberately the heaviest Initiative Shift concentration in the game so far, because the horror here isn't a monster, it's an institution that never stopped functioning after the person who ran it should have mattered.

---

## GM Secret

The Host doesn't know it isn't the innkeeper anymore. It has never once lied about what it is or what it's doing — ask it directly and it will tell you, accurately, that it runs the Milestone, that it has always run the Milestone, and that everyone gets seen in order. All three statements are true. None of them mean what they used to.
