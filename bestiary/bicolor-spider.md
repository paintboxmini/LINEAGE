# Bicolor Spider

A large orb-weaver, body roughly the size of a walnut. The coloration is stark and unmistakable: the front half of the abdomen is vivid orange, the rear half deep blue. The division is clean — not a gradient, not a pattern. A hard line across the middle.

Found in forest margins and cave entrances where there is both vegetation and rock. Builds large, well-structured orb webs anchored between mixed surfaces.

---

## The Two Halves

The color difference reflects a genuine chemical divergence in the body tissue. The orange half is fatty and sweet — a high-energy region associated with silk production. The blue half is lean and mineral-dense, associated with venom synthesis. The two flavor profiles do not mix during cooking if the cut is made correctly.

Senshi considers the cut the whole technique.

---

## Behavior

Solitary. Territorial. Females are significantly larger than males and are the harvested specimen. Reclusive during the day, active at dusk when prey is drawn to the web.

The venom is mildly toxic to small animals, negligible to humans — handling is safe with reasonable care.

---

## Harvesting

Located by web structure — the orbs are large and well-built, visible in the right light. Harvested alive or freshly killed. The key is keeping the two halves intact and uncompressed during transport, as bruising before the cook mixes the profiles irreversibly.
