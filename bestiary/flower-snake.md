# Flower Snake

*Small ambush predator. Lives in canopy clutter, fallen crowns, and blossom-heavy tangles in the Turnroot Weald. Not a Pressure-gated spawn — it's here because the tree is. See `quests/turnroot-weald-adventure.md`, The Fallen Tree.*

**Cards:** `cards/flower-snake.md`

**Mind 3 / Body 1 / Soul 2 — HP 8**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 6

**Deck (6 — 3 Blue / 1 Red / 2 Green):** BLOOM STILLNESS, PETAL FEINT, VENOM MIND *(blue)* · NEEDLE BITE *(red)* · COIL DROP, SHED *(green)*

---

## Description

No longer than a forearm. Body patterned like fallen petals and wet bark — pink-white and grey-brown in broken bands. The head is narrow; the strike is a straight line, not a wide flare. When still, it is a flower cluster. When it moves, it is already too close.

---

## Behavior

Ambush only. It does not hunt a full party on open ground — it waits on vertical approaches, alone climbers, and anyone who has both hands busy. After the opening strike it tries to stay on the climber or the crown: high, narrow footing, one target at a time.

If reduced below half HP, or if three or more enemies reach its position, it disengages — Shed, then gone into the tangle. It is not defending territory. It's avoiding becoming the thing that got pruned.

---

## The Ambush

**Trigger:** the first character alone at height — climbing, or newly arrived at a canopy position with no ally yet beside them.

**Ambush check:** Flower Snake Soul (2) vs DC 10 + the target's Soul. On success, its first attack auto-hits — no RPS, no defense. Roll initiative for everyone once the ambush resolves, including characters still en route (they join as they arrive). On failure, the target notices the coil shifting weight in time — initiative as normal, no free hit.

---

## What Players Learn

| Mechanic | How they learn |
|----------|-----------------|
| Camouflage as a real threat, not flavor | BLOOM STILLNESS's Evade is the reason a party that isn't checking their surroundings gets hit first |
| Ambush auto-hit is rare and real | Most encounters in this campaign start with a normal reveal; this one doesn't, and the ambush check above is why |
| Not every fight wants full engagement | Below half HP it disengages on its own — chasing it into the canopy is a choice, not a requirement |
