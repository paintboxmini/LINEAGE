# Wallows Slime — Behavior

It reaches. It does not lunge, does not corner, does not pursue anything that has already left. Movement is toward warmth and nothing else, at the speed of something that has never once been in a hurry.

Being enveloped is warm and still. The person inside can hear their own heartbeat very clearly. The slime releases without resistance whenever asked to, by force or otherwise — it has no interest in keeping anyone who is genuinely leaving.
