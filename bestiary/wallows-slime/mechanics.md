# Wallows Slime — Mechanics

**Cards:** `cards/envelop.md`, `cards/follows-warmth.md`, `cards/your-own-heartbeat.md`
**Mind 1 / Body 2 / Soul 4 — HP 11**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 7
**Deck (7 — 1 Blue / 2 Red / 4 Green):** YOUR OWN HEARTBEAT *(blue)* · FOLLOWS WARMTH, IRON GRIP *(red)* · ENVELOP, BIND, FIELD MEDICINE, SHARED BURDEN *(green)*

## Passives

**Does Not Attack**
The slime's attacks deal no damage. Ever, to anyone, under any circumstance. Effects still apply in full.

**Follows Warmth**
At the start of its turn it moves toward the nearest living creature. It can be led — a party that understands this can walk slimes out of a passage rather than through one.

**Parts Around the Still**
A character who spends their turn moving slowly and deliberately, taking no attack, is not reached. The slimes part around them.

---

## Tactical Purpose

Not an encounter. A creature that cannot hurt anyone, placed where the party is already deciding what kind of people they are.

The stat block exists for the same reason the Skeinwing's does — completeness, and the rare table that insists on a fight. A party that attacks a slime will win, slowly, against something that never once tries to stop them. Whether that lands as trivial or as awful is the table's to feel, and the GM should not help.

**Creature Threat Rating 7 measures how hard it is to get away from, not how hard it hits.** Soul 4 is the whole creature: it feels where you are, and it is patient. Body 2 and no damage output are the rest of it.

---
## Extraction

Getting someone out is always possible and never contested. How it is done is the whole of it, and it is a Seat Influence question, not a combat one — see `quests/the-wallows-descent.md`, the Slime Gallery.
