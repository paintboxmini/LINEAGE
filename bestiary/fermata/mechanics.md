# Fermata — Mechanics

**Cards:** `cards/dirge.md`, `cards/sealed-chord.md`, `cards/the-held-note.md`
**Mind 10 / Body 12 / Soul 20 — HP 66**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 42 *(above-curve legendary — deliberately well past the standard 3-player baseline of CTR 27, `CLAUDE.md`, Stat Blocks. This is not calibrated to be a fair fight. It is calibrated to be survivable.)*
**Deck (12 — 3 Blue / 3 Red / 6 Green):** DIRGE, STILLNESS, FOCUS *(blue)* · STRIKE, ENDURE, BRACE *(red)* · THE HELD NOTE, SEALED CHORD, READ, DELAY, INSTINCT, BRISTLE *(green)*

## Encounter Setup

One Fermata, Frontline, always. It does not pursue outside the Choir's own boundary — the boundary is the entire point, and abandoning it to chase anyone defeats the reason it holds the note at all. A party that leaves is simply let go.

**A non-combat path exists, and it is not a trick.** A party that enters, does not speak, does not attack, and simply waits — long enough that the waiting itself becomes uncomfortable — may find Fermata lets them approach the Blade's old resting place, or leaves the Silent Ring (`items/the-silent-choir-items.md`) somewhere they'll find it, without a single card ever being played. This isn't a puzzle with a right answer to solve. It's closer to a test of whether the party can stand to be quiet on purpose, in a world that mostly forgets silence is a thing you can choose.

## What Players Learn

| Mechanic | How they learn |
|----------|-----------------|
| Locked | THE HELD NOTE takes a card out of a hand entirely, not just a turn — the first time most parties feel a card removed from play rather than merely countered |
| Sealed | SEALED CHORD shuts off equipped item passives mid-fight — a real cost for a party that's been leaning on gear rather than cards |
| Reading a tell in a fight with no sound | Every card here still resolves on sight, not sound — a lesson in what actually carries information at the table once talking it through stops being an option |
| A fight that rewards *not* fighting | The waiting-it-out resolution is the first time a party's best move might be doing nothing at all, on purpose, for real minutes |
