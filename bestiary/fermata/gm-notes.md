# Fermata — GM Notes

**This is a legendary-tier encounter on purpose.** CTR 42 against a 3-player baseline of 27 means a straightforward fight is not the intended default outcome — Fermata is meant to be *found*, respected, maybe retreated from, and returned to later once a party has grown past their first look at it. Winning outright the first visit should feel like a genuine, surprising achievement, not a math problem solved correctly.

**Defeating it is not obviously the right choice**, and the file doesn't resolve which way is better. Ending Fermata's watch might simply free something that never asked to be doing this forever. It might also let the Blade's own nature start spreading again with nothing left to hold the line. Both are true possibilities and neither is confirmed — leave it open at the table the way the Root Heart's own aftermath was left open before its cause was ever named.

---

## What It's Guarding

The Silent Blade (`items/the-silent-choir-items.md`) was cut here, or found here, or given here — Fermata has never specified which, and nothing forces the question to a single answer. What isn't ambiguous: a blade that makes no sound when it cuts is not a neutral fact about a weapon. Left alone, that quality doesn't stay contained to the blade's own edge. It spreads, slowly, the way any silence spreads once nothing is actively pushing back against it.

Fermata's note is the push-back. One sustained, continuous, unbroken sound, loud enough and *true* enough to hold a boundary the blade would otherwise erode past its own edge, one room at a time, for as long as it existed anywhere in the world.

Whether this was Fermata's job before it became this, or the reason it became this in the first place, is not written down here. Same register as Aege's fate, the Root Heart's cycle before it had a named cause. A GM who wants the answer can build one. The silence doesn't volunteer it.
