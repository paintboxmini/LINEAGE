# Kiwi Bird — Description

Round-bodied, long thin beak, feathers more like fine hair than plumage — flightless, low to the ground, built for pushing through root-tangle rather than open air. Forages at night, root-first, sweeping the beak side to side through leaf litter for grubs and fallen fruit.

Nobody's fully explained the flesh. Cut into it and it doesn't behave like a bird — pale, faintly sweet, texture closer to a ripe fruit's than a muscle's. Senshi has opinions about why (something about a diet of nothing but fallen fruit for generations) and no actual proof.
