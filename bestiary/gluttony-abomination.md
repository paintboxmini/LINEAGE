# The Gluttony Abomination

*Boss encounter. Location: the Altar Room of the Cathedral, Abyssal Ruins. For full encounter context, see `quests/abyssal-ruins-cathedral.md`.*

**Cards:** `cards/gluttony-abomination.md`

**Mind 3 / Body 8 / Soul 6 — HP 25**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 17

**Deck (17 — 3 Blue / 8 Red / 6 Green):** UNNAME, VEIL, THE ROOM LEANS IN *(blue)* · GORE, TRAMPLE, CERTAIN CONTACT, REND, PULL, RECOVER, GROUNDING STANCE, ALWAYS ONE MORE *(red)* · CONSUME, PATIENCE OF STONE, HEAVE AND HAUL, EDDY, LEVEL THE FIELD, MAWS *(green)*

---

## Origin

Once a devoted priest of the cathedral. Respected by his congregation. His sermons were passionate. His guidance was sought.

Beneath that: an inability to resist physical indulgence, particularly food. Secret gluttonous feasts. Escalating excess. His body began to change in response to what he fed it — or what fed on him.

He is now the thing he preached against.

---

## Appearance

Massive. Covered in layers of flesh that writhe and pulse. Multiple gaping maws across the body, each exuding decay. Wields a colossal meat cleaver, stained dark — a history of consumption made visible.

Its presence seems to warp space slightly. Reality bends around its appetite.

---

## Passives

**Layered Flesh**
Armour 2. There is too much of it between the outside and anything that matters.

**Gorge**
The first time each enemy Collapses, the Abomination heals 6 HP and gains Deadly. Once per character, per combat — reviving someone does not put another meal on the table.

**Bent Space**
Its melee attacks are legal against Backline targets. The Backline is not out of reach; it is only further away.

---

## Actions

**The Cleaver** *(attack)*
Body + d8. A single descending stroke. Hits one target in either position.

**Chorus of Maws** *(attack)*
Soul + d4. Every mouth on that side of the body opens at once. Hits all Frontline targets.

**Swallow** *(on a Collapsed target)*
The Abomination begins consuming a Collapsed character where they lie. At the start of its next turn, if that character is still Collapsed and still in its position, they take 6 unpreventable damage. Allies have one turn to heal them, stand them up, or drag them clear.

---

## Tremors

Every melee attack the Abomination lands shakes the cathedral, and the door deeper in responds to the vibration (`quests/abyssal-ruins-cathedral.md`).

A tremor is not enough on its own. On the turn immediately following one, a character may spend their action to move, strike, or sound in time with it — Reason or Senses, DC 13. **Three successes open the door.**

The party needs the Abomination swinging, needs it connecting, and needs someone to stop fighting long enough to answer. A party that turtles perfectly never opens the door. A party that never breaks off to sync never opens it either.

---

## The Artwork

The altar carvings and the cathedral's friezes correspond to the Abomination's behavior. A Reason check (DC 15) against them, made during combat, reveals which of its three attack profiles it will use on its next turn. The check may be repeated each cycle.

---

## GM Notes

Body 8 against Mind 3 is the whole creature. It hits harder than anything else in the bestiary and it holds three cards. Hand size is the seam — a party that forces it to spend defenses runs it out of options in a way no amount of HP would.

Bent Space removes the party's usual answer to a heavy melee boss. Retreating to the Backline buys nothing. What it does buy is *spacing* against Chorus of Maws, which only reaches the Frontline — so the position choice becomes which attack you would rather eat, not whether you get hit.

Tremors and Gorge pull against each other on purpose. The door needs the Abomination swinging and landing; Gorge means every character who goes down feeds it. Running the fight long is correct and dangerous at the same time.
