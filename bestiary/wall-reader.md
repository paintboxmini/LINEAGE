# Wall-Reader

**Cards:** `cards/wall-reader.md` *(pending)*

Taller than a human by a full head. Pale — the color of stone that has never seen light. No eyes. Smooth skin where they would be, unbroken. What it has instead: hands. Many of them, arranged along its body at different heights, all pressed flat against the wall as it moves. Each hand reads the stone independently. Together they give it a picture of everything that touches the labyrinth walls.

It is not hunting. It is navigating. It has been navigating for a very long time. The distinction between those two things depends entirely on whether the party keeps their hand on the wall.

**Mind 3 / Body 4 / Soul 4 — HP 18**

**Passive — Wall-Sense:** The Wall-Reader cannot be Blinded. It is always aware of the position and movement of any creature in contact with the walls of its current passage. Darkness, the Silent Room's silence, and Obscure do not affect it.

**Passive — Triggered:** The Wall-Reader is non-aggressive. If the wall-following rule is broken within its current passage, it stops. Turns toward the disruption. Waits three seconds. Then engages.

---

## Behavior

**If the party follows the same wall:** It moves alongside them. Unhurried. It does not acknowledge them. If the party stops, it stops. If they move, it moves. At a fork where their wall-following diverges from its own, it peels away without reaction and is gone.

**If the party follows the opposite wall:** It passes them at junctions going the other direction. A glimpse of something large and pale, many hands trailing the stone, and then it's around the corner.

**Being followed by it:** The party will hear it before they see it — not footsteps, but the soft continuous press-and-drag of many hands across stone. Getting closer. It is following the same wall they are. It has no interest in them as long as the rule holds.

**In combat:** Methodical. It does not rush. It uses its many hands to grapple and press targets against the walls. A creature it has pinned is Rooted until it breaks free.

---

## Notes

The Wall-Reader does not follow the party out of The Coil. It does not approach the center. It has a route and it keeps it.

It has been in the labyrinth long enough that the stone near its usual passages is worn smooth at multiple heights — not just shoulder-height like a human hand, but at knee-height, chest-height, above the head. The party may notice this before they see it.
