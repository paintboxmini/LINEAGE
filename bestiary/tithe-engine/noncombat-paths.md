# Tithe Engine — Noncombat Paths

- **Maintenance Language** (DC 16 Mind): A Scroll-Crafter or Forge-Master can attempt to speak the engine's maintenance language and issue a shutdown command. It listens. Once.
- **The Prisoner** (DC 13 Soul / Read): The prisoner in the cage knows the engine's exhaust cycle. Getting a message through reveals the timing to act between pulses — no Ash Tax that cycle.
