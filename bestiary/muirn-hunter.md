# Muirn Hunter

**Cards:** `cards/muirn-hunter.md`

**Vaun's hunters** (`characters/vaun.md`) — family, every one of them, sent out to find Osha and finish it (`characters/osha.md`). **Three ride with him.** *One of the three is Tamm* (`characters/tamm.md`), who runs from this sheet like the others until the moment he cannot.

**Mind 2 / Body 3 / Soul 2 — HP 16**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 7

**Deck (7 — 2 Blue / 3 Red / 2 Green):** CAST THE NET, PINNED *(blue)* · INTO THE MUD, GRAPPLE, SKEWER *(red)* · CLOSE THE RING, SHADE AWAY *(green)*

**Trait — Better in the Water:** while fighting in or from water, a hunter's attacks deal +2 damage — the same as Vaun's. *Lizardkin are far more dangerous submerged than on land* (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`).

## How They Hunt

**As a family, around Vaun.** They hold a target in place — the mud, the net, PINNED — and close in together; CLOSE THE RING is what three people who grew up together do without discussing it. **Vaun's FOR THE REST gives every one of them Resist** (`cards/vaun.md`), and he brought them to bring them home.

**They are not trying to kill the party.** They are trying to reach Osha, and the party is in the way. *A party that steps aside is a party they leave alone — which is a choice worth putting in front of the players before the first card is played.*

**In water, the fight is theirs.** Four Lizardkin with +2 on every attack is a different encounter from the same four on a dock. **Pull them onto dry ground** and it gets much fairer.

## How Hard It Is

*Measured 2026-10-01 against Chris, Kevin and Pat, on dry ground, everyone fighting to the end:* **three hunters alone are a real fight** — the party wins about 85% of the time and usually loses someone to a Down. **Vaun is worth more than one more hunter**, so the full group of four sits somewhere between a coin flip and a likely loss, *before* the water. **That is deliberate: this is not a fight the party is meant to take head-on early.** Step aside, split them up, get them out of the water, or turn Tamm — any one of those changes the sum.

## Unnamed on Purpose

**The two hunters besides Tamm have no names, and that is a decision, not a gap** *(Drew, 2026-10-01)*. They exist almost entirely to be fought and will likely never get a line, so they get a name when a player makes them matter, and not before — *detail written ahead of that only gets in the GM's way when the sheet comes up.* Whether either would follow Tamm is the same: played in the moment.
