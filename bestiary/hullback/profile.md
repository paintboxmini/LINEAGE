# Hullback — Profile

## Description

A hermit-crab shape wearing a fragment of an actual hull as its shell — planking, a curve of rusted rail, whatever plating held together after however many years underwater. Nobody sails past the coast (`world/the-unheld.md`), so nothing has disturbed this stretch of it in longer than anyone's kept count. Hullback has had the whole beach to itself long enough to pick clean everything it's ever wrecked, gets attached to, or simply outlived.

It doesn't hunt. It doesn't need to — prey this far out is rare enough that waiting costs it nothing, and its own shell already does most of the work a chase would. When something finally gets close enough, it moves fast for something its size, drags what it catches back toward its den among the rocks, and goes back to waiting.
