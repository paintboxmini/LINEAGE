# Gene Thief Tardigrade — Mechanics

**Cards:** `cards/adaptive-bite.md`, `cards/dissolve-and-keep.md`, `cards/genetic-sample.md` *(baseline only — variant cards are DM-built)*
**Mind 3 / Body 3 / Soul 3 — HP 15**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 9
**Deck (9 — 3 Blue / 3 Red / 3 Green):** GENETIC SAMPLE, FOCUS, SIDESTEP *(blue)* · ADAPTIVE BITE, STRIKE, BRACE *(red)* · DISSOLVE AND KEEP, PATIENCE, FLOW *(green)*

## Passives

**Genetic Absorption**
Track each card color separately throughout the combat.
- First hit from a color: full damage.
- Second hit from that color: the Tardigrade gains Resist against it.
- Third hit from that color: Resist holds, and the Tardigrade gains an acquired ability:
  - *Red absorbed* → Thorns 2
  - *Blue absorbed* → gains Evade at the start of each of its turns
  - *Green absorbed* → heals 2 HP at the start of each of its turns

The party needs to burst it before it adapts, or accept that it finishes the fight with 1–3 of those abilities running.

**Amalgam Body**
Each Tardigrade reflects its dietary history. The baseline form starts combat with no pre-existing absorptions. Variants begin with absorptions already active — listed under their entry. Track absorbed colors on a separate note during play.
