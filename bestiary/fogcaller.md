# Fogcaller

*Neutral encounter — Basin Fog, outskirts of Eclipsera.*

**Cards:** `cards/fogcaller-basin.md`

**Mind 2 / Body 4 / Soul 3 — HP 18**
Initiative: 1d6 + 3

---

## Description

Large flightless hunting bird. Chest-high on a human. Feathers the exact gray of low fog — not camouflage, *confluence*. The wings are wrong. Too many joints. Clawed tips that grip earth when it walks, giving it an unsettling four-point gait when stalking. Eyes forward-facing. Beak hooked and silent.

It never calls. It doesn't need to.

---

## Abilities

**Fog Vision** — Passive
Immune to Blind. Hunts perfectly in its own weapon.

**Grip Walk** — Passive
Cannot be pushed or pulled from its position while in Frontline. PUSH, PULL, and EARTHQUAKE have no effect.

**Pack Signal** — Triggered
If Fogcaller drops below half HP, a second Fogcaller enters from the opposite treeline at the start of the next cycle.

---

## Encounter Behavior

It doesn't attack immediately. It fogs first. Watches the party react. Learns who panics, who goes still, who reaches for a weapon.

It's reading them.

Then it picks the most isolated target and moves.

---

## The Tell

- DC 13 Sense — you hear the wings fold a half second before the fog hits.
- DC 16 Read — you realize it's not hunting randomly. It targeted the Backline first. It understands positioning.

---

## Tone Note

This isn't a monster that wants to kill them. It's hungry and they wandered into its fog. If the party retreats or makes themselves large, DC 13 Soul check — it lets them go.

It was here before the road was.

---

## Relationship with Pell

Fogcallers don't attack Pell. They circle at the fog line — patient, watching.

They're not afraid of him. They're in transaction with him. Pell leaves offerings at certain junctions: smoked meat, bone charms soaked in fogwater, memory-tokens from travelers. One Fogcaller will step forward — not aggressive, just curious — take the offering, and melt back into the fog.

They are apex negotiators of the basin. They understand territory, offerings, and the patterns of travelers. Pell is the only human who learned the etiquette.
