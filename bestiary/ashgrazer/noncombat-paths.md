# Ashgrazer — Noncombat Paths

- **Read the Frequency** (DC 13 Soul / Read): A Song-Healer or Dream-Hunter can read the Pack's hunger frequency and redirect them toward a heat source elsewhere in the Sink. Buys 2 turns, not a solution.
- **Empty Hand:** A player who voluntarily drops to 0 cards in hand becomes invisible to the Pack until they draw again. Dangerous positioning. Powerful sacrifice.
