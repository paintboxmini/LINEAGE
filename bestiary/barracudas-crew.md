# Barracuda's Crew

*Pirates of Captain Barracuda's ship (`characters/barracuda.md`). Created 2026-11-15.*

**Cards:** `cards/barracudas-crew.md`

**Unnamed, and staying that way** — *they exist to be fought* (`CLAUDE.md`, combat-only NPCs).

## The deckhands

**Variations on five stat points** *(Drew, 2026-11-15)* — *the same spread Drew ran on the Muirn hunter ship* (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, The Muirn hunters). **For example:** Mind 2 / Body 2 / Soul 1 (HP 11) · Mind 1 / Body 3 / Soul 1 (HP 14) · Mind 1 / Body 2 / Soul 2 (HP 11).

**Deck 5**, colours matching stats, **one of them JAGGED RAPIER** — *their only way to Wound anyone.*

## The three officers

**The three who fight directly alongside Barracuda get seven stat points each** *(Drew)*. **For example:** Mind 2 / Body 3 / Soul 2 (HP 16) · Mind 2 / Body 4 / Soul 1 (HP 19) · Mind 3 / Body 3 / Soul 1 (HP 16).

**Deck 7, one of them JAGGED RAPIER.** *They are what makes his fight hard* (`characters/barracuda.md`, How hard he is).

## Running them

**Six at a time, refilled from the reserve, one shared deck for the deckhands** (`rules/gm-guide.md`, Running a crowd). *Barracuda's officers can share a second deck; he keeps his own.*

**Wounds:** **JAGGED RAPIER shuffles one into the defender's deck when it deals damage**, and that is all a pirate can do. *The captain is worse* (`characters/barracuda.md`, Stats).
