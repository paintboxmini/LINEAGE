# Fenwool

Domesticated grazer, kept across Briarwatch's fields.

## Appearance

Broad, splay-toed feet, spread wide enough that they don't sink into the limestone country's soft ground the way a narrower hoof would. The coat is oiled rather than absorbent, and still shears the way wool does, so the economics of keeping them work the same way a farmer would expect. Pale, thick-bodied, low to the ground. Nothing about a Fenwool looks built to run.

## Behavior

Skittish. Herd tight, and herd tighter than the situation usually calls for. A Fenwool herd goes quiet before anything else on the farm does.
