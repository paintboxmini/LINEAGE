# Fenwool

Domesticated grazer, kept across Briarwatch's fields. Not a reptile, not a predator, not anyone's encounter — the thing farmers actually lose sleep over.

---

## Appearance

Broad, splay-toed feet, spread wide enough that they don't sink into the limestone country's soft ground the way a narrower hoof would. The coat is oiled rather than absorbent — sheds wet instead of soaking it, and still shears the way wool does, so the economics of keeping them work the same way a farmer would expect. Pale, thick-bodied, low to the ground. Nothing about a Fenwool looks built to run.

---

## Behavior


Skittish. Herd tight, and herd tighter than the situation usually calls for. A Fenwool herd goes quiet before anything else on the farm does — Briarwatch keeps half an eye on them the way river folk keep half an eye on the water.

---

## Livestock Loss


Stonecoils take them when hunting range expands upward (`bestiary/stonecoil.md`) — no drama, no trace, which is exactly why the pattern is so hard to prove from a ledger entry alone.
