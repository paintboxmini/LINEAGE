# Redjaw

*The wolves of Elowen (`places/elowen.md`). Created 2026-10-06: Drew wanted a wolf on the island.*

**Cards:** `cards/redjaw.md`

**Ordinary forest wolves with red mouths.** They lick the sap off the ringed bloodelm (`flora/bloodelm.md`, Harvesting), and the sap stains their jaws, so **every redjaw looks like it has just fed** — *and nobody can tell a fed one from a hungry one.* That is the whole of what is strange about them.

**Mind 1 / Body 3 / Soul 2 — HP 15**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 6

**Deck (6 — 1 Blue / 3 Red / 2 Green):** EYES IN THE TREES *(blue)* · DRAG IT DOWN, MAUL, BLINDSIDE *(red)* · CIRCLE THE CAMP, INSTINCT *(green)*

**Blank deck:** Blue — EYES IN THE TREES *(Ranged)* · Red — DRAG IT DOWN *(Melee)* · Green — CIRCLE THE CAMP *(Both)* (`rules/gm-guide.md`, Running simple creatures from a blank deck)
**Trait — Last of the Pack:** when a redjaw is the only one of its pack still standing, it runs on its next turn instead of acting. *A wolf alone does not fight for a meal.*

Hand size 1. Initiative 1d6 + 2.

---

## Elowen and the Wolves

**They have always been there, and the village has always lived with them.** Loggers work in crews, come out of the trees before dark, and **never sleep in the woods.** *Those three rules are older than anyone on the island, and the redjaws are why.*

**This month the rules are slipping.** A village that is not sleeping is a village making mistakes, and the wolves have started coming closer to the clearing at night. *Nobody has been taken inside the village yet.*

## How They Hunt

**As a pack, and always for the easy one.** They go round before they go in (CIRCLE THE CAMP), pick whoever is alone, hurt, or furthest from the others, and **one drags them down so the next bite cannot be blocked.** *DRAG IT DOWN on its own is a nuisance. Two redjaws on the same person in the same round is the whole danger.*

**Intent: food, not a fight.** They want the one they can take and they do not want to die for it. **Kill enough of them and the last one runs** (the Trait), which ends most fights before the last body hits the ground.

**How to beat them:**
- **Stand together.** A party in one position gives them nobody to single out
- **Ward** stops the Stagger before it lands (`rules/card-glossary.md`, Ward)
- **Kill one fast.** Every redjaw down is one less bite after the drag

## How Hard It Is

*Measured 2026-10-06 against Chris, Kevin and Pat, run from blanks the way the table runs it, fighting to the end:*

- **Three redjaws are a skirmish** — the party wins about 99% of the time and somebody occasionally goes Down
- **Four are a real fight** — about 93%, and **about one Down a fight**
- **Five are a coin flip**, and six are a likely loss

*The simulator does not run Last of the Pack, so every fight it measured went to the last wolf.* **At the table the last one runs, and these fights are a little easier than the numbers say.** The count to reach for is **four**, five if the party has been careless.
