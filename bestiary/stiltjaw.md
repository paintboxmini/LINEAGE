# Stiltjaw

*Drew's creature, 2026-10-23. **The name is a placeholder** — rename freely.*

**Cards:** `cards/stiltjaw.md`

*A hazard of the water around Vulture's Nest — something to meet **while sailing**, in the shallows and the wrack-choked channels between the islands (`places/vultures-nest.md`).*

**Mind 2 / Body 8 / Soul 4 — HP 38**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 14

**Deck (14 — 2 Blue / 8 Red / 4 Green), run from blanks only:** every Blue is HIGH STEP, every Red is SNAPPING JAW, every Green is TAIL (`cards/stiltjaw.md`).

**Blank deck:** Blue — HIGH STEP *(Both)* · Red — SNAPPING JAW *(Melee)* · Green — TAIL *(Both)* (`rules/gm-guide.md`, Running simple creatures from a blank deck)

**Trait — Shell:** Armour 2, permanently.

**Not a boss.** *A solitary hunter, heavy enough to be a fight on its own, and the dial is how many Wrackclaws are in the water with it* (How Hard, below).

## What It Looks Like

**An alligator snapping turtle on stilts.** Around **200 pounds**, most of it shell — a ridged, mossed, heavily armoured back like a sunken stone — **standing on four legs four or five feet long**, thin and jointed like pilings. *It does not swim so much as wade, and it can wade where a boat cannot.*

**The jaws are wrong for the size of it.** A hooked beak far larger than the animal needs, **big enough to close around a man's torso.** *It holds its mouth open in the weed and waits.*

**A long tail that grabs or whips** — it wraps an ankle and hauls, or it cracks across a deck like a rope end.

## What It Eats — Wrackclaws

**Wrackclaws are its main food** (`bestiary/wrackclaw.md`). **That is what the legs are for.** A Wrackclaw is a black shell in black weed — *you do not see one, you see the weed shift* — and the Stiltjaw stands **above** the wrack, head high on those legs, **looking down for the shift.** Then the jaws go in.

**So where there is a lot of wrack, there may be one of these standing in it.** *And where a Stiltjaw has been working a bank, there are fewer Wrackclaws than there should be.*

**And the party is carrying five live Wrackclaws in a barrel** (`campaign/session-1-log.md`, The dock). *A boat that smells of its dinner is a boat it follows.*

## How It Fights

- **SNAPPING JAW is most of its deck** — eight Reds in fourteen. *It bites, and what it bites stays bitten* (Rooted). Body 8 behind a d4 is a hit worth respecting every time.
- **HIGH STEP** — it strides over the wrack to wherever it wants to be, and whoever it was looking at is now marked (Vulnerable).
- **TAIL** — grabs somebody and hauls them to the Frontline, into reach of the jaws. **On the block it whips** — 3 unpreventable to whoever swung at it.
- **The shell** takes 2 off every hit. *Small cards do very little.*

### On the water

**It meets a boat where a boat is slowest** — aground on a bar, nosing through wrack, held by a slow tide. **The Frontline is the rail**; the Backline is the far side of the deck. *TAIL pulls someone to the rail, and the jaws are waiting over it.* **Nobody needs a swimming rule** — the Stiltjaw is in the water and the party is on the boat, which is the whole fight.

## How Hard

*Measured 2026-10-23, 1,000 fights a row across two seeds, Armour 2 on, everyone fighting to the end. Win rate and Downs are the numbers to use; the simulator's deaths are a ceiling.*

| Fight | Code, Flint, Caine | Code + Caine |
|---|---|---|
| **Stiltjaw alone** | ~99% · ~0.5 Downs — **a solid fight** | **~60% · ~1.2 Downs — hard** |
| **+ 1 Wrackclaw** | — | ~41% · too much |
| **+ 2 Wrackclaws** | **~91% · ~1.2 Downs — strong** | ~22% |
| **+ 3 Wrackclaws** | ~76% · ~1.6 Downs — hard | — |
| **Two Stiltjaws** | ~35% — *they are solitary; don't* | — |

**For three: the Stiltjaw plus two Wrackclaws is the strong fight.** **For two: the Stiltjaw alone already is.**

*The Wrackclaws are not on its side.* **They are its food, and they are in the water because the wrack is.** *Whether they keep fighting the party or scatter from the thing that eats them is the GM's call in the moment — scattering is the more honest animal and a softer fight.*
