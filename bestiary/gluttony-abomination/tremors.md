# Gluttony Abomination — Tremors

Every melee attack the Abomination lands shakes the cathedral, and the door deeper in responds to the vibration (`quests/abyssal-ruins-cathedral.md`).

A tremor is not enough on its own. On the turn immediately following one, a character may spend their action to move, strike, or sound in time with it — Reason or Senses, DC 13. **Three successes open the door.**

The party needs the Abomination swinging, needs it connecting, and needs someone to stop fighting long enough to answer. A party that turtles perfectly never opens the door. A party that never breaks off to sync never opens it either.
