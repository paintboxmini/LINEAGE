# Phantom Tail Slug — Harvest

## Harvesting

Hand collection at night. A lit lantern draws them — they're attracted to competing light sources, possibly as a territorial response to the glow of rival specimens.

Harvesting the tail separately is possible but Senshi considers it wasteful. He takes the whole animal.

---
## Culinary Note

See `places/capital/gilded-tusk.md` — Phantom Tail Tempura. The detachable tail section is battered and fried separately. It still glows after cooking — faintly, then not at all as it is eaten. Served to Tables 1–4 only.
