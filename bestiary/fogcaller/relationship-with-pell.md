# Fogcaller — Relationship with Pell

Fogcallers don't attack Pell. They circle at the fog line — patient, watching.

They're not afraid of him. They're in transaction with him. Pell leaves offerings at certain junctions: smoked meat, bone charms soaked in fogwater, memory-tokens from travelers. One Fogcaller will step forward — not aggressive, just curious — take the offering, and melt back into the fog.

They are apex negotiators of the basin. They understand territory, offerings, and the patterns of travelers. Pell is the only human who learned the etiquette.
