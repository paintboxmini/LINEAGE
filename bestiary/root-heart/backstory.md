# Root Heart — Backstory

---

## Origin — Not Player-Facing

*Drew's call, 2026-08-16.*

Nothing was planted here. Turnroot was already Resonant with the Seat of Change (`world/seats.md`, Change) long before anyone acted on it — the quiet way any place can be before anyone notices. What's now the Weald was ordinary forest once, cleared by machine for generations out of Ashfall (`places/ashfall-wastes.md`). The civilization at Abyssal Ruins watched that deforestation and answered it with a working that pushed the forest's existing Resonance to Alignment (`world/resonant-places.md`) — and sentience is what that Alignment looked like, here. It was never a separate thing grafted on. It's the same claim "What It Is" already makes, just further back: there was never a moment the Heart was something other than Turnroot itself, even at the start — only a moment its Resonance became Alignment. See `places/abyssal-ruins.md`, "Where the war actually started."
