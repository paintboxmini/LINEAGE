# The Root Heart

*Boss encounter. Triggers at Pressure 4, or whenever a party pays gold into Seeker's Price and is routed here directly. Defeating it opens escape from the Turnroot Weald.*

**Mind 2 / Body 5 / Soul 4 — HP 30** *(bespoke — boss exception; formula baseline is 26 under the 2026-09-06 formula, (4 × Body) + Soul + Mind. It was 21 under both prior versions, coincidentally: 3×5+4+2 landed on the same 21 that 3×5+6 did)*
**Creature Threat Rating:** 11

Deck (11 — 2 Blue / 5 Red / 4 Green): FOREST MEMORY, TRACE · ROOT LASH, IRON GRIP, REND, PRESS THE WOUND, DIG IN · BIND, SWAY, PATIENCE OF STONE, GRAFT

*Core fill completed 2026-09-06; the list had declared 11 and named 9 since it was written. **TRACE** is FOREST MEMORY's mechanic in another key — both read the party's colour habits and charge them for repeating themselves, which is what a forest that "remembers every path ever walked" should feel like from the inside. **SWAY** was a core Green card until 2026-09-08, when a rewrite turned it into the pool's third identical Evade card; rather than lose the effect, it comes here as a signature, where an immobile thing that repositions everyone but itself is the whole joke — it took BRISTLE's slot. **DIG IN** is Anchored, and the Root Heart is Immobile: Anchored ends when its holder moves, so on the one creature that can never move, it never ends. Its immobility stops reading as a limitation and starts paying.*
**Position:** Immobile. Always counts as being in both Frontline and Backline (gigantic).

**Immobile means immobile.** No card moves it — not CALCULATE, not HEAVE AND HAUL, not a pull, not a Rushdown, nothing that would reposition an ordinary combatant. It is a tree the size of a building growing out of a crater. Moving it takes genuine table invention: a plan, the fiction to support it, and the GM agreeing the plan earns it. That is a story the party tells, not a card they play. This is also why DIG IN never stops paying for it — Anchored ends when its holder moves, and this one doesn't.

## Read-Aloud Description

At first, it looks like a tree that grew in the wrong direction.

Its trunk does not rise — it descends. The canopy is below the earthline, a knotted mass of pale wood and red-veined bark suspended in a bowl of torn soil. The surrounding trees lean inward, not toward light, but toward it.

What seemed like roots anchored in earth peel free in long, muscular cords. They do not lash wildly. They reach with purpose — tasting the air, pressing against stone, brushing bark.

The bark itself shifts as if something underneath is trying to turn over in sleep.

Where the trunk splits, a dark chamber pulses — not hollow, not solid. A fibrous mass contracts and expands in slow, deliberate rhythm. Sap seeps upward, but it is too thick, too dark, almost granular. It smells green and metallic.

The surrounding forest does not scream. It tightens. Leaves curl slightly at the edges. Moss flattens. Wildlife goes still.

## Retaliation Phase

Triggered at **20 HP** and **10 HP** thresholds.

When HP drops below a threshold: the Root Heart triggers a Pulse immediately, outside of its normal turn. Each threshold crossed also permanently adds +1 to Pulse's damage: 1 → 2 after 20 HP, 2 → 3 after 10 HP.
