# The Root Heart

*Boss encounter. Triggers at Pressure 4. Defeating it opens escape from the Turnroot Weald.*

**Cards:** `cards/root-heart-weald.md`

**Mind 2 / Body 5 / Soul 4 — HP 30**

**Position:** Immobile. Always counts as being in both Frontline and Backline (gigantic).

---

## Read-Aloud Description

At first, it looks like a tree that grew in the wrong direction.

Its trunk does not rise — it descends. The canopy is below the earthline, a knotted mass of pale wood and red-veined bark suspended in a bowl of torn soil. The surrounding trees lean inward, not toward light, but toward it.

What seemed like roots anchored in earth peel free in long, muscular cords. They do not lash wildly. They reach with purpose — tasting the air, pressing against stone, brushing bark.

The bark itself shifts as if something underneath is trying to turn over in sleep.

Where the trunk splits, a dark chamber pulses — not hollow, not solid. A fibrous mass contracts and expands in slow, deliberate rhythm. Sap seeps upward, but it is too thick, too dark, almost granular. It smells green and metallic.

The surrounding forest does not scream. It tightens. Leaves curl slightly at the edges. Moss flattens. Wildlife goes still.

It is unclear whether this thing feeds the forest or feeds on it.

---

## Abilities

**Basic Attack — Root Lash**
- Attack: Body + d8
- Range: Any / Any
- Effect: On hit, pull target to Frontline.
- *Muscular cords reach out, tasting the air, dragging prey closer.*

---

**Forest Memory**
- Attack: Mind + d4
- Effect: Name a card color — until the Root Heart's next turn, anyone who plays that color triggers a heal of 1 for the Root Heart.
- Defensive Bonus: Return 1 card from discard pile to the top of the deck.
- Range: Both
- *"The Weald remembers every knot ever tied, every path ever walked."*

---

**Passive — Pulse**
At the start of each of the Root Heart's turns, all Frontline enemies take 1 damage.

*The fibrous mass contracts. Sap seeps thick and dark. The forest tightens.*

---

## Retaliation Phase

Triggered at **20 HP** and **10 HP** thresholds.

When HP drops below a threshold: the Root Heart triggers a Pulse immediately, outside of its normal turn.

---

## Win Condition

When HP reaches 0, the cavity pulses open. All players may make a **free final action** before the encounter ends.

---

## Post-Defeat

The Root Heart does not rot.

The cavity it leaves behind does not empty. Within days, thin white threads begin knitting the wound closed from every direction. The forest never rejects it. It only contains it.

In some places along its exposed body, you can see rings — not of age, but of interruption. Growth that was cut away before. Growth that returned thicker.
