# Briarwatch Jackrabbit

*Starter encounter. Surface layer of `quests/hollow-below-briarwatch.md`. Teaching encounter for positioning and initiative.*

**Cards:** `cards/briarwatch-jackrabbit.md`

**Mind 1 / Body 1 / Soul 3 — HP 11**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 5

---

## Description

Large ruin scavengers, pale fur, fast-breeding. Territorial around nesting hollows. Disturbed by recent sinkhole activity in the Watchtower Ruins.

They don't fight to the death. At 1 HP, they attempt to flee. If one escapes, the den remembers the party — future encounters may include more rabbits than expected.

---

## Encounter Setup — Ruined Courtyard

Start with 3 Jackrabbits: 1 Frontline, 2 Backline. They rotate constantly using Bolt.

*Teaching moment: Rushdown becomes the obvious tool. The system teaches positioning naturally.*

---

## What Players Learn

| Mechanic | How they learn |
|----------|----------------|
| Positioning | Rabbits constantly change positions |
| Rushdown | Best tool against constant repositioning |
| Initiative | Rabbits move early (Soul 3) |
| Low HP enemies | Fights resolve quickly |
| Enemy intent | Survival, not slaughter |
