# Masaharu

*Regency-backed detective. Loner. Traveling. Always in the middle of something.*

**Cards:** `cards/masaharu.md`

**Mind 4 / Body 2 / Soul 2 — HP 12**

**Position:** Backline. He does not come to Frontline.

---

## Appearance

Long coat, wide-brimmed hat. Moves like someone who has already decided where the exits are.

The boomstick is custom — longer barrel than it needs to be, adjustment rings along the stock, a small carved sigil near the grip that he never explains. He makes his own ammunition. Different loads for different jobs. He counts his shots.

---

## Behavior

Patient. Reads before he acts. Will let a round go by gathering information rather than waste a shot. He knows when you've telegraphed a move and he files it.

Not cruel. Not interested in pain. Interested in resolution.

When he fires, the sound is wrong for the space — too clean, too focused. Like it came from somewhere quieter.

---

## Passive — Loaded

Masaharu begins each combat Loaded.

While Loaded, all his attacks deal +d6 damage.

After any attack — win or lose the reveal — he becomes Unloaded and loses the +d6 bonus until he Reloads.

**Reload:** Costs his full action. Or — free reload triggers automatically when he Scrys 3 or more cards total in a single round (accumulated across all card effects that round). Or — a card effect says "Reload."

---

## Combat Identity

High Mind. Low Body. Fragile, precise, dangerous at range.

- Stays Backline. Creates Blind and Staggered to set up the shot.
- Builds toward a loaded OPEN FIRE through Scry chains.
- Does not rush. Does not overcommit.

He is most dangerous when he has had one full round to read the party.
