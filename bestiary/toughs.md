# Toughs

*Hired muscle, any port, any purpose. Created 2026-10-18 at Drew's ask: **one group of thugs that can be smugglers, Bellows men, Mercer's men, or low-lifes shaking down the Red Scarves for their stolen goods** — whatever the scene needs them to be.*

**Combat-only, and unnamed until a player makes one matter.** *Who they work for is the fiction of the scene. The sheet does not change.*

**Three sheets: the muscle, the shooter, and the one giving orders.** Mix them; the measured counts are under How Many, below.

---

## Tough — the muscle

**Mind 1 / Body 3 / Soul 2 — HP 15**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 6

**Deck (6 — 1 Blue / 3 Red / 2 Green):** DISTRACT *(blue)* · STRIKE, GUARD, SHOULDER *(red)* · MOCKERY, SHADE AWAY *(green)*

**Blank deck:** Blue — DISTRACT *(Melee)* · Red — STRIKE *(Melee)* · Green — MOCKERY *(Ranged)* (`rules/gm-guide.md`, Running simple creatures from a blank deck)

*A cudgel, a hook, a fist. STRIKE is the whole job; GUARD is one of them stepping in front of another; MOCKERY is the jeering.*

## Slinger — the shooter

**Mind 1 / Body 2 / Soul 2 — HP 11**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 5

**Deck (5 — 1 Blue / 2 Red / 2 Green):** ENFEEBLE *(blue)* · RHYTHM BREAK, BLEED *(red)* · DELAY, SHADE AWAY *(green)*

**Blank deck:** Blue — ENFEEBLE *(Ranged)* · Red — RHYTHM BREAK *(Ranged)* · Green — DELAY *(Ranged)*

*A sling, a crossbow, a handful of thrown iron. **Everything it does is at range, which is the point**: Thorns only bites melee attackers (`rules/card-glossary.md`, Thorns X), so **the Slinger is the answer to a character who has stacked Thorns** — Caine on PAIN IS FUEL, for one. **And it stands at the back**, where a party built for close work has to spend Actions reaching it.*

*Lighter than it first was, on the measurement: a Mind 2 Slinger with CALLED SHOT and CERTAIN STRIKE turned every group with two of them into a likely loss.*

## Boss — the one giving orders

**Mind 3 / Body 4 / Soul 3 — HP 22**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 10

**Deck (10 — 3 Blue / 4 Red / 3 Green):** ANTICIPATE, DISTRACT, INTERCEPT *(blue)* · STRIKE, OPEN GUARD, OFF BALANCE, TRAMPLE *(red)* · BIND, INSTINCT, SHADE AWAY *(green)*

**Blank deck:** Blue — INTERCEPT *(Melee)* · Red — OPEN GUARD *(Melee)* · Green — BIND *(Melee)*

**Trait — Not Paid Enough:** **when the Boss goes Down, every Tough and Slinger still standing breaks and runs** on their next turn. *They were hired. Nobody hired them to die.* **This is the fight's release valve**, and the party should be able to find it: drop the Boss and the fight ends.

## How Many

*Measured 2026-10-18 against Code, Flint and Caine as drafted, 1,000 fights a row across two seeds, everyone fighting to the end — the hardest version. **Not Paid Enough** ends a real fight sooner, and the simulator does not run it.*

| Group | Party wins | Downs a fight | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3 Toughs** | ~98% | ~0.6 | **A warm-up.** Somebody gets hurt |
| **2 Toughs + 1 Slinger** | ~96% | ~0.7 | Warm-up, and the Slinger teaches the range lesson |
| **Boss + 1 Tough + 1 Slinger** | **~81%** | **~1.4** | **The strong fight.** Somebody goes Down; they should win, and they will feel it |
| **3 Toughs + 1 Slinger** | ~69% | ~1.6 | **Hard.** A real chance of losing |
| **2 Toughs + 2 Slingers** | ~64% | ~1.7 | **Hard**, and nastier for a melee party — two shooters at the back |
| **Boss + 2 Toughs + 1 Slinger** | ~46% | ~2.2 | **Too much** for this party now |

**Read the deaths with a pinch of salt.** *The simulator fights to the last character and keeps hitting the Downed; a table rarely does. Six Wrackclaws measured as a death in one fight in three, and at the table the party rolled over them. Use the win rate and the Downs; the deaths are the ceiling.*

**With two players, every group is a size smaller** — Boss + one Tough is the strong fight for Code and Caine alone (`campaign/session-2-scarves.md`, Fights for two).

**The lever is the Slinger.** *One more Slinger moves a fight further than one more Tough does.*

## Using Them

- **Smugglers** — on a back dock, at night, guarding something that is not on Jonas's ledger
- **Bellows men** — sent to find out who has the Bellows-marked crate (`campaign/session-1-log.md`, The three crates)
- **Mercer's men** — collecting a runaway, or making a point to Hojan (`characters/mercer.md`, `characters/hojan.md`)
- **Low-lifes** — leaning on the Red Scarves for a cut of what they lift (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Red Scarves)

**The Slingers are what make this a different fight from the Wrackclaws.** *Six Wrackclaws walked into Caine's Thorns one bite at a time. A Slinger never comes near it.*
