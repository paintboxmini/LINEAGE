# Corrupted Bather

*Tide Pulls Back arc. Dock encounter. Mid-tier threat.*

People who went into the Unheld Ocean and came back wrong. Not evil. Unpredictable. The Void Runners use them because they're hard to read — which cuts both ways.

- *Fluid:* gains Evade, may reposition freely this turn
- *Grounded:* gains Resist 1, deals +d4 on win
- *Surging:* deals +d6 on win, takes 1 damage after attacking

Their abilities should surprise the party once each. Inconsistency is the mechanical expression of what they are.
