# Emerald Frog

A forest frog. Vivid green, smooth-skinned, soft-bodied like any ordinary frog — roughly the size of a thumb. Found in damp undergrowth near standing water. Rare, not common, and thinning further: a slow-breeding, thumb-sized population doesn't recover quickly from being harvested in volume for a single dish (see Harvesting, below), and that's exactly what's been happening to it. Rarer again in the limestone country around Briarwatch, where the soil drains too fast for the species to thrive at all.

The size is a function of habitat, not deficiency. Emerald Frogs from wetter, richer environments grow slightly larger. The ones Senshi sources come from a specific woodland region with mineral-rich groundwater. He considers them better.
