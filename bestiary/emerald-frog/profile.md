# Emerald Frog — Profile

## Behavior

Insectivorous. Active at dawn and dusk. The call is a soft, rapid trill that carries surprisingly far through forest undergrowth. Males are louder and slightly smaller than females.

Not toxic. Some visually similar species are — Senshi is aware of this.

---

## The Burrow Population

A separate, far rarer population lives in the Hollow Below Briarwatch's own burrow networks — Borrower territory, not Senshi's supply chain. The real difference isn't just color, it's substance: a Burrow Emerald Frog's body is solid, hard as the gemstone it's named for, nothing like the soft flesh of its forest cousin. One of the Hollow's natural habitats, and one of the few places left where they're still found at all — the population is going extinct, and everyone who deals in them knows it.

Prized for both uses the forest frog gets, only more so: a genuine culinary rarity rather than a demanding specialty ingredient, and — because the body itself is already hard and gem-like — the only one of the two that can be preserved and set into jewelry outright, worn the way an actual emerald would be, without ever needing to fight decay the way ordinary tissue would. A live specimen, caught rather than bought, is a real find. See `quests/hollow-below-briarwatch.md`, Unstable Cave, for one such find.
