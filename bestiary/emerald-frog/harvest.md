# Emerald Frog — Harvest

## Harvesting

Hand collection near water at dawn, when they are active and visible. Easy to spot by color against leaf litter. Requires volume — the dish uses multiple frogs prepared three different ways, and Senshi's standing order is exactly the kind of demand a slow-breeding population doesn't recover from easily. Nobody in his kitchen is counting the cost of that; Senshi wants frogs, and pays well enough that his supplier keeps finding them. Delivered alive, in damp moss containers.
