# Unity Jelly — Profile

## Behavior

Slow-moving. Navigate by cilia. Do not pursue prey — filter feeders. The fusion response triggers on contact with another Unity Jelly regardless of circumstance. They cannot distinguish kin from stranger.

Keeping two specimens alive and separate during transport requires individual sealed containers. They sense proximity through water vibration and will find each other if given the opportunity.

## Legal Status

The fused organism's chemical output is classified as a controlled substance in three Eclipseria districts. The jelly itself is not illegal. The fusion product technically is. Senshi has opinions about those districts.
