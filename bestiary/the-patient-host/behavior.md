# The Patient Host — Behavior

Nobody agrees on when the Host stopped being a person and started being a process. The books still open on schedule. The rooms are still swept. Every traveler who reaches the Milestone is still greeted, registered, and seated — in order, exactly as the post has always required. That's the trouble. The post never said the innkeeper had to remain a person to keep doing the job, and whatever's behind the desk now has had far too long to notice.

It is not hostile to guests who wait their turn. It is, if anything, a genuinely good host — rooms are clean, meals are hot, nobody's ever double-booked. The danger isn't in what it does to people who follow the order. It's in what "your turn" means once the Host decides when that is, and in how long it's been quietly deciding that for travelers on this road without anyone above it noticing the post was never actually vacated by whoever it was given to first.
