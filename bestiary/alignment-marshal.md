# Alignment Marshal

*Chase boss of the War Engine. Correction mechanism, not guardian. Activates when players interfere with a critical subsystem.*

**Mind 2 / Body 4 / Soul 1 — HP 18**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 7

**Deck (7 — 2 Blue / 4 Red / 1 Green):** TARGETING LOCK, FAULT EXPLOIT *(blue)* · CORRECTION LOAD, IRON GRIP, CERTAIN STRIKE, REND *(red)* · SYSTEM PURGE *(green)*

For encounter structure and reveal phases, see `quests/war-engine.md`.

---

## Appearance

Tall and segmented. Not bulky — elongated. Like a siege lance that learned to walk.

Four backward-jointed legs. Upper body narrow. Head shaped like a targeting prism, rotating slowly. When it focuses on something, faint geometric lines sketch across nearby surfaces.

Its back: hollow channeling rails, as if something massive once slid along its spine.

When it moves: *Tick. Tick. Tick.* Like an enormous clock correcting itself.

It doesn't roar. It calibrates.

---

## Behavior

It is not a guardian. It is not a villain. It is a correction mechanism.

**Its purpose:** Return system to operational alignment. Foreign bodies detected. Calibration compromised.

It doesn't target intelligently. It corrects interference. When a player pulls a lever, disables a vent, or adjusts a rail, the Marshal shifts focus toward them.

It moves through maintenance shafts. Climbs internal scaffolding. Emerges where it shouldn't. It uses vertical movement. It collapses bridges behind them. It shoves players toward unstable platforms.

Not a damage sponge. A positioning predator.

---

## Passive — Correction Protocol

Whenever the Marshal takes damage from an attack, shuffle 1 Exhaust into the attacker's deck.

It does not retaliate. It logs the interference and loads the cost into the system.

---

## Combat Identity

Heavy Body. Low Soul. Procedural, not strategic.

- Restrain. Pin. Redirect.
- Seal doors. Force repositioning.
- Push players toward dangerous terrain.
- It does not taunt. It does not monologue.
- It corrects.

It doesn't need to kill them. It needs the firing sequence clean.

---

## It Never Truly Dies

At most: stalled. Because the engine must fire.

If players destroy it, its pieces don't fall apart. They realign. Like it's still trying to complete a trajectory.

---

## Player Agency Options

**Destroy it:** The engine loses correction. It may misfire, overfire, or drift unpredictably.

**Use it as a bullet:** It integrates into the firing sequence, locking itself into the barrel chamber.

**Reprogram it:** It becomes escort instead of pursuer. Or turns the shot slightly — the last degree of arc the players couldn't reach alone.

---

## Post-Firing

If the Marshal lives, it doesn't look at them when the shot completes. It recalibrates. One leg stutters. A prism flickers. Then it folds slightly smaller than it should be able to and pulls itself into a crawlspace meant for something much simpler.

Metal scraping on metal. Gradual. Unhurried.

The event was a scheduled function. The schedule continues.
