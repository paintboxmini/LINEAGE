# Skeinwing — Appearance

A wingspan wider than a grown man is tall, membrane stretched thin between long finger-bones, no fur, no fat — nothing built for cold, because it was never built to need it. The tail is long, almost disproportionate, more limb than afterthought. The eyes don't work, or work so little it doesn't matter; the whole creature is built around a voice too high to hear and an ear built to catch its echo.

Its color reads its metabolism before anything else does. At rest, drifting, it runs a deep, cold blue — slow-blooded, slow-hearted, barely burning. Active and hunting, low and warm, the blue shallows toward something closer to grey. A Skeinwing that's flushed pink or red isn't excited. It's dying.
