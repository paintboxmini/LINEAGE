# Rootstalker

*Signature predator of the Turnroot Weald. Appears at Pressure 2+ when navigation fails.*

**Cards:** `cards/rootstalker-weald.md`

**Mind 2 / Body 2 / Soul 3 — HP 12**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 7


**Deck (7 — 2 Blue / 2 Red / 3 Green):** CAMOUFLAGE STRIKE, VEIL *(blue)* · LIMB-SNAPPER, SPLINTER-BURST *(red)* · THORN-BIND, BRISTLE, PATIENCE *(green)*
---

## Description

It doesn't kill to eat — it kills to prune.

The Rootstalker severs whatever part of the player violated the forest's etiquette: a hand that named a destination, a tongue that thanked the forest, a foot that stepped around an obstacle. The severed part is dragged underground and replanted.

If the party later finds a tree with a human hand growing from it like a flower — still twitching — that's their missing piece.

---

## Forest-People as Prey

Rootstalkers used to pass a forest family without interest — pruning answers a violation, and people who keep the customs never gave one. That's no longer true. They now hunt the Weald's remaining forest families whether or not anyone has broken a rule. **It no longer needs a violation.**

Older stories, and anyone who left the Weald years ago, won't know this. Anyone who's been under canopy this season will. See `characters/aege.md` (GM Secret) for what's behind it — this file only states what changed, not why.

## Abilities

**Basic Attack — Root Lash** (same core card as Root Heart's, `cards/root-heart-weald.md`)
- Attack: Body + d8
- Range: Melee
- Effect: Pull target to the Frontline.
- Defensive Bonus: Gain Rooted.

**Passive — Predator Focus**
Each consecutive attack on the same target deals +1 damage (stacks up to +3). Resets if target changes or dies.

*The roots tighten around the same limb, digging deeper each time.*

---

## Playstyle

Ambush from roots → Rushdown → Grapple weakest player → Stack Focus damage.

Spawns on failed navigation at Pressure 2+. At Pressure 3+, may appear in the Stalker Nest (see `quests/turnroot-weald-adventure.md`).

## Loot

Harvesting takes a short rest or a successful DC 12 Body/Senses check to avoid wasting.

**Note:** If Pressure is high, harvesting raises it by 1. The forest notices you taking pieces of itself.

**Table Prompt:** *"The roots retract, leaving a hollow tunnel and some severed strands still twitching in the dirt. You can try to collect what's left if you want."*

---

### Root Fibers
*Common — 1d6+2 strands per kill*

Thin, flexible, incredibly strong. Still faintly warm; pulse slowly when held. Wither and die if left in sunlight too long.

**Uses:**
- Rope: a usable length per 3 strands, stronger than hemp — see `items/turnroot-weald-items.md` for the spec
- Bowstring: +1 to ranged attack rolls if used for crafting
- Armor weave: add +1 temp HP to a single piece (breaks after use)

---

### Sap Vial
*Rare — 1 per kill. DC 14 Senses/Body check to collect without wasting.*

Thick, dark-green sap. Smells metallic and green.

**Uses (single-use):**
- Drink: Heal 4 HP
- Apply to weapon: +2 damage on next melee attack

**Side effect:** After use, gain Rooted — the sap tries to anchor you.

---

### Rootstalker Core
*Very rare — 1 in 6 chance, or only at Pressure 3+*

A fist-sized knot of pale wood with red veins. Still twitches occasionally, as if dreaming of prey.

**Uses (craft into one of the following):**
- **Root Lash Charm:** Throw as action — deals Body + d4 damage and pulls target to Frontline.
- **Defensive Barrier:** Plant it — grows into a small barrier that blocks one choke point for 1 encounter.
