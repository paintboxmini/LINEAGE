# Alignment Marshal — Post-Firing

If the Marshal lives, it doesn't look at them when the shot completes. It recalibrates. One leg stutters. A prism flickers. Then it folds slightly smaller than it should be able to and pulls itself into a crawlspace meant for something much simpler.

Metal scraping on metal. Gradual. Unhurried.

The event was a scheduled function. The schedule continues.
