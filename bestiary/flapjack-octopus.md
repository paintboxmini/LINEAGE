# Flapjack Octopus

**Cards:** `cards/flapjack-octopus.md`

*Also a general field encounter — any riverbed or riverbank the GM wants an ambush-and-camouflage fight, not exclusive to The Trench. Not tied to a single body of water; wherever there's a river, a Flapjack Octopus can be there.*

The body is genuinely flat — pancake-flat when at rest, pressed against whatever surface it's lying on. Eight short arms radiate from the edge rather than the center. Skin color-shifts to match whatever is beneath it: pale stone, dark silt, mottled calcification.

When it moves, it moves fast. When it stops, it disappears.

Found in The Trench beneath the Abyssal Ruins. Hunts by ambush. Has learned the safe paths through the sponge colonies.

---

**Mind 2 / Body 3 / Soul 1 — HP 15**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 6


**Deck (6 — 2 Blue / 3 Red / 1 Green):** INK BURST, CAMOUFLAGE SHIFT *(blue)* · SURGE, ENVELOPING PRESS, DEPTH SLAM *(red)* · FLATTEN *(green)*
---

## Passives

**Floor Mimic**
While stationary and Flattened, requires a DC 14 Mind/Reason check to detect. Attacks against an undetected Octopus have Weak until detected or until it attacks.

**Self-Tenderizing**
While Flattened, gains Resist 1 from natural compression of its body.

**Flattened**
The Octopus presses flat against the nearest surface. Gaining Flatten costs an action — played as a card. While Flattened, Floor Mimic and Self-Tenderizing are both active. Flatten ends when the Octopus plays Surge, Enveloping Press, Depth Slam, or Ink Burst.

---

## GM Notes

The Octopus wants to Flatten, wait for detection to lapse, Surge once, then Flatten again. It doesn't commit to extended melee — it scores one hit and resets.

Detection collapses the strategy. A party making active Reason checks removes most of its advantage. The counter is keeping it moving: it can't mimic while repositioning.

Ink Cloud is the reset button. Use it when the party has it pinned — it wipes the board and lets it re-enter stealth.
