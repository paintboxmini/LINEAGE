# High-Altitude Bat

A cave-roosting bat found at elevation — cliff faces and rocky overhangs above the fog line, where the air is thin and cold. Wingspan of roughly two feet. Lean build. The wing membranes are unusually fine, almost translucent at the edges.

The thin air produces a distinctive physiology: less fat, denser muscle fiber, more developed circulatory system relative to body size. Meat that is darker, firmer, and more flavorful than lowland variants of comparable size.

---

## Behavior

Colonial roosting in cliff cavities. Hunts insects at dusk using echolocation. Not aggressive toward humans — flees when disturbed rather than engaging.

The colony structure means harvesting a single specimen is difficult without disturbing others. Experienced collectors know which roost margins produce isolated individuals.

---

## Harvesting

Harvested at roost during daylight hours when the colony is inactive. A single trained collector with the right approach can take one specimen without waking the colony. Rushing or using light wakes the entire roost.

Senshi's supplier works the cliffs above Glasslight Reach. He does not share the contact.
