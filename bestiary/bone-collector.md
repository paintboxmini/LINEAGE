# The Bone Collector

*A Tales Untold Starter Encounter*

---

## Setting

Fog thickens. Ancient spider webs span between trees like abandoned bridges.

**Environmental Hazards:**
- Thick webs slow movement
- Fog limits visibility
- Some web sections are traps that collapse under weight

**Discovery:** Fresh corpses hang in the webs — but they're *missing pieces*. Precise cuts. A merchant's arm. A guard's helmet. A horse's leg. The bodies look *edited*.

**Investigation reveals:**
- Cuts are too clean for animal predation
- Missing parts were removed *after* death
- No spider large enough to make these webs appears in any bestiary

**The Fakeout:** A massive spider corpse (horse-sized) hangs center-web. Investigation reveals the spider died first. Something else has been using its territory.

**Hook:** Soft scraping sounds move deeper in the web-maze. Something large, moving between silk highways.

## The Bone Collector

**Cards:** `cards/bone-collector.md`

**Mind 4 / Body 4 / Soul 2 — HP 17**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 10

**Deck (10 — 4 Blue / 4 Red / 2 Green):** AXIOM, STILLNESS, UNNAME, SILK THREAD MEASURE *(blue)* · GORE, SPARK OF VIOLENCE, RETALIATE, PRECISE REMOVAL *(red)* · CONSUME, STITCHED CASE *(green)*

**Passive — Stitched Armor:** Armour 2.

**Appearance:** A massive caterpillar wearing a patchwork case of large insect husks, leather bracers, bone fragments, weapon hilts, and jewelry — all precisely woven together.

**Behavior:** Methodical. Studies the party. Takes measurements with silk threads before attacking.

**Special:** On a kill, the Collector immediately begins measuring and cutting specific pieces for its collection.

**Retreat:** Retreats at 5 HP.

## Combat Setup

**6 corpse piles** are scattered around the web-zone. Each contains mixed remains: human limbs, animal parts, spider legs, armor pieces.

The Bone Collector hides within one pile each cycle of the initiative wheel.

### Cycle Structure

1. **Collector Phase:** Moves to a new pile (unseen).
2. **Player Phase:** Actions + Perception attempts.
3. **Resolve:** Attacks resolve from the Collector's true position.

### Finding the Collector

| Method | DC | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| Active Perception | DC 14 | Notice which pile has fresh movement |
| Passive Perception 16+ | Automatic | Detection without a check |
| Wrong guess | — | Attack hits corpse pile, no damage |
| Correct guess | — | Normal attack resolution |

## Aftermath

**What they find:** The creature's lair — a workshop where it arranges its collections by size, color, and material. Hundreds of precisely organized remains, sorted like components. No nest. No eggs. No sign of reproduction.

### Investigation Options

**Academic:** Is this natural behavior or something else?
**Practical:** How many people has this thing killed?
**Theological:** Does this violate natural order?

### The Disturbing Truth

This isn't just predation — it's *curation*. The Bone Collector was building something. The arrangement of parts follows patterns none of the party recognizes:
- Human remains sorted by bone type
- Animal parts organized by species
- Metal pieces arranged by composition

### Investigation Checks

| DC | Skill | Reveal |
|----|-------|--------|
| 15 | Investigation | The arrangement follows some kind of blueprint |
| 18 | Nature | This isn't normal caterpillar behavior — something taught it to organize |
| 20 | Insight | The patterns suggest it was building toward something larger |

## Story Seeds

- Are there more Collectors, and do they communicate?
- Who or what taught a caterpillar to curate like this?
- What was it building toward?
- Moral question: Do you report this to Eclipseria authorities, and what would they do with the information?

## DM Notes

The key is selling the spider fakeout early, then letting the Collector's true nature become gradually horrifying as players realize this thing has been *studying* and *organizing* its kills. The transition from "big scary spider" to "methodical caterpillar archivist" should land as wronger, not safer.

The investigation aftermath is as important as the combat. Give players room to sit with what they found.
