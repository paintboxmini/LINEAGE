# Phase Leach — Profile

## Appearance

A six-legged panther built from shifting violet smoke and jagged obsidian glass. It does not move smoothly — it blinks. Short-range teleportation every few seconds, irregular interval, making it difficult to track and harder to hit.

---

---

## Behavior

Feeds on arcane energy, not flesh. Will ignore non-magical targets if a more energetically active one is available.

Near the Echoing Plaza, it may absorb the ambient screams from the air and project them as voices — mimicking sounds to lure targets into ambush positions. Whether this is intentional hunting behavior or just a property of its rift-bleed absorption is unclear.
