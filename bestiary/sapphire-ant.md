# Sapphire Ant

A small ant, workers roughly half a centimeter long, with an iridescent azure coloration that intensifies in direct light. Colonial. Found in mineral-rich soil environments — rocky outcroppings, cliff margins, dry riverbeds where mineral deposits are close to the surface.

The colony's diet is the notable feature: foraging in mineral-dense substrate produces a salt compound in the body chemistry unlike standard ant pheromone profiles. The result is a genuine umami concentration in the dried form, with a faint blue shimmer on pale surfaces when ground.

---

## Behavior

Standard colonial structure — queen, workers, soldiers. The soldiers have a more saturated blue coloration and a stronger mineral compound from their diet. They are the preferred harvest target.

Colonies are not aggressive unless the mound is directly disturbed. Foragers are easily collected away from the colony.

---

## Harvesting

Foragers collected individually with small glass tubes — a slow process requiring patience. Soldiers collected near the mound margin when the colony is calm. Dried and ground to order. Senshi keeps a supply and adds it without asking when he thinks the dish needs it.
