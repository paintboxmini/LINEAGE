# Emerald Frog

A forest frog. Vivid green, smooth-skinned, soft-bodied like any ordinary frog — roughly the size of a thumb. Found in damp undergrowth near standing water. Rare, not common, and thinning further: a slow-breeding, thumb-sized population doesn't recover quickly from being harvested in volume for a single dish (see Harvesting, below), and that's exactly what's been happening to it. Rarer again in the limestone country around Briarwatch, where the soil drains too fast for the species to thrive at all.

The size is a function of habitat, not deficiency. Emerald Frogs from wetter, richer environments grow slightly larger. The ones Senshi sources come from a specific woodland region with mineral-rich groundwater. He considers them better.

---

## Behavior

Insectivorous. Active at dawn and dusk. The call is a soft, rapid trill that carries surprisingly far through forest undergrowth. Males are louder and slightly smaller than females.

Not toxic. Some visually similar species are — Senshi is aware of this.

---

## Harvesting

Hand collection near water at dawn, when they are active and visible. Easy to spot by color against leaf litter. Requires volume — the dish uses multiple frogs prepared three different ways, and Senshi's standing order is exactly the kind of demand a slow-breeding population doesn't recover from easily. Nobody in his kitchen is counting the cost of that; Senshi wants frogs, and pays well enough that his supplier keeps finding them. Delivered alive, in damp moss containers.

## Culinary Note

See `locations/gilded-tusk.md` — Miniature Emerald Frog Dumplings. Prepared three ways: steamed, fried, in broth — served together. The party at Table 7 refused on the basis of size. Senshi has not forgiven them.

## The Burrow Population

A separate, far rarer population lives in the Hollow Below Briarwatch's own burrow networks — Borrower territory, not Senshi's supply chain. The real difference isn't just color, it's substance: a Burrow Emerald Frog's body is solid, hard as the gemstone it's named for, nothing like the soft flesh of its forest cousin. One of the Hollow's natural habitats, and one of the few places left where they're still found at all — the population is going extinct, and everyone who deals in them knows it.

Prized for both uses the forest frog gets, only more so: a genuine culinary rarity rather than a demanding specialty ingredient, and — because the body itself is already hard and gem-like — the only one of the two that can be preserved and set into jewelry outright, worn the way an actual emerald would be, without ever needing to fight decay the way ordinary tissue would. A live specimen, caught rather than bought, is a real find. See `quests/hollow-below-briarwatch.md`, Unstable Cave, for one such find.
