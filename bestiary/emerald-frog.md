# Emerald Frog

A forest frog. Vivid green, smooth-skinned, roughly the size of a thumb. Found in damp undergrowth near standing water — common in temperate forests, less common in the limestone country around Briarwatch, where the soil drains too fast.

The size is a function of habitat, not deficiency. Emerald Frogs from wetter, richer environments grow slightly larger. The ones Senshi sources come from a specific woodland region with mineral-rich groundwater. He considers them better.

---

## Behavior

Insectivorous. Active at dawn and dusk. The call is a soft, rapid trill that carries surprisingly far through forest undergrowth. Males are louder and slightly smaller than females.

Not toxic. Some visually similar species are — Senshi is aware of this.

---

## Harvesting

Hand collection near water at dawn, when they are active and visible. Easy to spot by color against leaf litter. Requires volume — the dish uses multiple frogs prepared three different ways. Senshi's supplier delivers them alive in damp moss containers.

---

## Culinary Note

See `locations/gilded-tusk.md` — Miniature Emerald Frog Dumplings. Prepared three ways: steamed, fried, in broth — served together. The party at Table 7 refused on the basis of size. Senshi has not forgiven them.

## The Burrow Population

A separate, far rarer population lives in the Hollow Below Briarwatch's own burrow networks — Borrower territory, not Senshi's supply chain. Deeper, truer coloring than the common woodland variety: less "vivid green," more the actual color of the gemstone the species is named for. One of the Hollow's natural habitats, and one of the few places left where they're still found at all — the population is going extinct, and everyone who deals in them knows it.

Prized for both uses the common variety gets, only more so: a genuine culinary rarity rather than a specialty ingredient, and — unlike the common frog — valuable enough to be preserved and set into jewelry outright, worn the way an actual emerald would be. A live specimen, caught rather than bought, is a real find. See `quests/hollow-below-briarwatch.md`, Unstable Cave, for one such find.
