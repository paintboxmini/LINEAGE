# Weeping Wolf

*The grown Ocellus. Who it is, what it eats and why the trade runs on its pups: `bestiary/ocellus.md`, The Grown Ocellus. This file is the fight.*

**Cards:** `cards/weeping-wolf.md`

**A mini-boss** *(Drew, 2026-10-02)* — one wolf is an encounter on its own.

**Mind 4 / Body 5 / Soul 4 — HP 28**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 13 *(and it acts twice a round — read it as well above that)*

**Deck (13 — 4 Blue / 5 Red / 4 Green):** OUT OF MIND, PETRIFYING GAZE, MARKED, FOCUS *(blue)* · LAST THING YOU SEE, MAUL, BLINDSIDE, GORE, SKEWER *(red)* · WEEPING VEIL, SHADE AWAY, INSTINCT, QUICKEN *(green)*

**Hand size 4.** Its own deck, not a blank deck — it is a boss.

---

## Traits

**Trait — Never Still:** **the weeping wolf has two tokens on the initiative wheel** (`rules/combat.md`, Initiative), rolled separately, and **takes a full turn at each** — draw, Action and free action. *An exception to one token each, and it says so here because a creature may break a rule only on its face.* Initiative Shift moves one token at a time, so a party that delays one has bought a gap, not stopped the wolf.

**Trait — A Hundred Eyes:** **the weeping wolf cannot gain Blind.** *There is no covering that many eyes.*

**And it starts unseen.** A weeping wolf is never noticed until it is close (`bestiary/ocellus.md`, The Grown Ocellus) — **the first exchange of the fight is its attack, undefended**, unless somebody had a real reason to be watching for it.

---

## How It Fights

**It takes the party apart one sense at a time.** WEEPING VEIL whips the tears across a whole position and Blinds everyone in it. OUT OF MIND makes a character lose track of it altogether. PETRIFYING GAZE holds one of them still for a moment that costs them their next attack or defence. **Then the red cards land on whoever is left looking at it.**

**Two turns a round is the danger, not the damage.** Its die and its stats are a strong creature's, not a monster's. What makes it a mini-boss is that it gets twice as many chances to stack those effects, and **a character who is Blind, has lost track of it and is Staggered is a character it gets to eat.**

**How to beat it:**
- **Ward** stops the next Blind, Staggered or Rooted before it lands (`rules/card-glossary.md`, Ward) — the party's best answer to the whole kit, if it is up before the wolf moves
- **Spread out.** WEEPING VEIL only hits one position
- **Watch for it before it arrives.** The undefended opening is the most dangerous single exchange, and the only one the party can stop by preparing
- **Delay a token.** It still gets two turns, but not back to back

## How Hard It Is

*Measured 2026-10-02 against Chris, Kevin and Pat, fighting to the end.* **One wolf taking one turn a round, the party wins 94%**; two whole wolves, 64%. **The real wolf sits between those** — two turns a round on one body of HP — **so expect the party to win most of the time and to lose somebody to a Down doing it.** *That is before the things the simulator cannot run: the undefended opener, OUT OF MIND, and WEEPING VEIL hitting a whole position.* All three push it harder, which is the right direction for a mini-boss, and **the party that prepares for it — watching for it, spreading out, Ward up — takes most of that back.**
