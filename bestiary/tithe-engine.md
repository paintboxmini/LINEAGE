# The Tithe Engine

*War relic encounter. Location: the Calcine Flats, where the war machines died and didn't stop moving.*

**Cards:** `cards/ash-exhaust.md`, `cards/ash-redirect.md`, `cards/cinder-spiral.md`, `cards/harvest-chain.md`, `cards/thermal-vector.md`, `cards/tithe-collection.md`, `cards/vent-cycle.md`

---

## The Tithe Engine

RED — BODY dominant. It is the Frontline. There is no going around it.

**Trigger:** Players cross a stretch of open ground. Something beneath it remembers what open ground is for.

**Pressure — The Ash Tax:** At the top of each turn after drawing, players must discard 1 card face-up. Players choose what they're willing to lose.

**Cost:** Even if they destroy it, the engine vents on death. Everyone in Frontline makes a Body save DC 13 or drops 1 permanent Body until long rest. Ashfall doesn't let you leave clean.

**Reveal:** The engine isn't attacking them. It's harvesting. There's a cage on its undercarriage. Someone is already inside. Alive. Deciding whether to fight changes the math on that life.

---

## Scorch Drones ×2

GREEN — SOUL, Backline. They circle, they redirect, they punish anyone who tries to flank.

**Behavior:** Drones punish players who tunnel the engine. They coordinate — if the engine wins a reveal, drones gain Resist.

---

## Noncombat Paths

- **Maintenance Language** (DC 16 Mind): A Scroll-Crafter or Forge-Master can attempt to speak the engine's maintenance language and issue a shutdown command. It listens. Once.
- **The Prisoner** (DC 13 Soul / Read): The prisoner in the cage knows the engine's exhaust cycle. Getting a message through reveals the timing to act between pulses — no Ash Tax that cycle.

## Oracle Seed

A card that costs something to play — but pays it back to an ally. The Ashfall lesson: nothing is free, but debt can be redirected.
