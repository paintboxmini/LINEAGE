# Minotaur

**Cards:** `cards/minotaur-coil.md`

It knows every passage. It has been here longer than anyone the party has met, longer than the dock badges on the bodies, longer than whatever built the labyrinth remembers. When it moves through the corridors, it doesn't hurry. The labyrinth does that work for it.

The pressure track exists because of how the minotaurs navigate — not by walls, but by disturbance. Noise, rule-breaks, running, fear. They read disruption the way the Wall-Reader reads stone. The higher the pressure, the more precisely they know where the party is.

**Mind 3 / Body 6 / Soul 5 — HP 32**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 14


**Deck (14 — 4 Blue / 5 Red / 5 Green):** MAZE SENSE, CUT OFF, PATTERN READ, CORNER *(blue)* · HEADLONG, WIDE SWING, GORE, IRON GRIP, CERTAIN STRIKE *(red)* · BOUND TO THE STONE, LABYRINTH ECHO, INEVITABILITY, BIND, PATIENCE *(green)*

**CORNER arrived here on 2026-09-26, out of the core Blue list and out of the Oracle 63** (`cards/minotaur-coil.md`). *It was never a card a player should have been handed:* you cannot corner anybody in a field, and a starting deck holds cards for fights the party does not choose the ground for. **The Minotaur chooses the ground** — it fights in corridors and never in chambers, which is the whole reason the card works here and did not work there (`rules/cards.md`, A card that needs terrain belongs to whoever picks it). **CLOSE IN came out for it**, which costs nothing: Rushdown is a card for a creature that needs to close distance, and this one has a labyrinth and Corridor Fighter doing that already. *The deck is one off the colour ratio now and that is allowed* (`rules/cards.md`, Paying for an off-ratio deck).

**CORNER is not redundant with Corridor Fighter, it is the absolute version of it.** Corridor Fighter leaves a disengage — an action and a Body check. CORNER leaves nothing, for one round, *and locks the Minotaur in place too.* It is the creature spending a card to make the corridor final, and paying for it by standing still itself.
**Trait — Labyrinth Bond:** The Minotaur is aware of the current pressure track value at all times. While the track is 5 or higher, all Minotaur attacks deal +d6 damage.

**Trait — Corridor Fighter:** The Minotaur does not suffer movement penalties in narrow corridors. Creatures in Frontline with a Minotaur inside a corridor cannot move to Backline without first disengaging — this requires an action and a successful Body check (DC 13).

**Trait — Relentless:** At the start of each of the Minotaur's turns, deal 1 damage to each Rooted enemy.

---

## Behavior

**At track 3–4:** They know where the party is. They are not moving yet.

**At track 5–6:** They are close. The party may see one at a junction ahead — large, unhurried, turning away. It is not retreating. It is positioning.

**At track 7+:** Encounter. One Minotaur engages. Others may be present but will not pile in unless the fight extends — they wait at junctions, cutting off exits.

**In combat:** They fight in corridors by choice, never chambers. Frontline, always. They use the walls — shoulder-checks, driving targets into stone, using their bulk in tight space as the weapon it is.

**Retreat:** Minotaurs do not pursue the party to the center. They do not exit the labyrinth. If the party reaches the entrance arch and passes through, the Minotaurs stop at the threshold.

---

## Notes

The Soul stat is not decorative. The Minotaurs are bound to the labyrinth in a way that is closer to spiritual than territorial. They do not eat the people they kill. The bodies are left where they fell, which is why the party will find them at the junctions.

What they want — if they want anything — is unclear. The pressure track exists because something is listening, and whatever built this place wanted it to.
