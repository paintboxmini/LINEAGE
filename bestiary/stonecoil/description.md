# Stonecoil — Description

Blind ambush reptilians. Several have grown large due to increased diet of Borrowers and Fenwool (`bestiary/fenwool/README.md`). Thick-scaled hide. Low metabolic rate. They do not pursue — they wait, and they remember the vibration of every footstep in their territory.

They hunt via vibration. They prefer narrow choke points. They are territorial but not social. They will not cross heavy water flow.
