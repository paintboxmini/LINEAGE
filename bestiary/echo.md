# Echo

*Ambient encounter — Lowland Fog Basin, outskirts of Eclipseria.*

Not a threat. Not quite.

---

## Description

A shape in the fog. Vaguely humanoid. Moving parallel to the party at the edge of visibility. It doesn't approach. It doesn't flee. It just keeps pace.

- DC 11 Read — it's mimicking someone's gait. Whoever it's mimicking feels faintly cold.
- DC 13 Reason — there are two more behind the first.

If ignored, they disperse before the Fogcallers arrive.

If engaged, they scatter instantly — no combat, no roll, just gone.

If someone speaks to one directly, it stops. Tilts its head. Repeats the last word spoken back in that person's own voice.

Then disperses.

---

## What They Are

The fog remembering people who walked through before. The basin has a long memory. Echoes are not the dead — they're impressions left by the living, looping in the threshold space.

In Appalachian folk tradition these are haints: the dead who don't know they're walking wrong ground. They repeat. They mimic. They remember one thing too clearly.

Echoes are harmless. But they are a signal. Where Echoes are, Fogcallers are not far behind. The basin sends the memory before it sends the predator.

---

## Notes

No stat block. No combat. Do not escalate unless the GM has a specific reason.

They are the fog's way of saying: *you are not the first.*
