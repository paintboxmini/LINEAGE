# Chitterer — Profile

## Behavior

Neutral unless provoked. They operate on their own logic — the labyrinth, the deer, the crystal. The party is an inconvenience they chitter about and work around.

If attacked: they fight back, then scatter. They do not pursue.

**In The Coil:**
- Freeing the Trapped Deer causes them to flee without fighting
- Leaving the caged Chitterer trapped or harming it triggers Noise immediately, before combat begins
- They will not enter the Silent Room
