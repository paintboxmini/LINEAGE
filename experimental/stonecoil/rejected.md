# Stonecoil Cards — Rejected

Cards cut during the dev loop. Kept for future reference — some may find solutions.

---

**TERRITORIAL SWEEP**
RED — BODY — HOLLOW
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: If target attempts to move this round, deal +3 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Counter Attack.
Range: Melee

*Cut reason: Timing unresolvable. Effect checks a condition that occurs after attack resolution — no clean way to handle "attempts to move this round" within the turn structure.*

---

**DEAD AIR**
BLUE — MIND — HOLLOW
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Target discards 1 card at random.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Ward.
Range: Ranged

*Cut reason: Duplicates STILLNESS (blue-mind.md) — same effect, same defensive bonus type. Insufficient differentiation.*

---

**CHOKE POINT** *(v1)*
BLUE — MIND — HOLLOW
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Target cannot move to Backline until end of your next turn.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Melee

*Cut reason: "Cannot move to Backline" is mechanically equivalent to Rooted expressed in extra words. Replaced with a card using Rooted directly or a genuinely different effect.*

---

**STONE PATIENCE** *(v1)*
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Anchored — your attacks deal +2 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Apply Rooted to attacker.
Range: Melee

*Cut reason: Anchored +2 attack damage is identical to DIG IN (red-body.md) — only color differs. Not distinct enough as an Oracle reward.*

---

**SCENT OF FEAR**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: If target has fewer cards in hand than you, deal +3 damage.
Defensive Bonus: All enemies in Frontline gain Blind until start of your next turn.
Range: Melee

*Cut reason: Mass Blind as defensive bonus duplicates FOGBURST (fogcaller-basin.md). Also the attack condition ("fewer cards than you") is complex to track for a monster with a small card set.*
