## Concept

A combat chef — and combat bartender, really. Raised in a temple — which one isn't decided; Drew suggested one of the Capital's (`places/capital/capital.md` — Sun, Moon, Rivers, or Forge), but Kevin's leaning toward something more isolated instead, and there isn't an existing isolated temple in canon that already fits, so this is genuinely open rather than a pick between known options.

Chronically shirked his actual studies and rituals to cook instead — and the cooking is where his signature tool comes from. **No gunpowder, and no explosives of any kind.** Everything that goes bang in Kevin's kit is food he prepared until it would.

**Crafting is a real part of his identity, not just flavor — he's partially an artificer, just working through food.** Two lines of it so far: the incendiary oranges (probably made, not just carried — a downtime activity), and energy-drink-inspired beverages for the whole party. Concrete example given for the beverages: the easy/basic ones grant **Quick** or a small **Initiative Shift** (`rules/card-glossary.md`) — real, already-established keywords, not new ones invented for this. Stronger recipes presumably exist above that tier, not specified yet. The general rules gap this was flagging is closed — `rules/items.md`, Crafting — table talk decides feasibility and result, checked against the consumable pricing scale if a number's wanted; what's still open is just Kevin's own specifics (below).

**What he can make is gated by what he's actually got — ingredients, not a free list.** This is why `places/capital/gilded-tusk.md`'s Senshi and his whole Naturalist Collection menu exist: Drew built that connection in ahead of Kevin's concept existing, specifically for this. Full ingredient list — Sapphire Ant Seasoning Crystals (already real, already Blue-shimmer-coded, sold at Senshi's counter), proposed Red and Green counterparts, and a still-uncolored electric wildcard: `experimental/kevin-ingredients.md`.

## How It Works — Settled

**He doesn't use gunpowder or explosives.** He has learned to make *produce* explode, by putting it through some kind of cooking process. Peppers and oranges are the two worked out so far; other fruits are presumably possible and simply haven't come up yet. This is the answer to the propulsion question the Gear section used to hold open, and it lands somewhere neither Drew nor Kevin had proposed: not gunpowder, not a wound spring, but the ammunition being its own propellant.

**The grinder.** Feed a pepper into it. Turning the handle grinds the pepper, and the grinding ignites the flesh — the flesh is the propellant, and the seeds are the projectiles. One crank, one shot. This is why the weapon reads as a shotgun: seeds, spread, short range.

The mechanism carries its own limitation without anyone having to invent one, which is exactly what Drew wanted out of the propulsion question. Kevin's ammunition is perishable produce he has to have prepared in advance, and **what's loaded determines what the shot does** — a different pepper is a different round. That connects straight into the ingredient list (`experimental/kevin-ingredients.md`) and into Senshi's counter at the Gilded Tusk, which is where the good stuff is bought.

**The oranges.** In the kit, no longer deferred. Throwable incendiary grenades — he found a way to enhance the oils in the skin until they're properly combustible. Same craft as the peppers, different delivery: the pepper is a bullet, the orange is a bomb.
