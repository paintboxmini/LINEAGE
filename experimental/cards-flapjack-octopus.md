## Red Team Notes

**Balance:** Red cards build a Stagger/Root combo — SURGE into DEPTH SLAM is the power line. Strong but telegraphed. Blue cards protect and reposition. Green cards are the Flatten loop. No single card is broken in isolation.

**Keyword compliance:** Staggered, Rooted, Disadvantage, Evade, Armour — all defined in glossary. Clean.

**Creature fidelity:** Cards express the ambush-flatten-surge identity. CAMOUFLAGE SHIFT and DISSOLVE CONTACT are the reset cards that let the creature re-enter stealth. ENVELOPING PRESS adds a control angle. Feels true to the stat block.

**Strongest:** DEPTH SLAM (conditional +d4 on an already-staggered target is a punishing finisher). DISSOLVE CONTACT (the escape/reset card, high Oracle value for players too). CAMOUFLAGE SHIFT (no attack but the Disadvantage-until-Surge effect has real defensive weight).

**Weakest:** FLATTEN (pure defensive, no attack — it exists to feed the Armour loop but may be cut if Drew wants more offensive density in the green slot).
