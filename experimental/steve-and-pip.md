# Steve (and Pip)

*Kaine cult initiate. The most unremarkable person in any room — until he isn't.*

---

## Steve

New member of Kaine's radical Promise sect. Hasn't ritual bathed yet when the party encounters him in Mirel's arc. Vanilla. Agreeable. The kind of person whose name you forget while they're still talking.

He does something notably foolish during the Mirel arc. The party will remember him for it — not fondly, but clearly.

His name is Steve. In a world of Mortises, Pazuzus, and Cartographers-with-no-name, his name is Steve.

---

## Pip

Formed from fragments of Steve's consciousness during the ritual bathing.

A bubble dragon. Small, iridescent, made of something that's already halfway gone. Big rainbow eyes. Wings like soap film. Orbits Steve constantly. Has been slowly popping since the moment he existed — small pieces of him disappearing at irregular intervals, each one gone with a sound too quiet to hear.

He will be entirely gone within a week.

**What Pip does:** people flock to Steve. Not to Pip — they may not even fully register Pip is there. But wherever Steve goes, people find themselves wanting to be near him, to listen to him, to follow. The pull is warm and sourceless, like the labor level of the Wallows. Steve has no idea what to do with this.

**What Steve does with it:** probably something foolish. Consistently.

---

## The Arc Shape

Pip forms → Steve becomes an accidental Messiah → the People of Promise gain an influx of followers, drawn by something they can feel but not name → Pip finishes popping → the pull evaporates → everyone who upended their life for Steve is still there, looking at Steve, who is still just Steve.

The fallout lands hardest on whoever organized their life around the following. The Promise community has to absorb what happened. Kaine's sect may try to exploit the chaos.
