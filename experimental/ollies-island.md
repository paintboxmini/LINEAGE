## Approved 2026-09-25

*Relayed by Drew. **Ollie's decisions, recorded — not extrapolated from.** Where he offered a guess alongside them it is in Open Questions at the bottom of this section rather than up here.*

### Siren Seabed

**An underwater village, and that is its name.** Where they live.

### The body

**Their humanoid form has gills, and they stay underwater.**

**They can go up onto land** — to trade, and to deal with other races and groups. **And even in humanoid form they prefer to be under.** *Land is somewhere they go, not somewhere they are.*

*This refines what was approved before rather than replacing it: they walk, and they spend time on land. What is new is that the water is the preference and the humanoid shape does not change that.*

### The transformation

**They take the form of fish they have seen, in the region around them.** Their own habitat is the limit — **a shape has to have been in front of them.**

**Two are stated.** A **Deepmane** (`bestiary/deepmane.md`), and a **giant betta fish**.

**They hunt the betta.**

### Open — and these are Ollie's to answer

- **Whether the giant betta has a home in the bestiary yet.** It does not. Nothing else in the repo mentions it, so the animal is hers first and the file can follow whenever she wants one
- **Whether the limit is *fish* specifically.** Drew's guess is yes — no Hullbacks, no Wrackclaws, no Driftfire — *but that is his guess about her race and it is not written as settled*
- **Whether they hunt Deepmanes as well as betta.** Also Drew's, also unsettled, **and it touches two people's material** — Deepmanes are his. A predation relationship between her people and his animal is a decision the two of them make, not one either side takes alone
- **Whether misleading people is a defining thing about sirens.** *Offered, not assumed* (`bestiary/deepmane.md`, The signal can be counterfeited). Because sirens can wear a Deepmane's shape, and a Deepmane standing in open water is the most trusted danger mark on the river, **that signal can be faked and would be believed.** The repo currently explains that by population size alone — in a large enough people, some will do anything — **which says nothing about what sirens are like.** *If Ollie wants it to be more than that, and for her race to be known for misleading people, the Deepmane file gets rewritten around her answer rather than the other way round*
