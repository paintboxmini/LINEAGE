# The Temple of Unity, and the City's Rings

Built out for Kevin's backstory, but touching real existing canon — kept in `experimental/` until it's ready to actually revise `places/capital/capital.md` and `characters/vescal.md` rather than just sit alongside them.

## What Already Exists (real canon, not invented here)

**The Temple of Unity is real.** `places/capital/capital.md`'s Temple District already lists it: *"Interfaith cathedral at the district's heart. Rarely full."* **Vescal** (`characters/vescal.md`) trained there — censer-bearer, now doing freelance peacekeeping work as half of Trutinate. His account of the temple's philosophy: *"The door was open. People kept choosing to close it. The temple's answer was patience. Keep the door open. Keep the scales level."* Passive, waiting, not going out.

**That's in real tension with what Kevin's backstory needs**, and worth flagging rather than quietly overwriting: Kevin's father runs a temple that's rarely full *because the priests go out into the city instead of waiting for people to come to them* — active outreach, not patient waiting. Both can be true without contradiction if they're different eras: Vescal's "keep the door open" describes the temple *before* Kevin's father became head priest, and the shift to active outreach is a change his father made. That's my read, not confirmed — real fork, not silently resolved.

## What's New (Kevin's backstory)

**Kevin Herb** — surname Herb. Son of the current head priest of the Temple of Unity. A large family, mostly clergy — priests scattered across other temples around the city, only his father a head priest specifically. That family network is where his ingredients actually come from: varied peppers collected from relatives stationed elsewhere, not grown or bought himself.

**What the temple actually does.** Priests don't wait at the cathedral — they go out into the city and hold their services there, which is the real reason the building sits mostly empty. **Kevin goes against that specifically.** He stays back, skips the rituals and rounds entirely, and works in the kitchen instead. Not generic laziness — a direct inversion of the one thing Unity actually stands for (going *to* people), which is a sharper character beat than "shirks his chores."

**Wine as communion.** Kevin wants the temple famous for its wine — and the wine isn't just a vanity project, it's the actual mechanism of the outreach itself: the priests share it with the people they visit, out in the city, as their form of communion. Kevin wanting the temple's wine to be excellent is, underneath the cooking obsession, still wanting Unity to do its actual job well — he's just contributing from the kitchen instead of the road.

## The Three-Ring City

Drew wants the Capital reorganized (or built out further) into three concentric rings, separated by walls, with temples distributed across them rather than clustered in one district the way `places/capital/capital.md`'s current Temple District has them. Genuinely new structure, not yet reconciled with the existing single-district layout — worth deciding whether the rings *replace* that district model or sit alongside it (branch chapels distributed through the rings, the main cathedral still anchoring the original district).

**The Temple of Unity sits in the outermost ring** — the most isolated ring, farmland, closest to the water. Kevin's own ask, tying his father's temple to the quietest, least central part of the city rather than the administrative core near the Citadel (`places/capital/capital.md` already has "the Inner Precinct... true administrative ring around the seat of power" as an existing ring-shaped concept near the center — the new outer ring would be the far opposite end of that same idea).
