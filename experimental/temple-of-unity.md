# The Temple of Unity — What's Still Open

Most of this got canonized into `places/capital/capital.md` (Temple District, and the new Horizontal Layout — The Three Rings section): the outreach practice, wine-as-communion, the outer-ring placement, and the reconciliation with Vescal's older account. This file now holds only what's genuinely still undecided.

## Open

**Who Kevin's father actually is — resolved as a new character, not Lefty.** Ruled out for a concrete reason: Lefty reads as roughly Kevin's own generation from the previous campaign, and making him Kevin's father would force every other character from that campaign years older just to keep the timeline honest. Not worth the cost for one connection. Kept a light echo instead, per Drew's own call — the new character is also a war veteran, same as Lefty, without being Lefty or needing to have ever met him.

**Draft: Cassian Herb.** Head priest of the Temple of Unity, Kevin's father. Former soldier before he was clergy — and that order matters: the temple's shift from Vescal's-era patient waiting into active outreach isn't abstract theology to him, it's a soldier's instinct carried into a new life. You don't wait for the wounded to find you. You go to them. That's the whole reform, in one sentence he's probably said at the pulpit more than once.

The wine has the same root. Not a hobby he picked up in peacetime — something he learned to make or value during or just after his service, carried into the priesthood as the actual vehicle for communion once he took over. Warm, physically present, weathered in a way that reads as earned rather than decorative. Often away doing the rounds himself, which is part of the real distance between him and Kevin — not a cold father, just one who's rarely home, which is its own quiet reason Kevin found the kitchen instead of the road.

Deliberately not mirroring Lefty's specific details (the missing arm, the unspoken trauma) — same category of backstory, distinct person, so the two don't read as accidentally the same character wearing different jobs.

**The war is resolved: the Ashfall War, roughly thirty years ago** (`places/ashfall-wastes.md`) — a real, newly-canonized conflict, not the ancient mythic Engine-strike that made the Wastes what they are (that stays untouched, deep past, a separate thing entirely). Fought over one of the lesser relics buried in the ash, not the Engine itself. This is also where Turnroot Weald's people actually came from (`places/turnroot-weald.md`) — the same war that gave Cassian his service is the one that sent refugees fleeing into the Weald, which puts him on the same timeline as that displacement rather than a coincidentally-similar-sounding separate conflict. Whether he fought defending the border, helping the evacuation, or both isn't decided — but seeing that displacement firsthand is a clean, earned reason a soldier would come out the other side believing you don't wait for people to come to you.

**The middle ring's specifics beyond "Marketplace, Commons, most people live there."** Nothing named yet beyond what's already in the Temple District and Marketplace sections.
