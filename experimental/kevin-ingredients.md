# Kevin's Ingredients

What actually loads into the pepper grinder, and what goes into the beverages. The whole point of an artificer-by-cooking build: what he can make is gated by what he's actually gotten his hands on, not a free list. Staged here, alongside `experimental/kevin.md`.

## The grinder's actual mechanism — resolved, supersedes the gunpowder/crank fork

No gunpowder, no wind-up spring. **A whole pepper feeds into the grinder; cranking the handle grinds it, and grinding ignites the flesh** — a combustibility Kevin achieves by running the pepper through a cooking process first, not anything the pepper does naturally. The burning flesh is the propellant. **The seeds are the actual projectiles**, same shape as shot out of a real shotgun shell. Different peppers (and their seeds) are what actually vary the effect — which is why the family-network supply line below matters as more than flavor.

**Oranges use the same underlying trick, thrown instead of fired.** Enhanced combustible oils in the peel — the skin itself becomes the incendiary payload, thrown like a grenade. Confirmed as part of his starting kit now, not deferred.

**Ingredient supply line:** Kevin's family is mostly clergy, scattered across other temples around the city (`experimental/temple-of-unity.md`) — that's where his varied peppers actually come from, collected from relatives rather than grown or bought himself.

## Already in canon — no invention needed

**Sapphire Ant Seasoning Crystals** (`places/capital/gilded-tusk.md`, `bestiary/sapphire-ant.md`) — dried, ground azure ants. Already established: genuine umami, and a **blue shimmer** on whatever it's added to. That blue shimmer is sitting right there as a Blue/Mind-coded seasoning already, cheap (2 copper, "add to any dish") and sold at Senshi's counter. First real candidate for pepper-grinder ammo that needs nothing new written — just picked up.

**Senshi himself** (`places/capital/gilded-tusk.md`) — cook, Thessian, runs the Naturalist Collection: an entire menu of monster ingredients with real mechanical properties already attached (Gene-Thief Tartare grants damage-type resistance based on what the creature absorbed; the Bicolor Spider's two halves have different flavor profiles). Not yet decided whether he's Kevin's supplier, teacher, rival, or something else — but he's the obvious connection point once Kevin reaches the Capital, and his existing menu is the proof of concept for everything Kevin's trying to do.

## New — proposed, not yet built

**Static-charge hedgehog.** Working concept, no name yet: builds and stores electrical charge in its quills through friction/static cling rather than anything biological-lightning about it. Processed into **quill extract** or **ground quill** as an ingredient — an electric seasoning, for the grinder or the drinks. Doesn't have a bestiary entry, a stat block, or a location yet; started life as "lightning eels" before Drew landed on this instead as the better version. Which color/effect this maps to isn't decided — resisting the urge to force it into Blue just because Sapphire Ant already sits there.

## Open questions

- Does firing a seasoning work like choosing a card's color at the point of loading, the way the color-mirror mechanics elsewhere already require a locked choice before resolution? Or is it looser than that?
- Peppers come from family at other temples; rarer non-pepper ingredients (like the hedgehog quill, or Sapphire Ant Crystals) still need their own answer — bought, hunted himself, commissioned the way Senshi commissions the party for specimens?
- Whether "what he can make" should be tracked as a real inventory (specific ingredients in hand) or more abstractly (recipes known, ingredients assumed available if plausible).
