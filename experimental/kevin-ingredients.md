# Kevin's Ingredients

What actually loads into the pepper grinder, and what goes into the beverages. The whole point of an artificer-by-cooking build: what he can make is gated by what he's actually gotten his hands on, not a free list. Staged here, alongside `experimental/kevin.md`.

## The loads

Each seasoning carries one bonus, and that bonus is added to GRIND SHOT's Effect when the grinder is loaded with it (`experimental/kevin-cards.md`). One load at a time; spent when the shot resolves.

| Load | Colour | Bonus added to GRIND SHOT's Effect | Source |
|------|--------|------------------------------------|--------|
| **Plain** | — | None. It fires; that's all it does. | Anywhere |
| **Sapphire crystal** | Blue | Defender gains Vulnerable | `bestiary/sapphire-ant.md` — 2 copper at Senshi's counter |
| **Cinder flake** | Red | Deal +3 damage | `bestiary/scorchback-beetle.md` — the Cindersink |
| **Hush petal** | Green | Defender gains Rooted | `bestiary/hush-bloom.md` — the Turnroot Weald |
| **Quill grind** | *undecided* | Apply Initiative Shift -2 to the defender | Static-charge hedgehog — not built yet |

Deliberately one bonus each, and deliberately small. GRIND SHOT is already a d8 on Kevin's best stat; the load is a rider on a good card, not the reason the card is good. The spread across Vulnerable / damage / Rooted / initiative means the choice of load is a read on the fight rather than a strict upgrade path — cinder flake is not simply the best one.

**Plain rounds are the floor and they matter.** He is never out of ammunition, only out of *charged* ammunition, which is what makes restocking a story rather than a bookkeeping problem (`experimental/kevin.md`, Supply and restocking).

## Already in canon — no invention needed

**Sapphire Ant Seasoning Crystals** (`places/capital/gilded-tusk.md`, `bestiary/sapphire-ant.md`) — dried, ground azure ants. Already established: genuine umami, and a **blue shimmer** on whatever it's added to. That blue shimmer is sitting right there as a Blue/Mind-coded seasoning already, cheap (2 copper, "add to any dish") and sold at Senshi's counter. First real candidate for pepper-grinder ammo that needs nothing new written — just picked up.

**Senshi himself** (`places/capital/gilded-tusk.md`) — cook, Thessian, runs the Naturalist Collection: an entire menu of monster ingredients with real mechanical properties already attached (Gene-Thief Tartare grants damage-type resistance based on what the creature absorbed; the Bicolor Spider's two halves have different flavor profiles). Not yet decided whether he's Kevin's supplier, teacher, rival, or something else — but he's the obvious connection point once Kevin reaches the Capital, and his existing menu is the proof of concept for everything Kevin's trying to do.

## New — proposed, not yet built

**Static-charge hedgehog.** Working concept, no name yet: builds and stores electrical charge in its quills through friction/static cling rather than anything biological-lightning about it. Processed into **quill extract** or **ground quill** as an ingredient — an electric seasoning, for the grinder or the drinks. Doesn't have a bestiary entry, a stat block, or a location yet; started life as "lightning eels" before Drew landed on this instead as the better version. Which color/effect this maps to isn't decided — resisting the urge to force it into Blue just because Sapphire Ant already sits there.

**Scorchback Beetle** *(Red/Body seasoning)*. **Built** — `bestiary/scorchback-beetle.md`. Stores volatile plant oils in an abdominal gland and vents them under pressure, the same mechanism a bombardier beetle uses. Dried and ground into **cinder flake**: capsaicin-grade burn, faint ember-glow, the hot counterpart to Sapphire Ant's cool blue shimmer. Lives on the warm ground at the Cindersink margins. The gland has to come out whole, which means either a torpid beetle before dawn or accepting a near-boiling spray — the harvest is the gate.

**Hush Bloom** *(Green/Soul seasoning)*. **Built** — `bestiary/hush-bloom.md`. A pale flower of the Turnroot Weald's deep shade that only opens in genuine stillness and closes at the first vibration. Dried petals keep indefinitely and impart a calm, numbing-sweet flavor with a soft green luminescence in liquid — the base of Kevin's better drinks as much as a grinder load. Its gate is patience rather than danger, which is the exact inverse of the Scorchback's, and a cook who reliably produces both is proving something.

Between the three: Blue, Red and Green are all real and all sourced from different places for different reasons — bought, hunted, waited for. The hedgehog stays the deliberate wildcard: it has a bonus now (Initiative Shift -2) but still no colour, because the bonus never needed one. Initiative is the one thing all three colours already touch.

- The static-charge hedgehog still needs a name, a bestiary entry, and a place to live
- Whether Senshi stocks cinder flake and hush petal, or whether those stay things Kevin has to go and get himself

Resolved, generally: `rules/items.md`'s Crafting rule settles "real inventory, not abstract recipes" for everyone, Kevin included — what he can make is gated by what he's actually got, same as any other crafter.
