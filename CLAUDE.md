## Key Terminology
- **Seats** — universal fundamental laws (Time, Memory, War, etc.) on a higher plane
- **Archons** — highest biological expression of a Seat. Cannot act in contradiction to their domain — there is no gap between their will and their function. The domain doesn't constrain them, it defines them. Can abdicate (which feels like annihilation, not freedom).
- **Echoes** — living beings whose identity has aligned with a Seat's domain. Three stages: Resonance → Alignment → Incarnation.
- **The Unheld** — unmeaning existence beyond held reality. Grey water to the masses, destabilization force to the Masons, infinite potential to the People of Promise.
- **The Three Cuts** — Name, Price, Distance. The cosmological origin myth.
- **Wounds** — a card mechanic. Several cards shuffle Wounds into decks; others interact with or exile them.
