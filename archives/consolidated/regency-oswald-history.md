# The Pendragon Attempt — Origin Layer

This is the earliest seed material for the Regency's hidden history — the Pendragon Attempt, Aurora's binding, and the council's animal titles — preserved as it was first written, before a long later pass reworked nearly every detail of it. It is not a full design trail and it is not current canon. It exists to show where this thread of lore actually started, because the version below is meaningfully thinner and in places flatly wrong compared to what `world/the-regency.md` says now.

## Where this came from

This material was moved into `archives/key-design-decisions.md` on 2026-07-19, as part of a wholesale relocation of `memory.md`'s old "Key Design Decisions" section. That section's own header is explicit about what it is: entries preserved exactly as originally written, none edited or corrected even where later canon moved past them. The Pendragon Attempt entries sit near the top of that file, among the very first batch of lore ever compressed into `memory.md` — this is close to day one of the cosmology existing at all.

## The seed, as originally written

Three short entries, all dated to the 2026-07-19 batch, laid down the original shape:

**The Pendragon Attempt.** The Regency council began as an adventuring party that attempted, together, to claim the Seat of Love/Binding. They failed. Aurora held the Seat. Five survivors — Oswald, Cedric, Elara, Archibald, Eveline — became the original council. Each lost internal contradiction and gained an animal mask as scar tissue from the attempt. Isabella and Percival were recruited afterward and don't share full knowledge of the founding event. Oswald's long game, even in this earliest version, was already in place: claim the Seat fully with Aurora suppressed, and the letter hidden at Table #9 of the Gilded Tusk was already understood to be about that plan.

**Aurora.** Bearer of the Seat of Love/Binding, bound beneath Eclipsera. The city's unity runs on her. Even at this early stage, the chains were written as preparation rather than a permanent solution — not containment for its own sake, but staging for Oswald's eventual claim.

**Animal titles as scar tissue.** The council's animal symbols — Lion, Panther, Bear, Fox, Owl, Pig, Elephant — were established from the start as *not heraldic*. They were the shape the failed Seat-attempt left on each survivor, not chosen regalia.

That's the whole seed. No stated headcount for the original party beyond "the Regency council," no combat scene, no brothers, no murdered Prince, no reason given for *why* Oswald wants Aurora removed beyond "claim the Seat fully." The chains-as-preparation and animal-titles-as-scars ideas were both right from the very first pass — they never needed correcting later, only deepening.

## What this early version does not have

Worth naming plainly, since the gap is exactly what makes this an origin layer and not a history: no mention that Pendragon himself was in the room (the original entry only names five survivors and never explains what the sixth or seventh person's fate was, let alone that there were eight); no Pendragon-is-the-Scar identity; no fight against Aurora as an Archon-level Contest; no brothers; no murdered-Prince retcon; no reason Oswald's real motive differs from "claim the Seat." All of that came later, through many separate rulings.

## Where the fuller correction lives

The reworking of this material — the founding party's headcount corrected from an implicit six up to eight (with two companions actually dying in the fight), the discovery that the attempt was a real Contested-state battle against Aurora rather than a bloodless discovery of an occupied chair, the foundational retcon removing a living Prince from the setting entirely, and the reveal that Oswald's true motive is saving his brother rather than personal ambition — is not repeated here. It already lives, in full, ship-by-ship, in `changelog.md`. Search that file for "Pendragon" and "Oswald" to read the whole correction trail in the order it actually happened; each entry there names what was wrong, what Drew said, and what changed.

## What's true now

Current canon for the Pendragon Attempt, Aurora, Oswald, and the council lives in `world/the-regency.md`. This file is the earliest layer underneath it, kept for the record — not a second source of truth, and not to be read as if it were still accurate on its own.
