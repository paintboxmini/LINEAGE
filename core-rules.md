# Core Rules

## Relevance of Cards
The determining factor for a card's relevance is its name, which acts like a parser verb.

## Collapsed Rules
The effects of collapsed rules end when the HP goes above 0.

## Rushdown Mechanic
Rushdown represents a movement mechanic where a Frontline character forces a Backline enemy into their Frontline without altering the rusher's position if they are already in the Frontline.
