# Resonant Locations

*The place-equivalent of a Resonant Person or Resonant Item.*

---

A Resonant Location is what a place becomes when it aligns so closely with a Seat's domain that the domain begins to manifest through it. Like a Resonant Person or Resonant Item, it does not hold the Seat. It expresses one.

---

## How a Place Becomes Resonant

The same way a person or an object does: through history, not decree. What's lived, repeated, and left behind on a place over enough time can align it with a domain as surely as it aligns whoever walks through it.

Unlike a person or an item, a place can't act — only accumulate. Everything that becomes true of it is the residue of what was done there, again and again, long enough for the pattern to hold.

---

## Known Resonant Locations

**Glasslight Reach** — the cliff fractures sing when wind passes through them, and nobody in the town knows why. Song and Silence is the closer fit among the Seats (`mythology/seats.md`, Song and Silence), but the alignment is unconfirmed — the same register Fermata's alignment sat in before it was confirmed. The resonance is real. Which domain it belongs to isn't settled yet.

**Turnroot Weald** — a forest whose sentience isn't metaphor, and whose pressure escalates the longer anyone stays inside it. Named here as a Resonant Location because its nature already behaves like one. Its Seat is unestablished — nothing in the current roster (`mythology/seats.md`) accounts for what it is, and this file isn't the place to manufacture an answer nobody's found yet.

---

## No Confirmed Ceiling

A Resonant Person's ladder ends at Archon. A Resonant Item reaches the same summit by its own separate path (`mythology/archons.md`). Whether a place can reach an equivalent culmination — and what that would even mean for something that can't act — is unestablished. Left open on purpose rather than resolved just to complete the pattern.
