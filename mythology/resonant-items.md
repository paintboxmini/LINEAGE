# Resonant Items

*The object-equivalent of an Echo.*

---

A Resonant Item is what an object becomes when it aligns so closely with a Seat's domain that the domain begins to manifest through it. Like an Echo, it does not hold the Seat. It expresses one.

---

## How an Object Becomes Resonant

**Intentional Resonance.** Built toward alignment on purpose — the maker shapes purpose, construction, and use to draw one specific domain through it, from the first stroke of work.

**Accumulated Resonance.** Earned rather than built. An ordinary object, used again and again in acts that align with a domain, can become an expression of that domain without ever having been made for it. A sword carried through enough acts of War becomes Resonant with War. A tool worn smooth by enough seasons of Harvest becomes Resonant with Harvest. The object's history isn't decoration — it's the actual mechanism. Repeated aligned use changes what a thing is, the same way it changes what a person becomes on the way to being an Echo.

---

## People and Their Items

An Echo in the making rarely acts alone. The things they carry pass through the same repeated, aligned action that's remaking them — and can come out the other side transformed alongside their bearer, not incidentally.

This isn't coincidence. Person and object are living through the same pattern. Both may end up expressions of the same Seat, each carrying evidence of how they got there: the Echo in what they've become, the item in what it was used for.

---

## Expression, Not Ownership

Neither an Echo nor a Resonant Item contains a Seat. Neither owns the domain it carries. Both are alignments — proof that something reached far enough toward a domain that the domain started reaching back.

This is why a Resonant Item doesn't need its Seat Filled to work. A domain can stay real, and reachable, through an Echo or an object aligned to it, even while the Seat itself stands Empty (`mythology/seats.md`, Seat States). The bearer thins. The alignment doesn't have to.
