# The Shunka

*Dogkin. Working lore lives at `experimental/the-shunka.md` and stays there until it settles — this is the roster entry, and the experimental file is the source of truth for anything the two disagree on. Pat's character is Shunka.*

**A warrior people.** Strength of body, discipline, and honour won openly on the field are the whole measure of a person. Cane Corso build — broad-headed, heavily made.

**They carry a Price nobody in the line agreed to pay.** An invading people, the Tithebound, couldn't beat them in the field and didn't try twice; their curse-worker laid an unspoken Price directly on the Shunka queen, so that every child of her line would be born on four legs as an ordinary dog. Three were born that way and they were the last of the royal line — the line the curse targeted is the line it ended.

**Why it's the wound it is:** losing a battle is a cost this culture understands and can live inside. Having something taken that you never agreed to pay, that no strength of arms could have stopped, is the one kind of loss it has no framework for absorbing. That's why it still defines the royal line however long ago it happened.

See `experimental/the-shunka.md` for the full account, `experimental/the-tithebound.md` for who did it, and `rules/character-creation.md`, Declaring a Price, for the mechanic being violated.
