# Factions and Races

Two kinds of thing share this folder because they answer the same question from different directions: *who is this, and what do they want.* A faction is a group people join. A race is a people someone is born into. Both are described the same way here — what they are, how they live, how the world reads them — and neither is a stat package.

**Races carry no mechanical modifiers.** Nothing in `rules/character-creation.md` keys off ancestry. Mind, Body and Soul come from the player, Passives are chosen, and a Glasskin and a human built to the same numbers play identically. What a race gives a character is where they're from, who completes them, and what a room assumes about them before they speak.
