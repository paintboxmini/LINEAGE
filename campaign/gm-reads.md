## The Oracle readings — session 3

*Drew, 2026-11-07.* **Tonight's advancement runs as the Oracle ritual on a liminal dream island** — *the first time each player draws from their own advancement deck.* **The veil thins, and the Oracle says aloud what the GM reads in each of them**, to the characters directly, **so they can decide whether that is who they want reality to see them as.** *Read who they are, never what they don't know; end each reading on a question.*

- **Flint** *(pleasure)*: *"You go wherever the laughter is loudest. What do you do when the room goes quiet?"*
- **Code** *(status)*: *"You say you want to be free. Everything you reach for is a way to be seen. If you could have only one, which would you keep?"*
- **Caine** *(obligation)*: *"You carry what you were handed and call it yours. Who would you be if someone lifted it off you?"*
- **All three, last** *(belonging)*: *"And who is waiting for you to come back?"*

**What each one answers is worth writing down afterwards** — *a yes and a no say equally much.*

**What they answered** *(2026-11-07)*:

- **Caine: "I would still be me."** *Obligation, answered as identity — he does not think the duty is what he is. Worth testing: what is left when it is lifted?*
- **Code: freedom.** *Against the status read. He has now said it to reality itself; watch whether what he reaches for agrees.*
- **Flint: he'll think about what it means.** *Pleasure deferring the question — which is its own answer, for now.*
- **"Who is waiting?" — the Oracle answered for them: their families.** *Ossian, Rynmori, Caine's mother's line.*
