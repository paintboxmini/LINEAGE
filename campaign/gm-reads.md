# GM Reads

*Drew's private reads on the characters — **speculation, not canon**, and never something to tell a player. Dated, so a read can be seen to change.*

## What each one values most

*Drew, 2026-11-07.*

| | Top value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Flint** | **Pleasure** | The Thieves Den, the rum, the pirate ship *for the fun of it* — the honest trade is the thing to automate so he can go on the raids |
| **Code** | **Status** | The Many Mask, a church of his own, the heroic pose. *He says it is freedom — "freedom, autonomy, a place to call my own" — and the gap between what he says and what he reaches for is worth watching* |
| **Caine** | **Obligation** *(2026-11-07, shifting from status)* | The royal dead sent him; his Price is a duty to anyone worse off than he is; he left the army *on a charge*, not as a deserter. *The duty is the point, not the standing it once bought him* |

**Under all three: belonging** *(Drew, 2026-11-07)*. *Pleasure, status and obligation are each one character's way of finding where he belongs.*
