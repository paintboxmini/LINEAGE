## Barracuda, and the Water Wing

- **The party went to Captain Barracuda to buy Samuel's ship back.** He would not sell (`characters/barracuda.md`).
- **But they convinced him that Samuel already had a new ship and was out pirating again.** **Barracuda was furious — and was already readying to leave the Nest that day.** **He finished his preparations in a rush and sailed almost at once**, *presumably after his rival.*
- **With Barracuda gone, they stole the Water Wing** (`characters/samuel-gullis.md`):
  - **A deadwood log was chained to her rudder. Code went under and turned his hand into a giant lockpick** to open the padlock on the chain.
  - **The mooring chains ran through her portholes and were wrapped round her rails in sections** — *getting them off meant damaging the ship quite a bit.*
  - **Too few hands to sail her properly, so she crawled out through the docks very slowly.**
  - **Code wore Barracuda's face at the helm** (Mimicry). **When dockworkers asked what he was doing, "Barracuda" said he was trolling the docks to find the Mangy Dog and sail right past him shouting insults — rubbing his prize in Samuel's face.** *Drew let it stand: a good explanation.*
- **They got her out of the city**, **then painted her, changed every sail, replaced a lot of the wood and repaired what they'd broken.**
- **She is renamed *Fortune's Fancy*.**

*The party has two ships now: the **Holly Mint** — the legitimate trader, **released from the impound in session one** — and **Fortune's Fancy**, Samuel's ship under a new name.*

- **2.5 days out; 1 day back**, *running with the current on the return* (`world/geography-overview.md`, River system).
- **On the way out they stopped at a small unnamed village** — about twenty people — to restock. **Code asked whether anyone there had ever sailed.** *Drew rolled for the odds and rolled very high:* **a retired master sailor lives there, and they hired him.** *Not yet named.*

### The Shred

**A stretch of river called the Shred**, on the way to Pneum *(named at the table)*. **Drew ran it as a sailing section: a few skill rolls, the ship took damage and was repaired, and they came through well overall.** **Rapids and reefs together** — *white water over reef* (`world/geography-overview.md`, Named on the water).

## Pneum

**It went about as expected.** **They arrived, and were immediately repulsed** when they understood what the island was doing (`places/pneum.md`).

**They agreed to help with the island's Foulhaul** — *the old one the congregation calls the Receiver and rivermen call the Grandfather* (`bestiary/foulhaul.md`, The Grandfather). *How the congregation feels about outsiders "helping" with something it treats as sacred is worth watching.*

## Caine at the Thieves Den

- **Caine found a Vessk Lizardkin working security at the Den, guarding the liar's dice table** (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`). **He tried to question him about the family he is hunting.**
- **Hostility was instant, and the Vessk insulted the queen** — *the Shunka queen the curse was laid on, generations ago* (`factions-and-races/races-shunka.md`).
- **Caine went to the bar, bought a lot of liquor, took it outside, poured it out, and set the Den alight.**

**New at the Den** *(Drew)*: **the liar's dice game is owned and run by the Strays** — *one of their money-makers* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Strays, and Lusk).

## Back at the Nest

**They killed the Foulhaul** — and **did not go back to the village to report it.** *Pneum asked them for help and would surely have thanked them — gold, or some reward — but the party was too disgusted by the place to return.* **The reward is unclaimed.** **They are back in Vulture's Nest.**

### Bartho, and the barge

- **They reported Pneum to Bartho.** **He was annoyed**: they had refused him last time, so **he had already hired another crew to check the island — due back in a day — and would not pay the party.**
- **Code had a plan.** **Fine — they always meant to help for free. And they'd do Vulture's Nest one more good turn: take the quarantined barge safely out of the city.** **His framing: the people aboard are going off to die quietly somewhere of their own, and handled carefully they are no danger to anyone.**
- **A very high roll.** **Bartho bought it completely** and **handed the barge to the party, on condition that they get it out of the city** so he can wash his hands of the whole thing.
- **Jonas did the paperwork.** **Code — as himself, in his own face** *(retcon, Drew, 2026-11-13: the Compact has only ever seen Code's face, never Alden's)* — **signed the barge over to "the Red Button Society", a charity that helps those in need, and named Alden Coise as its leader.** *The red button is Code's calling card* (`campaign/session-2-log.md`, Between sessions).
- **Jonas asked for everyone's identification papers.** **Flint slipped away first** — *known on the dock as the wine-and-pepper trader's son, so taken to be keeping his name off a charity; minimal suspicion at most.* **Code patted himself down — he'd left his papers on the ship.** **Jonas just needed somebody's ID from the Red Button Society. Caine stepped forward and gave his**, and **Jonas imprinted Caine's papers into his ledger by light cartography.**

**Caine's papers** *(Drew)*: **made for him on entry to the Nest** — Compact guards took him to a small room and **a light cartographer made them on the spot.** **Very bare: where he came from, and his stated purpose — to explore the region and learn its plants and animals.** **They mark him a traveller, with a one-month pass to stay.**

**What that means:** **Caine's papers are in Jonas's ledger** — *the one cross-referenced against Quartermaster Voss's intake at Eclipseria South Gate* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Tideward Compact) — **as the Red Button Society's papers on file.** **The name Alden Coise is on record as its leader, with no face and no papers behind it.** *Code's own face is now known to the Compact as the man who took the barge.*
