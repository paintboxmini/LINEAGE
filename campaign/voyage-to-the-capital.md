## Barracuda, the first time — a chase that ends in escape

**A sail on the horizon that sits wrong.** *He knows the ship under her paint; she's his rival's, and he wants the party flayed in front of his crew* (`characters/barracuda.md`).

**The lead track: start at 3. Escape at 6. Caught at 0** — *at 0 he comes alongside and boards.* **Each round the party picks one move and rolls; success +1, a great success +2, a failure −1.** *Built so escape is likely and costs something:*

| Move | Roll | The cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Crack on all sail** | Sailing — the master sailor's skill, *or Code's Mind if he's learned enough* | Strain: a failure loses a hand overboard or a spar |
| **Run the rough water** — rapids, reef, shoals he won't follow | DC 16, Mind or Body | **Hull damage either way.** *The Shred showed them how* (`world/geography-overview.md`, Named on the water) |
| **Lighten the ship** | No roll: **+1, automatically** | **Throw cargo over.** *The only heavy cargo is Harlock's crates* |
| **Ride the tide** — find the fast stretch, or wait out his on a slow one | Mind, DC 13 | Takes reading the water; a failure loses a round of lead |
| **Something clever** | Whatever it is | *They blew up the last ship that chased them. He's heard* |

**Barracuda's own move each round, on a d6:** **1–2** he closes (−1); **3–4** he holds; **5–6** he tries something — *a long shot that takes a hand, a hook in the rigging, a signal to a second sail.*

**When they reach 6, he loses them** — *in a fog bank, behind an island, into water too rough for his keel.* **He doesn't give up. He knows where they're going.**

*The cargo choice is the scene: dumping Harlock's FourthEye buys the escape and costs them with Harlock. Keeping it keeps the evidence Barracuda most wants aboard.*

## The water between — thinning the hired hands

*Hired pirates are the cost of a voyage. Lose them to the water rather than to dice; the party survives, the crew list shrinks.*

- **A storm.** *Wind on the river web doesn't blow from anywhere in particular — it breathes, every side at once* (`world/geography-overview.md`, River system). **A squall that hits from three sides takes a hand or two overboard.** *The four Muirn are at home in the water — and a storm is a good moment to slip their bonds.*
- **Fast tide toward the centre.** **The water runs hardest near Eclipseria** (`world/geography-overview.md`, River system) — *the thing sailors plan around most.* A crew that misreads it loses a mast or a man.
- **A Driftfire bloom** (`bestiary/driftfire.md`). **Boats get through it; anyone in the water does not.** *A hand who goes overboard in a bloom is gone — or held, for days, and somebody has to decide whether to go back.* **It also kills Deepmanes, so the river stops marking its bad water** behind it.
- **The Shred again**, or water like it — *rapids over reef* (`world/geography-overview.md`, Named on the water).

## A beast

| Beast | What it does | How hard |
|---|---|---|
| **Stiltjaw** (`bestiary/stiltjaw.md`) | Stands in the shallows; drags people to the rail. **It eats Wrackclaws — the party's barrel may still be aboard** | **Stiltjaw + 2 Wrackclaws: the strong fight for three. Alone: hard for two** |
| **Hullback** (`bestiary/hullback.md`) | *Mind 3 / Body 7 / Soul 3, HP 34* — a big, old patient animal | *Not measured against this party; measure before using* |
| **Gowra** (`bestiary/gowra.md`) | Takes one person under a bank on a fast tide | **One, never two.** *Only if they tie up at a bank* |

**A beast that takes hired hands rather than fighting the party**: the Stiltjaw pulling a pirate off the rail, the Gowra taking whoever is nearest the water. *The party fights to get them back — or doesn't.*
