# Session 4 — plans

*Drew's plans, 2026-11-08. **Plans, not events** — what happens at the table goes in a session log.*

## If time is short: Muirn raiders on the water

**Two threads in one fight.** **Muirn hunters attack the party's ship as pirates** while they sail — *the Muirn hunt vessels that come close to their territory* (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`, The Muirn). **After the party wins, they question the losers — who mention Barracuda** (`characters/barracuda.md`).

**Why it serves Caine's thread** *(Drew)*: **when the family's hunters truly come for him, it will be because he has drawn attention more than once** — *the scrap, the Vessk at the Den, the fire, and now a fight with Muirn on the water.* **These are raiders, not Vaun's family** (`characters/vaun.md`) — *the family's hunt stays for later.*

**Running it:**

- **Use the Muirn Hunter sheet** (`bestiary/muirn-hunter.md`) for the raiders. **In or from the water their attacks deal +2** — *a fight at the rail is theirs; pull them onto the deck and it gets fairer.*
- **Size:** three hunters measured ~85% against the party's earlier decks, before the water. *Probably a real fight with the water bonus; two is safer with only two players.*
- **What they could say about Barracuda** *(suggestions)*: **his ship went through their water days ago, heading somewhere** — *which tells the party where he is* — **or they took the party for one of his**. *Either keeps him as the sea encounter waiting ahead.*
- **What they will not say: anything about the cursegiver family.** *They are raiders. If Caine shows the scrap, they go quiet in a way that tells him the emblem means something to them.*

## Barracuda's summons

*Drew, 2026-11-11.* **Barracuda is back in the Nest and has sent men to bring the party before him.** *He thinks they lied to send him off, and stole the ship while he was gone.*

**His men:** **the Toughs sheet** (`bestiary/toughs.md`) — **a Boss leading, a Tough or two.** *They have come to fetch, not to kill: a fight starts only if the party refuses.* **Boss + Tough is the strong fight for two; Boss + Tough + Slinger for three.**

**What he knows, and what he doesn't:**

- **He knows the ship is gone** and that he was sent chasing a rival who was never at sea.
- **The dock will tell him "he" took her out himself** — *"Barracuda" at the helm, trolling for the Mangy Dog* (`campaign/session-3-log.md`). **So he knows somebody wore his face.** *That is the question he will most want answered, and Code is the only answer.*
- **He does not know where the ship is** unless the party brought Fortune's Fancy into harbour.

**If he learns what Fortune's Fancy carries** *(Drew, 2026-11-11)*: **FourthEye is Harlock's illegal business** — *outside the Guild's licence, off every ledger* (`characters/harlock.md`, The Pirate Guild). **Barracuda could use that to try to overthrow Harlock**, or **simply take the cargo off Fortune's Fancy at sea.** *Either way the party's secret becomes his weapon — and Flint, who has never opened a crate, is the man carrying it.*

**What he wants:** *answers first.* **His ship, or what she is worth, second** — *and a man who wore his face, if anyone will give him one.*

## Also coming *(Drew, 2026-11-12)*

- **The Lizardkin come next session, one way or another** — *Muirn raiders on the water* (above).
- **Erubeth tries to board Fortune's Fancy to inspect the cargo** (`characters/erubeth.md`). *He is Regency-backed, with papers in order and authority to act* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Key NPCs) — **so he can ask lawfully, and refusing him is its own answer.** *He is the one man in the Nest who would know FourthEye on sight.*
- **The new pirate crew wants to know what they are carrying** — *not only fair terms. Common pirate sense: you don't carry something until you know what it is.* **That pushes Flint to open a crate** — *and whoever is in the room when he does finds out with him.*

## Getting out in the bush — the Coil? *(Drew, 2026-11-12)*

**The players want to leave the Nest and explore somewhere nobody sent them** — *feeling the heat, not following an errand.* **Drew's instinct: something crunchy. The Coil** (`places/the-coil.md`) — *a labyrinth grown out of a petrified coil in the deep lake east of the Nest, with a navigation rule, a pressure track, minotaurs, chitterers and modular rooms.*

**The one snag: the Coil is written as found through the Night Ferryman** — *an NPC giving them the reason, which is what they don't want.* **The fix: they stumble on it.** *Island-hopping on the deep lake, a dark hump breaks the water where no chart has an island — warm water around it, a low stone arch.* **The Ferryman's line can still come later, as the explanation, not the invitation.** *And it surfaces for a while and then goes back under, so it is there to find once.*

**How hard** *(measured 2026-11-12, everyone fighting to the end)*:

| | Code, Flint, Caine | Code + Caine |
|---|---|---|
| **One minotaur** | ~97%, ~0.5 Downs | **~70%, ~1 Down — strong** |
| **Two minotaurs** | **~54%, ~2 Downs — a coin flip** | ~8% |
| **Chitterers** | three are nothing | — |

**One minotaur at a time.** *The pressure track is what brings them; let it bring one.* **And the Lizardkin are coming the same session** — *a Coil night and a raider fight together is a long, bloody evening.*
