# Session 2 — Harlock's crate

*Drew's plan, 2026-10-23, with the scene built out. **Harlock catches the party alone, asks them to move a crate, and the job is the carry**: off the Arcadia, through the stacks, into the market, and down in the open at **Buttons and Such**, at the market's centre. How they move it is theirs.*

**What is in it** (GM only): **an Ocellus pup in a cage**, inside an ordinary sealed packing crate (`bestiary/ocellus.md`; `places/vultures-nest.md`, The Arcadia's hold). **Sealed, it is inert** — it feeds on nothing until somebody unpacks it. *Harlock has never seen it open either.*

---

## The ask

**Harlock finds them when nobody else is watching** — on the Arcadia, in the lee of a stack, at the end of a gangway with his back to the dock.

**The irony the table may not see:** *these are the people who kept three of his crates* (`campaign/session-1-log.md`, The three crates). **He does not know.** He is about to trust them with the one crate he cannot afford to have counted.

**What he says:**

- *"One crate. Off my deck, through the market, and set down in the open at the button stall — dead centre. Then you leave it and walk away."*
- *"Don't open it. Don't sit on it. Don't stand next to it after."*
- *"Strangers. That's the qualification. My lads carrying my cargo off my ship in the middle of a lockdown is a confession."*
- *(asked what's in it)* *"Freight. Same as everything on this river."*
- *(asked why the open market)* *"Because that's where it goes. I'm paying for the walk, not the questions."*

**The money is good and offered without haggling** — *which, from a man famous for his temper, is information.* **Suggested: 30 gold**, the same as the salvage. *If they push, he pays more and says nothing.*

**Why now.** **Jonas is due aboard to count the hold** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Beat five). A living crate in a Regency-cross-referenced ledger is the end of Harlock (`characters/harlock.md`, The Pirate Guild). *He needs it off before the count, and he cannot be seen doing it.*

---

## The crate

**A sealed packing crate, heavy, unremarkable**, no marks worth reading. **Two people to carry it**, or one with a handcart. *Every so often the weight inside shifts — not much, just enough for whoever is holding the low end to feel it.*

**The rule that makes it a crime.** **The Arcadia's berth is impounded with the rest of the stretch** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Quarantined Barge), **and Compact hands are under orders not to let cargo off an impounded berth.** *Walking a crate off that ship is exactly what is forbidden — which is why it has to look like something else.*

---

## The carry

**Three legs, one check each**, and the stat is negotiable as always (`rules/resolution.md`, Core Resolution). **How they disguise it sets the DC and the stat**; a good plan drops a leg to Easy or skips it.

| Leg | What is in the way | DC |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Off the ship** | The gangway in full view of the frontage, and **Amelia on deck** (`characters/amelia.md`) — *she keeps the logs, and this crate is not in them* | 13 |
| **2. Through the stacks** | Weaving between cargo stacks past **Compact hands** with orders, and the wrecked berth from the crash. **Jonas is walking this way with his ledger** | 13 · **16 if Jonas is close** |
| **3. Into the market** | The crowd, the stalls, **nowhere to set down until the centre** — and somebody who notices things (below) | 11 |

### How they might move it — and what each is good at

- **As dock workers.** **Code wears a dockhand's face** — studied, so strangers are free (`campaign/chris.md`, What it can't do). *A crate on a dockhand's shoulder is the most invisible thing on the frontage.* **Best on leg 2.** *The risk is a Compact hand who knows the man Code is wearing.*
- **As merchants with a purchase.** *Carrying something they just bought off a ship*, with the story ready. **Flint's Persuasion and his name on the dock** (`campaign/kevin.md`, Skills) — **but the dock knows the Pepper boy**, and a priest's son buying freight off a pirate during a lockdown is its own story. **Best on leg 3.**
- **Under a tarp on a handcart**, loaded with something legitimate on top — *the Wrackclaw barrel* (`campaign/session-1-log.md`, The dock). **Caine's Animal Handling** covers *whatever that weight shift is*.
- **Anything else they come up with.** *A card whose name fits the aim buys Advantage* (`rules/resolution.md`, Advantage & Disadvantage).

### When a check fails

**A failure is a complication, not an arrest.** Pick one, or let the fiction choose:

- **A Compact hand stops them:** *"That off the impounded berth?"* — **one more check to talk past him**, or they drop the crate and walk
- **Jonas looks up from his ledger** and makes a note. *He does not stop anybody. He writes it down* (`campaign/chris.md`, When somebody asks — the check)
- **The crate tips and something inside shifts hard** — a dockhand flinches back from it
- **A Red Scarf is tailing them.** *The kids watch everything that moves on that dock — and one of them already watched the party take Harlock's crates*

**Two failures and the route is blown**: they need a new plan, a new disguise, or a new way round.

## The drop — Buttons and Such

**Dead centre of the market.** *A box, a price chalked on a slate, coin left and buttons taken. Nobody knows whose stall it is, and it is always stocked* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Market).

**They set the crate down in the open and walk away.** *That is the whole instruction, and it sounds wrong, and Harlock will not explain it.*

**What happens after, if they hang around:**

- **Somebody else comes to set it up** — unnamed, and deliberately so. **They break the packing crate down, carry the boards away, and leave a slatted cage dressed as freight somebody forgot** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Arcadia's hold). *A party that watches sees the handover without understanding a second of it.*
- **From then on it is the Moving Crate's twin.** *The first one is still at the east end.* **Nobody stands near either, and nobody notices they aren't** (`bestiary/ocellus.md`, Telling One Is There).
- **Erubeth is watching.** *The man in the long coat who has been asking about the Moving Crate sees who put the second one down* (`characters/erubeth.md`). **He does not approach. He files it.**

**Whether the button stall's owner has anything to do with this is open** — *it is the one stall in the market nobody can put a name to, which is exactly the kind of place this chain likes.*
