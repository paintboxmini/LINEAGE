## The Ribs — the grabbings

- **Code went to Kino meaning to take Bean along on a voyage to the capital** — and **learned that Mercer's men had grabbed Scarves off the streets.** He made a rescue plan on the spot.
- **He gave the older girl in charge of the Ribs 30 gold**: *"It's not safe outside for the Red Scarves right now. Use this money to keep hidden. No stealing until we get back. There's more than enough gold there."*
- **Code took Kino to rescue Bean**, and met up with Caine.

## Mercer's warehouses

- **They scouted the warehouse district and drew a rough map**, and **correctly picked the smallest, most isolated building** as where the grabbed kids were held.
- **Over a fence, in quietly: one guard asleep in the corner, and Bean and two other kids (unnamed) tied to chairs in the middle of the room, held for questioning.**
- **Code killed the sleeping guard — with KILLSWITCH.** *The stance card, the switch that flips, used to end a man in his sleep.* They got the kids back over the fence and sent them to the Ribs.
- **Code went on alone to the central warehouse** — *again guessing right that Mercer's office was there* — **wearing the face of the guard he had just killed.**
- **In the office he went straight to threats: he shifted into tentacles** and told Mercer to **leave the orphans of Vulture's Nest alone.** **He formed a blade from his hand and drove it through a large book on the desk** — *"Do I make myself clear?"*
- **Mercer folded at once**: *"Oh yes, no more kids, of course — please don't hurt me."*
- **Code pulled his arm free with the book still on the blade** — *"If I find out you lied to me, I'll be back"* — **and went out the window.**

## Leaving the Nest

- **Everyone met at Fortune's Fancy.** *"We have to leave town right now, at least until the heat dies off."*
- **Kino and Bean came too** — *"Wait, you mean us too?!"* — *"The Red Scarves are safely hidden away and they've got enough gold to hold them over. We're always coming back; we just need to leave now."*
- **They got out of Vulture's Nest.**

**Caine left without a word to Barb.** *He'd agreed to a date that night, and ditched town instead. Chris tried to make time for a goodbye; Pat said no — he's playing the long game and will wait until he's back.* **So Barb is in the Nest, stood up, on the night the Strays planned to come for Caine at that date.**

**The table nearly didn't leave** — *time, resources and story invested in the Nest against a shiny new city with no enemies breathing down their necks (yet).*

## The Muirn hunters

- **A ship of Muirn hunters caught up with them outside the Nest.**
- **The party gathered every flammable and explosive they had** — *none of Flint's* — **and made an improvised bomb, dropped off the stern at the perfect moment. The pursuing ship hit it: a hole blown in her side, and fire.**
- **They took the dinghy and rowed straight at the oncoming ship — and rammed it through the hole**, into the hunters below decks fighting to save her. **The impact killed three Lizardkin outright and mortally wounded a fourth.**
- **Code and Caine rolled excellently and jumped clear at the perfect moment**, straight into the fight. **Two of Fortune's Fancy's hired pirates** came with them, and recovered a little slower. **Bean came through the crash untouched and hid behind the dinghy.**
- **Nine hunters at once**, *stats lowered slightly from the sheet*, **no Flint — and the party won easily.** *Drew: they got really lucky.*
- **The last four Muirn surrendered**, and **were pressed into the crew of Fortune's Fancy.**
