## Before they sailed

**Kevin never got with Drew for his text week** (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, Flint's week). **So Flint spent his last time in the Nest hiring full crews for both of the party's ships** — *Fortune's Fancy and the Water Wing* — **and rejoined the party in time to leave.** *The Den, and Barracuda's men trying to grab him, did not happen.*

## On the water

**Halfway to the capital.**

**They built a ram: the Bowsprit Lance** — *a long wooden pole on the Fancy's bow, made to splinter off inside an enemy hull.* *(Drew's spelling was "bowsprint"; a bowsprit is the spar on a ship's bow.)* **They stopped at a small island to attach it.**

**They saw the Coil, and noped right out of it.** *They want to reach the capital fast.* **The Coil is unexplored** (`places/the-coil.md`) — *it surfaces and sinks, and it will be somewhere else the next time.*

## The Gowra

**While Code helped work on the ship, Caine searched the island**, harvesting **Burnrind** (`flora/burnrind.md`) with **Borager** — *the forager of the four Muirn, named by Chris* (`characters/the-four-muirn.md`) — **and Bean.**

**A Gowra ambushed them** (`bestiary/gowra.md`).

- **Bean ran back to the ship for help.** **Borager stayed with Caine to hold the line so Bean could get away.**
- **The Gowra hit Caine for 15 in the first round, and dropped him to 0 the next.**
- **Borager kept the fight going just long enough for help to arrive.**
- **Code distracted the Gowra while a couple of the Fancy's pirates dragged Caine out of danger.** **They took it down without risking anyone else.**

## The harvest

**Code had Flint help harvest the skin, the tusks, a hundred pounds of meat, and two venom sacks from the head.** **The tusks inflict Weak, so Drew ruled it a toxin** — *the venom sacks are where it comes from.*

**The Fancy became a macabre scene.** **Half the meat was salted; the rest hung in strips off the rigging to dry.** **Code repainted the Fancy in another layer of red, in the Gowra's blood.**

*The venom sacks and the meat are Kevin's kind of ingredient — what they do is decided with him at the table* (`campaign/kevin.md`, The Ingredients).

## The crew

**Sailing roleplay and crew bonding for the rest of the night.**

- **The diver — the Muirn with the swimming skill — secretly resents that his old crew were killed.** **Code caught on, and is working on building trust** (`characters/the-four-muirn.md`).
- **Borager held the line for Caine.**

## Drew's calls

## Consequences

- **The party has proof a Gowra is real.** *Everywhere on the web, "a gowra story" means a lie, and nobody who has seen one has ever been believed* (`bestiary/gowra.md`). **They have the skin, the tusks and the meat.** *Somebody will cheerfully ask whether they brought a tooth back — and this time they did.*
- **A ship painted in fresh blood with meat hanging in the rigging** is a ship people talk about at the next port.
