*Drew's plan, 2026-10-18, with the scenes built out. **Three beats, in this order: the kids mock him, Kino comes to see him, and the trial.** What Code does in each of them is Chris's; this file is the world's side.*

**When it runs.** **The mockery comes with the Moving Crate**, whenever that lands. **Kino and the trial are held for a night Kevin can't make, or is late to** *(Drew, 2026-10-18)* — **Code and Caine at the table, and Drew bouncing between them.**

### Caine, on the same night

**Two ways to play it, and both can happen in one evening:**

- **With Code.** Caine helps on the trial — a second adult is another door the kids can't open, and **mark B tests Caine as much as Code**: a man whose Price is *"I always share with those in greater need than me"* (`campaign/pat.md`, The Price) standing next to the satchel of papers that binds children to Mercer's floors.
- **On his own.** Caine breaks off after his own thread while Code works the job. What is waiting for him in the Nest:
  - **The word *cursed*** — the dock's name for the barge's people, which is what sent him looking in the first place (`campaign/session-1-log.md`, For session 2)
  - **The cloth in Harlock's crate** — the same plant fibre as his scrap (`campaign/session-1-log.md`, The three crates). *Somebody in the Nest knows where that plant grows*
  - **Corvel**, held by the Compact, whose congregation's Speaker is a Lizardkin (`places/pneum.md`, The Speaker) — *the first person who can tell him one of them lives out on the water*
  - **Bartho's request** to see whether anyone is alive on Pneum (`campaign/session-1-log.md`, For session 2) — *or Caine digs into the barge himself*
  - **Hojan's ask** — escort the Ashbys to Mercer's warehouse to buy back their son's contract (`characters/hojan.md`, The Ashbys — Hojan's ask). **The son is Tib**, who is with the Scarves on Code's side of the table. *The cleanest split this night has*

*Two players on two threads, cut between at the cliffhangers: Code with a hand in a stranger's coat, Caine with a question somebody won't answer.*

## 1. The mockery — at the Moving Crate

*Drew:* **During or after the Moving Crate**, a few Red Scarves turn up. **They know one thing**: Lusk has told them a grown man wants a scarf. **They know nothing about the food or Hojan.** They mock him, throw something, and run. **The point is to darken Code's mood and raise what joining costs him**, before Kino puts it right.

**Where they stand.** *Not right at the crate.* Nothing is ever stolen within a few paces of it, and the kids give that patch the same wide berth everyone does without knowing why (`bestiary/ocellus.md`, Telling One Is There). **They call to him from just past it** — which, if anyone is paying attention, is a tell about the crate.

**Who** — three kids, named because they are a social scene:

- **Bean** — Kino's second, scarf halfway off, the loudest (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Kino and Bean)
- **Sprat** — small, fast, the one who throws
- **Tib** — eleven, quiet, hangs back and does not join in (`places/vultures-nest.md`, People looking in). *The Ashbys' boy. Nobody at the table knows that yet.*

**What they say** — dock-kid talk, nothing modern:

- *"Oi! It's him — the big 'un who wants a scarf!"*
- *"Look at the size of him. You need a scarf or a nursemaid?"*
- *"Mummer. That's what you are. Dressed up as one of us."* — *a mummer is a costumed player, a fake by trade. **It lands harder on Code than they know**: he is a man wearing a borrowed face* (`campaign/chris.md`, Trait — Mimicry)
- *"Sham."* · *"Counterfeit."* · *"Grown man playing ragamuffin."*
- *"Scarf's earned, mister. Can't buy one off Lusk."*

**What they throw: a fish head**, or a rind of chewfat. *Not fruit — the Nest has almost no fresh produce, and a kid with an apple would eat it* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, What Grows Here).

**Then they run**, three ways at once, into the stalls. *If anyone chases, it ends at a gap in the Ribs nobody grown can fit through* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Ribs) — **unless Code pours himself through after them** (`campaign/chris.md`, Trait — Silverling, Fluid). *That would be a very different first meeting, and it is entirely his to try.*

**Optional — the crates.** Bean starts to say *"and we know about the —"* and Sprat elbows him quiet. **The kid who saw the party take Harlock's crates is in this group** (`campaign/session-1-log.md`, The three crates). *They don't say it. Kino would skin them. But the table hears that there's something.*

## 2. Kino — the apology

*Drew:* **Later in session 2, Kino appears for the first time.** He apologises for his crew, **thanks the party for the food**, and says he had to see for himself who sent it — and when it turned out to be the same man asking to join, he had to come.

**How he found out.** **The food reached the Scarves through Hojan** (`characters/hojan.md`) — *the first thing Hojan has ever had to offer them that they took.* Kino does not take anything without knowing where it came from, so **he asked Hojan, and Hojan told him** whose money it was. *Which means Hojan has just had his first real conversation with Kino — and the party made it happen.*

**Kino** — fifteen, messy brown hair, green eyes, red scarf tied properly. Gives the name **Sawyer**, and only the name Sawyer (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Kino and Bean). Charming, unhurried, and in control of every conversation he is in.

**What he says:**

- *"Name's Sawyer. My lot gave you some grief. That's on me — they don't speak for the scarf."*
- *"A crate's worth of bread, dried meat and fish came to us this week. I don't eat what I can't trace. So I traced it."*
- *"Turns out it's the same fella who wants in. Had to see that for myself."*
- *"Here's the thing. Nobody your age has ever asked. We're for getting kids to fourteen. What's a grown man want with that?"* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Fourteen)
- *"You want a scarf, you work like we work. One job. We watch."*

**He also knows about the crates.** *Whether he says so here is Drew's call — it is the strongest card in his hand, and Kino does not play his best card first.*

**The likely outcome: a trial.** *Kino offers it; Code takes it or doesn't.*

## 3. The trial — one job

**Kino's rules**, said once (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Red Scarves, The operation):

> *"We don't run. We carry bags and point out stalls, and the stalls are real. Nobody ever has anything on them — it's three kids down the chain before the mark's turned round. And we don't take from people who've got nothing."*

**Why Kino might want an adult at all.** *There are doors that do not open for a child* — a counting-house, a merchant's own table, the back room at the Keel. **A grown man can stand where the kids get chased off.** *That is Code's case for himself, if he makes it — and a man who can be anybody is the strongest version of it.*

### The mark

**Kino picks it, and the pick is the test.** Three options, Drew's choice:

| | Mark | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **A merchant's purse** at a busy stall — fat, careless, can afford it | The plain version: can he do the work |
| **B** | **One of Mercer's agents** — the men who sign families' children away with a mark (`characters/mercer.md`). A purse, and the satchel of binding papers he carries | Whether he takes from the right person. *Tib was signed away by one of these men* |
| **C** | **A Bellows clerk** at the warehouses | Nerve. The Bellows are the power under the docks (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Many Mask's legacy) |

*B is the one with weight. Kino would not tell Code why he chose him; Tib would know.*

### Running it — roles, rolls and heat

**Code picks a role**, or Kino gives him one:

- **The face** — the distraction that holds the mark's attention. *Bean's job, and Bean is not good at it.*
- **The hand** — the lift itself.
- **The first link** — takes the goods from the hand and passes them on within seconds.

**Three rolls, at most, and each one is the fiction first.** The stat is negotiable as always (`rules/resolution.md`, Core Resolution):

| | Normal | With the mark wary |
|---|---|---|
| **Getting close** — the approach, the story, the borrowed face | DC 13 | DC 16 |
| **The lift or the hold** — hands, timing, keeping the mark talking | DC 13 | DC 16 |
| **The handoff** — goods off him and down the chain before anyone turns | DC 11 | DC 13 |

**What Code brings, and it is a lot.** **Mimicry** — he can walk up wearing the face of somebody the mark trusts, studied rather than glanced at (`campaign/chris.md`, What it can't do). **Performance +2** holds the room. **Netrunner +1** covers the talk. **Fluid** lets him pour through a gap no adult could use. **A card name that fits the aim buys Advantage** (`rules/resolution.md`, Advantage & Disadvantage). *Mimicry is not combat, so nothing stops him using it here.*

**A failed roll adds Heat; it does not end the job.**

| Heat | What it means |
|---|---|
| **0** | Clean. Nobody saw anything |
| **1** | **Noticed.** The mark pats a pocket; the next roll is against the wary DC |
| **2** | **A chase.** The kids scatter three ways, and Code has to choose: go with them, or draw it off |
| **3** | **Caught** |

### If he is caught — and why it matters more for him

**A caught Scarf is put back to work in the warehouses** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, What they ran from). **A caught grown man goes to the Compact** — and the Compact means **Jonas, writing names down.** *That is the papers check Code has dreaded since the count* (`campaign/chris.md`, When somebody asks — the check). **The worst outcome of a pickpocket's trial is a Regency ledger opening on him.**

*And if he draws the chase off himself so the kids get clear, that is the thing Kino was actually watching for.*

### What Kino judges

**Not the haul.** Kino watches three things:

1. **Did the goods leave him fast**, so nobody had anything on them?
2. **Did he keep the kids out of the danger**, or use them as cover?
3. **Did he take from the right person?**

**Pass and the scarf is his — earned, not bought.** *Where it hangs, how he wears it, and what Kino calls him afterwards are worth leaving for the night.*
