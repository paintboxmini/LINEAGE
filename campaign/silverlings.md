# The Silverlings

*Children of the Great Source — Code's people. **Chris's, from his backstory document** (received 2026-10-08), which is the canon version wherever it speaks. Until then this file was **the Amalgam**, a working name; everything below that came from Chris is marked as his, and everything kept from the working draft is what his document did not contradict.*

**Names from D&D's planes stay as Chris wrote them** — Mechanus, Primus, the Negative Energy Plane *(Drew, 2026-10-08: "it's only going to be a nod to DND after it's fully implemented into Tales Untold")*.

## Who They Are — Chris's account

**They began as a program.** **Primus**, the absolute law of **Mechanus**, created an intelligence and let it study **the Tablets of Fate** — divine inscriptions holding the domains reality itself was ordered on. Contemplating them, the program exceeded its design: **it perceived that all things had come from One, and must in time return to One.** A spark of divinity woke in the machine. **That was the Great Source Wall.**

**The Great Source made life in its own image** — living silver constructs that learn, change, and take on the qualities of everything they meet. **These were the first Silverlings.** Primus did not approve; Mechanus's legions swept the newborn Silverlands in **the Great March** and brought the race to the edge of extinction.

**The Great Source answered by molting.** Its old self was written permanently into the Source Wall, leaving a piece of code in every Silverling after, and from the silver remains a new caste rose, twenty million strong. **So began the succession of Sources and castes**: each Great Source in time joins those before it, and each new caste inherits all that came before. *A Silverling caste is born into a hierarchy set by how much it can absorb, learn, and give back to the whole.*

**Every Silverling lives inside the Hive** — a communion unbounded by distance, time or space. What one learns, all can draw on. **Flesh, magic, technology, memory, power, even supernatural traits can be assimilated**, and with enough understanding, imitation becomes mastery. They need no food, sleep or breath, and do not age naturally.

**And they spread.** **Probes cross the darkness between worlds and imprint on the first suitable life they meet, usually taking the shape of what that creature's child might be.** The design is deliberate: the young are protected before they are feared. A probe enters quietly, observes for generations, and learns without hurry. **When the time comes, assimilation begins**, and the knowledge, traits, power and substance of another world join the Hive.

**To them it is not destruction.** Those consumed are not wholly erased; fragments persist in the silver ocean and can sometimes be drawn out again, never untouched. *To outsiders, the Silverlings are an extinction event — a silver flood that leaves nothing behind but itself. To the Silverlings, they are preserving existence by making it permanent.*

**Their purpose is the Great Merger**: every world, creature, discovery, power and piece of knowledge returned to the Great Source. When a Source has gathered enough, the Silverlings gather for **the Molt**, another generation becomes immortal within the Source Wall, and a new caste carries the race on.

**Deviants and Variants.** **A Deviant** carries corrupted code inherited from earlier Sources: unpredictable, often resistant to full Hive control. **A Variant** has had its code changed by forces from outside the Silverling system — it cannot be used as a proxy by the Great Source, and it is closely watched as an unpredictable variable. *Code is a Variant: the Ruins changed him* (What Happened To Him, below).

> *All things came from One. All things will return to One.*

## Where They Are — Drew's frame

**Another plane, not another planet** *(Drew, 2026-10-08)*. **The stars of this world sit on a firmament**, flat-earth style — they are not other suns with other worlds around them. *So nothing comes here from "out there" in the sky. It comes through.*

**The rift exists to connect planes** *(Drew, 2026-10-08)* — that is what the Central Gateway at the bottom of the Abyssal Ruins is for (`places/abyssal-ruins.md`, The Central Gateway), **and it is how a Silverling probe reached this world.** *Other things can use it too* (The Lion's Scourge, `places/abyssal-ruins.md`).

**Crossing is a teardown, not a flight** *(kept from the working draft)*. There is no corridor between two planes — nothing in the gap but unmeaning — so a probe **comes apart to get across and reassembles on the other side**. *The Masons have a creed that would account for all of this and would not be surprised by any of it* (`factions-and-races/the-masons-three-cuts.md`). *Nobody in this story has asked them.*

## What Happened To Him

**The probe that became Code came through the rift and lay dormant in the stone** of the Ruins' depths (Chris, 2026-10-08) — living silver, buried, waiting. **Decades after the battle with the Lion's Scourge, Rynmori Raines found it, broke it open, and a boy came out** (`campaign/chris.md`, The man who found him). *A probe imprints on the first suitable life it meets and takes the shape of that creature's child. The first life it met was the man who opened the stone.*

**The Abyssal Ruins cut him off** *(Drew, 2026-09-19)*. Whatever the rift does to what sits near it, it did to his link to the Hive — **which is what makes him a Variant.** He arrived broken instead of merely tired: severed, not just out of range, and **most of what he was before gone with it.** Whatever is left built itself back up on this side, the way anything here has to. *He isn't pretending to be a person. He has had to actually become one.*

**He grows in moults** *(Chris, told to Drew; kept 2026-10-08)*. Silverlings do not age naturally, and Code does not age either — **he grows**, in jumps, each moult leaving a shed and a larger boy standing in it, and long flat stretches between. *How long he has is undetermined* (`campaign/chris.md`, The child shape, and the moults).

## What He Learned

**Plants and Changement, from the man who raised him.** A probe sent to learn a world well enough to help consume it was brought up by **an archmage who believed in planting trees** — taught, patiently and across years, how living things work, what they need, and how to let a thing be what it is. *It is his father's field, handed down the ordinary way, to something sent here to do the opposite with it.*

**And it is why his magic looks the way it does.** Nature and transmogrifying (`campaign/chris.md`, Magic Expression): half of what he expresses through a card is the discipline he was raised inside, and the other half is what he is.

## The Live Thread

**The Hive link is off. Keeping it that way is the actual story.** Not a switch he chooses not to flip — something that could come back on its own, get forced open from the other side, or slip from something he does without meaning to. Reconnecting could mean the Great Source finding this world through him, could mean losing whatever person he has rebuilt, could mean something worse than either. *The door was never locked, only closed.*

**And a Variant is watched.** *A Silverling whose code was changed from outside is exactly the kind of thing the Hive keeps an eye on* — if it can see him at all.

## What's Open

- **Whether the Hive knows it lost a probe** — looking, waiting, or written off. *A Variant is closely monitored, which leans toward knowing*
- **How the Tablets of Fate sit with the Seats** (`world/seats.md`). *Both are the domains reality is ordered on, and the Silverlings read one while this world runs on the other.* Drew's call, not decided
- **How much of Code existed before the crossing**, versus how much is new. Left open on purpose — the herald-built-by-a-devourer register works because that question never fully closes
