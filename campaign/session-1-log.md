## The market — Code and Flint

**Code and Flint started in the market.**

- **Lusk put in a good word for Code with Kino** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Strays, and Lusk). **Code wants to join the Red Scarves.** *He has not met them yet — that is still to come* (Still open, below).
- **They set up to supply Marta's stall with meat and flour**, and **gave her money, so she is passing food on to the Red Scarves** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Market). *Marta was already slipping the kids bread; now she has the means to do it properly, and she knows who paid for it.*

## The dock

- **They came on the quarantine** — the held barge and the lockdown (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Quarantined Barge).
- **The Arcadia crashed into the dock** and lost cargo into the river.
- **During the salvage, Wrackclaws came up the stones**, and **the party saved Bartho's dock labourers** from them (`bestiary/wrackclaw.md`).
- **They talked Bartho into letting them use their own ship** — the impounded Merry Mint — **to bring the lost goods back out of the river.**
- **Three crates came up that nobody else noticed, and they kept them.** *Harlock does not have them back.*
- **Harlock paid them 30 gold** for the salvage, after some persuading. **Bartho added 10**, because it was his dockworkers they saved.
