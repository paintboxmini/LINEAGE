## The market

**Code and Flint started in the market.** Caine was with them.

**Lusk's shop.** *A narrow counter off the back of the dock with no sign, a hundred small things on the shelves and none of them in sets* — the description used at the table, so it is the shop now (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Strays, and Lusk).

- **Lusk put in a good word for Code with Kino.** **Code wants to join the Red Scarves.** *He has not met them yet.*
- **Caine asked Lusk about a symbol on his scrap of cloth** (`campaign/pat.md`, The scrap). **It is a family emblem**, granted to Pat at the table. **Lusk rolled and failed** — he did not know it. **Flint rolled and knew it for Lizardkin.** *It didn't move Caine's search on much. The gesture was the point.*

**Marta's** — *a small, run-down brick establishment*, the description used at the table (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Market).

- **Caine bought five jerky** — each heals 1 HP — for 3 gold, all five.
- **Caine gave Marta 20 gold to feed the Red Scarves**, and **Code added 5.** **Marta will give the food to Hojan** (`characters/hojan.md`) **rather than to the kids directly** *(Drew, 2026-10-18)* — *the Scarves have stolen from her for years, and Hojan is the one person on the dock trying to give them somewhere to eat.* *Caine's Price is "I always share with those in greater need than me", and it fired on its first morning at the table* (`campaign/pat.md`, The Price).
- **Flint gave Marta some of his grace peppers** and is working toward **a steady trade for the family business.** She was blown away by the quality, and **traded him her whole remaining supply of cinder flake — 5 measures.**

> **Marta:** *"Easiest choice I've ever made."* There was no comparison in the heat. **She has been looking for a way to make a spicier jerky, and now she has a sustainable one.**

*She was already cooking with Nobody's Pepper, cautiously, in a fish brine (`flora/nobodys-pepper.md`). Grace is a different animal.*

## The dock

- **They came to the quarantine.** **The infected were never seen** — they are in a warehouse, out of sight, waiting for Bartho to question them (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Quarantined Barge).
- **The Arcadia crashed into the dock** and lost cargo into the river.
- **During the salvage, six Wrackclaws came up the stones**, and **the party saved Bartho's dock labourers** (`bestiary/wrackclaw.md`). **Code arrived first** and used Performance — *a crossed-arms pose worthy of a ninja* — to rally the dockworkers holding the cargo. **The party rolled the Wrackclaws over.** Drew sent every attack at Caine and got him to half HP; **Caine had PAIN IS FUEL running before the fight** (`cards/red-body.md`) — *Resist and Thorns 2 every time he is hit*, and Thorns stacks, so every bite at him made the next one cost more.
- **They talked Bartho into letting them use their own ship** — the impounded Holly Mint — **to bring the lost goods back out of the river.**
- **The cargo went two ways** *(Drew)*: **some was swept away, and most was pushed onto the bank.** So the Arcadia's logs will be short **more than three crates** — and the party's three are hidden inside a loss nobody can account for exactly.
- **Three crates came up in their nets that nobody else noticed, and they kept them.** Harlock does not have them back.
- **The nets also brought up five Wrackclaws**, alive. **The party is keeping them in a barrel.** *Edible, but not good.* **They plan to give them to Marta** — to try as jerky, or baked into a bread, maybe with her new grace.
- **Harlock paid them 30 gold** for the salvage, after some persuading. **Bartho paid 18**, because it was his dockworkers they saved.

## For session 2

**Early: the word *cursed*, for Caine.** It never came up. The dock calls the barge's people cursed, and that word is what sends Caine looking (`campaign/session-1-convergence.md`, Pneum first, and the barge is why). **Get it in front of him early.**

**Bartho's request, when he makes it** *(Drew)*. **His fear is that the barge got infected on the way here.** He has their story and cannot believe it without somebody checking, and if he can be convinced they are no danger to Vulture's Nest, he will let them go.

> *"Just go see if there's anyone alive on that island. If there's people there who have the same kinds of illness and they aren't dead, then I'll believe their story about taking their dead for a glasskin burial ceremony at the quartz cliffs. But as it stands I've got a barge full of either dead or infected people and I can't take their word at face value."*

**Watch Pat.** *He is quiet at the table* (Drew) — **give Caine the first beat of the night**, and the *cursed* rumour is exactly that beat.

**A fight that bites.** Six Wrackclaws were no contest. For a real fight in the Nest:

- **Toughs** (`bestiary/toughs.md`) — **Boss + 1 Tough + 1 Slinger** is the strong fight (~81%, about one Down). **The Slinger shoots from the back, which is the answer to Caine's Thorns.** Reskin as smugglers, Bellows men, Mercer's men, or low-lifes leaning on the Scarves
- **An optional creature fight — a light on the mud.** At night, on a slow tide, a steady low light on the bank reads as *somebody down there in trouble*, and people go (`bestiary/spooklight.md`). **What is on that mud is a Foulhaul family** (`bestiary/foulhaul.md`). **Three Foulhauls: ~92%, about one Down** — and their trait is the drama: **a Foulhaul that drops somebody picks them up and walks off with them.** *The smell gives a minute's warning first, and that minute is the scene.* **The hook writes itself from session one**: one of the dock labourers they saved goes to answer a light at two in the morning
- **Later, a set piece — the Gowra** (`bestiary/gowra.md`). One, never two. *Nobody in the Nest believes in it, and the party will not be believed either*

## The three crates — the big new thread

*Drew, 2026-10-17.* **They are from a recent target of Harlock's — a Muirn boat**, the Guild's standing enemy (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`, The Muirn).

1. **Textiles — a coarse, sturdy fabric, woven from the same plant fibre as Caine's scrap** *(Drew, 2026-10-17; not the emblem)*. *The first real clue in his search: his scrap is Muirn work, from the southern tribe.*
2. **Muirn shaman's goods** — dried swamp plants, bone charms, wax-sealed clay jars. **For Flint, these are hints, not effects** (`campaign/kevin.md`, The Ingredients): what they smell of, what they do to the hands, and nothing about what they do in a round or a drink
3. **Goods the Muirn had already taken off somebody else** — cargo stamped with **the Bellows' mark**, from a Nest boat the Muirn seized before Harlock took it back off them. *The Bellows are the strongest of the five families and the masters of the docks* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Many Mask's legacy) — **they will want it, and they will want to know who has it**

**A Red Scarf saw them take the crates** *(Drew)*. **Kino knows.** *Which lands just as Code is asking to join — the kids have leverage over the party before the first conversation.*

**How Harlock finds out is not settled, and need not be soon.** The cargo was a prize not yet before the court (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`, The Prize Court), so **everything missing weakens his claim**; Amelia logs every crate, but with so much swept away the count says *more is gone*, not *these three*. *The witness is the sharper route — and it belongs to Kino.*
