# Back in the Nest

*Drew's notes for whenever the party returns to Vulture's Nest, 2026-11-17. **Plans, not events.***

---

## Who is after them

**Almost everyone** *(Drew, 2026-11-17)*. **The Tideward Compact is the only major faction in the Nest not after the party.**

- **The Bellows and the Strays, together.** **Mercer sent men after Code** when he fled to Fortune's Fancy from the warehouse threat (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, Mercer's warehouses). *They were far too slow to catch him* — **but in time to see the Strays send the Muirn hunters after the ship** (`characters/the-four-muirn.md`). **Mercer reached out to Lusk to find out what was going on**, and **the two families are now allied against the party** (`characters/mercer.md`; `places/vultures-nest.md`, The Strays, and Lusk). *The strongest family and the best-informed one, pointed the same way.*
- **Barracuda** — *chasing them up the river* (`characters/barracuda.md`).
- **Mercer is on his guard** (`characters/mercer.md`, On his guard). *The easy version of his warehouse is gone.*

## Mercer and Lusk compare notes *(Drew, 2026-11-17)*

*The scene, as Drew wrote it:*

> **Mercer:** "A man in a red scarf just threatened me. Why did you send those hunters after his ship?"
> **Lusk:** "Oh, you just had a mysterious red-scarved man break into your office? How curious. I sold that man a stolen ID — he went by the name Code. I sent those hunters after the ship because another man, named Caine, burned down my establishment. I've seen them together before."
> **Mercer:** "I'm pretty sure Code has something to do with my stolen work contracts too."
> **Lusk:** "Makes sense — he recently joined up with the Red Scarves. He even bought the church above their hideout from me. Caine is a Shunka; he was asking around about the southern lizard tribe. I figured, who better to send after him."
> **Mercer:** "Once your hunters bring them back, you hand Code over to me. Nobody interferes with my business."
> **Lusk:** "Fair enough. Just so you know, there's another member of their group. Flint — a human who runs trade for his father, the head priest of the Temple of Unity in the capital. I don't know how he plays into this, or why he'd involve himself."
> **Mercer:** "What's their aim? … Doesn't matter. What about the Red Scarves? They have to pay as well. Look into anyone else that party has spoken to."

**What they now hold between them:**

- **Code's name, his stolen papers, his Red Scarf, and the church** — *and the church sits over the Scarves' entrance* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Ribs). **Lusk sold it to him.**
- **Caine for the fire**, and **that he is asking after the Muirn** (`characters/preet.md`).
- **Flint's family: Ossian, head of the Temple of Unity** (`campaign/kevin.md`; `characters/ossian.md`). *The party is sailing to the capital, where his father is. **Anyone in the Nest with a ship going that way can send word ahead.***
- **The deal:** *when the hunters bring them back, Code goes to Mercer.* **They don't know yet the hunters failed** — *or that four of them crew the Fancy* (`characters/the-four-muirn.md`).

**Mercer's last order is the dangerous one: the Red Scarves pay too, and so does anyone the party has spoken to.** *Who that reaches, with the party a river away:*

## Harlock looks the other way

**He has heard the party took the Water Wing out from under Barracuda** *(Drew, 2026-11-17)*. **As head of the Guild he would normally owe Barracuda support in this** (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`). **But he likes the party, and nobody has told him officially — so he looks the other way, for now.** *"For now" is the hook: the day somebody brings it to him formally, he has to choose* (`characters/harlock.md`).

## A new family to meet

**Have at least one of the other three ready when they come back** *(Drew)* — **the Marrows, the Rooks or the Vells** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Many Mask's legacy).

**The Vells** *(Drew, 2026-11-17)*: **loan sharks who trap families in debt and buy their children to settle it, supplying the Bellows' floors, and cleaning the money through the jeweller** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Vells). *That makes the window-shopping trip for Barb the way in: the jeweller is their laundry. **And it's a third family on the same side as the two already hunting the party** — or a family that doesn't know yet what the party did to its best customer.*

It never happened.*
