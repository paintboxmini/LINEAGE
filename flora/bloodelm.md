# Bloodelm

*The forest of Elowen (`places/elowen.md`), and the reason anybody lives there. Set 2026-10-05: Drew made Elowen a logging village on an exotic, valuable wood; the tree and its gate are written to that.*

**A tall elm with a red heart.** The sapwood is ordinary pale timber. **The heartwood is deep red, close-grained and heavy**, and it polishes to a shine that looks wet. *It is not a working wood. It is what a rich house makes its best table out of, and its doors, and its box for the things it keeps.*

**It bleeds when it is cut.** The sap runs red, and a lot of it. *That is where the name comes from, and nobody on Elowen thinks twice about it.*

**What marks it as a strange tree** is only the colour of the sap. *Nothing else about it is strange. It grows, it is felled, and it burns like wood.*

---

## Growth

**Elowen, and nowhere else anybody trades from.** The whole island is a stand of it. *Whether it grows anywhere else is not set; if it does, nobody is selling it.*

**Slow.** A tree worth felling is older than anyone working it. The village fells for its grandchildren and plants for them as well, which is why the forest is still there after generations of logging.

---

## Harvesting

**The gate is the village.** *Felling it is easy. Felling it so the heart stays red is not, and only Elowen knows how.*

**A bloodelm has to be bled before it is felled.** The trunk is ringed through the bark and left standing **for months** while the sap runs out. Fell it full and the sap sets in the heartwood and turns it a dull dark brown — still wood, worth firewood prices. **Fell it bled and the heart stays red.** *So a stranger with an axe gets timber. A stranger with an axe and a lot of patience still gets it wrong, because when to ring, how deep, how long to leave it and when it is ready are taught by hand inside the families and written down nowhere.*

**Which is why the forest looks the way it does.** At any time, a good share of the trees on Elowen are ringed and weeping red down the bark, waiting. *It is ordinary forestry, and it looks like a wood that has been wounded everywhere.*

**And it is why this month is a catastrophe twice over.** Half the people who knew how are dead (`places/elowen.md`). *Every felling family that loses its last teacher takes the craft with it, and the trees already ringed are standing there bleeding with nobody awake enough to judge when they are ready.*

---

## Redgum

**The sap that runs out of a ringed tree sets on the bark as a dark red gum**, thick at the bottom of the ring. *The villagers scrape it off and burn it, or leave it; it has never been worth anything to them.*

**It might be worth something to Kevin** (`campaign/kevin.md`, The Ingredients) — *what, is his and Drew's to work out at the table* (Drew, 2026-10-10). **The hints:** it stays tacky for days, it does not wash off hands so much as wear off them, and it burns slow and smoky with a smell like hot iron.

**The gate: it is on somebody's tree.** *Every ringed tree on Elowen belongs to a felling family*, and so does whatever runs out of it. **Taking it means asking**, in a village that has started looking for somebody to blame (`places/elowen.md`) — and for the families that are gone, there is nobody left to ask. *A stranger scraping gum off a dead man's tree is exactly the story this village is ready to believe.*

**And it is running out.** Nobody has ringed a tree since the dying started, so once the trees already bleeding have finished, there is no more until somebody is awake enough to ring new ones.

---

## Use

**Fine furniture, panelling, carving, inlay, and boxes.** Anything a wealthy buyer wants people to notice. *It sells on looks alone, and it sells well.*

**It is not boat timber.** Stiltwood does that job on the river web (`flora/stiltwood.md`). *Bloodelm in a hull would be a waste of a fortune.*
