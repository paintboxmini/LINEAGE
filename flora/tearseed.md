# Tearseed

*Floats in the Dark Garden, the second layer of the Abyssal Ruins (`places/abyssal-ruins.md`, The Descent). Set 2026-10-02.*

---

**Teardrop-shaped seeds that float.** They hang in the dark air of the Dark Garden at every height, drifting a little, glowing so faintly that **nothing more than a few inches away is lit by one.** *You see a tearseed when it is close enough to touch, which is the problem.*

**Touch one and it goes off like a flashbang** — a blinding burst of white in a place that has never had light. **In play: everyone in that position gains Blind** (`rules/card-glossary.md`, Blind), and outside a fight, nobody there can see anything for a while. *A cluster sets itself off in a chain.*

**They are the garden's one light, and it is a trap.** The rest of the garden grows pale and turns away from a lamp (`places/abyssal-ruins.md`, The Descent). The seeds are what it makes instead.

## The Weeping Wolf Uses Them

**The weeping wolf cannot be Blinded** (`bestiary/weeping-wolf.md`, A Hundred Eyes), and it hunts as if it knows it. It drives prey into a cluster, or knocks a seed into the party itself — and **the flash is usually the first sign it is there.** *The undefended attack that opens a fight with it often comes out of the white.*

## The Gate

**Finding them is the danger.** Picking one is nothing — close a jar around it slowly and it stays whole. **But a seed is only visible from inches away**, so finding one means being close enough to set it off, in a garden where they float at every height and nobody can see the next. *The gate is the dark, and the only way through it is slowly.*

**A carried tearseed is a flashbang the party owns.** That is why anybody bothers.
