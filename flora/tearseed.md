# Tearseed

*Floats in the Dark Garden, the second layer of the Abyssal Ruins (`places/abyssal-ruins.md`, The Descent). Set 2026-10-02.*

---

**Teardrop-shaped seeds that float.** They hang in the dark air of the Dark Garden at every height, drifting slowly, glowing so faintly that **nothing more than a few inches away is lit by one** — *but the seed itself shows from a long way off*, a pale point in total dark.

**And the garden flinches from them.** The plants fold shut as a seed drifts close, the same way they turn from a lamp (`places/abyssal-ruins.md`, The Descent), so **a drifting seed leaves a moving lane of closing leaves behind it.** *It is a trap the party sees coming.*

**Touch one and it goes off like a flashbang** — a blinding burst of white in a place that has never had light. **In play: everyone in that position gains Blind** (`rules/card-glossary.md`, Blind), and outside a fight, nobody there can see anything for a while. *A cluster sets itself off in a chain.*

**They are the garden's one light, and the garden itself will not stand near it.**

## The Weeping Wolf Uses Them

**The weeping wolf cannot be Blinded** (`bestiary/weeping-wolf.md`, A Hundred Eyes), and it hunts as if it knows it. It drives prey into a cluster, or knocks a seed into the party itself. **The tell is a seed moving against the drift** — faster than the others, in a straight line, with the leaves snapping shut along its path. *A party that reads that has a moment. A party that does not gets the undefended opening attack out of the white.*

## The Gate

**Left open on purpose** *(Drew, 2026-10-02)*. Every plant here has a gate (`flora/README.md`), and this one's gets found at the table: **see what the players try first**, and let the answer come out of that. *All that is set is what the seed does when it is touched.*

**A carried tearseed is a flashbang the party owns.** That is why anybody bothers.
