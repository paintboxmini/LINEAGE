# Forget-Me-Not

*The ones Harlock wants. Set by Drew, 2026-10-01.*

*Grows on Forget-Me-Not Island, in a ring round the hut at its centre (`places/forget-me-not-island.md`).*

---

**Small, blue, low and thick.** Ordinary forget-me-nots grow in plenty of places and nobody pays for them. **These are the ones that grow in the ring**, exactly on the line where the island's effect begins, and nowhere else on it.

**They are an ingredient in FourthEye** — used in the refining step, up in the rock north of Vulture's Nest (`items/fourtheye.md`; `places/vultures-nest.md`, The finishing house). *What they do in the recipe is not written.*

## The Gate

**Where they grow.** Picking them is trivial. **They grow on the line where names and faces stop holding**, so every flower is picked with one foot inside it, and a picker who works the ring long enough starts losing the person working beside them. *The flowers are the warning and the harvest at once.*

**And the way back is the second half of it.** Anyone who has been there remembers the flowers and very little of the route (`places/forget-me-not-island.md`, How the Party Gets Here). *Getting there twice takes somebody who wrote it down.*
