# Waymoss

A flat grey-green moss that grows on root and trunk in long parallel lines rather than patches — lines that run for yards, turn together, and stop together. It reads almost exactly like handwriting in a language you do not have.

It is not handwriting and it is not directions. **But it moves**, slowly and constantly, and what it is doing is related to what the forest is doing.

---

## Growth

Anywhere shaded and damp, which in the Weald is everywhere. It is the most common plant under the canopy and the least collected.

**The lines redraw themselves over hours.** Not fast enough to watch and not slow enough to miss — a stretch of trunk noted in the morning is differently written by dusk, and a party that walks past the same root twice will have walked past two different pages.

---

## Harvesting

**It can be cut, and cutting it destroys the only thing it was good for.** A strip taken off the trunk stops redrawing within the hour and dries flat, holding whatever it happened to be saying at the moment of the cut. It is then a piece of dead moss with some marks on it, worth about what dead moss is worth.

So the Weald's most useful plant is the one the forest people never take. That is not reverence and nobody frames it as a rule; it is simply that a cut strip does nothing and an uncut one does, and everybody there found that out young.

**Reading it is the real skill, and it cannot be done on purpose.** Forest families do not read waymoss so much as notice it, the same way they navigate — habits instead of maps, cords on the wrist, route-songs (`places/turnroot-weald.md`). Somebody who stops, squints and tries to work out what the lines mean gets a confident wrong answer every time. Somebody who has been under canopy a season finds they have already known for an hour that the forest is moving them, and could not tell you which stretch of moss told them.

**What it actually tells you is weather, not geography.** Whether the Weald is currently routing you, and how hard. It is the same information the Pressure Track is keeping (`quests/turnroot-weald-adventure.md`), arriving through a person's feet instead of a number, and a guide who has it will change a plan without being able to justify the change.

A party will not get this in one visit. They can absolutely get the wrong version of it in one visit, from a confident member who has been studying the lines.

---

## Use

Nothing. There is no trade in it, no reagent in it, and nobody has found a preparation that keeps it alive off the trunk.

It is here because a forest whose signature is that it routes you has exactly one organism that shows the routing happening, and it is worth knowing that the thing is on every trunk in the Weald in plain sight, being walked past.
