# Flinchthorn

***Named 2026-11-19.*** *For what sets it off.*

*Grows inside the Coil, where roots have hollowed the walls (`places/the-coil.md`).*

---

**A low, grey-green cushion of a plant, packed with needles, that grows out of cracks in the Coil's walls at about knee height.** It looks like a tuft of dead thistle. **It is quiet, and it fires at a fright** — *a sudden noise, a fast step, a body moving past it quicker than something careful would.* **Walk past it slowly and it does nothing.**

**When it fires, it empties itself across the passage.** *A hiss, then a sound like hail on stone.*

## At the table

**It fires when anyone runs, shouts or fights in its passage** — *the same things that raise the pressure track* (`places/the-coil.md`, The Pressure Track). **Everyone in that stretch of passage takes 1d6.** *Then it is spent until it regrows.*

**It makes no noise worth hearing.** **The danger is the yelp** — *a character who cries out when the needles land is combat noise, and that is +1 pressure.* *A party that takes it in silence pays only the damage.*

**Spotting it before it fires:** Perception, Senses — *the needles catch the light like frost, and there are always a few spent ones on the floor.*

## The Gate — somebody has to set it off

**It only drops its seed husks once it has emptied its needles.** *Before that, the husks are sealed inside the cushion and tearing it open sets it off in your face.* **So the harvest costs a volley** — *somebody runs past it on purpose, or throws something, or makes the noise the labyrinth punishes.* **In the Coil, that is never free.**

## What it might be good for

*Hints only* (`flora/README.md`). **The needles numb where they go in**, a slow cold creeping out from each prick. **The husks smell of resin and wet stone.** **A needle that has been sitting in a wound for an hour is still sharp.**
