# Flora

Plants, filed by organism the way the bestiary files animals. One file each, same shape: what it is, where it grows, and — the part that matters at a table — **what makes it hard to get.**

**Every plant here has a gate, and no gate is a stat check.** A roll tells you whether a character is good at something. A gate tells you what kind of person or situation gets the thing at all, which is more interesting and produces better scenes. The ones on record so far:

| Plant | Gate |
|-------|------|
| Hush Bloom | **patience.** It only opens in genuine stillness, and two people working a patch do worse than one |
| Stiltwood | **a tide window.** The good wood is underwater except on the deep slow tide |
| Nobody's Pepper | **records.** Picking is trivial; knowing which spoil bank it came off is ledger work |
| Dock Beard | **neglect.** It has to be three years old, and the crews scrape on a schedule to prevent exactly that |
| Hardrun | **conditions.** It is only worth cutting while the water is running hard enough that nobody wants to be out in it |
| Siftreed | **the neighbours.** It grows on old deposit, which is where the Foulhauls live, for the same reason |
| Daycress | **it cannot travel.** Hours out of moving water and it is worthless, in a port that exports everything |
| Black Thorn Vine | **it is all one plant.** Seed costs it nothing; every cut anyone has ever made is a debt to the same creature, and nobody has found an end to count from |
| Luminova | **the harvest lights you up.** Picking is trivial, and every leaf taken makes the walk out brighter |
| Waymoss | **taking it destroys it.** A cut strip stops redrawing within the hour, so the only way to use it is to leave it alone |

Four of the ten are in the Vulture's Nest region, where the framing fact is that almost nothing is native — the ground is made ground and what grows on it came off a boat (`places/vultures-nest.md`, What Grows Here).

The other four are Turnroot Weald, where the framing fact is a law instead: **nothing is taken without a reciprocal gesture** (`quests/turnroot-weald-adventure.md`). That is a floor every Weald plant sits on before its own gate applies, and it is why the Weald's gates tend to be about debt and consequence where the Nest's are about logistics. **Waymoss is the odd one in the whole folder** — it has no use, no trade and no preparation, and it is here because a forest whose signature is that it routes you has exactly one organism that shows the routing happening.

**These lived in `bestiary/` until 2026-09-18**, under a note on the Hush Bloom reading "flora rather than fauna, filed here with the other ingredient organisms." That was a workaround and it is gone; anything pointing at the old paths has been repointed.
