# Black Thorn Vine

*The vine of the Thorne Throne. Its seed is on the market as the* Echothorn Seed *(`rules/items.md`, `items/turnroot-weald-items.md`).*

Black, finger-thick, and thorned along its whole length, running through the Turnroot Weald at every height from root to canopy. It is everywhere in the forest and it is easy to walk past.

**It is one plant.** A single continuous strand, wrapped back over itself so many times that nobody has ever found either end — not a species, not a stand, one individual with an unknown amount of forest inside its loops. That is the actual echo in the Thorne Throne's name, and the faint ring the seeds carry when shaken is only the smaller half of the joke.

---

## Growth

It does not seed new plants, because there is nothing to seed. The strand extends. Where it finds a way up it goes up, where it meets itself it wraps, and over a long enough stretch of time the difference between growing and knotting stops mattering.

The pods hang in clusters wherever a loop crosses a loop, which is the only part of the plant that behaves like an ordinary plant and the only part anybody harvests.

---

## Harvesting

**The seeds are free and the vine is not.** Taking pods costs the plant nothing — they come away clean, the loop is undisturbed, and the reciprocal gesture the Weald asks for (`quests/turnroot-weald-adventure.md`) is easy to make honestly because you have genuinely taken very little.

**Cutting it is the problem, and the problem is that you cannot tell how much you have cut.** There is no second vine. The strand a party severs to clear a path in the morning and the strand they cut for cordage that afternoon are the same organism, and so is the one four families cut last winter on the other side of the canopy. Nobody has ever been able to total it, because nobody has ever found an end to count from.

The forest does not distinguish between one cut and forty. It is one being and the debt is cumulative, which makes Black Thorn the single worst thing in the Weald to be casual with — the gesture is owed to a creature whose extent is unknown and whose memory is not being kept by anyone the party can talk to.

**The sound is the tell, and nobody reads it.** Every pod in the forest rattles at the same pitch, in the deep canopy and at the margin and forty miles apart. Two people comparing seeds from opposite ends of the Weald would hear the same note and conclude they had found a consistent species.

---

## Use

Ground to dust and mixed with reagents, the seed amplifies spells and incantations — the established magical-reagent trade out of the Weald, and the reason a forest with no road has buyers.

As the **Echothorn Seed** it is sold whole and used raw: +2 to the next damage or healing roll, no Action spent, about 45 gold. That price is for the seed. Nothing in it is priced for the vine.
