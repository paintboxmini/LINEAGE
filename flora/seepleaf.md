# Seepleaf

***Named 2026-09-25.*** *For the seam it lives in — the sandstone seeps and this is what grows where it does.*

*Grows in the sandstone high country north of Vulture's Nest (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Shape of the Ground).*

---

**A low, thick-leaved plant that lives in the wet cuts.** The cliffs behind the Nest are dry brown rock on their faces and soaked in their folds — drizzle runs off the flats and collects in the shaded seams, and that is the only standing water in the whole range. Seepleaf is what grows in it. Grey-green, close to the stone, leaves like short fat tongues that stay cool to the touch in a country with no shade worth the name.

**Bite one and your eyes stream.** Sharp, mineral, aggressively green, and nothing like food — it is closer to horseradish than to a vegetable, and the sensation arrives in the nose and the eyes before the tongue has an opinion. *A mouthful is unpleasant. A ground measure of it, worked into something, is the one flavour this town does not have.*

## Why it matters to the Nest

**The Nest eats salted strip, dock broth and chewfat** — meat, fish and fat, and very little green on any plate (`places/vultures-nest.md`, What Grows Here). It is not a hardship and nobody thinks of it as one, because there has never been an alternative.

**Seepleaf is the alternative, and it is up a cliff.** Which is why it turns up as an ingredient somebody climbed for rather than as a thing on a stall, and why it is priced like an ingredient and not like a vegetable.

**It is also native**, which almost nothing near this town is. The Nest's ground is made ground and what grows on it came off a boat (`flora/README.md`). **The high country is the one stretch near Vulture's Nest that nobody built**, and the things growing up there have been there the whole time.

## The Gate — hands

**You need both of them on the rock.**

Finding Seepleaf is not hard once somebody has shown you what a wet seam looks like, and picking it is nothing at all. **The gate is the descent.** Going up, a climber can be encumbered and careful. Coming down the same cliff with a harvest is a different problem, and it is the only problem: *anything that has to be held is anything you are not climbing with.*

So what comes back is what fits in a closed pack against your spine and survives being pressed against rock the whole way down. A haul is small, and it is small for a reason that has nothing to do with how much is growing up there. **There is always more Seepleaf than anyone can carry.**

**Which sets the real cost: more people, or more trips.** And that is where the gate stops being a logistics problem and starts being a decision — *the thing that lets you carry more is more bodies in the high country*, and the high country belongs to somebody (`bestiary/gollop.md`, They Hold Ground). **A party that sizes its harvest up sizes the encounter up with it**, and nobody has to explain that out loud for it to be felt on the second trip.

## What it does

**Nothing set, on purpose** *(Drew, 2026-10-10: Kevin's ingredients get hints, not effects — `campaign/kevin.md`, The Ingredients)*.

**What it hints at:** it makes your eyes stream and your nose run before it is even in your mouth, and a pinch of the dust in the air clears a room of cooks. **Whatever Kevin makes of it is a conversation between him and Drew at the table.**
