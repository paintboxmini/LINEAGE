# Wakebark

*An understory shrub of Elowen (`places/elowen.md`). Created 2026-10-06, for the village that cannot sleep and for Kevin's kitchen.*

**A low, scrubby bush that grows under the bloodelm** (`flora/bloodelm.md`), and **its inner bark keeps you awake.** Chewed, it is bitter and it sharpens you — *the way a strong cup does, no more than that.* Elowen's loggers have always chewed it on early mornings. **It is ordinary.** It was ordinary until a month ago.

**It puts sleep off. It does not replace it.** Chew enough and you stay up; keep it up for days and you are a frightened, shaking person who is still awake. *What it does against whatever comes for a sleeper is part of the sleep question, and that is not set* (`places/elowen.md`, Not Yet Set).

---

## Growth

**Only under bloodelm, only in shade**, in the leaf litter between the big trunks. Plentiful once, everywhere in the forest, right up to the edge of the clearing.

## Harvesting

**The gate: the near ones are gone.** *Picking it is nothing. Getting to one that still has bark on it is everything.*

**A month of a whole village chewing it day and night has stripped every bush within an easy walk of the clearing.** What is left grows **deep in the woods** — where the redjaws are (`bestiary/redjaw.md`), where the woman in the shroud is said to be, and where the search party went and did not come back (`places/elowen.md`). *Every villager knows exactly where there is more. Nobody is going.*

**The last of what was picked is behind Kerra's counter**, and she is rationing it (`places/elowen.md`, Kerra's).

## Use

**In Elowen, it is how the village is still alive.** Chewed raw, as often as they can stand.

**In Kevin's kitchen, it is a drink and nothing else** (`campaign/kevin.md`, The beverages). **It does not survive the burn**, so it cannot charge a round — the same exception Burnrind is in the other direction (`flora/burnrind.md`). **One measure makes two servings of Up All Night**, and there is nothing else the measure could have been.
