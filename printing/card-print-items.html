TERRORMITE CAPSULE
Consumable
Use (1) — Duration: 1 combat.
Gain Armour 1 and your attacks deal +1d6 damage.
End of combat: Shuffle 2 Wound cards into your deck.
ECHO SHELL
Consumable
Use (1): Repeat the action you took last turn. Does not work if you cannot retrieve the previously used card from your discard pile.
BLOOD PHIAL
Consumable
Use (1): Add the effect of the last card the target used this combat to an attack you make now.
IMPRINT SIGIL
Consumable
Use (1): Target must follow a simple command for 1 turn.
UNIVERSAL PIN
Consumable — Does not require an action to use.
Use (1): Fix a target in place. They cannot be moved until their next turn.
PHASE DRAUGHT
Consumable
Use (1): Take your turn normally. At the end of your turn, return to your previous position and state.
BARBED WRAP
Consumable — Strips of wire-wrapped leather
Duration: 1 battle.
Effect: When you are targeted by an attack, deal 1 damage to the attacker.
CARRION FEATHER
Consumable — Single dark feather, tied with road-worn cord
Activates automatically: The first time you would be forced to reposition, negate that effect instead. Then this item is spent.
SPLIT WEDGE
Consumable — Iron wedge, worn grip, field tool
Use (1): Gain Anchored — +2 damage this turn.
DOCKHOOK LINE
Consumable — Weighted hook on heavy rope
Use (1): Pull yourself or a target enemy to Frontline.
LOW LANTERN
Consumable — Squat iron lantern, smoked glass
Use (1): Gain Obscure for 1 combat.
SALTED STRIP
Consumable — Dried, heavily salted meat
Use: Heal 2 HP.
DOCK BROTH
Consumable — Hot, murky, smells like low tide
Use: Remove 1 status card from your deck. Gain Disadvantage on your next damage roll.
CHEWFAT RATION
Consumable — Dense rendered fat block, travel-wrapped
Passive (1 combat): Gain Armour 1. At the end of each of your turns, discard 1 card.
