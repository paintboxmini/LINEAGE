## Observations

**Balance is working.** Both players went under half HP from 3 starter rabbits. Intentionally difficult. They survived but felt the pressure.

**Hand size as information.** Players figured out mid-fight that rabbits have a hand size of 2 (Mind 1 + 1). Once they understood that a rabbit who just attacked could only block once, they doubled into the same target to guarantee damage. This is exactly the strategic layer the system is built for — they found it themselves, first session.

**RPS reads.** GM was hitting consistently. With only 2 cards in hand the rabbits had limited options, making reads easier. This will not remain true as encounters scale.

**Flee at 1 HP.** Not noted whether any rabbit fled — worth tracking in future sessions.

---

## Range Distribution — Emergent Design Note

Neither player chose cards based on range during deck creation — selection was driven by character vision. End result: each player had 2 cards restricted to their preferred range, 1 card opposite, and 6 cards with Both. Just enough restriction to make positioning matter without overwhelming new players.

The distribution emerged naturally from thematic card selection. Players who visualize a ranged character and pick accordingly will produce a deck whose range requirements reflect that posture — without ever engaging with range as a mechanic during selection. Thematic identity and mechanical identity are aligned closely enough that choosing by feel produces correct results.

Both as a training wheel for early game is intentional. Flexibility while learning, restriction as the card pool deepens.
