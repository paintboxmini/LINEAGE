# Session 1 Combat Review — Briarwatch Jackrabbit

**Date:** 2026-05-06  
**Players:** Kevin, Ollie (Drew DMing)  
**Encounter:** 3 Briarwatch Jackrabbits, Watchtower Ruins

---

## Data

| | Start HP | End HP | Damage Taken |
|--|----------|--------|--------------|
| Player 1 | 18 | 8 | 10 |
| Player 2 | 15 | 7 | 8 |

- Players hit rabbits **6 times total** (2 each)
- Rabbit damage range: **2–3 per hit** (typical)
- Outlier: **6 damage** from a Soul-based attack (Bolt, Soul 3 + d4)
- RPS outcomes roughly even — expected given rabbit hand size of 2

---

## Observations

**Balance is working.** Both players went under half HP from 3 starter rabbits. Intentionally difficult. They survived but felt the pressure.

**Hand size as information.** Players figured out mid-fight that rabbits have a hand size of 2 (Mind 1 + 1). Once they understood that a rabbit who just attacked could only block once, they doubled into the same target to guarantee damage. This is exactly the strategic layer the system is built for — they found it themselves, first session.

**RPS reads.** GM was hitting consistently. With only 2 cards in hand the rabbits had limited options, making reads easier. This will not remain true as encounters scale.

**Flee at 1 HP.** Not noted whether any rabbit fled — worth tracking in future sessions.

---

## Verdict

Huge success. System landed clean. Players hooked. Kevin specifically wants a boss encounter next. Ollie engaged but prefers shorter sessions — design toward faster payoffs for her.

No rules confusion reported. No mechanical breakdowns.
