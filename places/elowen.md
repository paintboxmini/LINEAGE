# Elowen

*An island, and the logging village on it. Named and set by Drew, 2026-10-05.*

**A logging village that is dying in its sleep.** *What is doing it is Freta, Osha's great-grandmother* (`characters/freta.md`) — GM only, below.

---

## The Village Before

**A logging village, and that is all it has ever been** *(Drew, 2026-10-05)*. **The island is forest, and the forest is bloodelm** (`flora/bloodelm.md`) — a red-hearted wood worth a great deal to people who will never see the tree it came from. *Elowen fells it, works it, and sells it off the island. Everything else the village has, it bought with that.*

**About sixty people, a month ago.** A handful of families who have felled the same forest for generations, and who know each other the way a village that size does: everybody's business, everybody's grudges, everybody at everybody's funeral. **The felling is taught by hand inside those families**, and it is what makes them worth trading with (`flora/bloodelm.md`, Harvesting).

**What it looks like.** Steep-roofed timber houses built close together in a clearing, with the forest standing right up against the back walls. A landing where the timber goes out. *The trees are taller than anything in the village, and half of them are ringed and weeping red down the bark* (`flora/bloodelm.md`), **which is ordinary forestry here** and does nothing for a stranger's nerves on the first walk up from the water.

**An ordinary hard-working place.** Early mornings, heavy work, a lot of sawdust, a tavern, a bell. *Nobody on Elowen thought of themselves as living somewhere strange until a month ago.*

## What Is Happening

**Then they started dying in their sleep.** Visions first, then nightmares, night after night. **If they die in the dream, they die for real.** Nobody has found a mark on the bodies, because there is nothing to find.

**When the party arrives, there are between twenty and thirty left** — half the village, or a little less. *A month is long enough that everybody has buried somebody, and short enough that nobody has stopped counting.*

## When the Party Arrives

**Everybody is fighting to stay awake.** That is the whole village's day now, and its night. *What that looks like is the thing to play*: people keeping each other talking, nobody sitting down for long, somebody nodding off mid-sentence and being shaken before they go under. **They are not afraid of the party. They are afraid of closing their eyes.**

**And they have started looking at each other.** A village this small, losing people this fast, to something nobody can see, does what small villages do: **it decides one of its own is doing it.** *Who gets blamed, and how far it has gone, is the GM's to play — but by the time the party lands, somebody already has a name in mind.*

**The work has stopped.** Nobody fells a tree on no sleep and walks away from it. *So no timber has left Elowen in weeks, and somebody who buys it has noticed* (Not Yet Set, below).

*It is a month of not sleeping, so they are slow, short-tempered and frightened, and some of them are past the point where they can tell a dream from a waking moment.* **Anybody the party talks to gets a name when they do** — none is written yet.

## What Is Doing It — GM Only

**Freta** (`characters/freta.md`). **A hag**, and a holder of the cursegiving line (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, What they are). **She feeds on suffering, and a village's nightmares are a great deal of it.** *She is not heartless. This is what she is.*

**She left Forget-Me-Not hut about a month ago, and the dying started when she arrived** *(Drew, 2026-10-05; `places/forget-me-not-island.md`, The Hut)*. **If Caine has smelled the hut, he knows her the moment he is near her** — the scrap says yes, and the scent is the one he found there (`places/forget-me-not-island.md`, The scent).

**Nobody in the village suspects a stranger**, because nobody has seen one. *They are blaming each other, and the one person they should be looking for is not one of them.*

**Lily, the Dream-Hunter, is not part of this** *(Drew, 2026-10-05)*.

## Tone

**Red Riding Hood (2011)'s Daggerhorn, crossed with A Nightmare on Elm Street** *(Drew, 2026-10-05)*.

- **From Daggerhorn:** a timber village hemmed in by tall trees, a small community with every old grievance within arm's reach, and a fear that turns neighbours on each other — *the monster might be one of us*. **The red is the bloodelm**, and it is everywhere.
- **From Elm Street:** ordinary people in an ordinary place, the one rule that you must not fall asleep, and the night as the enemy. **Exhaustion is the horror as much as the dying is.** *People who have not slept in a week make terrible decisions, and the GM gets to play every one of them.*

**Play it like a working village with the work stopped.** The axes are leaning by the doors. The sawpit is full of rainwater. *What has stopped tells the party more than anything anyone says.*
