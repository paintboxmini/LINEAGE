# Elowen

*An island, and the logging village on it. Named and set by Drew, 2026-10-05; finished 2026-10-06.*

**A logging village that is dying in its sleep.** *What is doing it is Freta, Osha's great-grandmother* (`characters/freta.md`) — GM only, below.

**Ready for the table** *(Drew, 2026-10-06)*. Villagers, a shop, a tavern, the woods and what is in them. **Anything more waits until it is about to be used.**

---

## The Village Before

**A logging village, and that is all it has ever been** *(Drew, 2026-10-05)*. **The island is forest, and the forest is bloodelm** (`flora/bloodelm.md`) — a red-hearted wood worth a great deal to people who will never see the tree it came from. *Elowen fells it, works it, and sells it off the island. Everything else the village has, it bought with that.*

**About sixty people, a month ago.** A handful of families who have felled the same forest for generations, and who know each other the way a village that size does: everybody's business, everybody's grudges, everybody at everybody's funeral. **The felling is taught by hand inside those families**, and it is what makes them worth trading with (`flora/bloodelm.md`, Harvesting).

**What it looks like.** Steep-roofed timber houses built close together in a clearing, with the forest standing right up against the back walls. A landing where the timber goes out. A bell on a post in the middle. *The trees are taller than anything in the village, and half of them are ringed and weeping red down the bark* (`flora/bloodelm.md`), **which is ordinary forestry here** and does nothing for a stranger's nerves on the first walk up from the water.

**Three old rules, older than anyone living:** loggers work in crews, they come out of the trees before dark, and **nobody sleeps in the woods.** *The redjaws are why* (`bestiary/redjaw.md`).

## What Is Happening

**Then they started dying in their sleep.** **Every one of them dreams of the same thing: an old woman in a black shroud, hunting them** *(Drew, 2026-10-06)*. Through the village, through the trees, through their own houses. *Nobody has seen her face.* **If she catches them in the dream, they die for real.** Nobody has found a mark on the bodies, because there is nothing to find.

**When the party arrives, there are between twenty and thirty left** — half the village, or a little less. *A month is long enough that everybody has buried somebody, and short enough that nobody has stopped counting.* **They stopped ringing the bell for the dead about two weeks in.**

**The search party.** About two weeks ago, **six men went into the woods to find her**, led by **Jory** (People, below). *Everyone had dreamed her; some of them had started saying she was real, and out there.* **They did not come back.** Nobody has gone after them.

## When the Party Arrives

**Everybody is fighting to stay awake.** That is the whole village's day now, and its night. People keep each other talking, nobody sits down for long, and somebody nods off mid-sentence and gets shaken before they go under. **Everybody is chewing wakebark** (`flora/wakebark.md`), and there is almost none left. **They are not afraid of the party. They are afraid of closing their eyes.**

*It is a month of not sleeping, so they are slow, short-tempered and frightened, and some of them are past the point where they can tell a dream from a waking moment.*

**The work has stopped.** The axes are leaning by the doors. The sawpit is full of rainwater. *No timber has left Elowen in weeks, and somebody who buys it has noticed* (Not Yet Set, below).

### Rumours

**What the village is saying, to anyone who will listen** — none of it reliable, some of it dangerous. *Hand them out one at a time.*

- **"She's one of us."** An old woman in the dream means an old woman in the village. *This is the one that is getting people hurt* (Wenna, below)
- **"She's real, and she's out there."** People have seen a dark shape at the treeline at dusk. *Jory believed this one, and went*
- **"The search party found her."** And stayed. Or are with her now. *Said mostly by people who need them to be alive*
- **"It's the trees."** A forest that bleeds was always going to want something back. *The oldest families hate hearing this*
- **"Don't dream of the woods."** Whoever dies, the last thing they dreamed was the woods. *True, so far as anybody can tell*

### Paranoia, and the violence

**The village has started turning on itself** *(Drew, 2026-10-06)*. Two men fought in the tavern over who fell asleep on watch. A door has been marked. **Wenna's window has been put in with a stone** (People, below). *Nobody has been killed by a neighbour yet. The party arrives somewhere that is about to have its first.*

## People

**Anybody the party can talk to face to face has a name** (`CLAUDE.md`, Not yours to write). *Five, and that is the village for now — everybody else is a face in the tavern until a player makes them matter.*

- **Kerra — keeps the shop** (Kerra's, below). Practical, tight-lipped, and the one person in the village who still has some wakebark. *She is rationing it, and everybody hates her for it, and she is right.*
- **Jago — keeps the tavern** (The Heartwood, below). Big, tired, kind. **He has kept the place lit every night since it started**, so nobody has to be awake alone. *He has not slept properly in a month and it shows.*
- **Wenna — the oldest feller left.** **The last person in her family who knows how to bleed a bloodelm** (`flora/bloodelm.md`, Harvesting), and an old woman who lives alone at the edge of the clearing. **Which is why the village has started saying she is the woman in the shroud.** *She is not. The one person who could save Elowen's trade is the one the village is closest to killing.*
- **Bryok — Jory's brother.** Angry, frightened, and **the loudest voice saying it is Wenna.** He wants two things — to go into the woods after Jory, and to make somebody pay — and *he cannot do the first, so he is working up to the second.*
- **Jory — led the search party, and is gone.** *Not met; talked about constantly. Everybody has an opinion about whether he was brave or a fool.*

## Kerra's

**The village's one shop**, and it sells what the timber boats bring in: rope, oil, salt, cloth, nails, tools, flour, ordinary supplies at ordinary prices (`rules/equipment.md`). *Nothing exotic; the exotic thing on this island grows outside.*

**Behind the counter, the last of the wakebark** (`flora/wakebark.md`). She gives it out a little at a time, to the families with children first. **She will not sell it to strangers** — not for gold, because gold does not keep anybody awake. *Somebody who goes into the woods and brings more back is somebody she will trade with for anything in the shop.*

**She buys, too.** She is the one who deals with the timber buyers, so **she knows who they are and where the wood goes** — and she knows the boats have stopped (Not Yet Set, below).

## The Heartwood

**The tavern**, named for the red at the centre of the tree. Low beams, bloodelm panelling gone dark with smoke, one long table, a fire that has not gone out in a month.

**It is where the village goes at night now.** *Nobody wants to be awake alone, so they come here, and Jago keeps the fire and the talk going until morning.* **It is where the party will hear the rumours, see the fights, and meet everybody at once.**

**Rooms upstairs**, and Jago will give the party one. *Whether anybody sleeps in it is the question below.*

## The Woods

**Bloodelm, all the way across the island**, with wakebark in the shade under it. **Close in, the bushes have been stripped bare. Deep in, they have not.** *Every reason to go into the woods and every reason not to are in the same place.*

**The redjaws** (`bestiary/redjaw.md`) — a pack of four is a real fight. **They have been coming closer to the clearing at night.**

## What Is Doing It — GM Only

**Freta** (`characters/freta.md`). **A hag**, and a holder of the cursegiving line (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, What they are). **She feeds on suffering, and a village's nightmares are a great deal of it.** *She is not heartless. This is what she is.*

**She is hiding in the woods** *(Drew, 2026-10-06)*, deep in, away from the clearing. **In the dreams she is an old woman in a black shroud.** *The shroud is why nobody has seen her face — and why nobody in the village has guessed she is not human. She is Lizardkin.*

**She left Forget-Me-Not hut about a month ago, and the dying started when she arrived** *(Drew, 2026-10-05; `places/forget-me-not-island.md`, The Hut)*. **If Caine has smelled the hut, he knows her the moment he is near her** — the scrap says yes, and the scent is the one he found there (`places/forget-me-not-island.md`, The scent).

**What happened to the search party — one that fits, not decided.** They went in, did not find her, and **night came while they were still in the trees.** They broke the oldest rule and slept in the woods. *She found them there, and the redjaws found what was left.* **Their camp is still out there** — six bedrolls, a cold fire, no marks on anybody — *and finding it tells the party exactly how this works without anybody having to explain it.*

**Lily, the Dream-Hunter, is not part of this** *(Drew, 2026-10-05)*.

## Tone

**Red Riding Hood (2011)'s Daggerhorn, crossed with A Nightmare on Elm Street** *(Drew, 2026-10-05)*.

- **From Daggerhorn:** a timber village hemmed in by tall trees, wolves in the woods, a small community with every old grievance within arm's reach, and a fear that turns neighbours on each other — *the monster might be one of us*. **The red is the bloodelm**, and it is everywhere.
- **From Elm Street:** ordinary people in an ordinary place, a figure who hunts you in your sleep, the one rule that you must not fall asleep, and the night as the enemy. **Exhaustion is the horror as much as the dying is.** *People who have not slept in a month make terrible decisions, and the GM gets to play every one of them.*

**Play it like a working village with the work stopped.** *What has stopped tells the party more than anything anyone says.*
