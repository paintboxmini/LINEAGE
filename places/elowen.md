# Elowen

*An island, and the small town on it. Named and set by Drew, 2026-10-05.*

**A town that is dying in its sleep.** *What is doing it is Freta, Osha's great-grandmother* (`characters/freta.md`) — GM only, below.

---

## What Is Happening

**A month ago, about sixty people lived here.** An ordinary small island community, getting on with an ordinary life.

**Then they started dying in their sleep.** Visions first, then nightmares, night after night. **If they die in the dream, they die for real.** Nobody has found a mark on the bodies, because there is nothing to find.

**When the party arrives, there are between twenty and thirty left** — half the town, or a little less. *A month is long enough that everybody has buried somebody, and short enough that nobody has stopped counting.*

## When the Party Arrives

**Everybody is fighting to stay awake.** That is the whole town's day now, and its night. *What that looks like is the thing to play*: people keeping each other talking, nobody sitting down for long, somebody nodding off mid-sentence and being shaken before they go under. **They are not afraid of the party. They are afraid of closing their eyes.**

*It is a month of not sleeping, so they are slow, short-tempered and frightened, and some of them are past the point where they can tell a dream from a waking moment.* **Anybody the party talks to gets a name when they do** — none is written yet.

## What Is Doing It — GM Only

**Freta** (`characters/freta.md`). **A hag**, and a holder of the cursegiving line (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, What they are). **She feeds on suffering, and a town's nightmares are a great deal of it.** *She is not heartless. This is what she is.*

**She lived in Forget-Me-Not hut before she came here** (`places/forget-me-not-island.md`, The Hut). **If Caine has smelled the hut, he knows her the moment he is near her** — the scrap says yes, and the scent is the one he found there (`places/forget-me-not-island.md`, The scent).
