# The Collection Plate

A carved-out hollow in the Fog Basin (`places/fog-basin.md`). Not more fog — a circular clearing the basin tolerates, held open by hanging sheets and human habit.

---

## Shape

- **Circular clearing.** Packed ground, worn paths that are habits more than streets.
- **Walls:** hanging sheets (cloth, sail, tarp, stitched hide) in a rough ring. **They keep the fog out** — not a symbolic boundary, an actual one. Beyond them: basin. Inside: the clearing and the inward-facing shacks, clear air, ordinary visibility. `places/fog-basin.md`'s Blind terrain rule doesn't reach past the sheet-line.
- **Shacks face inward**, backs to the sheets. Doors open toward the center, never toward the white.
- **Center:** trampled common ground; often a landmark (dry well collar, post cluster, or the path of the nightly collection bowl). Paths radiate inward.

No crown seal. No Regency payment. The Plate persists because people keep using it and keep fearing the way out.

---

## Who is here

Displaced people. Travelers who took a wrong path and never found the true one again. Some who came looking for escape and found a circle instead.

They are **not metaphysically trapped**. They got lost. Most are too afraid to try the sheets a second time without a guide, a debt, or a reason stronger than comfort.

---

## Fogcallers and Echoes

- **Fogcallers hunt in the fog only.** The Plate is not their hunting ground. They may attend the threshold or escort a claim to the sheet-line; they do not work the interior as prey ground.
- **Echoes** still walk old paths *toward* the ring. A lost traveler can follow an Echo in and become a resident by accident.

---

## Folk ritual (past coherence)

Gestures kept after meaning frayed. Origin is basin etiquette and lost-teaching-lost instruction, worn down by repetition:

| Basin etiquette root | Plate habit |
|----------------------|-------------|
| Junction offering | Scraps on nails at the sheets |
| Don't chase Echoes | Don't speak to shapes at the cloth |
| Speak low / don't map loud | Face inward when talking about the white |
| Trade, don't only take | A literal collection plate (bowl/tin) passed among doors |

Rites coordinate the camp. They do not need to be theologically correct. Breaking them marks you as someone who might walk into the white wrong — social consequence first, basin consequence if you actually do.

**Origin (GM):** Junction manners brought indoors; each generation of the lost teaching the next; labels washed off until only choreography remained. Not Seats doctrine, not Regency procedure, not Pell's invention (he would recognize the bones and dislike the drift).

---

## Tone

A collection plate passes through a crowd and gathers what people can spare. This place gathers who the basin mislaid. Hard to leave. Not impossible — just terrifying.

**Do not rush a full NPC roster.** Let the place breathe as location before it becomes a cast list. Faces arrive when the table walks into them.
