# The Unheld Shoreline

*The southern coast where the continent meets the grey water, at roughly **(0, −11)** on the Session 1 relative grid (`world/geography-overview.md`) — directly south of the Roadhouse at (0, −7), about a day on foot. Unnamed by anyone. Nobody lives here and nothing is built here.*

*The party's arrival on this beach is `quests/washed-ashore.md`. This file is the ground itself, which stays here whether or not that adventure is being run.*

---

## The Water

Dark grey, folding inward and crashing onto itself over and over. The sound arrives before anything else registers and it is the wrong sound — a surf breaking the way no surf should, low and even, as though the water were made of static.

Nothing sails it. Nothing fishes it. The coastline is a hard threshold and unheldness does not cross it, not by flowing and not by being carried (`world/the-unheld.md`).

## The Ground

Pale white sand, giving way inland to rolling grass. Beyond that, rock in long jagged outcroppings.

A river runs down to meet the water here, and the outcroppings form a **cave entrance in the river** itself. Sections of that river turn to coral — muted rather than tropical, dusty pink and grey-green, keeping to the world's flat light.

**The riverbank is where anything washed up ends up**, which makes it the natural place for a find (`items/washed-ashore-items.md`).

## What Lives Here

- **Wrackclaw** (`bestiary/wrackclaw.md`, CTR 4) — a swarm of small scavengers working the riverbank and just past it. They read anything freshly arrived the way they read anything else freshly dead.
- **Hullback** (`bestiary/hullback.md`, CTR 17) — solitary and much older, holding the rock outcroppings cave in the river. It wins by patience, not pursuit, which is why it keeps the high ground and the cover inland of the tideline rather than the open sand. Years of accumulated salvage are packed into the rocks it's called home.
- **Flapjack Octopus** (`bestiary/flapjack-octopus.md`) — works the coral shelves. One dart of movement, then gone. Not tied to this water specifically.

## Inland

A day's walk north to the Roadhouse (`places/roadhouse.md`), and the only reason it takes a day is what's in the way: **one pocket of In-Between sits across that stretch** (`world/eclipseria-overview.md`). Bounded, unmarked, and unroutable — nobody has surveyed a road out here, so there's no way to know it's coming and no way around. Enter it, cross it, and the far side is ordinary country again.

That makes the Roadhouse the first real haven in any direction from this beach.
