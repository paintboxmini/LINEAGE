# Havenrise

*This location is under active development. GMs should treat it as a blank region until further material is available.*

---

A region of dense green and high cliffs at the eastern edge of the map. Havenrise does not advertise itself. Travelers who have been there describe a place that has made deliberate choices about what to keep out — which implies it has made choices about what to keep in. Further details vary significantly between accounts.
