# The Pirate Guild

*Vulture's Nest's privateers. Set by Drew, 2026-10-12.*

**Legally sanctioned privateering.** The Guild's ships sail out of Vulture's Nest under Regency licence, and **attacking, raiding and plundering the Nest's rivals is legal work** — logged, judged and taxed like any other cargo. The water around the Nest is crowded with tribes and nations that are not the Regency's, and some of them raid back.

**In the Nest it is a respectable trade.** A privateer captain is a person of standing on the dock; a crew signs articles, shares a prize by rule, and comes home with money in a port that knows how to spend it. *Fast ships, boarding actions, rival captains and a full hold on the way home* — **the door to pirate adventures, for a party that wants one.** Offered, never pushed.

---

## Who

- **Harlock**, captain of the **Arcadia**, **head of the Guild on this stretch of the web** — which is why he holds the third seat on the Tideward Compact (`characters/harlock.md`; `places/vultures-nest.md`, The Tideward Compact)
- **Amelia**, his first mate, a Rasha — runs the Arcadia, keeps its charter, signs on crew (`characters/amelia.md`)
- **Captain Drake** — the Guild captain who is *not* its head, and the one a party works with if they want a pirate other than Harlock (`characters/drake.md`)

## The Licence — Cedric

**Commissions come through Lord Cedric, the Bear** (`world/the-regency.md`) — external defence and force of last resort. *A privateer is the Regency's force at the edges, paid for by what it takes.* A commission names the ship, the captain, and **who the ship may take**. *Taking anybody else is piracy, and the licence is what makes the difference.*

## The Prize Court — Isabella

*Historically inspired: this is how real prize courts worked, and it is the system the table will use.*

**A capture is not a prize until a court says so.** **Lady Isabella, the Panther**, Supreme Magistrate, presides over the Temple of Justice (`world/the-regency.md`; `places/capital/capital.md`, Temple of Justice), and **prize cases are hers.**

**The prize courts sit in Vulture's Nest** *(Drew, 2026-10-15)*. Different people run them, **and every one is under Isabella's jurisdiction** — a capture is condemned where it lands, not eight days away in the capital. *Who sits a given bench is worth naming once the party is standing in front of one.*

1. **Take the ship and bring it in.** Crew, cargo and hull, to port.
2. **Do not open the hold.** Breaking into cargo before the court has ruled is the oldest way to lose a prize — *the court wants to see what was taken, not what is left.*
3. **The court condemns it.** It hears whether the capture fell inside the commission. If it did, the prize is **condemned** — declared lawfully taken — and sold.
4. **The money is shared.** The Regency's share, the Compact's percentage, the ship's owner, and the crew by the shares in their articles.
5. **A bad capture is released.** If the court finds against the captain, the prize goes back and the captain can be made to pay for the trouble.

**And it is Isabella's court, which means it is for sale** (`world/the-regency.md`: the law serves whoever's paying). *A doubtful prize can be condemned for a price, and a sound one can be released for a bigger one.* **Every capture has a second fight in it, and it is fought with paper.**

## The Muirn

**The Guild's standing enemy is the Muirn** *(Drew, 2026-10-12)* — the southern Lizardkin tribe, from the swamps south of the Nest (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Muirn). **The Muirn hunt vessels that sail close to their territory, and the Nest's pirates return the favour.** Muirn boats are lawful prizes under the Guild's commissions.

**What the Nest knows, and what it does not.** To a privateer they are *the southern Lizardkin*, deadly in the water and worse in the reeds. **The name Muirn and the island Gharial are not dock talk** (`places/gharial.md`): Muirn boats come out of the swamp and go back into it, and no Guild ship has ever followed one home. *Which keeps Gharial hard to find, and makes a privateer who has fought them the closest thing the Nest has to an expert.*

*This crosses Caine's search* (`campaign/pat.md`; `characters/osha.md`): **the people the Guild raids are Osha's people.** How that plays is the table's.
