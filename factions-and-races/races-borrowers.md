# Borrowers

*Subterranean humanoids. Full entry, stat block and cards at `bestiary/borrower.md` — a people who also have a stat block, which is the ordinary case down there rather than a contradiction.*

**Small, mole-like, and precise.** They carved most of the tunnel network in the Hollow Below Briarwatch over generations and know every seam, soft pocket, and load-bearing column in it. Children learn to read stone before they learn much else; a Borrower who can't tell a load-bearing seam from a soft one by feel doesn't get the chance to need a second skill.

**They are not fighters. They are survivors who have learned to fight.** A Borrower's ideal outcome to almost anything is the version where nobody had to fight at all — not principle, experience: fighting costs tunnels, and tunnels are the one thing they can't dig fast enough to replace.

**Elders hold real authority**, more than one at a time, chosen for accumulated trust rather than any single skill. Underneath that, everything runs on competence — the tunnel-sense expert calls the dig, the listening-post keeper calls the danger, the cook calls the ration, and nobody defers to a Borrower outside their own competence. Anything touching the whole warren, including what an outsider walks away with, goes to the Elders (`quests/hollow-below-briarwatch.md`, Factions).

**They live under pressure and have for a long time** — flooded stores, collapsing nursery tunnels, and a Stonecoil range that keeps expanding upward. Homes are shared sleeping-mat chambers, several families to a room, moved deeper or shallower as that range shifts. Nothing is built to last, because nothing down there has stayed safe long enough to be worth building permanent.

**They are already trading with Briarwatch and neither side knows it.** Roots and tubers work down through cracks from the fields above and get harvested where they hang — one-directional, unnoticed, and running for generations (`places/briarwatch.md`).

## Trait and Passive

**Listening Post** is their Trait — at the start of its turn a Borrower may Scry 1 of any one combatant's deck, no Action spent (`bestiary/borrower.md`). The clay bowls pressed to the walls aren't decoration.

**BURROW is a card**, not a Trait — it spends an Action, so it was never anything else (`cards/borrower-hollow.md`).

**No player-facing Passive is written yet.** Reading stone by feel is the likeliest candidate: innate, specific, and with a natural Applies When that fails the moment there's no stone to read.
