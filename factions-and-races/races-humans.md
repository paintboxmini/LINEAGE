# Humans

**The most numerous people in the known world, the most widely scattered, and the one nobody names.** Human is what a place assumes unless it's told otherwise, which is itself the interesting fact about them: every other people in this world is a people, and humans are the background the others get described against.

**They hold the institutions.** The Regency is formally a council of mixed races (`world/the-regency.md`) and is human-run in practice at almost every level below it. The People of Promise are mostly human pilgrims settled in a town that isn't theirs by blood (`factions-and-races/people-of-promise.md`, `places/glasslight-reach.md`). Briarwatch farms, the Kings Road, the Adventurers' Hall, the river trade at Vulture's Nest — human, without anyone saying so.

**They have no homeland in the way the others do.** There is no place humans are from. There are places humans have been long enough that the question stopped being asked.
