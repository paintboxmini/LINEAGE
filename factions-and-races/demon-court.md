# The Demon Court

## The Masks

The oni masks serve two functions: rank signifier and fear instrument. Prisoners see demon faces at every level of authority. 

## Warden Pazuzu

Runs the Wallows labor operation on behalf of the Boar. Manages the debt slave workforce. His authority in the Wallows is absolute.

Visions of grandeur are a flaw in his character. He secretly considers himself the eighth council member.

The Warden's mask is distinct enough that no one mistakes who is in charge.

## Overseers

Heavily armored.

Their authority is performed through presence — the weight of the armor. They command. When an Overseer moves through the work tunnels, everyone moves.

## Guards

The active threat in the labor tunnels. Oni masks, chain whips, the enforcement layer between the Overseers' authority.

They run shifts, maintain order, respond to disturbances. A prisoner who causes problems meets a chain whip guard first and an Overseer after.

## Initiates

Yellow. Bottom rung.

They work the entry checkpoint — the shaft up from the tavern, the first face anyone coming out of the mine sees.

Their masks are the least elaborate.

## The Work Detail

A rotating group of prisoners who mine various minerals.

## The Direwolves

Trackers and pursuit animals.

Used for escape prevention. If something gets out of the labor level and into the deep, Pazuzu sends the direwolves.
