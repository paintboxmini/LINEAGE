# The Thessians

**A dying race, scattered thin across the world.** Not concentrated anywhere. There is no Thessian town, no Thessian quarter, no Thessian claim on any ground — a Thessian is met one at a time, wherever they happen to have ended up.

**Believed, not confirmed, to have originated at the Abyssal Ruins** (`places/abyssal-ruins.md`) — the civilization that held that ground before the strike, already losing its war when the War Engine fired. Nobody connected the two until long after both facts were old. Whether living Thessians know this, believe it, or care is not established.

**Named:** Senshi, cook at the Gilded Tusk, who runs the Naturalist Collection and treats food as the only sacred subject (`places/capital/gilded-tusk.md`).

## Open

- Appearance. Nothing is written down.
- Whether Thessians know where they came from, and what they do with knowing.
- Whether "dying" means a falling birth rate, a scattered population that no longer meets, or something the Ruins did that hasn't finished happening.
