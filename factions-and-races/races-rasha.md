# The Rasha

*Cat-folk. Named by Drew, 2026-10-12, alongside Amelia, first mate of the Arcadia (`characters/amelia.md`), the first Rasha written.*

**Slender, upright and feline.** Short fur over the whole body, ears high on the head, a long tail that counterweights everything they do. Faces closer to a cat's than to a person's, eyes that take up more of the face than a human's do, and a stillness at rest that most people find either restful or unnerving. **Built light and built for balance** — narrow through the waist, long in the leg, and sure-footed on anything that moves under them.

**They see in very little light.** Not in none. **By nightfall the world is pitch black** (`world/eclipseria-overview.md`) — to everyone else. *A Rasha on an open deck under the firmament can still see the rail, the rigging and the shape of the bank, by light nobody else is using.* In a sealed room with no light at all, they are as blind as anyone.

*That is the one thing about them this file sets, and it is set because it is what makes Amelia who she is: the person on the Arcadia who can bring a ship through a night that has stopped everybody else.*

---

## How the World Reads Them

**A Rasha on a ship is unremarkable at the Nest.** Balance and night eyes make good sailors, and the docks have seen enough of them to stop looking. *Away from the water that is not set* — whether a Rasha in a farming town is a familiar face or a stranger is open.

## Passive — CATCH THE LIGHT

**Set by Drew, 2026-10-15.** The night eyes, written the way ADAPTIVE CAMOUFLAGE writes Glasskin skin (`factions-and-races/races-glasskin.md`).

**CATCH THE LIGHT**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Applies When: There is some light, however little — the firmament, a lantern three streets off, a banked coal — and the problem is seeing by it. Nothing in true darkness, and nothing in daylight, where everyone else sees as well.
Range: Both
*"Dark is a word for people who stopped looking."*

**Both range and d4**, the default for a Passive with no range restriction (`rules/character-creation.md`, Passives).
