# Riverdogs — the Otterkin

**Otterkin.** *Riverdogs* and *otterkin* are the same people *(Drew, 2026-10-16)* — two names in use for one folk. *Which one they use for themselves is not set.*

*Baseline from Drew, 2026-10-16. Everything below that came from him is written as set; the two Passives are proposals and say so.*

---

## What They Look Like

**About five feet tall and around 130 pounds**, slender or compact — built to move through water, not to stand their ground on it.

**Dense, waterproof fur** in browns, honey and dark tones, often with white highlights. **Whiskers and a dark nose.** Water runs off them; a Riverdog out of the river is dry in minutes.

**Built for the water:** **webbed hands and feet**, claws, and **a flat tail** that does the work of swimming. **They hold their breath for a long time** — long enough that going under to look at something is an ordinary errand rather than a risk.

**They see in murky water.** Silt, a riverbed, the brown water a bank gives off after rain — *the river is not dark to them the way it is to everyone else.*

## What Is Already Set

*Drew, 2026-09-22 and 2026-09-25.*

- **They swim**, and well enough that deep water is not a barrier to them.
- **They have an arrangement with the Deepmane** (`bestiary/deepmane.md`, The arrangement): scraps and bread over the side, and in exchange an enormous bright-maned animal stands up out of the river over the dangerous water. **Neither side finds this remarkable.**
- **A Driftfire bloom forces them out of the water** (`bestiary/driftfire.md`). It burns on contact and it sits on a stretch for as long as the tide is slow, so the people most at home in deep water are the ones it puts on the bank. **The Deepmanes are down too — it kills them — so the danger marks stop at the same time.** *Their hardiness against poison does nothing here: a bloom has no toxin, only barbs.*

## How They Live

**Riverbank burrows, coastal outposts, and floating raft communities**, living off **fishing, aquaculture and trade.** *A people who farm the water as well as fish it.*

## Who They Are

**Playful, resourceful and community-minded.** **And proud** — **treat one as a cute animal rather than a capable warrior, craftsperson or trader and you will find out how defensive that pride is.** *The fastest way to lose a Riverdog's goodwill is to be charmed by them.*

**They frequently run high in Soul.** *A tendency, not a rule — ancestry never changes a stat here* (`rules/character-creation.md`, Passives, Skills, and Ancestry).

**Common Skills** — what a Riverdog is often trained in, not a list anyone must take: **Swimming, Fishing, Survival, Stealth** (in and around water), **and Trade.**

## Passives *(proposals)*

**Offered, not set.** Drew's baseline names claws, an affinity for the water, and hardiness against poison. Ancestry reaches the sheet through Passives (`factions-and-races/README.md`), so these are those three things in that shape.

**RIVERBORN**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Applies When: The water is part of it — you are in it, under it or crossing it, or what you face is in it with you.
Range: Both
*"Everybody else is visiting."*

**Green because they run high in Soul**, Both and d4, the default (`rules/character-creation.md`, Passives). *It is also the Passive that carries the rest of the baseline*: a Passive grants **Advantage on any check its name plausibly supports** (`rules/resolution.md`, Advantage & Disadvantage), and RIVERBORN plausibly supports holding a breath, seeing through silt, slipping unseen along a bank — **and shrugging off what the river carries, poison included.** *No separate rule needed for any of it.*

**WEBBED CLAWS**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Applies When: Claws are the answer — something in reach that tearing or holding on would matter to. Not against stone, an Object, a swarm, or anything a claw would not catch on.
Range: Melee
*"They look soft. That is the first mistake."*

**Melee-only, so d6** — the same trade STRONGJAW and MIMETIC BLADE make (`campaign/passives.md`).
