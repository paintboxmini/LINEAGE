# The Three Cuts — Mason Doctrine

**This is what the Masons believe. It is not the world's official account of its own origin, because the world does not have one.**

The Cuts are the order's creed and the order's working theory of its own trade, and the two are the same thing — a Mason finds seams where something presses on held reality, cuts a glyph to force a legible shape onto it, and walks the rotation forever because nothing stays held without being paid for. Name, Price, Distance. They did not derive the doctrine from the work or the work from the doctrine; as far as anyone in the order is concerned there was never a distinction.

**Whether any of it is true is not established and is not going to be.** It explains what the order observes. So do other accounts held by other people, and nothing in the world has ever adjudicated between them.

---

Before anything Held, there was the Unheld — pattern without insistence. It could become anything. Therefore it became nothing.

The Unheld is what exists where no one reaches. It is not chaos or malice — it is unmeaning, existence without aboutness, pattern without insistence. It could become anything, and so it became nothing.

Then there was the Knife. Not a tool. Not a blade. An edge.

The Knife injured reality into holding through three wounds:

## The First Cut — NAME

The Knife selected one shape from infinite maybes and forced it into clarity. What was Named became legible. What was not fell away.

## The Second Cut — PRICE

Clarity wounded the Fabric. The Knife made the chosen thing costly to maintain. From then on, nothing real could exist without loss. This was anchoring.

## The Third Cut — DISTANCE

The Named-and-Paid thing strained to swallow everything else. The Knife separated it from what it could consume. This was boundary.

Where all Three Cuts held, reality persisted. Where any Cut weakened, the Unheld pressed close.

## Additions

The Cuts have never stopped bleeding. They cannot — healing would close them, and a closed wound holds nothing open. Everything that exists does so out of an injury that is still being sustained. This is what is meant by a world born dying: not failing, not cursed, but paying continuously for the fact of being anything at all.

The edge has never needed you.

## Using this at the table

**The events the doctrine describes are real. The doctrine is the description.**

Things do get unmade. Places do go thin, and people in them do struggle to say clearly what they saw. Nothing holds without ongoing cost. All of that happens whether or not a Mason is there to name it — and where a file elsewhere in this repo reaches for *Name*, *Price* or *Distance*, it is reaching for the Masons' vocabulary for something that would still be happening if the order had never existed.

**Other people have other words for the same events**, and the repo should not settle which set is correct. An Apnean says *Out There*. The People of Promise have their own account and it is not this one. **Nobody in the world is in a position to check.**
