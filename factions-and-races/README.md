# Factions and Races

Two kinds of thing share this folder because they answer the same question from different directions: *who is this, and what do they want.* A faction is a group people join. A race is a people someone is born into. Both are described the same way here — what they are, how they live, how the world reads them — and neither is a stat package.

**Ancestry reaches the sheet through Passives.** A Passive is innate — what a character *is*, not what they were taught (`rules/character-creation.md`, Passives) — and for most characters the thing they are is their people. STRONGJAW is a dogkin's bite. ADAPTIVE CAMOUFLAGE is Glasskin skin. There is no ancestry table, no stat modifier, and no list of granted abilities: what ancestry does is constrain what a player can justify at creation, and that constraint is the whole mechanic.

**Which means a race entry that has a Passive says so, and one that doesn't, doesn't yet.** Where a race's Passive is written, it's on that race's page. Where it isn't, that's an open design question rather than a statement that the race has none.

**Humans are the case that proves the rule.** A human with a genuine natural ability takes the corresponding Passive like anyone else. A human without one takes extra Skills instead — the trade is in `rules/character-creation.md`, Passives, Skills, and Ancestry.

Beyond the sheet, what a race gives a character is where they're from, who completes them, and what a room assumes about them before they speak.
