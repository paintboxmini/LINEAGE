# The People of Promise

A Glasslight Reach religion, mostly made up of glasskin.

## Philosophy

The Unheld is not a threat. It is infinite potential — the origin of all things and the destination of all things.

The Promise: Everything eventually returns to the Unheld. This is not loss. It is completion.

The sacred and the mundane are not separated. A marketplace is a temple. A meal is a ritual. The distinction between ordinary life and spiritual practice does not exist here.

## Practice

The dead are lowered into the Unheld and released. Congregations lower the deceased down the cliffside into the Unheld Ocean using wooden platforms suspended by ropes and pulleys.

## Where They Actually Are

**Most of the Promise never leaves Glasslight Reach.** The faith is built around a cliff and a rite performed at that cliff; there is nowhere else to be, and the overwhelming majority of the faithful live their whole lives within sight of the water their doctrine is about.

**They are not the Cartographers' Guild, and the two get confused constantly** — including by people who should know better. The populations overlap heavily at Glasslight and the guild's atlases read as half scripture (`factions-and-races/the-cartographers-guild.md`), but the Cartographers are the ones who travel. The Promise are the ones who stay.

**Pneum is the exception, and it is the only one.** A subgroup that left the Reach for a lake island inland and now runs ritual barges down the rivers and back (`places/pneum.md`). Being the congregation that left is the single most important fact about them, and everything strange about their doctrine follows from it.
