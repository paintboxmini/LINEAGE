# The Demon Court

*Warden Pazuzu's enforcement hierarchy in the Wallows. Officially the Boar's underground army. Unofficially, a court.*

---

## The Design

The Regency council wears animal masks. The Demon Court wears demon masks. Pazuzu did not arrive at this by accident.

He built his hierarchy to mirror the council above — ranked insignia, titles earned by demonstrated authority, a clear chain that terminates in a single figure whose mask sets him apart from all others. He considers himself the Boar's eighth councillor. The Boar has not confirmed or denied this. The Regency does not acknowledge the Demon Court exists.

Pazuzu finds this arrangement provisional. He is working on it.

The masks serve two functions: rank signifier and fear instrument. Prisoners see demon faces at every level of authority. The Warden's mask is distinct enough that no one mistakes who is in charge.

---

## Warden Pazuzu

The lead demon. His mask is a clear standout — larger, more elaborate, the apex of the hierarchy made visible.

Runs the Wallows labor operation on behalf of the Boar. Manages the debt slave workforce, maintains the boundary into the Deep, keeps the alternate path sealed. His authority in the Wallows is absolute and he exercises it like a man who believes the surface is watching and approving.

Visions of grandeur are not a flaw in his character. They are his character. He genuinely believes the Demon Court is a legitimate institution. He is not wrong that it functions like one.

He has never gone to the deep. He has told himself this is because the operation runs fine from the labor level. He has not examined this too closely.

---

## Overseers

Heavily armored. Unarmed.

Their authority is performed through presence — the weight of the armor, the stillness of it, the fact that they do not need a weapon to make the point. They command. They do not fight. When an Overseer moves through the work tunnels, workers move.

Their masks are mid-tier demon faces. Distinguished from the chain whip guards by the armor grade and the absence of a weapon. The absence is the statement.

---

## Chain Whip Guards

The active threat in the labor tunnels. Demon masks, chain whips, the enforcement layer between the Overseers' authority and the Initiates' exposure.

They run shifts, maintain order, respond to disturbances. A prisoner who causes problems meets a chain whip guard first and an Overseer after.

---

## Initiates

Yellow. Bottom rung.

They work the entry checkpoint — the shaft up from the tavern, the first face anyone coming out of the mine sees. They know this. There's a joke among the Initiates about being the first line of defense if anything comes up from below.

They fail to mention that the prisoners they're guarding would meet any danger first.

Their masks are the least elaborate. They've earned the insignia but not much else. Most of them are trying to move up. Some of them have started to understand that moving up means knowing more about what's below the labor level. Most of those stop trying to move up.

The Diamond Shelf assignment goes to Initiates. Low ceiling, belly crawl, the back section past the fissure where things come up from below. Nobody in the Court describes what those things are — they don't have to. Every prisoner has heard what happens to someone who gets lost in the deep. The story circulates on its own. The Shelf enforces itself.

---

## The Collection Detail

One work assignment among many, and treated that way. A rotating handful of prisoners tend a cluster of black-crystal fixtures in a side gallery off the main corridor, coiling something that catches the light strangely and handing full spools up to a maintenance crew that collects on a set schedule.

It is filed, guarded, and rotated exactly like every other labor-level detail, because as far as anyone assigned to it can tell, that's exactly what it is. Overseers already know and have decided not to think about it — the same posture the Court takes toward everything below the labor tunnels. Nobody in the chain has ever been told what the spools are. Nobody has asked. See `factions/the-weavers.md`.

---

## The Direwolves

Not ranked. Not masked. Used.

Trackers and pursuit animals. They follow a scent partway into the deep — trained to operate at that depth without losing themselves to it. This makes them dangerous in ways the guards aren't: they don't hesitate at the warping boundary, they don't feel the warmth, they don't slow.

Used for escape prevention. If something gets out of the labor level and into the deep, Pazuzu sends the direwolves.

---

## Relationship to the Boar

The Demon Court is the Boar's apparatus in the Wallows. Pazuzu runs it, staffs it, and answers to Lord Archibald when answers are required. The Boar funds the operation and does not attend it.

The Boar knows what is at the bottom of the mine. Pazuzu knows that the Boar knows. Neither of them discusses it. The labor level is maintained at its current depth by mutual unspoken agreement.
