# Jonah

*Priest of the Rivers, at Vulture's Nest (`places/vultures-nest.md`). Named and set by Drew, 2026-10-07.*

**A priest who wants to give the Red Scarves a home**, and the one person on the dock the party can help do it. **The kids do not trust him**, and they are right not to — *yet* (Why the Kids Won't Come, below).

---

## His Faith

**The river god, as the Temple of the Rivers keeps it** (`places/capital/capital.md`, Temple of the Rivers). Its teaching, said at every gathering: **the rivers are the blood of the world, and the world lives because they move.** *Vulture's Nest sits at the heart of the river web* (`world/geography-overview.md`), *so this is the faith a river port would have, and the Nest is where it is most at home outside the capital.*

**What Jonah takes from it is one thing: water held still goes bad.** A pond with no outlet rots. **So does a child who is held** — bound to a floor, locked in at night, kept. *That is his whole argument against the warehouses, and he makes it from scripture rather than from anger, which annoys the people he makes it to more.*

**His shrine is the water itself.** A step cut down to the river at the quiet end of the dock, a post with the faith's mark on it, and Jonah sitting there in the mornings. *The capital temple has a pool. The Nest has the real thing.*

## Who He Is

**He was a climbing boy** — sent up flues by a master sweep from about seven until he was too big to fit (The sweeps, `places/vultures-nest.md`). **At fourteen he walked out**, the day the law let him, and **a Pepper took him in** — a priest of the Rivers, serving at the Nest, who made him her apprentice and then her son. *He has the sweep's knees and elbows, scarred pale, and he does not explain them.*

**So he is a Pepper, and Kevin's cousin** *(Drew, 2026-10-08)*. The Peppers are a priestly family with somebody in nearly every faith (`characters/ossian.md`), and Jonah is theirs by adoption rather than by birth — *which the family has never once treated as a difference.* **He and Kevin know each other** the way cousins in a family that size do: well enough to be glad, not well enough to be close. *A Pepper who was given a home is trying to give one; the son who would not take the order is the cousin who might help him do it.*

**Patient, plain-spoken, and stubborn.** He does not preach at the kids and he does not chase them. *He knows exactly what being chased by somebody who wants to help feels like.*

## What He Wants

**A house with no lock on the door.** Food, a dry floor, and nobody bound to anything — *the river's teaching, built in timber.* **Kids come and go as they like.** That is the point of it, and it is the one thing no one who ever "took them in" has offered.

**And a way out at fourteen.** Fourteen is when a child can be hired and taught a trade on their own word (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Fourteen). **Jonah means to find every kid who reaches it a real apprenticeship, with a master he has vetted himself** — *the thing the warehouses promise families and never deliver.*

## What He Needs

*This is the party's part, and none of it is written as solved.*

- **A building.** He has a shrine on a step and nothing with a roof
- **Money**, to buy or rent one and to feed what comes through the door
- **Masters who will take an apprentice from him**, which means somebody with standing vouching for him on the dock
- **Room to work without Mercer stopping him** (`characters/mercer.md`). Every kid in Jonah's house is a kid not on Mercer's floors, and Mercer will notice
- **The kids' trust.** The hard one, and the only one money does not help with

## Why the Kids Won't Come

**Because everyone who ever said they wanted to help them sold them.** The man who promised their family an apprenticeship; the kind woman with the hot soup and the cart waiting round the corner. *Most people who claim to want to help end up selling the kids off to a warehouse or a sweep* (Drew, 2026-10-07). **A priest offering a home is the oldest version of that trick there is.**

**So they test him**, and they will go on testing him. *He passes by doing the same thing every day for a long time.* **The way in is Kino** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Red Scarves, Kino and Bean), who decides what the Scarves do — and who does not sleep in the Ribs, and so has his own reasons to think hard about a door with no lock.
