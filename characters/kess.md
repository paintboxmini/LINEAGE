# Kess

*Underground Bazaar. Granddaughter of the Cartographer. See `locations/underground-bazaar.md`.*

---

## Appearance

Geometric tattoo on the forearm — marks laughter from her sister's wedding day. The real memory is gone, sold at some point for reasons that made sense at the time. The tattoo is all that's left. Hollow where humor used to live. Not sad about it. Functional.

She has other tattoos. One of them reacts near Giblets' stall (`characters/giblets.md`). She has not asked why. She knows better than to ask what she sold.

---

## What She Does

Moves through the bazaar with a legitimate-seeming reason to be anywhere, using the Cartographers Guild as a working cover — has a contact named Tomás Kettlewright, Glasslight Reach chapter, who owes her for a job. Her grandmother's intelligence network (the Cartographer trades maps for secrets, never coin — see `locations/underground-bazaar.md`) runs partly through her. She doesn't advertise this.

She is working methodically toward paying off the full balance and getting out. She knows where the mine map is — the one her grandmother made once, for free, and has never made another like since. She has plans that extend past her own exit.

---

## Backstory

Her mother died in the mine below the bazaar. The Warden had imprisoned her on fabricated debt — leverage against the Cartographer's intelligence network. Kess went in to fix it, with the only escort who could keep a secret: an ex-adventurer with faulty memory — Giblets (`characters/giblets.md`), the same "gaps in emotional register, memories that didn't reassemble correctly" his own repeated resurrections already left him with.

Something went wrong in the deep. She traded away most of what happened — a real, specific loss, not a metaphor, same as anything sold into the bazaar's Soul Economy (`locations/underground-bazaar.md`). Mortis (`characters/mortis.md`) pulled them both out. He knows what she lost. He has never mentioned it to her, or to Giblets.

Her mother's echo persists in the Wallows' Echo Cavern (`locations/the-wallows.md`, `quests/the-wallows-descent.md`) — fragments of the rescue attempt survive there: her own voice, Giblets', something that stopped mid-sentence. The GM decides how much surfaces and when; it should say one true thing, not explain everything.
