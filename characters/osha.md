# Osha

Muirn, of Gharial (`places/gharial.md`). A Lizardkin of the southern tribe (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`). **The descendant Caine was sent to find** (`campaign/pat.md`, The Call). *Named, and her shape set, by Drew on 2026-09-30.*

**The one living member of her family who holds the technique.** The rest are kin who hide the line and guard it. She is the one who can actually do it.

---

## What She Knows

**Everything that happened, except the woman it happened through.** The war, the Vessk defeat, the curse, what it cost and who it was aimed at — her family has never hidden any of it from her, and could not have, because a loss that size is not something a people forgets. **What nobody can give her is her ancestor's name or face** (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The gap in the line). The woman was unmade, and the family inherited a skill from somebody it cannot describe.

**She knows the curse can be undone, and what it would cost her.** Another cursegiver giving up her own existence to lift it — which, in her family, means her (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, It can be undone, once, on the same terms).

## Why She Left

**The Vessk will want another one.** The northern tribe is rebuilding an army and the land problem that started the first war was never solved. If they go again and want a curse to go with it, **she is the one they will ask to pay for it.**

**She will not decide anything until she knows why the first one was done.** Undo it, repeat it, or refuse both — every one of those depends on a question her family answers too easily: *did her ancestor choose it, or was she put to it?* The family says she walked in willingly, for the queen's guilt and for ground the Lizardkin could thrive on. **Osha wants to find that out for herself**, and the family has no answer to that except to bring her home.

## What She Is Looking For

**What is left of her ancestor** — the place the woman lived and practised, apart from the tribe. *A thing that can erase a person was never going to be practised in the middle of the village.* Somewhere on the islands near Gharial there is a shack, or a ritual ground, or both, and whatever the unmaking did not take is still there: things she made, things she kept, marks she left. **Nothing there can have her name on it. Everything there is still hers.**

**Which island is not set** — it wants to be a place the party can reach from the Nest, and it wants to wait until Drew knows the route.

## Who Is Looking For Her

**Her family**, to bring her back and keep the line hidden (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The family now). They are the hostile ones, and they are the obstacles the party meets first.

**Caine**, because his dead told him a descendant was alive near Vulture's Nest.

*So the campaign shape is a search with three parties in it:* **Osha alone, looking for her ancestor; her family, looking for Osha; and Caine, looking for both.** The party keeps arriving where she has just been.

## The Scrap

**Caine carries a scrap of her ancestor's cloth, and it still smells of her** (`campaign/pat.md`, The scrap). *It is the only trace of that woman anywhere, and it is the one thing Osha has left home to find.* **A meeting between them is a trade before it is anything else**, and it does not have to become a fight at all.
