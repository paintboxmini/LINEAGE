# Captain Barracuda

*Pirate Captain **Hex Barracuda** — "Captain Barracuda" to everyone. **The Mangy Dog's rival**, and **the man who won his ship** (`characters/mangy-dog.md`). Set by Drew, 2026-11-05. **Barbossa is the inspiration.***

## How he won the ship

**At the Thieves Den, at liar's dice** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Thieves Den), **a month ago.** On the final hand **Barracuda had all five dice left and the Mangy Dog was down to his last one.**

**The Dog bid six sixes.** **Barracuda had five sixes — because he was playing the loaded dice** (`items/the-mangy-dogs-dice.md`). **He called *spot on*** — *the Den's house call: not "liar", but "I've caught you telling the truth"* — betting that the Dog's one die was a six. **One chance in six. It was.**

**The Dog was caught telling the truth, at liar's dice, in a place called the Thieves Den** — and that is how he lost his ship.

**He was playing the loaded dice.** *The Mangy Dog's own set had been swapped out from under him* (`characters/mangy-dog.md`, The ship he lost). **Whether Barracuda swapped them, had them swapped, or simply ended up with them is not set** — and whether the Dog knows is not set either.
