# Captain Barracuda

*Pirate Captain **Hex Barracuda** — "Captain Barracuda" to everyone. **The Mangy Dog's rival**, and **the man who won his ship** (`characters/samuel-gullis.md`). Set by Drew, 2026-11-05. **Barbossa is the inspiration.***

**A Riverdog** *(Drew, 2026-11-05)* — *which is half of why a human cabin boy on his deck got called a mangy dog* (`characters/samuel-gullis.md`, Why "the Mangy Dog").

**What drives him: Samuel.** *(Drew, 2026-11-14)* **The rivalry with the Mangy Dog is everything** — **he'll spit fire at the sight of Samuel at the helm of the reskinned Water Wing.** *The party matters to him because they gave her back to his rival.*

**He does not know about the FourthEye** — *he has never had a lead on it.* **How he could find out without anyone whispering to him:** **he fishes a crate out of the water when the party lightens ship in the chase**; **he boards** at the second meeting; **one of the hired pirates talks** *(the crew asked what they were carrying)*; **or the four pressed Muirn tell him**, if they change sides.

**Wounds come in with him** *(Drew, 2026-11-15)* — **the party's first** (`rules/card-glossary.md`, WOUND). **His crew's jagged rapiers shuffle Wounds into a deck; Barracuda puts them straight into a hand.** *The crew's cut turns up later, on a draw. His is felt now: a card slot gone, or an action spent tearing it out.* **The crew stay unnamed** — *they exist to be fought.*

**Cards:** `cards/barracuda.md` (FLAY) · **his crew:** `bestiary/barracudas-crew.md` (JAGGED RAPIER, `cards/barracudas-crew.md`)

**Trait — The Hooked Blade** *(Drew, 2026-11-15)*: **whenever Barracuda deals attack damage, shuffle 1 Wound into the defender's deck.** **So FLAY lands two Wounds** — *one shuffled in by the blade, one straight into the hand by the card.* **His pirates have no such blade: their one Wound is their one card.**

## How hard he is

*Drew is aiming for more than one Barracuda fight* — **the party could win the first time, and probably won't.**

*Measured 2026-11-15 against Chris, Kevin and Pat, 300 fights a row, fighting to the end, with the Hooked Blade and FLAY running.* **Treat these as harsh on the party**: the simulated party never stops to tear a Wound out of its hand, and has none of Kevin's loads or drinks — *and the real table beat sixteen Muirn hunters easily.*

| With him | Barracuda at HP 30 | at HP 40 |
|---|---|---|
| **Alone** | the party always wins | 99% |
| **+ 1 officer** | 93% | 85% |
| **+ 2 officers** | **about half** | 45% |
| **+ all 3 officers** | **15%** | **12%** |

**The officers are the dial, not his HP.** *Each one changes the fight far more than ten points of health does.* **Full strength — him and all three — is a likely loss**, which is the fight a party meets once and comes back from. **Two officers is the fair fight.** *Every officer the party drops before he arrives, or keeps busy elsewhere, moves it a row up.*

## How he won the ship

**At the Thieves Den, at liar's dice** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Thieves Den), **a month ago.** On the final hand **Barracuda had all five dice left and the Mangy Dog was down to his last one.**

**The Dog bid six sixes.** **Barracuda had five sixes — because he was playing the loaded dice** (`items/the-mangy-dogs-dice.md`). **He called *spot on*** — *the Den's house call: not "liar", but "I've caught you telling the truth"* — betting that the Dog's one die was a six. **One chance in six. It was.**

**The Dog was caught telling the truth, at liar's dice, in a place called the Thieves Den** — and that is how he lost his ship.

**He was playing the loaded dice — the Dog's own set, swapped out during the game by one of Barracuda's men** *(Drew)*.

**The Dog is Samuel Gullis** (`characters/samuel-gullis.md`), **and his first voyage, as a young man, was on Barracuda's deck** — *where he got the name.* **The ship was the Water Wing**, and **Barracuda leaves her to rot at the docks** with Samuel watching.

**How he loses the dice** *(Drew's plan)*: **through a Red Scarf thread.** *Not set beyond that.*

**Session three** *(2026-11-07)*: **told that Samuel had a new ship and was at sea again, he finished preparing in a rush and left the Nest almost at once** — *presumably after his rival.* **While he was gone, the Water Wing was stolen** — **by a man wearing his face**, who told the dock he was trolling for the Mangy Dog (`campaign/session-3-log.md`).

**He would know the Water Wing under any paint** *(Drew, 2026-11-07)* — *same hull, same cabin, same hold, same mast; years of feuding.*

**He is back in the Nest** *(Drew, 2026-11-11)*. **He got word while chasing a rival who was never at sea, and came home during the party's four days at Pneum.** **His intuition: they lied to him so they could steal the ship.** **The first thing he does is send a group of his men to bring the party in front of him. He wants answers.** *He was a sea encounter in waiting; he is a summons now.*

**And a pursuer** *(Drew, 2026-11-14)*: **he lost days on a wild goose chase and came home to find the ship really gone — and he wants to flay the party in front of his crew.** **He chases Fortune's Fancy on her way to the capital.** *His men try to grab Flint in the Nest while Code and Caine are at the warehouses* (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, Flint's week).

**What he could do with the FourthEye runs, if he learns of them** *(Drew, 2026-11-11)*: **use them to try to overthrow Harlock** at the head of the Guild, **or take the cargo off Fortune's Fancy.**
