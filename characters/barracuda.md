# Captain Barracuda

*Pirate Captain **Hex Barracuda** — "Captain Barracuda" to everyone. **The Mangy Dog's rival**, and **the man who won his ship** (`characters/samuel-gullis.md`). Set by Drew, 2026-11-05. **Barbossa is the inspiration.***

**A Riverdog** *(Drew, 2026-11-05)* — *which is half of why a human cabin boy on his deck got called a mangy dog* (`characters/samuel-gullis.md`, Why "the Mangy Dog").

## How he won the ship

**At the Thieves Den, at liar's dice** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Thieves Den), **a month ago.** On the final hand **Barracuda had all five dice left and the Mangy Dog was down to his last one.**

**The Dog bid six sixes.** **Barracuda had five sixes — because he was playing the loaded dice** (`items/the-mangy-dogs-dice.md`). **He called *spot on*** — *the Den's house call: not "liar", but "I've caught you telling the truth"* — betting that the Dog's one die was a six. **One chance in six. It was.**

**The Dog was caught telling the truth, at liar's dice, in a place called the Thieves Den** — and that is how he lost his ship.

**He was playing the loaded dice — the Dog's own set, swapped out during the game by one of Barracuda's men** *(Drew)*.

**The Dog is Samuel Gullis** (`characters/samuel-gullis.md`), **and his first voyage, as a young man, was on Barracuda's deck** — *where he got the name.* **The ship was the Water Wing**, and **Barracuda leaves her to rot at the docks** with Samuel watching.

**How he loses the dice** *(Drew's plan)*: **through a Red Scarf thread.** *Not set beyond that.*

**Session three** *(2026-11-07)*: **told that Samuel had a new ship and was at sea again, he finished preparing in a rush and left the Nest almost at once** — *presumably after his rival.* **While he was gone, the Water Wing was stolen** — **by a man wearing his face**, who told the dock he was trolling for the Mangy Dog (`campaign/session-3-log.md`).

**He would know the Water Wing under any paint** *(Drew, 2026-11-07)* — *same hull, same cabin, same hold, same mast; years of feuding.*

**He is back in the Nest** *(Drew, 2026-11-11)*. **He got word while chasing a rival who was never at sea, and came home during the party's four days at Pneum.** **His intuition: they lied to him so they could steal the ship.** **The first thing he does is send a group of his men to bring the party in front of him. He wants answers.** *He was a sea encounter in waiting; he is a summons now.*

**What he could do with the FourthEye runs, if he learns of them** *(Drew, 2026-11-11)*: **use them to try to overthrow Harlock** at the head of the Guild, **or take the cargo off Fortune's Fancy.**
