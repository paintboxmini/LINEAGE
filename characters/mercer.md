# Mercer

*A warehouse owner at Vulture's Nest (`places/vultures-nest.md`). Created 2026-10-07: Drew asked for a ruthless businessman.*

**He owns the worst floors on the dock, and he fills them with children.** **He is a Bellows man** *(Drew, 2026-10-08)* — the strongest of the five families behind the Red Scarves, the masters of the docks (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Many Mask's legacy). Polite, well-dressed, never raises his voice, and **counts kids the way he counts crates.** *He would tell you he is the only man in the Nest who feeds them.*

---

## What He Does

**He takes kids in.** Children with nobody, children whose families cannot feed them, children the dock has no use for. **He binds them to work until they are fourteen** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Fourteen), feeds them, and locks them in at night. *It is legal, and it is called charity, and both of those are true in the Nest.*

**Then he places them.** Some stay on his own warehouse floors. **The rest he hires out** — to the master sweeps (The sweeps), the smokehouses, the ropewalks — **and takes a fee for every one.** *He is a broker in children, and the word he uses is "placement."*

**His agents work the families**, upriver and down: *the boy will be fed, and taught a trade, and have a future.* **The paper they sign says none of that**, and most of them sign it with a mark because they cannot read it (The Ashbys, `places/vultures-nest.md`).

**At fourteen the binding ends** and the kid walks out with no trade, nothing saved, and a back that already hurts — **and Mercer hires them back as dockhands at the bottom wage.** *He has them coming and going.*

## Why He Matters This Week

**The impound filled his floors.** The Compact's sweep put a whole stretch of berth into warehouses at once (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Quarantined Barge), **and his are among them** — rent from the Compact, and more cargo than the floors were built for, stacked fast by kids under orders. *This lockdown is the best month he has ever had.*

## The Scarves

*There is a bitter shape to this.* **The Bellows are a family of the Red Scarves**, sworn once to a Creed of freedom for the unwanted, **and Mercer binds the unwanted to his floors.** *The kids on the docks wear the scarf of the outfit his family runs.*

**Every Red Scarf who ran from a binding is, by the paper, still his.** He keeps **a standing reward for returned runaways**, and every dockhand knows it. *A party that catches Bean and hands him over can collect* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, What they ran from).

**And Hojan is a problem** (`characters/hojan.md`). A house with no lock is a house that empties his floors, and a priest finding kids real apprenticeships at fourteen is a priest telling every family on the river what Mercer's paper actually says.

## At the Table

**Not a fighter.** He has men for that — unnamed, and they stay unnamed until a player makes one matter. **He fights with paper, the Compact's ear, and the reward.** *He is never doing anything illegal, and that is what makes him hard.*

**Ruthless, not cruel.** He does not enjoy any of it. **A crushed child is a line in his ledger and a cost against the month**, and he will tell you, reasonably, what it would cost the Nest if he stopped. *The party should come away wanting to hit him and unable to point to the law he broke.*

## On his guard *(Drew, 2026-11-14)*

**Session four caught him complacent.** *He relied on his position for safety — a Bellows man on his own dock, in a town where everybody knows who he is* (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, Mercer's warehouses). **That is over.** *A shapeshifter walked into his office in a dead guard's face, drove a blade through his book, and took it.*

**When the party next returns to the Nest, he is ready for them.** *What that looks like is open — more men, a locked office, the Compact's ear, the Bellows told what Code is — but the easy version of his warehouse does not exist any more.*
