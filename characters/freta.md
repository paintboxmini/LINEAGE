# Freta

*Muirn, of Gharial, a long time ago. **Osha's great-grandmother** (`characters/osha.md`). A hag. Named and set by Drew, 2026-10-05.*

**She is a holder of the cursegiving technique, and alive.** Every holder's first-born daughter is born holding it, and her mother keeps it (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Cursegivers) — so Freta has held it since the day she was born, and she held it alongside her own daughter, and her daughter's daughter, and Osha. *It was this rule that changed to make her possible. Before 2026-10-05 there was one holder alive at a time.*

---

## What She Is

**A hag, like every holder** *(Drew, 2026-10-05)*. **She feeds on suffering, and that is what her magic runs on** (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, What they are).

**That does not make her heartless or soulless.** It is what she is. *Play her as a person with an appetite, not as an appetite wearing a person.* She can be fond of someone, keep a promise, grieve. She also eats what she eats.

## The Hut

**Forget-Me-Not hut is hers** *(Drew, 2026-10-05; `places/forget-me-not-island.md`, The Hut)*. **It was her ancestor's first** — the cursegiver, who cast the curse from it and was unmade there. **Freta found it, lived in it, and never told her family.** *They have spent generations unable to find the one place that would answer their question, and one of their own was living in it.*

**Her scent is what is in it now.** If Caine tries to smell out the ancestor there, the scrap finds Freta instead, and to him she reads as the woman he is looking for (`places/forget-me-not-island.md`, The scent). *So if he ever stands near her, he knows her.*

## Elowen

**She is on Elowen, killing people in their dreams** *(Drew, 2026-10-05; `places/elowen.md`)*. Nightmares, night after night, and **if they die in the dream, they die.** *A month ago the town was about sixty people. When the party arrives it is twenty to thirty.*

**She left Forget-Me-Not hut about a month ago, and the dying started when she arrived** *(Drew, 2026-10-05)*. *Why Elowen, and why now, is not set.*

## What She Could Do

**She could lift the curse.** Any living holder can, by paying her own existence (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, It can be undone, once, on the same terms). *Nobody in the family is counting on her, and nobody hunting Osha has put her in the sum* (`characters/vaun.md`).

**She may know the answer Osha left home for.** The spot inside the hut answers to the line, and Freta has lived beside it. *Whether she has stood on it, and what it gave her, is not set* (`places/forget-me-not-island.md`, What It Is — GM Only).

## Not Yet Set

- Appearance, voice, age — *old enough to be a great-grandmother and still strong enough to empty half a town*
- **Why Elowen, and why now**
- **Whether her family knows she is alive**, and what they think happened to her
- Whether she has stood on the spot in the hut, and what it told her
- **What she thinks of the curse**, and of a great-granddaughter out looking for its answer
- **How a dream-killing is fought** — whether the party can enter the dream, wake the sleeper, or only reach Freta while she is awake. *This is the mechanical question the island turns on*
- Stats and deck
