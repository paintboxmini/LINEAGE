# Corvel

*Runs an unmarked stall at Vulture's Nest. See `locations/vultures-nest.md`.*

---

## Appearance

Leather-skinned man. No name on the stall, no pitch. Sells necklaces strung with small vials of grey water.

---

## Backstory

Fifteen years ago he took a ship past the coast — the kind of thing that's only supposed to exist in ghost stories, because the ones who try don't come back to tell them. He came back. Alone. Doesn't talk about it. The vials are the closest thing he has to a reason for still being here.

---

## What He Actually Sells

The water itself is ordinary by the time it reaches the Nest — unheldness doesn't cross the coastline, ever (`world/the-unheld.md`). What isn't ordinary is what the vials pick up on the way: they're filled aboard Pneum's own ritual barges (`locations/pneum.md`), by hands belonging to a community that collects diseases as worship. Contact causes random illness.

Corvel doesn't disclose this, and doesn't privately distinguish it from the water itself. From his own perspective, the illness *is* the communion, arriving the way it always does: through the body. He genuinely doesn't understand the problem with this framing.
