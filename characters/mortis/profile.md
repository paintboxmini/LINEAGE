# Mortis — Profile

A precise necromancer. Comfortable with death magic in a way most people never get to be, because most people never had a reason to get that good at it.

He doesn't take jobs. He tends the Underground Tavern instead.

He pours Lily a drink without being asked every time she comes in. He does the same for Kess. Professional courtesy between people who have all spent too long in the space between.
