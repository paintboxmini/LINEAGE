# Lirren — GM Notes

---

## Play

Lirren should feel like someone who has found an answer recently and is still carrying the relief of it.

Their personal crisis does not need to be explained unless the table asks. What matters in play is that the ordinary bath did not resolve it, while the Final Current gave Lirren a way to understand what remained unresolved.

When encouraging Steve or another hesitant person, Lirren should not sound like a polished preacher. They are earnest. They can speak too quickly, lean too hard on Kaine's language about unlocking potential, or sound like they are trying to give someone else the answer that finally helped them.

On the platform night, Lirren is there to pull people forward, not to threaten them. If someone hesitates, Lirren believes the hesitation is the last barrier between that person and what they could become.
