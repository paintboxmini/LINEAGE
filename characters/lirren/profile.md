# Lirren — Profile

---

Lirren has only made a few descents. The change is still fluid on them, more suggestion than fixed mark. They have not yet taken on the hardened appearance of those who have gone down many times.

Their loyalty to the Final Current came from a personal crisis that the ordinary single bath did not resolve. Whatever the Promise gave them, it was not enough. The Current offered another possibility: that what had not been unlocked could still be reached.

Lirren believes that possibility fiercely. They echo Kaine's language about unlocking potential, often with the urgency of someone who has only recently discovered words that seem to explain what happened to them.

On the night of the group platform descent, Lirren is present with Kaine, Vesper, and Rook. When someone hesitates, Lirren is one of the people most likely to speak. They want Steve and the others to keep moving. Their encouragement is earnest rather than practiced, and their certainty has not yet been worn smooth by years of repetition.

Lirren is not hardened. They are recently converted, convinced, and still close enough to the fear and uncertainty they are asking other people to cross that their belief can feel personal.
