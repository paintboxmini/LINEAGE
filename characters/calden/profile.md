# Calden — Profile

---

Calden is a Glasslight man in the most ordinary sense: quiet, cliff-rooted, and accustomed to measuring everything twice before trusting it once.

His work and attention belong to the practical life of the Reach. Stone, water, terraces, maintenance, hand-lines, and the small repairs that keep a fragile place livable matter more to him than doctrine. The cliff-song is part of the environment he lives inside, not something he needs to interpret.

He is Sera's husband and Nyra's father. Mirel is his stepdaughter. He is not hostile to her. He simply does not understand the Mason path, or the idea of turning away from ordinary Reach life, as a natural way for someone raised here to live. To him, a life in Glasslight is built through the work of staying: tending what is yours, maintaining what can be maintained, and knowing which surfaces are safe to trust.

That difference does not need to become a household fracture. Calden can care for Mirel while failing to understand the direction she is considering. His resistance is quiet and solid rather than dramatic.

Keep him available if the table pulls him forward, but do not force him into the main Tide Pulls Back sequence. He belongs in the background of the Reach, where the infrastructure and the people maintaining it continue whether the central conflict notices them or not.
