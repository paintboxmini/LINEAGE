# Calden — GM Notes

---

## Play

Calden should feel solid without becoming imposing. He measures before he answers. If something can be checked with a hand, a rope, a stone, or the state of a terrace, he is more comfortable checking it than arguing about it.

He cares about Mirel as family. The Mason path is simply outside his understanding of what a life in Glasslight should be. Do not turn that discomfort into hostility unless the table creates a reason for it.

He is not a spokesman for Promise doctrine or the Final Current. His connection to the Reach is practical and local.

Keep him available as household texture or as a grounded voice if the party asks about Mirel's family. He does not need to enter the main Tide Pulls Back sequence on his own.
