# Sael — Profile

---

Runs the ropes for the Promise congregation's descents — holds the line while a bather goes down into the Unheld, brings them back up, has done this longer than most of the current congregation has been alive. Knows exactly how a rope should feel in the hand when everything's fine, and exactly how it feels wrong before there's any other sign of trouble.

Has pulled too many people back changed in ways that didn't settle right. Has decided, somewhere along the way, where the fault actually sits: not the water, not the Promise, not the descent itself — the people who show up from elsewhere, carrying questions the water was never built to answer for them. Locals bathe knowing what they're asking for, more or less. Outsiders come with a curiosity that reads, to Sael, like carelessness wearing a nicer coat.

Polite about it. Efficient about it. Never raises the subject unprompted — Glasslight doesn't reward that kind of volume — but if a party wants to descend and Sael is the one holding the rope that day, they will be handled correctly and without warmth, and if the timing can be arranged so someone else is holding the line instead, it will be.

Sael handles the ropes for the ordinary Promise congregation platforms. **Sael is not present for Kaine's group platform descent in the Tide Pulls Back sequence; that night Sael remains with the regular congregation.**
