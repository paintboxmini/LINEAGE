# Sael — GM Notes

*What Sael has never said out loud.*

---

**What Sael has seen that the party hasn't:** the exact moment, more than once, when someone came up from the water fluid instead of hardened, and the congregation's warmth toward them didn't survive contact with what they'd actually become. Sael has never said this out loud. It's most of the reason for the rope-work being the only part of the job Sael still fully trusts.

**What was declared and never collected.** Years ago, after one specific descent went badly, Sael said something like a vow — never again, to a version of a promise nobody was there to formally witness. The shape of it matches what a binding statement is supposed to do (`rules/character-creation.md`, Declaring a Price) closely enough that anyone who knows the ritual would recognize it. Nothing has come to collect on it. Sael has decided this means it didn't count. It might just mean the world hasn't gotten around to it yet.
