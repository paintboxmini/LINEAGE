# Hojan

*Priest of the Rivers, at Vulture's Nest (`places/vultures-nest.md`). Set by Drew, 2026-10-07; **named Hojan 2026-10-18** (was Jonah).*

**A priest who wants to give the Red Scarves a home**, and the one person on the dock the party can help do it. **The kids do not trust him**, and they are right not to — *yet* (Why the Kids Won't Come, below).

---

## His Faith

**The river god, as the Temple of the Rivers keeps it** (`places/capital/capital.md`, Temple of the Rivers). Its teaching, said at every gathering: **the rivers are the blood of the world, and the world lives because they move.** *Vulture's Nest sits at the heart of the river web* (`world/geography-overview.md`), *so this is the faith a river port would have, and the Nest is where it is most at home outside the capital.*

**What Hojan takes from it is one thing: water held still goes bad.** A pond with no outlet rots. **So does a child who is held** — bound to a floor, locked in at night, kept. *That is his whole argument against the warehouses, and he makes it from scripture rather than from anger, which annoys the people he makes it to more.*

**His shrine is the water itself.** A step cut down to the river at the quiet end of the dock, a post with the faith's mark on it, and Hojan sitting there in the mornings. *The capital temple has a pool. The Nest has the real thing.*

## Who He Is

**He was a climbing boy** — sent up flues by a master sweep from about seven until he was too big to fit (The sweeps, `places/vultures-nest.md`). **At fourteen he walked out**, the day the law let him, and **a Pepper took him in** — a priest of the Rivers, serving at the Nest, who made him her apprentice and then her son. *He has the sweep's knees and elbows, scarred pale, and he does not explain them.*

**So he is a Pepper, and Kevin's cousin** *(Drew, 2026-10-08)*. The Peppers are a priestly family with somebody in nearly every faith (`characters/ossian.md`), and Hojan is theirs by adoption rather than by birth — *which the family has never once treated as a difference.* **He and Kevin know each other** the way cousins in a family that size do: well enough to be glad, not well enough to be close. *A Pepper who was given a home is trying to give one; the son who would not take the order is the cousin who might help him do it.*

**Patient, plain-spoken, and stubborn.** He does not preach at the kids and he does not chase them. *He knows exactly what being chased by somebody who wants to help feels like.*

## What He Wants

**A house with no lock on the door.** Food, a dry floor, and nobody bound to anything — *the river's teaching, built in timber.* **Kids come and go as they like.** That is the point of it, and it is the one thing no one who ever "took them in" has offered.

**And a way out at fourteen.** Fourteen is when a child can be hired and taught a trade on their own word (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Fourteen). **Hojan means to find every kid who reaches it a real apprenticeship, with a master he has vetted himself** — *the thing the warehouses promise families and never deliver.*

## What He Needs

*This is the party's part, and none of it is written as solved.*

**Since session one:** *Marta is sending him food for the Red Scarves, paid for by Caine and Code* (`campaign/session-1-log.md`). **It is the first thing he has had to offer them besides a step by the water.** *He has not met the party.*

- **A building.** He has a shrine on a step and nothing with a roof
- **Money**, to buy or rent one and to feed what comes through the door
- **Masters who will take an apprentice from him**, which means somebody with standing vouching for him on the dock
- **Room to work without Mercer stopping him** (`characters/mercer.md`). Every kid in Hojan's house is a kid not on Mercer's floors, and Mercer will notice
- **The kids' trust.** The hard one, and the only one money does not help with

## The Ashbys — Hojan's ask

*Drew, 2026-10-18.* **Hojan seeks the party out.** **Dell and Maren Ashby** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, People looking in) **are going to Mercer's warehouse to buy back their son's work contract**, and **Hojan does not trust Mercer not to take advantage of them.** *He needs an escort — people Mercer has to be careful in front of.* **Hojan cannot be the escort himself**: Mercer knows exactly what Hojan is for (`characters/mercer.md`).

**What nobody in that room says out loud.** **Their son is Tib, and Tib is not on Mercer's floor** — he ran a year ago and is a Red Scarf. **Mercer will sell them the paper anyway.** *A contract for a runaway is worth more sold to his parents than it will ever be worth to Mercer.*

**And buying it still matters.** **The paper is what makes Tib a runaway with a reward on him.** *Bought back, the reward is gone and he is free in law — whether or not he ever forgives the people who paid for it.* **He thinks they sold him** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, People looking in).

**What Mercer might try** — *pick one, or let the escort's presence decide:*

- **Say nothing about the boy running**, take the full price, and hand over a paper for a child he no longer has
- **Raise the price at the counter** — *the boy has been fed two winters, and that is on the account*
- **Tell them the boy has been placed elsewhere** — a sweep, a smokehouse — and sell them the paper with a placement fee on top
- **Be scrupulously fair**, because there are armed strangers in the room. *Which is what Hojan wanted, and is its own kind of answer*

**Why it splits the party well.** On the same night Code is with the Red Scarves (`campaign/session-2-scarves.md`), **and Tib is one of them.** *One half of the table is buying a boy's contract from the man who holds it; the other half is standing next to the boy.*

## Why the Kids Won't Come

**Because everyone who ever said they wanted to help them sold them.** The man who promised their family an apprenticeship; the kind woman with the hot soup and the cart waiting round the corner. *Most people who claim to want to help end up selling the kids off to a warehouse or a sweep* (Drew, 2026-10-07). **A priest offering a home is the oldest version of that trick there is.**

**So they test him**, and they will go on testing him. *He passes by doing the same thing every day for a long time.* **The way in is Kino** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Red Scarves, Kino and Bean), who decides what the Scarves do — and who does not sleep in the Ribs, and so has his own reasons to think hard about a door with no lock.
