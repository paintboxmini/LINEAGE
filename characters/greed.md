# Greed

*Katana and current. He strikes twice before you finish flinching from the first.*

*Stat block and deck are a starting point. Scale to match where he enters the story — early campaign he's a hot-headed rival or a reckless ally, late campaign he's someone who's finally learned to time the storm instead of just throwing it.

**Mind 4 / Body 5 / Soul 3 — HP 21**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 12

---

## Appearance

Motion first, features second — you register the afterimage before you place the face. Dark hair with a static-frizzed fringe that never quite lies flat. The katana's fuller runs faintly bright, like the blade is holding a charge between draws. When he moves fast enough, the air behind him cracks a half-second late.

---

## Behavior

Impatient in one very specific way: He hates a fight that hasn't started yet — small talk before initiative rolls makes him more restless than the danger does.

What discipline he has, he earned from Master Gotetsu, in a mountain monastery, and hasn't found anywhere since that asked as much of him.

---

## Combat Identity

Fast, aggressive, built around one real combo: land VOLT - he moves, and whoever's in the way takes the chip damage on the way past. Then follow with THUNDERCLAP and it stops being one hit — it's the whole enemy Frontline eating the same bolt. THUNDERBOLT STRIKE punishes anyone who lets him sit still for a turn.
