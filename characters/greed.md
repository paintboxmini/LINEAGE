# Greed

*Katana and current. He strikes twice before you finish flinching from the first.*

**Cards:** `cards/greed.md`

*Stat block and deck are a starting point. Scale to match where he enters the story — early campaign he's a hot-headed rival or a reckless ally, late campaign he's someone who's finally learned to time the storm instead of just throwing it. Adjust Mind, HP, and card count before use.*

**Mind 4 / Body 5 / Soul 3 — HP 19**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 12

---

## Appearance

Motion first, features second — you register the afterimage before you place the face. Dark hair with a static-frizzed fringe that never quite lies flat. The katana's fuller runs faintly bright, like the blade is holding a charge between draws. When he moves fast enough, the air behind him cracks a half-second late.

---

## Behavior

Impatient in one very specific way: not reckless with allies, reckless with silence. He hates a fight that hasn't started yet — small talk before initiative rolls makes him more restless than the danger does.

What discipline he has, he earned from one teacher, in one place, and hasn't found anywhere since that asked as much of him. It shows up sideways: he never draws before the reveal, never wastes the first exchange proving a point he could prove better on the second. The waiting is its own kind of control — the only kind he's fully mastered, next to a temper he hasn't.

---

## Combat Identity

Fast, aggressive, built around one real combo: land LIGHTNING DASH, then follow with THUNDERCLAP and it stops being one hit — it's the whole enemy Frontline eating the same bolt. THUNDERBOLT STRIKE punishes anyone who lets him sit still for a turn; the same discipline that makes him hold back once is exactly what makes the payoff land harder.

Doesn't stay in one position long enough to be predictable. Reads a repeated color and punishes it as readily as he closes distance.

---

## Deck

*Red — Body*

*Green — Soul*

- WITNESS — Next ally to successfully defend heals 3; Defensive: target ally heals 3
- FLOW — You may change position; Defensive: you may change position
