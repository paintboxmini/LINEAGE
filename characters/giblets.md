# Giblets

*FourthEye stall, Underground Bazaar. See `locations/capital/underground-bazaar.md`.*

---

A ghoul. Has been brought back from death more times than is good for anyone. Each resurrection lost something — fragments of personality, gaps in emotional register, memories that didn't reassemble correctly. He is not tragic about this. He is unhinged about it, which is different.

He and Mortis used to work together. Mortis brought him back more times than Giblets can fully account for — sometimes because he needed him, sometimes because he could. Giblets has been brought back enough times to know the difference. They don't work together anymore.

---

## The Plan

Giblets has one. It connects to someone currently operating out of Vulture's Nest — someone he used to work with. He does not discuss it.

The supply chain keeping his stall stocked bypasses the Tideward Compact's ledger entirely and doesn't appear in Quartermaster Voss's intake at Eclipseria South Gate. The Regency noticed the downstream end of this chain first. They are working backward.

Three Regency hard lines violated: too addictive, too destructive, council gets no cut.
