# Giblets

---

A ghoul. Has been brought back from death more times than is good for anyone — every one of them on purpose, throwing himself into whatever was about to kill Mortis instead. Each resurrection lost something — fragments of personality, gaps in emotional register, memories that didn't reassemble correctly. He is not tragic about this. He is unhinged about it, which is different. He'd do it again tomorrow and has said so.

He and Mortis were partners for years, back when the work was adventuring instead of a stall and a bar three streets apart. Mortis never once left him dead when he could help it. They retired together and never really stopped being partners — just changed what they're partners in.

---

## The Plan

 It connects to someone currently operating out of Vulture's Nest — someone he used to work with. He does not discuss it.

The supply chain keeping his stall stocked bypasses the ledger entirely and doesn't appear in Quartermaster Voss's intake at Eclipseria South Gate. The Regency noticed the downstream end of this chain first. They are working backward.
