# Ryder — Profile

## Appearance

Ash never quite finishes settling on him — a fine grey dust in his hair, along his collar, that should have brushed off days ago and hasn't. His skin carries old burn scarring in places that should have killed the nerve, and doesn't; he still flinches from cold, never from flame.

When he casts, the light comes from underneath his skin before it comes from his hands — visible for a half-second through the back of a closed fist, like something bright is being held down rather than summoned up.

---
## Behavior

He takes risks other people flinch from, and it isn't bravado — he knows something the rest of the party doesn't get to know for certain: that this specific kind of dying isn't the last word for him, not this time. That knowledge doesn't make him careless with other people. It makes him careless with himself, which is a different thing, and the party usually figures out the difference the first time they watch him walk into a hit he could have avoided.

Underneath it there's a tiredness that doesn't show up in how he fights. Coming back costs something that isn't HP. He doesn't talk about what.
