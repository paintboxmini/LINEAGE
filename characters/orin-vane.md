# Orin Vane

Every circle he draws is an answer to a question you haven't asked yet.

This is Kino grown — the young man he becomes after he leaves Vulture's Nest, not the hungry fifteen-year-old running the Red Scarves on the docks.

## Appearance

Older than the docks remember him. Dress cleaner, blade real instead of borrowed. The circles he draws hang in the air a half-second after his hand moves, thin lines of light in whatever color he's called on, gone the instant they've done their work. He still ties a scarf the same proper way. Red, same as always.

Slim build. Wears the same grey-brown traveling coat regardless of weather. 

## Personality

Cautious. Careful. Slow to trust.

Does not discuss clients, past or present. Has opinions about weather. Does not have opinions about politics.

Eyes track positions, not faces. In conversation they go to your hands, your feet, the angle of your shoulders. This does not feel hostile. It feels professional.

Moves with the constant small adjustments of someone calculating distance at all times. The sword is a narrow foil. Left hand stays slightly ahead of the right at all times. That's the drawing hand.

Doesn't rush an opening — waits for the shape of one to become obvious, then takes it without hesitating twice. Tells you exactly what a circle will stop, in the color he draws it, and lets you attack it anyway if you want to find out for yourself. That confidence isn't new. Vulture's Nest just called it something else back when the stakes were bags of coin instead of blades.

Operates independently. Available for hire. Particular about clients in ways Orin does not fully explain. Has a reputation for always finishing the job.

## Combat Identity

A fencer who wins the fight before it reaches him. Red, Blue, or Green cards played against the matching active Sigil simply fails and each one also grants him Resist, Ward, or Evade once it's triggered.

Mage-sword fencer. Available for hire, particular about clients.
