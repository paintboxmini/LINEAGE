# Kess — Backstory

Her mother died in the mine below the bazaar. The Warden had imprisoned her on fabricated debt — leverage against the Cartographer's intelligence network. Kess went in to fix it, with the only escort who could keep a secret: an ex-adventurer with faulty memory — Giblets (`characters/giblets/README.md`), the same "gaps in emotional register, memories that didn't reassemble correctly" his own repeated resurrections already left him with.

Something went wrong in the deep. She traded away most of what happened — a real, specific loss, not a metaphor, same as anything sold into the bazaar's Soul Economy (`places/capital/underground-bazaar.md`). Mortis (`characters/mortis/README.md`) pulled them both out. He knows what she lost. He has never mentioned it to her, or to Giblets.

Her mother's echo persists in the Wallows' Echo Cavern (`places/capital/the-wallows.md`, `quests/the-wallows-descent.md`) — fragments of the rescue attempt survive there: her own voice, Giblets', something that stopped mid-sentence. The GM decides how much surfaces and when; it should say one true thing, not explain everything.
