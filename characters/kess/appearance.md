# Kess — Appearance

Geometric tattoo on the forearm — marks laughter from her sister's wedding day. The real memory is gone, sold at some point for reasons that made sense at the time, the way anything gets sold in the bazaar's Soul Economy when the reason is big enough. The tattoo is what she kept instead — not the memory, proof she had one worth marking.

She has other tattoos. One of them reacts near Giblets' stall (`characters/giblets/README.md`). She has not asked why. She knows better than to ask what she sold.
