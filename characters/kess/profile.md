# Kess — Profile

## Appearance

Geometric tattoo on the forearm — marks laughter from her sister's wedding day. The real memory is gone, sold at some point for reasons that made sense at the time, the way anything gets sold in the bazaar's Soul Economy when the reason is big enough. The tattoo is what she kept instead — not the memory, proof she had one worth marking.

She has other tattoos. One of them reacts near Giblets' stall (`characters/giblets/README.md`). She has not asked why. She knows better than to ask what she sold.

---
## What She Does

Moves through the bazaar with a legitimate-seeming reason to be anywhere, using the Cartographers Guild as a working cover — has a contact named Tomás Kettlewright, Glasslight Reach chapter, who owes her for a job. Her grandmother's intelligence network (the Cartographer trades maps for secrets, never coin — see `places/capital/underground-bazaar.md`) runs partly through her. She doesn't advertise this.

She is working methodically toward paying off the full balance and getting out. She knows where the mine map is — the one her grandmother made once, for free, and has never made another like since. She has plans that extend past her own exit.
