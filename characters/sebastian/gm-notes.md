# Sebastian — GM Notes

---

## Mirel

Sebastian learned Mirel existed only recently, after returning to Glasslight. Sera found him after hearing he was back in the Reach and told him that their daughter had been born and was grown.

He left Glasslight before Mirel was born to continue Mason duties. He had never met her before the seams encounter.

The knowledge changes the weight of that encounter without changing its surface. From Mirel's side, Sebastian is a stranger Mason who noticed that she had found the seams, spoke more than he meant to, and gave her a chisel with almost no explanation. He does not tell her that he is her father.

For Sebastian, the chisel is one of his own six, Time-aligned, and the first real instrument he has placed in the hands of the possible successor standing in front of him. He is aware that Mirel is his daughter and that, as the last active member of his line, she represents an unacknowledged possible continuation of it. He does not suddenly become warm or confessional because of this. The choice to give her the tool should still feel measured, restrained, and difficult to read.

Do not let the player-facing scene reveal the relationship. Sebastian can hesitate, watch, or choose his words carefully, but he should not explain why.

## The Line

Sebastian remains the last active Mason in his line. Mirel's existence creates a possible successor he did not know he had, but whether she ever becomes part of the line remains open.

The possibility should not turn the Mason work into a family legacy speech. Sebastian's isolation and caution remain intact.
