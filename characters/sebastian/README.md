# Sebastian

*The Mason. Last of his line, not the first of a new one. Keeper of the Lodge at the Soft Edge.*

---

Sole active member of the Masons (`factions/the-masons.md`) — the order narrowed to a single line of successors, master to apprentice, one at a time, somewhere in a history tied to whatever happened to the kingdom that stood on this ground before this one. Sebastian is where that line currently stands. What caused the narrowing isn't settled, and isn't something even the order's own surviving record fully explains anymore.

He works out of a lodge at the Soft Edge (`places/glasslight-reach.md`) — one threshold among the many the order tends, walked, measured, and repaired the way a fence line gets checked after a storm.
