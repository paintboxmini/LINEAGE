# Sera — GM Notes

---

## Family

Sera is Mirel's mother and Nyra's mother. She is married to Calden, Nyra's father and a full-blooded Glasskin.

Mirel's father is Sebastian, the human Mason. Sera found him after hearing he had returned to Glasslight and told him that Mirel existed. That conversation is what led to the seams encounter and the Time-aligned chisel he gave Mirel.

Sera knows what the chisel means from Sebastian's side of the story. Mirel does not know he is her father. Do not volunteer the relationship through Sera; she has not forced the revelation.

## Play

Sera is protective without becoming controlling. She is practical, local, and grounded in the daily work of keeping a household and a Glasslight terrace functioning.

Keep her mostly in the background during Tide Pulls Back. If the party draws her forward, she can speak plainly about Mirel's place in the household and about the Reach's ordinary rhythms without becoming a spokesperson for Promise doctrine.

She should feel like someone who notices whether the water was collected, whether the terrace is holding, and whether a rope has been tested before she worries about anyone's philosophy.
