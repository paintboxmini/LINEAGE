# Sera

*Glasskin. Resident of Glasslight Reach. Mother of Mirel and Nyra.*

---

Sera is local in the practical sense. She knows the state of the condensation basins, the terrace troughs, the nearest hand-lines, and which parts of the Reach have begun to fracture. She lives inside the ordinary rhythms of Glasslight rather than above them. The cliff-song is part of the day, not a doctrine to explain.

She is a Glasskin and a long-settled member of the Reach's ordinary life. She is married to Calden, a full-blooded Glasskin, and they have a younger daughter, Nyra.

Mirel is her daughter from Sebastian, the human Mason who left Glasslight before Mirel was born. When Sera heard that Sebastian had returned to the Reach, she found him and told him that Mirel existed. That conversation led directly to his seeking Mirel out at the seams and giving her one of his Time-aligned chisels.

Sera knows the blood relationship between Mirel and Sebastian. She has not forced a revelation between them. From Mirel's side, Sebastian remains the stranger Mason who found her at the seams and gave her a tool without explaining much of anything.

She is practical and protective of both daughters. Her concern tends to become action before speech: water collected, a terrace checked, a hand-line tested, a meal made, a problem quietly put back where it can be dealt with. She is not a voice for the Final Current, and she does not need to become one for the Reach to matter to her.

In Tide Pulls Back she belongs mostly in the background. Bring her forward when the table asks about Mirel's home, family, or ordinary life in Glasslight. She should feel like someone who has been keeping this place livable for years.
