# Lefty

Doesn't flinch, doesn't retreat, doesn't need two hands to hold the line.

Mind 3 / Body 5 / Soul 4 — HP 22
Creature Threat Rating: 12

---

## Appearance

One sleeve pinned back instead of filled — has been for longer than anyone in the party has known him. The anchor rides slung across his back on its own chain when he isn't swinging it; the cross at its head is scratched down to bare iron on the edges that see the most work. Built like someone the world stopped being able to move a long time ago.

---

## Behavior

Puts himself between the fight and whoever's closest to going down without being asked, and without making a thing of it afterward. The kind of steady that younger, louder people in the party have started to lean on without quite admitting it out loud.

Doesn't talk about the cave. Doesn't have to — everyone who was there already knows, and everyone who wasn't can tell not to ask twice.

---

## Combat Identity

Built to make a fight happen where he wants it, not where it started. HEAVE throws the enemy back; HAUL drags the enemy Backline in — the chain doesn't discriminate, it just decides which direction the fight moves. IRON ANCHOR staggers what needs staggering and lets him volunteer to eat a hit meant for someone else in the same motion. HOLD FAST keeps him standing through all of it, since the whole plan only works as long as he's the one holding the line.
