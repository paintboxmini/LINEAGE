# Barb

*A Riverdog of Vulture's Nest (`factions-and-races/races-riverdogs.md`). Met at the table, session two (`campaign/session-2-log.md`).*

**Beautiful, and an experienced river navigator.** Found at the Listing Keel surrounded by Riverdog men. **Code talked her outside to meet Caine; Caine talked her into sailing for the party** — and into his room.

**She is the Holly Mint's navigator, hired**, with two more Riverdog sailors hired alongside her (unnamed). **Nobody in the party took a sailing Skill, so the crew does the sailing** — *which makes Barb and the two of them recurring faces aboard.* 

**How Caine met her** *(corrected 2026-11-04)*: **Code played wingman** — cut through the Riverdog men around her and asked her to give his Shunka friend a chance. Three of those men took it badly, and that was the fight. *Caine is with her at the Keel tonight.*

**Stood up** *(session four, 2026-11-14)*: **Caine agreed to a date, then left the Nest that night without a word.** *She was waiting when the Strays came looking for him.*
