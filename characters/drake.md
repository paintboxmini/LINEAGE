# Captain Drake

*A captain of the Pirate Guild at Vulture's Nest (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`). Created by Drew, 2026-10-12, after Sir Francis Drake.*

**The pirate a party works with if they want one who is not Harlock.** Harlock runs the Guild and carries things he will not explain. **Drake runs one ship, takes small prizes often, and explains everything** — at length, cheerfully, mostly about himself.

---

## Who He Is

**A gentleman adventurer with a commission and a reputation he is actively building.** Charming, generous with his crew, quick to laugh, and completely certain that history is watching. **His ego is enormous, and he is good enough to mostly earn it.** Cool under pressure to the point of showing off — *he will finish his drink, his hand of cards or his sentence before he goes to see what the shouting is about, and the crew love him for it.*

**The Muirn have a name for him**, and he is delighted by it, and he cannot pronounce it (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`, The Muirn).

*Proposal: his ship is the **Pelican**. The name is open.*

## What He Does — the low-stakes trade

**Local privateering, mostly, and that is the point of him.** Short runs to the swamp margins south of the Nest, Muirn hunting boats taken or fought off, back in a few days with a prize to put before the court. **He hires for a single run**, so a party can sign on for one voyage, see what the work is, and walk away with their share.

**He does it by the book**, because the book pays: a clean prize condemned is money, and a sloppy one is a quarrel with Isabella's court (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`, The Prize Court). *A party sailing with Drake learns the whole system — commission, capture, hold sealed, condemnation, shares — on a ship where nobody is hiding anything.*

**He and Harlock get on.** Drake does not want the Guild; the Guild is a desk. He wants a legend. *Harlock finds that restful.*

## The Circumnavigation

**Drake wants to be the first captain to take one ship all the way round the river web and back to the Nest.**

**The water goes round** — the web is a circuit, not a drainage, clockwise from Glasslight down past the Nest, west to the Turnroot, and north again (`world/geography-overview.md`, River system). **Every stretch of it has been sailed. Nobody is known to have sailed all of it, in one hull, in one voyage.** No single boat is built for every stretch — rowed water, pullways, hard tide at the centre, the Glasslight cliffs, and the far northern reaches where the Vessk do not let anybody near (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`).

**He has a chart, half filled in, and he talks about it constantly.** *Nobody takes it seriously, and he has noticed.* **If the party ever wants the long voyage, this is the captain who is already planning it.** *Otherwise it is the thing he says after the second drink.*
