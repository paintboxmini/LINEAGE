# Mirel — Connections

- **Steve** — Wren's son, a young adult who is curious about the Final Current. Early in the Tide Pulls Back sequence, Mirel finds him difficult to take entirely seriously, but she notices him more than she admits. Something real is developing between them, and neither has named it. She is present for later key moments involving him and the Final Current.
- Her arc is the entry point for the Pip story (Steve's ritual bathing, Pip's formation) — and where it ends for her personally. Each bubble Pip loses is a memory Steve loses with it, and one of them is her (`characters/steve-and-pip/README.md`). He never grieves losing her. He never knows there was anything to grieve. She's the only one who has to carry it.
- **Sera** — Mirel's Glasskin mother.
- **Calden** — Mirel's full-blooded Glasskin stepfather.
- **Nyra** — Mirel's younger half-sister, Glasskin-leaning and closer to ordinary Reach life. If Mirel leans harder into the Masons and away from the Reach, Nyra is the natural point of family tension.
- Connected to the Mason lodge hidden in the Glasslight cliffs — she knows the entrance.
- **Sebastian** (`characters/sebastian/README.md`) — the Mason who found her at the seams and gave her the chisel. Mirel does not know he is her father. He left Glasslight before she was born and only recently learned she existed when Sera found him after his return to the Reach.
