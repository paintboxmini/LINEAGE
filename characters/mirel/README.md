# Mirel

*Young adult. People of Promise, Glasslight Reach. Central character of the Tide Pulls Back arc.*

---

Promise-raised. Grew up with the ritual bathing, the philosophy, the inevitability of return. Ocean-blue robes, faded at the edges — worn like something she hasn't decided whether to keep.

She's been having doubts about the Promise. Not loud doubts. The quiet kind that feel like blasphemy at 3am. Specifically: the inevitable return to the Unheld. She doesn't want to dissolve. She isn't sure she's allowed to not want that.

She's half Glasskin on her mother's side and half human on her father's. Her mother, Sera, is Glasskin. Her stepfather, Calden, is full-blooded Glasskin. Her younger half-sister, Nyra, is younger than Mirel and more closely aligned with ordinary Glasslight life. Mirel's family is intact; the tension around her is about where her life may lead, not about the household breaking apart.

She found the Masons. She's been learning constraint magic in secret. Carries a Mason's chisel under her cloak like a confession she hasn't made yet — not a training tool. One of Sebastian's own (`characters/sebastian/README.md`; the order itself, `factions/the-masons.md`), aligned to Time.

**How she found them:** Stabilization glyphs on the cliffside kept catching her eye. She started dreaming about the symbols — couldn't stop thinking about them. Following that pull, alone, she made her way out to the seams near the Lodge at the Soft Edge — real ones, the kind that take training to even notice — and was scratching one of the glyphs from memory into the rock when Sebastian found her.

From Mirel's point of view, Sebastian was a stranger Mason who found her at the seams. She had never met him before that moment. He was impressed she'd found the seams at all. Asked how. She said she'd just followed some marks — nothing more dramatic than that, no sense she'd done anything unusual. That plainness is what actually settled it for him; someone chasing significance would have made more of the moment than she did. He opened up more than he meant to — talked about the work, maybe even let her inside the Lodge itself. Without thinking about it, he handed her a chisel. Told her she'd have to find her own hammer if she wanted to put it to practice. No glyph lessons. Nothing else to go on. That was the beginning.

She wasn't looking for the Masons. The glyphs found her first. That distinction matters to her.

She does not know Sebastian is her father. He left Glasslight before she was born, and she has no reason to connect the stranger at the seams to her family.

There is another quiet thread in her life now: Steve. Early on, she finds him difficult to take entirely seriously. He is curious about the Final Current, and she notices him more than she lets the party see. Her interest is real. So is his. Neither has named it. She will be present for later moments involving him and the Final Current.

When she first mentions Steve to the party, she describes him as curious about the Final Current. She does not describe him as someone who has already refused Kaine.

When she approaches the party she's looking for someone outside the community. Not to be talked out of her doubts. To be allowed to have them out loud for the first time.
