# Mirel — Gm Notes

---

## Character Function

The Promise/Mason tension runs through her personally. She's not a symbol of the conflict — she's a person caught inside it.

Her arc: deciding which way to go, on her own terms, without being rescued into it.

**She should not be saved.** She should be accompanied.

## Family

Mirel is half Glasskin and half human. Her mother is **Sera**, a Glasskin; her stepfather is **Calden**, full-blooded Glasskin; her younger half-sister is **Nyra**, Glasskin-leaning and younger than Mirel.

The household is intact. Nyra's likely tension with Mirel is about Mirel's possible movement toward the Masons and away from ordinary Reach life, not a pre-existing family fracture.

Mirel's biological father is **Sebastian**, the Mason. He left Glasslight before Mirel was born. He only recently learned she existed when Sera found him after hearing he was back in the Reach and told him. That revelation led directly to the seams encounter and the chisel gift.

Mirel does not know this. From her point of view, Sebastian is simply the stranger Mason who found her at the seams, was impressed she had located them, and gave her one of his chisels with almost no explanation. He has not told her he is her father.

Keep this distinction strict: Sebastian's private knowledge belongs in GM-facing material only. Player-facing Mirel material should not reveal the connection.

## Steve

Mirel's interest in Steve is real and developing. Early in the arc she can find him difficult to take entirely seriously, but the humor should not erase the attraction. When she first brings him up to the party, she describes him as curious about the Final Current. She does not know him as someone who has already refused Kaine, and that refusal has not happened yet at the start of this sequence.

The Tide Market moment where Steve clumsily attempts to ask her out should read as genuinely awkward rather than staged. His self-deprecating recovery gets a real laugh from her. Neither has named what is growing between them.

She is present for later key moments involving Steve and the Final Current. Keep the relationship available across the sequence rather than treating it as a finished romance.
