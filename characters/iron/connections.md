## The Duskwick

It started showing up a season back and never left. He doesn't feed it. It doesn't seem to need him to.

Asked whose it is, he'll say it isn't. Not his, exactly — it just hasn't gone anywhere. See `bestiary/duskwick/README.md`.

## The Neighbour's Field

He keeps the bundles up at the abandoned homestead down the road (`places/briarwatch.md`, The Abandoned Homestead). Two new ones every autumn, since the family left. He has never told anybody and he does not bring it up.

Asked who ties them, he says he does. Asked why, he says somebody should. Asked anything past that, he doesn't have more — it is not a story he has, it is a thing he does on the way back from his own fields.

He works that yard and nothing else. He has never been inside the house and has no reason to be, so he does not know what has taken up the doorway.
