# Iron — Mechanics

**No stat block.** Iron does not fight, and nothing in the encounter asks him to.

He has a deck anyway. A deck is not only for combat — a card's name is what lets it be discarded to support a noncombat action (`rules/out-of-combat.md`, Advantage & Disadvantage), so a man who never throws a punch still needs cards to spend on prying, reading, holding his ground and talking his way through.

**Deck (10 — 4 Blue / 4 Red / 2 Green):** FORGET, PARADOX, ALIGN, ANTICIPATE *(blue)* · BLOOD TITHE, GAMBLER'S RUIN, PAIN IS FUEL, REPEL *(red)* · MIRROR STEP, RENEWAL *(green)*

*This was Steele's deck, played by Kevin (`archives/steele/README.md`). Iron carries it deliberately — he is meant to read as a reflection of that character, not a coincidence.*
