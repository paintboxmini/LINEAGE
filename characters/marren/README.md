# Marren

*Glasskin. Prism-grinder, lower streets. Glasslight Reach.*

---

Makes the best prisms in the Reach and is almost never kind about anything else she makes contact with. Her gift is real and specific: she names the exact shade of a person's failing the way a light-cartographer names a hue nobody's bothered to write down yet, and she is never wrong about the color, only about whether saying it out loud was necessary.
