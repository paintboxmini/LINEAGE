# Marren — Profile

The town tolerates her for the prisms. Nobody defends the rest of it. She corrects apprentices' color-sense in front of whoever's in the shop, precisely, in a voice that never quite rises — Glasslight doesn't do rising, even Marren, even now — and somehow that makes it worse, not better. A shout would be over quickly. This isn't.

She turns the same eye on herself, without mercy, in private, and it isn't self-improvement — it's the same weapon, pointed inward on schedule, as if fairness demanded she take her own medicine before dosing anyone else. She would call this integrity. Nobody who's watched her do it for long calls it that.
