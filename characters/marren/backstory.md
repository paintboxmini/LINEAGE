# Marren — Backstory

**What was named and never paid for.** Once, a long time ago, she said something true about someone she loved — precisely, the way she says everything — and it landed exactly as hard as the truth always lands when it's aimed at someone who can't defend against being seen that clearly. She has spent the years since trying to make the world smaller than that one sentence turned out to be, correcting everyone else's shade so precisely that nobody notices she's never once corrected her own account of that day.
