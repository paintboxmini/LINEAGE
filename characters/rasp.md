# Rasp

## Appearance

A clean, old scar crosses her throat, pale against skin that tans everywhere else. Whatever cut it took more than skin — her voice comes out worn and quiet, like it's rationing itself. She dresses to be forgotten: nothing that catches light, nothing that catches a second glance. The daggers are the only things she keeps sharp.

## Behavior

She helps and then steps back before anyone can thank her properly. Doesn't need to be seen doing the work to know she did it.

Then, without warning, she'll do something completely absurd and it lands harder for how little of herself she otherwise shows. Whatever put that scar there stays in the past, she won't speak of it.
