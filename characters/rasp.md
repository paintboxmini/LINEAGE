# Rasp

*Doesn't raise her voice. Can't, really. Doesn't need to.*

**Cards:** `cards/rasp.md`

*Stat block and deck are a starting point. Scale to match where she enters the story — early campaign she's the ally nobody quite remembers helping them, late campaign she's the reason a fight everyone thought was lost, wasn't. Adjust Body (sets HP) and total stats (sets card count) freely. Mind is worth more than it looks when you scale it: it sets hand size 1:1 (`rules/card-glossary.md`, Stat Change), so raising it hands her more options every turn, not just a bigger number on the sheet — scale it deliberately, not as a free knob alongside the other two.*

**Mind 4 / Body 3 / Soul 5 — HP 15**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 12

---

## Appearance

A clean, old scar crosses her throat, pale against skin that tans everywhere else. Whatever cut it took more than skin — her voice comes out worn and quiet, like it's rationing itself. She dresses to be forgotten: nothing that catches light, nothing that catches a second glance. The daggers are the only things she keeps sharp.

---

## Behavior

She helps and then steps back before anyone can thank her properly — not modesty exactly, more like she's already moved on to the next problem, or already somewhere else in her head. Doesn't need to be seen doing the work to know she did it.

Then, without warning, she'll do something completely absurd — a bad pun delivered dead straight, an escape route that only makes sense in hindsight — and it lands harder for how little of herself she otherwise shows. Whatever put that scar there stays exactly where she keeps it: behind her, not spoken of.

---

## Combat Identity

Built to be ignored until it's too late for that to matter. OUT OF REACH is the whole thesis: while she holds the Backline, she isn't a valid target at all — not evaded, not resisted, not there. That ends the instant she acts.

Mostly chip damage — QUIET POISON ticks small and steady, more distraction than threat on its own. SEPTIC STRIKE is the rare payoff: harmless without a poison already working, genuinely dangerous the instant one is. The lining-up is the exception, not the plan. Most fights, she's chipping, not closing.
