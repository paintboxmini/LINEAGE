# Rasp

## Appearance

A clean, old scar crosses her throat, pale against skin that tans everywhere else. Whatever cut it took more than skin — her voice comes out worn and quiet, like it's rationing itself. She dresses to be forgotten: nothing that catches light, nothing that catches a second glance. The daggers are the only things she keeps sharp.

## Behavior

She helps and then steps back before anyone can thank her properly. Doesn't need to be seen doing the work to know she did it.

Then, without warning, she'll do something completely absurd and it lands harder for how little of herself she otherwise shows. Whatever put that scar there stays in the past, she won't speak of it.

## Trait — Out of Reach

**This is a Trait, not a Passive** (`rules/character-creation.md`, Passives and Traits) — it costs no Action, is never played, and has no die.

**While Rasp is in the Backline, enemies cannot target her with attacks.** It ends the moment she attacks or changes position, and it is live again the next time she is in the Backline having done neither.

*"You were never actually aiming at her."*

**What this does to the fight.** It is not a shield, it is a decision she has to keep making. Every turn she spends untouchable is a turn she is not attacking, and the moment she does anything the party can feel, she is a target again. STEP BACK (`cards/rasp.md`) is the card built around it — it puts her in the Backline and pays her for staying, so the Trait is what the card is for rather than something the card carries.
