# Vescal — Trutinate

Vescal and Orin operate as freelance peacekeepers under the name **Trutinate**. Bounties, escort work, negotiation contracts, extractions. Violence is the last resort — which makes them very good at it, because by the time it arrives they've already exhausted everything else.

Someone hired them for whatever situation the party finds them in. That someone's interests may or may not align with the party's.
