# Vescal — Appearance

Broad. Moves slowly and deliberately — the censer needs room to swing properly and Vescal has learned to take it. Wears the grey-white of the Temple of Unity, road-worn and smoke-stained from years of travel. The censer itself is old, dented, the chain repaired in several places with slightly mismatched links. He maintains it meticulously anyway.

Speaks in the measured cadence of someone who spent years conducting ritual. In combat it sounds like official pronouncement.
