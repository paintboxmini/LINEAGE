# Dorran

*Bonesetter, Vulture's Nest. Created 2026-10-01.*

**He patches up dock workers and does not ask how anybody got hurt.** Crush injuries, rope burns, a hand that went the wrong way through a winch, and the occasional wound that was very clearly a knife. **Same fee, same silence, either way.**

**Somebody recurring the party can come back to.** They will get hurt often, and the Nest has no proper physician — a fact the quarantine makes painfully clear (`quests/the-quarantined-barge.md`). *Dorran is not one either. He is what the docks have instead, and he is good at it.*
