# Xander

*A Riverdog sailor at the Thieves Den, Vulture's Nest (`factions-and-races/races-riverdogs.md`; `places/vultures-nest.md`, The Thieves Den). Met by Flint, 2026-11-05 (`campaign/session-2-log.md`, Between sessions).*

**Large, intimidating, and remarkably clean and well dressed for a pirate.** Found against the Den wall with his arms crossed, watching a darts game, visibly upset — *just back from a voyage, and owed money by men who never made it right.* **"I'm going to make it right tonight, one way or another. I don't care how many of them there are."**

**He does not announce things.** When Flint offered to help, he said nothing — he charged the man at the dartboard, **caught the dart thrown at him, put it into the man's cheek, and knocked him out cold.** *Then the whole bar fought, and Flint fought beside him.*

**He is Flint's first mate**, and the plan is that **he hires the rest of the crew.**

**The Mangy Dog has sailed with him before** and calls him *"an uptight ass"* (`characters/mangy-dog.md`). *Flint talked the Dog into sailing with him again. An uptight first mate under a drunk captain is a ship that might actually work.*
