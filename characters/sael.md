# Sael

*The Speaker of Pneum. Lizardkin, Muirn by birth, out of Gharial long ago. Named 2026-10-01.*

**Cards:** `cards/sael.md`

**Who Sael is lives in `places/pneum.md`** (The Speaker — and why a Lizardkin leads this island): a Muirn who left, came to a faith of chosen, permanent, witnessed marks, recognised it, and out-devoted a congregation two hundred years older than their arrival. **This file is the fight, for the rare table that has one.**

---

## In a Fight

*Just in case.* **Sael is the wrong person to threaten** (`places/pneum.md`, Pat's lead): a congregation's leader, on their island, among their people. **A fight with Sael is a fight with Pneum**, and it should feel like one.

**Mind 4 / Body 2 / Soul 4 — HP 18**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 10

**Deck 10 — 2 Red / 4 Blue / 4 Green.** Hand size 4 (Mind). Initiative 1d6 + 4.

**Red (2)** — OLD SHAPES *(signature)*, ANCHOR

**Blue (4)** — ASKED PROPERLY *(signature)*, DECODE, FORESEE, STUDY

**Green (4)** — THE CHAIR BESIDE *(signature)*, HEALING SONG, SHELTER, ABIDE

**Trait — Attended:** at the start of each of Sael's turns, one ally in Sael's position gains Protect (`rules/card-glossary.md`). *On Pneum the most devout are the most marked, and the most marked are never left alone* (`places/pneum.md`, Nobody was bred to survive this — they are kept alive). **Somebody always steps in front.**

**Trait — Better in the Water:** while fighting in or from water, Sael's attacks deal +2 damage — every Muirn's (`bestiary/muirn-hunter.md`).

**How Sael fights.** Barely, and through other people. Body 2 because the faith has been written on Sael's body for years. **Everything that matters is judgement and care**: ASKED PROPERLY saps whoever is pressing hardest, the Blue reads the room, and the Green keeps the congregation standing — HEALING SONG for everyone, SHELTER for whoever is about to drop, THE CHAIR BESIDE for the one at Sael's side. *Under the robes is still a Muirn, and OLD SHAPES is the one moment that shows.*

**Run it with the congregation**, not alone: Sael never is. *Who stands with Sael — and how many — is the GM's call in the moment.*
