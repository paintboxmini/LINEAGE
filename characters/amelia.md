# Amelia

*First mate of the **Arcadia**, Harlock's ship (`characters/harlock.md`). Created by Drew, 2026-10-12.*

**A Rasha** — slender, upright and feline. *The inspiration is Captain Amelia from* Treasure Planet: *clipped, precise, dry as a biscuit, the best navigator on any deck she stands on, and entirely unimpressed by anyone who has not earned it.* **The difference here is the rank.** She is not the captain. She is the reason the captain's ship works.

*The Rasha: `factions-and-races/races-rasha.md`. Night eyes are why she is the one who brings the Arcadia in after dark.*

---

## What She Does

**Harlock is the temper; Amelia is the ship.** Watches, stores, repairs, discipline, the course, and the crew's articles — all hers. Harlock decides where the Arcadia goes and what it takes. **Amelia makes sure it gets there, takes it properly, and comes back with everybody paid.**

**She keeps the charter.** The Pirate Guild sails under Regency licence (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`), and a licence is paperwork — what may be taken, from whom, where a prize is declared, what the Compact's cut is. **A prize she has logged is a legal prize. One she has not is just theft**, and she does not let the Arcadia do theft by accident.

**She hires.** Harlock picks strangers for a job; Amelia signs people onto a crew. *Those are different things and she will tell you so.* Anyone who wants to sail with the Arcadia ends up in front of her eventually, and **she wants to see competence, not enthusiasm** — what somebody did when it mattered, preferably something she watched.

## How She Carries Herself

**Formal on her own deck, and on nobody else's.** Orders are short and exact and given once. Ashore she is easier company than anybody expects — wry, quick, fond of a good argument — and she still notices everything.

**She and Harlock are not friends, and they are very good together.** He trusts her with the ship, which from a man who calls loyalty a transaction is the largest thing he has. *She has never asked what happened before the Nest. That is most of why he trusts her.*

## The Crates — a proposal

**The Ocellus crates are not on the charter** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Arcadia's hold). They are Harlock's private arrangement, outside the Guild's licence and outside her logs. **Proposed: she knows the crates exist and does not like them** — not because she knows what is in them, but because they are the one thing aboard that is not on paper, and on her ship *that* is the crime. *Whether that is true, and what she would do about it, is Drew's call.*

## At the Table

**The way into the pirate thread runs through her.** A party that impressed the Arcadia — on the dock in session one, the wrackclaw spill, the crate, the count — has impressed **her**, and she is the one who can offer them berths (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Work out of the Nest). *Nothing pushes it. It is a door that stays open.*

**A social scene first.** If it comes to a fight, she is not the one you should worry about — **she is the one who calls the people you should worry about** (All Hands, below).

**Red (4)** — STRIKE, GUARD, DART, GROUNDING STANCE

**Blue (4)** — ANTICIPATE, CALLED SHOT, MARKED, FORESEE

**Green (4)** — INSTINCT, SHADE AWAY, MOCKERY, BRISTLE

*Even across all three, the way she runs a ship: nothing neglected, nothing favoured. All core cards — her signature is the trait.* **Blue is the navigator** reading the fight like a chart; **Red is a deck that does not move under her**; **Green is the nerve to give an order once.**

**Passive — CATCH THE LIGHT**, the Rasha night eyes (`factions-and-races/races-rasha.md`).

**Skills — Intimidation +2, Sailing +3.** *Sailing is one above the +2 a player starts with: a life spent on it.* Intimidation is the voice that only has to say a thing once.

### Trait — All Hands

**She can call the crew into a fight.**

- **It costs her Action.** She shouts, and that is her turn.
- **At the start of her next turn, two Arcadia hands arrive** at her position. **They are summoned combatants**: their tokens enter the initiative wheel directly after hers (`rules/combat.md`, Initiative).
- **Once per combat.**
- **Only where the crew can hear.** On the Arcadia, on the dock beside her, anywhere within a shout of a Guild ship — yes. In a back room across town — nobody answers, and she knows it, so she does not waste the breath.

*The two turns are the warning.* **A party that hears her call has one round to finish it, leave, or brace** — the same clock the Gollops run on (`bestiary/gollop.md`, The encounter, as it actually runs), with a shorter fuse and a hard stop.

**How hard** *(measured 2026-10-12 against Chris, Kevin and Pat, 800 fights a row; the trait is not run)*: **Amelia alone, the party wins about 97%, but somebody dies in roughly one fight in eleven** — over the session budget of about one death in twenty sessions (`rules/gm-guide.md`, Pacing Sessions). **Two hands alone are a formality** (99%). *Amelia with her two hands together was not measured — the tool only runs copies of one creature — and will be harder than either.* **She is not a casual fight**, which suits a first mate on her own deck.

**An Arcadia hand** — combat-only, and unnamed until a player makes one matter.

**Mind 1 / Body 2 / Soul 1 — HP 10**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 4

**Deck (4 — 1 Blue / 2 Red / 1 Green):** DISTRACT *(blue)* · STRIKE, GUARD *(red)* · SHADE AWAY *(green)*
