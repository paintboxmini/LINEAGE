## What He Actually Sells

The water itself is ordinary by the time it reaches the Nest — unheldness doesn't cross the coastline, ever (`world/the-unheld.md`). What isn't ordinary is what the vials pick up on the way: they're filled aboard Pneum's own ritual barges (`places/pneum.md`), by hands belonging to a community that collects diseases as worship. Contact causes random illness.

Corvel doesn't disclose this, and doesn't privately distinguish it from the water itself. From his own perspective, the illness *is* the communion, arriving the way it always does: through the body. He genuinely doesn't understand the problem with this framing.
