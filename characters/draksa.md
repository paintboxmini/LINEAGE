# Draksa

*Vessk. Created 2026-10-01.*

**Cards:** `cards/draksa.md`

**A watcher, posted near Gharial** (`places/gharial.md`). The Vessk know where the island is — they ransacked it once (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Muirn) — and **Draksa is how the family is reminded of the deal.** She visits, she talks to Vaun (`characters/vaun.md`), and she leaves.

**Disciplined, and contemptuous of the Muirn.** To her they are a people that had one useful thing and cannot even keep track of the woman who used it. *She is polite to them the way you are polite to a tool.*

**The first face of the second war.** The Vessk are rebuilding an army in the north (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Vessk now), and long before the party goes anywhere near it, Draksa is what that army looks like: patient, informed, and certain the curse will finish its work.

## In a Fight

**Mind 4 / Body 4 / Soul 3 — HP 23**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 11

**Deck 11 — 4 Red / 4 Blue / 3 Green.** Hand size 4 (Mind). Initiative 1d6 + 3.

**Red (4)** — NO SECOND WARNING *(signature)*, STRIKE, GUARD, ENDURE

**Blue (4)** — KNOW YOUR PLACE *(signature)*, FOCUS, MARKED, CALLED SHOT

**Green (3)** — THE LONG GAME *(signature)*, QUICKEN, SHADE AWAY

**Trait — Reports Back:** when Draksa falls to half her HP or below, she leaves on her next turn instead of acting — cover, a direction picked in advance, no last word. *A watcher who dies reports nothing.* **Beating her is not the same as stopping her: whatever she saw reaches the Vessk.**

**How she fights.** A soldier, and the only trained one in this story so far. KNOW YOUR PLACE pushes people back down the turn order, THE LONG GAME and FOCUS set up the hit she wants, and NO SECOND WARNING lands it. *She is better than Vaun in a straight fight and less interested in having one — she is here to watch, and she will not trade her report for a win.*
