# Draksa

*Vessk. Created 2026-10-01.*

**A watcher, posted near Gharial** (`places/gharial.md`). The Vessk know where the island is — they ransacked it once (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Muirn) — and **Draksa is how the family is reminded of the deal.** She visits, she talks to Vaun (`characters/vaun.md`), and she leaves.

**Disciplined, and contemptuous of the Muirn.** To her they are a people that had one useful thing and cannot even keep track of the woman who used it. *She is polite to them the way you are polite to a tool.*

**The first face of the second war.** The Vessk are rebuilding an army in the north (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Vessk now), and long before the party goes anywhere near it, Draksa is what that army looks like: patient, informed, and certain the curse will finish its work.
