# Moth

*Underground Bazaar. Close childhood friends with Kess.

## What's Known

He spends freely. As if he decided, at some point, that getting out is not the goal.

Has been coming here long enough to know the rhythms, the vendors worth finding, the lines worth joining. He plays Liar's Dice at the tavern table most nights. The house gremlin has flagged him as a person of interest and cannot find a rule he's breaking.
