# Giblets — Backstory

Giblets and Mortis were partners for years, back when the work was adventuring instead of a stall and a bar three streets apart.

Giblets repeatedly threw himself into whatever was about to kill Mortis. Mortis never once left him dead when he could help it, and never once made him ask twice.

Every resurrection cost Giblets something: fragments of personality, gaps in emotional register, memories that didn't reassemble correctly.

They retired together and never really stopped being partners — just changed what they're partners in.
