# Giblets — Profile

Giblets is a ghoul. He has been brought back from death more times than is good for anyone — every one of them on purpose, throwing himself into whatever was about to kill Mortis instead.

Each resurrection lost something: fragments of personality, gaps in emotional register, memories that didn't reassemble correctly.

He is not tragic about this. He is unhinged about it, which is different. He'd do it again tomorrow and has said so.

He operates the FourthEye stall in the Underground Bazaar.
