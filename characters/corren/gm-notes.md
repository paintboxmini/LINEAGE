## Death (GM Secret — the founding moment of the Final Current, known to no one but Kaine, told by him to no one)

Low tide. Corren went down alone, onto rock the water only leaves bare a few hours at a stretch, standing where his first bath had never required him to stand. Kaine was already up in the cliffs, hidden, for reasons that had nothing to do with Corren at first.

Corren waited. Then it took him the way his first bath never had — thrashing, changing, faster and further than anything the congregation has a word for, going twice as far on a second try as he had on the first. Right before he was undone completely — the porousness finally winning, nothing left to climb back up the rocks — he passed through a form that Kaine, watching from above, read as powerful. Desirable. The single image the rest of Kaine's life has been built on top of — one man, one broken rule, one data point mistaken for a method.

Kaine never went down to him. There was nothing left to go down for by the time he might have. To the congregation, Corren simply went under one day and didn't come back — sad, and genuinely shocking, since as far as anyone knew there was no reason to expect a second bath. What Kaine knows, and has told no one, not even his most devoted: he watched a man break a rule nobody breaks, try to disappear doing it, and kept the shape of the moment before disappearance as the whole point. See `characters/kaine/README.md`, Where the Method Actually Came From.

**What actually happened, decades later (GM Secret — not known to Kaine's followers, never said aloud by Corren while he lived, not confirmed to the table unless a GM decides the moment has earned it).** He broke the norm — privately, without telling the congregation, without the ropes — and went back. He wasn't chasing more of what the first bath gave him. He was chasing the same completion the Last Bath gives the dead (`factions/people-of-promise.md`) — quietly, alone, without ever once calling it that.
