# Nyra — GM Notes

---

## Play

Nyra is most useful when she feels like someone who was already living her life before Mirel's choices became complicated.

She is closer to the ordinary congregation and to the practical rhythms of Glasslight. If Mirel talks about the Mason work or begins to pull away from ordinary Reach life, Nyra's response should come from genuine difficulty understanding it rather than contempt.

She loves her sister. She can disagree with Mirel without treating Mirel as a problem to solve.

Nyra can appear in family scenes, background congregation moments, or ordinary Reach life without requiring a separate plot. Bring her forward only if the table makes the sibling relationship relevant.
