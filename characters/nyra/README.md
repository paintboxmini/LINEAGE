# Nyra

*Young adult. Full Glasskin-leaning heritage. Sera's daughter and Mirel's younger half-sister.*

---

Nyra is younger than Mirel and was raised close to the ordinary life of Glasslight Reach. The cliff-song, the congregation, the terraces, the water, and the daily work of keeping the Reach livable are familiar to her in a way they are becoming less familiar to Mirel.

She is loyal to Sera, to the Reach, and to her sister. That loyalty does not make her hostile to Mirel. If Mirel moves further toward the Masons, constraint magic, or a life outside the ordinary patterns of Glasslight, Nyra may simply fail to understand why. The tension between them can come from that gap without either sister needing to be cruel.

Nyra should feel like a real young adult who belongs here, not a symbol for the Reach and not a device for pushing Mirel's story. She can be present in household moments, ordinary congregation gatherings, or the background of life in the Reach without needing to drive the main arc.

Keep her close enough to Mirel that the possibility of distance matters. Keep her local enough that the cliff-song and ordinary rhythms of Glasslight feel like home rather than an ideology she has chosen.
