# Lily — At the Tavern

She comes down sometimes. Always the same corner table if one is free. Mortis (`characters/mortis/README.md`) pours without being asked — has since before anyone can say when.

She and Mortis don't talk much. They don't need to. Two people who know exactly what they are and have made their peace with it in completely different directions.

If players have already met her in the bazaar and she has a commission open, she may be here between hunts. She does not appear troubled by this.
