# Wren — GM Notes

Wren is a quiet, careful member of the ordinary People of Promise congregation in Glasslight Reach. Do not play them as a faction leader or Final Current believer. Their authority is only the ordinary authority of a parent who has spent years making one decision sound like care.

The household is intact. Wren's conflict with Steve is protective rather than fracturing. If Steve accuses Wren plainly of controlling him, Wren is genuinely hurt. They do not perform the wound or immediately turn it into anger; the accusation strikes at the story Wren has told themselves about what their care means.

Wren has delayed Steve's first ordinary bath for years because of the change the Unheld brings. Their refusals should sound reasonable one at a time: readiness, caution, tiredness, another day. The pattern is the important part.

Wren once made a binding-style declaration about Steve's future without his consent. Keep the exact wording soft unless the table establishes otherwise. The existence of the declaration and its unpaid nature are solid. Wren does not know whether the eventual cost will fall on them or on Steve.

### Tide Pulls Back

Wren is present when Steve publicly states that he intends to join a Final Current descent. They do not ask the party to stop him. The moment matters because Steve is making the choice openly, while Wren is forced to confront the future they once tried to hold shut.

Wren is present again on the night Kaine runs the group platform. Kaine's people handle the ropes and the descent is a Final Current ceremony, not Wren's congregation. Wren remains at the edge rather than taking over the scene.

Play Wren as someone trying to stand beside Steve without pretending the old declaration never happened. Their fear is real. Their care is real. The harm in the protection is real too.
