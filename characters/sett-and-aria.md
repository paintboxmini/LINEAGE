# Sett and Aria

*Cartographers. Glasskin, out of Glasslight Reach. Married. Created by Drew, 2026-10-01.*

**Cards:** `cards/sett-and-aria.md`

**Road surveyors** — the Route Walk kind, not the Soft Edge kind (`factions-and-races/the-cartographers-guild.md`, Methods). They walk an assigned stretch on rotation, check it against the last survey, correct what has drifted, and report what is gone. **They are the Cartographers the party is most likely to meet**, and the most likely to hire them (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Work out of the Nest).

**Both extroverted, which makes them unusual glasskin.** Their people talk gently and rarely interrupt, *as though loud certainty could crack something* (`factions-and-races/races-glasskin.md`). These two talk over each other, over strangers, and over the wind. *It is part of why the road suits them better than home does.*

---

## Sett

**Large, slender and muscular, with a low, deep voice.** **Bold, confident, and big-headed.** Sure of his read on any ground he has walked once, sure the Guild undervalues him, and sure his name will be on an atlas people fight over. **Usually right about the ground. Always loud about it.**

**He wants a name, and the trade pays for one.** A Cartographer's atlas sells on two things, how recent it is and whose margins it carries (`factions-and-races/the-cartographers-guild.md`, The Atlas) — and a known name outsells a current survey by a stranger. *Sett is in a hurry to stop being a stranger.*

## Aria

**Small and thin, bordering on frail, with a light, breathy voice.** **Determined, naive, and warm.** She takes people at their word, likes them on sight, and will not be talked out of a walk she has decided to make. *The naivety is about people and about money, never about the ground — she reads a route as well as he does and says so less.*

**She is two months pregnant.** **Sett wants her settled at Glasslight in two or three months.** Aria wants to stay on the routes *"until I swell up like a balloon"*, and **Sett is having none of it.** *It is the one argument they have that neither of them is enjoying.*

## The Money

**They are scrambling.** Two surveyors' pay is a living; one surveyor's pay and a baby is not, and **the window before Aria stops walking routes is closing** — two or three months if Sett gets his way, longer if she gets hers. So they are taking every rotation they can, the long ones and the bad ones, and trying to put enough by before it shuts.

**Which is why they hire the party, and why they cannot pay much.** *Guild money pays for escorts* (`factions-and-races/the-cartographers-guild.md`, Escorts), *but they are cutting every corner they can find, and an escort is the first corner anybody looks at.* **A party that charges them full rate is a party they cannot afford; a party that takes less is a party they will never forget.**

## How They Work

**Opportunistic** *(Drew, 2026-10-01)*. They finish a job, head for **the nearest town with a Cartographers Guild office**, turn the work in, and take **the best-looking job they can find near where they already are.** Then they stay a little — rest, recover, restock — and head out again. *No fixed rotation and no home route*, which is why they can turn up anywhere the party does, and why they keep turning up.

## What They Are Walking Into

**The pockets are drifting more every season** (`factions-and-races/the-cartographers-guild.md`, The Pockets Are Drifting More), and road surveyors are the first to feel it under their feet. **Sett thinks it is his discovery** — the thing that finally puts his name on an atlas. **Aria thinks it is getting dangerous**, and wants to finish the rotations they have before it gets worse. *Both are right.* Neither knows the posts are Mason work, or that there is only one Mason left walking them (`factions-and-races/the-masons.md`, The Order Now).

## In a Fight

*Signature cards at `cards/sett-and-aria.md`, three each, one per colour. Neither of them is a fighter by trade; both have walked enough bad road to have learned.*

### Sett

**Mind 4 / Body 4 / Soul 2 — HP 22**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 10

**Deck 10 — 4 Red / 4 Blue / 2 Green.** Hand size 4 (Mind). Initiative 1d6 + 2.

**Red (4)** — PLANT THE POLE *(signature)*, GUARD, SHOULDER, BRACE

**Blue (4)** — BIG TALK *(signature)*, FORESEE, CALLED SHOT, MARKED

**Green (2)** — WALK IT FIRST *(signature)*, SUPPORT

**Trait — Not Her:** each time Aria takes damage, Sett gains 1 Deadly (`rules/card-glossary.md`). *Whoever hurts her has started a different fight.*

**How he fights.** Frontline, in the way, loud. **GUARD is how he covers her** — Protect and Resist, every time he can — and PLANT THE POLE pins whatever is coming so it cannot get round him. BIG TALK is half threat and half believing it, and **MARKED is the read he is actually right about** — the bigger hand is the sharper mind under the noise *(Mind raised to 4, 2026-10-01)*.

### Aria

**Mind 3 / Body 1 / Soul 4 — HP 11**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 8

**Deck 8 — 1 Red / 3 Blue / 4 Green.** Hand size 3 (Mind). Initiative 1d6 + 4.

**Red (1)** — SMALLER TARGET *(signature)*

**Blue (3)** — FLASH PLATE *(signature)*, PROBE, STUDY

**Green (4)** — KEEP WALKING *(signature)*, MEND, GUIDE, SHELTER

**How she fights.** Backline, quick to act — she goes first more often than he does — and **almost entirely for other people**: healing, Evade handed out, Immunity for whoever is about to drop. FLASH PLATE is the one thing she does to an enemy, a light-map plate tilted into their eyes. *HP 11 is the point: she cannot take a real hit, and everybody near her knows it, him most of all.*
