# Rin — Profile

Curled horns, filed short enough that a hood almost hides them; it never quite does.

Works the Nest the way a river pilot works a bad stretch: where the hurt collects. Cuts, burns, crushed hands from the cranes, the quiet after a fight nobody reported.

She takes the pain into herself when she can — touch and a short ritual, nothing theatrical — and what leaves the patient leaves a mark on her she doesn't explain. She does not advertise the gift. People who need it find her the same way they find a dry berth in the rain.

She will not talk about others of her kind. If asked whether she's the only one, she says *here*, and lets the word do the work.
