# Weck

*Travelling buyer of culls. Works the Vulture's Nest–Briarwatch road and further. See `quests/the-man-who-buys-wounds.md`.*

*No stat block. Weck does not fight — not as a tactic, not under threat. If the party attacks him he cooperates, and that is the whole of his response.*

---

## Appearance

A wool coat too clean for the road. A cart stopped neatly at whatever the road's widest point happens to be, pointed neither direction in particular. The bed is empty and scrubbed; it does not smell like animals, and it does not smell like anything. The horse doesn't look at him.

---

## What He Does

He buys culls. Fenwool the farms can't keep — wounded, marked, picked over. He pays fairly and in coin, and the Briarwatch farmers confirm it: good prices, no haggling, never early, never late. This part of his business is entirely legitimate and entirely verifiable, which is what makes the rest of it work.

He also buys wounds. He removes one Wound permanently — from hand, discard, or deck, wherever it sits — for a token payment of a few coins ("so it's a sale") and the seller's name, written in his ledger, in their own hand. One purchase per customer. He does not press, upsell, or repeat the offer to someone who has refused.

---

## Voice

Mild, unhurried, and completely honest. He answers questions truthfully and unhelpfully, and does not notice the difference:

*What do you do with them?* — "I keep them."
*What does the ledger mean?* — "That the sale happened."

He is never lying, which is worse than if he were. He recommends the Roadhouse stew to people who turn him down.

---

## The Ledger

The earlier pages hold many names in many hands. A few are neatly crossed out. He will not say what crossing-out means. He will say it isn't his doing.

The pages are blank to anyone whose name isn't written in them. A party that robs him gets a horse, a cart, and an empty book, handed over without resistance by a man who watched them do it politely. Somewhere behind them, the fair prices stop being mentioned.

---

## GM Secret

What Weck is collecting toward is not written down anywhere, including here. The ledger is a hook left open on purpose — the crossings-out are real, they are not his doing, and the table finds out what that means when the GM decides it does.

---

## Where He Appears

Anywhere on a road, at GM discretion. Same cart, same coat, further up whatever route the party takes next, buying what the ground has picked over.
