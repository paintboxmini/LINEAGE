# Iron

---

## Appearance & Manner

Hat in hand when he talks to strangers, and he keeps it there longer than the conversation needs. More tired than frightened, and has been for a season.

A Duskwick keeps to his heels the whole time, low to the ground, never quite touching him.

---

## What He Owns

The Western Property, bordered by the watchtower ruins. Good ground until it wasn't. A sinkhole opened in one of his fields last season — the first in living memory at Briarwatch — and he fenced it off himself with rope and splintered posts.

---

## What He Knows

Glimpses over years, never contact. He has seen enough to suspect something lives under his fields and nowhere near enough to know what, and he is careful to say so in those terms.

He does not know whether that's what caused the sinkhole. He does not know whether it's connected to the Fenwool the farms keep losing. He is not wrong to wonder about either, and he will not pretend either is settled.

---

## The Neighbour's Field

He keeps the bundles up at the abandoned homestead down the road. Two new ones every autumn, since the family left. He has never told anybody and he does not bring it up.

Asked who ties them, he says he does. Asked why, he says somebody should. Asked anything past that,and he replies with a stare.

---

## The Duskwick

It started showing up a season back and never left. He doesn't feed it. It doesn't seem to need him.

Asked whose it is, he'll say it isn't his exactly, it just hasn't gone anywhere and they've formed a bond.

---

## Running Him

No speech, no plea, no crisis in his voice. A man standing at the edge of a hole in his own field with his hat in his hands, answering questions accurately.
