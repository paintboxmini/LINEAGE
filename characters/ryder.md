# Ryder

*Phoenix-touched. Dies easier than he should, and hates that it never quite takes.*

**Cards:** `cards/ryder.md`

*Stat block and deck are a starting point. Scale to match where he enters the story — early campaign he's a reckless ally or a strange rumor, late campaign he's someone the party has genuinely learned to worry about. Adjust Mind, HP, and card count before use.*

**Mind 3 / Body 4 / Soul 5 — HP 17**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 12

---

## Appearance

Ash never quite finishes settling on him — a fine grey dust in his hair, along his collar, that should have brushed off days ago and hasn't. His skin carries old burn scarring in places that should have killed the nerve, and doesn't; he still flinches from cold, never from flame.

When he casts, the light comes from underneath his skin before it comes from his hands — visible for a half-second through the back of a closed fist, like something bright is being held down rather than summoned up.

---

## Behavior

He takes risks other people flinch from, and it isn't bravado — he knows something the rest of the party doesn't get to know for certain: that this specific kind of dying isn't the last word for him, not this time. That knowledge doesn't make him careless with other people. It makes him careless with himself, which is a different thing, and the party usually figures out the difference the first time they watch him walk into a hit he could have avoided.

Underneath it there's a tiredness that doesn't show up in how he fights. Coming back costs something that isn't HP. He doesn't talk about what.

---

## Combat Identity

High Soul, real Body behind it. Not fragile the way a pure caster is fragile — built to actually stand in the fire he starts.

- Gets more dangerous the lower his HP drops (ASHBORN FLARE), which reads as recklessness and is actually a read on his own math.
- Genuinely useful to allies (EMBER WARD) — not purely a glass cannon leaning on his own safety net.
- The real tension lives in his hand, not his HP bar: PHOENIX'S LAST BREATH is worthless the instant he plays it as a normal card, and worth everything the instant he doesn't. Watching whether he holds it is watching him decide how close to the edge he's willing to go this fight.

## Deck

**Signature cards:** `cards/ryder.md` — unique to Ryder, not transferable.

**Core cards (3 Blue / 3 Red / 3 Green):**

*Blue — Mind*
- PROFILE — Scry 2, then draw 1; Defensive: Attacker gains Staggered
- REFRACT — Defender gains Weak; Defensive: redirect this attack's full damage on a clean win
- STILLNESS — Defender discards 1 at random; Defensive: Attacker discards 1 at random

*Red — Body*
- STRIKE — Body + d8, no effect; Defensive: Deal 2 to attacker
- ENDURE — Gain Resist; Defensive: Heal 3 HP
- BRACE — Next successful attack against you deals -3; Defensive: Gain Resist 3

*Green — Soul*
- RENEWAL — Allies discard-then-draw or heal 2; Defensive: Attacker discards 1 at random
- WITNESS — Ally heals 3 on successful defense; Defensive: Target ally heals 3
- PATIENCE — +4 damage if you didn't attack last turn; Defensive: Move to any position
