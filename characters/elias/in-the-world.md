# Elias — In the World

Different names everywhere. No consistent description beyond the eyes — silver-blue, slightly wrong in strong light. Stories follow him: a stranger who was there when things got strange. Barmaids who swear their empty glass filled on its own. A man in Briarwatch who saw a chicken fly out of a second-story window going considerably faster than chickens should.

Nobody connects these stories to the same person. That's partly the memory fuzz, partly the names, partly the fact that Change doesn't stay in one place long enough to become a pattern.
