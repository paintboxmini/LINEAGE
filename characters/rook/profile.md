# Rook — Profile

---

Rook handles the practical side of the descents. Ropes, platform timing, positioning, the order people move, and the small details that make a ceremony work are Rook's responsibility. They notice what has to be secured before anyone else is asked to trust it.

Rook came from the ordinary Promise congregation and crossed fully into the Final Current. Unlike Vesper, Rook does not need to explain why. They make sure the descent happens the way Kaine intends it to happen.

There is no preacher in Rook. The work is enough. A rope is either ready or it is not. A platform is either positioned correctly or it is not. Someone is either where they need to be or they are moved there.

On the night of the group platform descent that includes Steve, Rook is present and watchful. Kaine has several Final Current members around him; open violence would mean choosing to fight through a group rather than simply confronting one person. Rook is the clearest expression of that practical barrier.

If the situation turns physical, Rook is the person most likely to step between the party and Kaine. Not because they need to make a spectacle of defending him, but because keeping the ceremony intact is the job they have already chosen.
