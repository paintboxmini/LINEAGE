# Rook — GM Notes

---

## Play

Rook is competent, quiet, and procedural. They should not sound like a second Kaine. They do not need to persuade anyone about the Final Current; they need to make sure the ropes are right and the ceremony proceeds.

On the group platform night, keep Rook moving through practical checks: ropes, positions, timing, who is on the platform, and who is standing where. Their attention is on the ceremony even when the party is nearby.

If the party threatens to interfere physically, Rook steps between them and Kaine. The response should be controlled and proportionate to the immediate threat. Rook is the person who makes the cost of forcing the issue obvious without turning the scene into a speech.

Rook came from the ordinary Promise congregation. That history can make the transition more unsettling, but it does not need an explanation unless the table asks for one.
