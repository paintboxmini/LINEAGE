# Samuel Gullis — the Mangy Dog

*A captain at the Thieves Den, Vulture's Nest (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Thieves Den). Met by Flint, 2026-11-05 (`campaign/session-2-log.md`, Between sessions). **Samuel Gullis** *(Drew, 2026-11-05)* — *close to* seagull, *to point at Sparrow* — **and "the Mangy Dog" is what he goes by.**

## Why "the Mangy Dog"

**He is human, and sailing is a Riverdog's trade** *(Drew)*. **A captain who is not a Riverdog is very rare** (`factions-and-races/races-riverdogs.md`), and it is one more reason the water treats him as an outsider.

**His first voyage, as a young man, was under Captain Barracuda** (`characters/barracuda.md`). **The name came from the deck:**

> *"What kind of sailor is this here, lads? If that's a proper Riverdog, then it must have got the mange. Best stay away, lest ye get it yerself."*

**He took the name.** *At least they were still calling him a dog.*

**A drunk, a risk-taker, and a captain without a ship.** Shrouded, slouched over a back-corner table with his head on one arm and his other hand round a jug of rum. **Maxa hesitated to even suggest him**: *"You're just as likely to not come back at all when you voyage out with him."* **Flint said that was perfect.**

**Jack Sparrow is the inspiration** *(Drew)* — *never quite where the fight is, never quite hit, always walking out the other side of it.* In the Den brawl he stumbled the length of the room promising he was on his way to help, and never arrived; everyone who went for him got blindsided by somebody else.

**His plans are short.** Asked for one, he gave two steps: *find the best sailor in here, make him first mate, let him hire the rest while we drink — and then, oh yeah, we have to get a ship.*

## The ship he lost

**The Water Wing.** **He lost her a month ago at liar's dice**, at the Den, **to his rival — and first captain — Barracuda** (`characters/barracuda.md`). **He was playing with an extremely high-quality set of loaded dice — and one of Barracuda's men swapped them during the game** *(Drew)*, without his noticing (`items/the-mangy-dogs-dice.md`).

**The Water Wing has sat unused at the docks ever since.** **Barracuda wants it that way**: *let her rot at her moorings, slowly, with Samuel watching it happen.*

**The last hand:** the Dog, on his last die, bid six sixes. **Barracuda, holding five sixes on the loaded dice, called *spot on*** — and the Dog's die was a six. **Caught telling the truth, at liar's dice, in the Thieves Den.**

**People make fun of him for it, endlessly.** *Sometimes he doesn't answer. Sometimes he threatens to gut them. Sometimes he just drinks more.*

**Session three** *(2026-11-07)*: **the party talked Barracuda into believing Samuel already had a new ship and was pirating again — Barracuda sailed after him — and they stole the Water Wing.** **She is *Fortune's Fancy* now**, repainted, re-rigged and repaired (`campaign/session-3-log.md`).
