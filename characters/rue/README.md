# Rue

*Glasskin. Light-cartographer, formerly of the Soft Edge survey. Glasslight Reach.*

---

Rue stopped recording the Soft Edge for the Guild after discovering that the official record could be softened when reality became inconvenient. She now privately measures the fractures in Glasslight's cliffs, with the same exacting discipline she once applied to the Guild's atlases.

She is controlled rather than gentle. Her anger manifests as precision: she refuses to let people use vague language for things she believes deserve exact names.

**Core tension:** She has built her life around measuring what others would rather not measure.
