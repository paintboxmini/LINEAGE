# Rue — Open

Somewhere in Rue, under the precision, is a sentence that starts:

> *I will never stop measuring until they see I'm right.*

It has never been spoken all the way through — not to the Guild, not to herself, not once.

Whatever a completed version of that sentence would cost her, it hasn't been collected yet. She would say, if asked, that she simply hasn't finished deciding what it means.

That isn't quite true, and she knows it isn't, which is its own kind of unpaid thing.
