# Rue — Backstory

Rue used to map the Soft Edge — one of the light-cartographers whose atlases record where the colors stop behaving, and whether the line has moved.

One year the Soft Edge moved farther than any atlas could hold steady. Rue turned in her findings and watched the senior cartographers argue about whether to record the real number or a gentler one.

She stopped recording color for anyone after that.

Now she walks the fractures in the cliff and names every place the quartz has cracked since — a private atlas nobody commissioned, kept the way the official ones used to be kept, before she decided the official ones were lying by omission.
