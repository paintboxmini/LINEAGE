# Rue — Profile

Rue does not raise her voice. Glasslight doesn't, as a rule — loud certainty is understood here to crack things — but Rue's stillness isn't the town's gentle kind. It's controlled the way a held breath is controlled.

Her anger is in the precision: an exactness that doesn't let anything, or anyone, get away with being vague about what it is. She will correct anyone who calls the Unheld gentle, calmly, completely, and at whatever length the correction requires.

## When Nobody's Watching

Rue re-measures the same three fractures on a rotation, alone, at hours when the streets are empty enough that the singing of wind through the fractures is the only other sound.

She has never told anyone this is a ritual. She would object to the word if someone used it.
