# Aege — Where She Appears

**Washed Ashore** (`quests/washed-ashore.md`) — the campaign's current opening. Met at the Roadhouse, already mid-journey on her own; she isn't the party's guide here and doesn't escort them anywhere.

**Vulture's Nest** (`places/vultures-nest.md`, Notable Roles) — the former opening, retired but still fully playable if a party arrives this way. Found near the east gate, the party's actual guide for the leg from Vulture's Nest to Briarwatch. Tells them about her family at the Roadhouse, the first night out. Delivers them to Briarwatch, hands over a Carrion Feather (`items/briarwatch-items.md`), continues on to the Turnroot Weald alone.

Same character both times. Not a coincidence, not a retcon — she goes home either way; the party just crosses her path at a different point in that same trip.
