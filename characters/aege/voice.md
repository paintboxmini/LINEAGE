# Aege — Voice

Speaks in terms of paths, not places. Doesn't explain decisions. If the party earns a comment, it arrives flat.

*"You're already ahead of yourselves."*
*"This path will suit your pace."*
*"You won't like the other way."*

**At a shared table, not on the trail** (`quests/washed-ashore.md`, Meeting Aege) — same person, a wider register. Earthy, plainspoken, more willing to talk when the setting is a meal instead of a road. Still doesn't perform mystery, still doesn't waste words on anything that isn't true — just more of them, when there's time to sit.

*"Eat. Road's longer on an empty stomach. You can owe the world something useful later."*
