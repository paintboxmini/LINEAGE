# Greed — Profile

## Appearance

Motion first, features second — you register the afterimage before you place the face. Dark hair with a static-frizzed fringe that never quite lies flat. The katana's fuller runs faintly bright, like the blade is holding a charge between draws. When he moves fast enough, the air behind him cracks a half-second late.

---
## Behavior

Impatient in one very specific way: not reckless with allies, reckless with silence. He hates a fight that hasn't started yet — small talk before initiative rolls makes him more restless than the danger does.

What discipline he has, he earned from one teacher, in one place, and hasn't found anywhere since that asked as much of him. It shows up sideways: he never draws before the reveal, never wastes the first exchange proving a point he could prove better on the second. The waiting is its own kind of control — the only kind he's fully mastered, next to a temper he hasn't.
