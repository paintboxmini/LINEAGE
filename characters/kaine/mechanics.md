# Kaine — Mechanics

## Cards

**STORM DEBT**
RED — BODY — PROMISE
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: On a win, target is Staggered.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Armour 1.
Range: Melee
*"The sky doesn't forget what you called down."*

---

**CONDUCT**
GREEN — SOUL — PROMISE
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: Your strongest ally gains Deadly.
Defensive Bonus: Your most wounded ally draws 1 card.
Range: Both
*"Belief moves like current — find the one who'll carry it best."*

---

**SILENCE THE THREAD**
GREEN — SOUL — PROMISE
Attack: Soul + d8
Effect: Exile 1 card from your discard pile
Defensive Bonus: Exile 1 ongoing effect card
Range: Both
*"The world reveals its rules in silence."*

---

**SCRIPTURE OF RESONANCE**
GREEN — SOUL — PROMISE
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Attacker and defender discard 1 random card each. If they are different colors, you heal 4 HP.
Defensive Bonus: Target ally may discard 1 Soul card to heal 4
Range: Ranged
*"The system remembers who pays attention."*
