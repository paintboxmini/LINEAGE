# Black Maggie Lore Fragments — Haunted Place Rumors

Each of these belongs to a different location. None of them reference the others. None of them mention a name.

---

**The Millwright's Field**

*"Nobody farms that land anymore. The Calloway family tried twice. First time the animals wouldn't cross the fence line — just stopped and wouldn't move no matter what. Second time old Carn Calloway walked the boundary himself to prove it was nothing and came back wrong. Didn't say what he saw. Sold the land cheap and moved north. That was thirty years ago. The fence is still there. The posts don't rot."*

What the locals believe: bad death happened there. A killing. Nobody knows whose.
What's true: completed site. Ninety-third through one hundredth sacrifices were made in the northwest corner, near the oldest fence post.

---

**The Watcher's Hollow**

*"There's a hollow about two miles off the Brenn road, just past where the creek bends. Travelers who camp there sleep fine but wake up crying and don't know why. Not frightened — just grieving. One merchant I spoke to said he dreamed of his mother who'd been dead fifteen years and woke up feeling like he'd lost her all over again. He said it wasn't a nightmare. He said it was worse than a nightmare because it felt true."*

What the locals believe: a lover's death. Or a child's. Something tender went wrong there.
What's true: completed site. The grief doesn't belong to travelers — it belongs to the site. They're feeling the residue of witnessed suffering without a witness.

---

**The Salter's House**

*"Used to be a house on the edge of town. Family lived there, kept to themselves. One winter they were just gone — no note, no forwarding, just gone. Nobody moved in after because the first night anyone spent there they left before morning and wouldn't say why. They knocked the house down eventually. Built nothing on the plot. You can still tell where it was because the weeds grow different there — lower, somehow. Like the ground doesn't want anything rising too high."*

What the locals believe: debt. Or shame. Something the family was hiding.
What's true: completed site. The house was not empty when Maggie used it. The family did not leave voluntarily.
