# The Four Muirn

*The four Muirn raiders who surrendered aboard their burning ship and joined the crew of Fortune's Fancy (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, The Muirn hunters). Written 2026-11-16, at Chris's request through Drew.*

**Chris names them** — *he captured them, and he is planning roles for them in the Red Button Society* (`campaign/chris.md`). **Until he does, they are known by what they do.**

---

## Who they are

**Muirn, the southern Lizardkin tribe**, from the swamps south of Vulture's Nest (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Muirn). **Raiders, not family hunters**: *their crew takes boats that come close to Muirn water, the way the Nest's privateers take theirs* (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`, The Muirn). **But this crew was hired: the Strays paid them to go after Fortune's Fancy** *(Drew, 2026-11-16)*, **through Preet**, the Vessk who guards the Strays' dice table (`characters/preet.md`). **Their orders: bring Caine back, dead or alive** *(Drew, 2026-11-17)*. *The Strays meant to come for Caine on date night over the Den fire; he had already sailed, so they sent the Muirn after the ship* (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, `places/vultures-nest.md`, The Strays, and Lusk). **Not Vaun's family** — *the family's hunt is still to come* (`characters/vaun.md`).

**Twelve of their crew died on that ship**, four of them in the dinghy crash. *They chose surrender when they were trapped between the party below and the Fancy's crew above. That is not the same as choosing the party.*

**What they'll say about home:** *the southern swamps.* **The island's name is not something they give a stranger** (`places/gharial.md`) — *no Guild ship has ever followed one home, and they mean to keep it that way.*

**In the water they are deadly**, and on deck they are ordinary — *+2 to their attacks in or from water, like every Muirn hunter* (`bestiary/muirn-hunter.md`). **Each one is a variation on five stat points**, as Drew ran them.

## The four

**The steersman.** *The one who handled their boat.* **Reads moving water better than anyone aboard the Fancy** — *reed channels, the fast tide, where a keel will catch* (`world/geography-overview.md`, River system). **Skill: Sailing +2.** *Proud, quiet, and the one most bitter about the boat they lost.* — *Mind 2 / Body 2 / Soul 1*

**The diver.** *Went over the side for them — hulls, anchors, cutting nets, coming up under a boat.* **Holds a breath far longer than any land-dweller, and works below the waterline.** **Skill: Swimming +2.** *Practical; treats the whole arrangement as a job until shown otherwise.* — *Mind 1 / Body 3 / Soul 1*

**The old one.** *The eldest of the four, and the one the others defer to.* **Trained in the tribe's small rites** — *the Tithe they keep each season* (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Tithe), *salves out of swamp plants, reading the water for weather.* **Skill: Foraging +2** *(swamp plants)*. *Watches everyone. Says least.* — *Mind 1 / Body 2 / Soul 2*

**The youngest.** *Their first raid.* **Has picked up the most of the Nest's trade-talk**, so they speak for the four. **Skill: Translation +2** *(Muirn and the Nest's trade-talk)*. *Curious about the party, and especially about Code. The likeliest of the four to commit — in whichever direction.* — *Mind 2 / Body 1 / Soul 2*

*The rite, the salves and the weather-reading are hints for the table to define, not effects — the same rule as Kevin's ingredients.*

## GM only

- **They know of the cursegiver family** — *every Muirn does* (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Cursegivers). **They will not talk about it to a stranger**, and **they go quiet if shown Caine's scrap.** *The old one recognises what it is.* **This is Pat's thread** (`campaign/pat.md`) — *what Chris's plans turn up should reach it through Caine, not around him.*
- **The steersman knows the way home.** *Gharial is found by following somebody, not by asking* (`places/gharial.md`). **They are that somebody** — *if he ever chooses to be.*
- **Whether they turn** (`campaign/voyage-to-the-capital.md`, Open). **Barracuda is the obvious moment.** *What the Red Button Society offers them is the strongest reason they have not to — a place, rather than a hold.*
- **They have every reason to hate the ship they're on.** *That fades with how they're treated, not with time.*
