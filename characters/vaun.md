# Vaun

*Muirn, of Gharial. Osha's uncle. Created 2026-10-01.*

**Cards:** `cards/vaun.md`

**He leads the half of the family hunting Osha to kill her** (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The family now).

**He is not cruel. He has done the arithmetic.** The Vessk have said that if their plan fails because of Osha, the whole family dies. One niece against everyone else he has, and a dead Osha can never lift the curse — **she has no daughter, so the line ends with her** (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Cursegivers). *He would give a great deal for the sum to come out differently, and it never does.*

**The Vessk talk to him**, through Draksa (`characters/draksa.md`), and he hates that it is him. Every visit is a reminder of what the family is for, in Vessk eyes.

**At the table: the family's hardest obstacle, and not a villain.** He will not stop, will not be bought, and will explain himself to anybody who asks, because he wants someone to tell him he is wrong and show him the number that makes it so. *A party that wants Osha alive has to give him a better sum than the one he has.*

## In a Fight

**Mind 3 / Body 4 / Soul 3 — HP 22**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 10

**Deck 10 — 4 Red / 3 Blue / 3 Green.** Hand size 3 (Mind). Initiative 1d6 + 3.

**Red (4)** — FROM THE WATER *(signature)*, GRAPPLE, SKEWER, ENDURE

**Blue (3)** — DO THE SUM *(signature)*, PINNED, MARKED

**Green (3)** — FOR THE REST *(signature)*, PATIENCE, INSTINCT

**Trait — Better in the Water:** while Vaun is fighting in or from water, his attacks deal +2 damage. *Lizardkin are far more dangerous submerged than on land* (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`), *and he will not meet anyone on dry ground if he can help it.*

**How he fights.** He hunts, so he holds people in place: GRAPPLE and PINNED stop them leaving, FROM THE WATER opens them up, and DO THE SUM takes the strength out of whoever is hitting back. **FOR THE REST is the family** — every hunter with him gets Resist, because he brought them and he means to bring them home. *He is a fight you win by getting him out of the water, or by giving him a reason to stop.*
