# Black Maggie — Backstory

## Origin

She only ever met three others of her race. Two were her parents.

Her parents had no hope for their people. Not cruelty — absence. They lived day to day because they couldn't see a point to anything further. They didn't teach her the full practice. They didn't teach her much of anything. She grew up alone, mostly in the woods, with time and fragments.

Human children mocked her. The horns. The otherness.

What she had were pieces of her heritage: the beliefs surrounding the Archon, and suffering as something sacred, as the domain of the only thing she could connect to. She didn't have the compassion that was supposed to accompany it — the full practice, the rituals for processing and witnessing another's pain with care. She had the part about suffering mattering. She had nothing else.

As a teenager she hurt herself first. Then animals. Then the boys who had mocked her. Then her parents. All in the same spot in the woods — the place she already knew, the place that was already hers.

She stayed there. She kept going. It took her several years to reach a hundred. She wasn't hurrying. She had nowhere else to be.

When the first anchor point formed she felt the Archon's plane draw closer to hers and understood she had been right about something. That was enough.

## The Thessians

The Thessians were nomadic healers, a small tribe people sought out when suffering was too great to carry alone. They could take on the pain of their patients through touch and ritual. What they did with it afterward was the practice: rituals for processing the absorbed pain and understanding it more fundamentally. They didn't just bear suffering. They witnessed it. They translated it into knowledge of what it meant.

They were not an evil people. The Archon they served was kind and benevolent.

They originated in the Abyssal Ruins region, in the ancient civilization that fell there. By the time other histories noticed them, they were already nomadic. They are a dying race. Fewer than one in a hundred people alive today can claim Thessian blood, and Maggie is one of the last, though not the only one. She has simply never found the others.

The name of her people is in a tongue almost nobody speaks anymore. She may be the only one who still knows it.

## What She Needed

What she actually needed was for her parents to show her a future worth living in. To show her what her people's gift looked like when it was whole. That version of her was real, and it was recent once. It is not gone — it is just very far down.
