# Black Maggie

*One of the last of a dying race. Servant of a forgotten Archon. The only practitioner left of a gift she has entirely inverted.*

---

Black Maggie is a Thessian who inherited only fragments of her people's sacred practice and learned its central truth without the compassion that once gave it meaning. Her people witnessed suffering so it could be held and transformed; Maggie creates suffering so it can be witnessed and used to anchor MoroM, the Archon of Witnessed Suffering.

She is carrying out a ritual that will eventually require seven hundred sacrifices. The work is also a preservation attempt: the Thessian practice is nearly gone, and MoroM is thinning from held reality.

The woman herself remains deliberately unresolved. Whether she grieves what she has become, believes this is the only way to preserve her people and their Archon, or has simply gone beyond caring has not been decided.
