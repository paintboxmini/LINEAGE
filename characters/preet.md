# Preet

*A Vessk Lizardkin in Vulture's Nest (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The Vessk). Met at the table in session three as the guard on the Strays' liar's dice table; named by Drew, 2026-11-17.*

**He guards the Strays' dice table**, and **keeps company with a few other Vessk** in the Nest (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Thieves Den). **Caine questioned him about the cursegiver family; Preet met it with instant hostility and insulted the Shunka queen to his face** (`campaign/session-3-log.md`).

## What he has done

- **He told Lusk about the Shunka asking questions** — *right before the old Thieves Den burned* (`campaign/session-3-log.md`). *He is why the Strays put the fire on Caine.*
- **When word came through the Strays' street eyes that Caine was fleeing the Nest, he sent Muirn mercenaries after him** — *the raiders who chased Fortune's Fancy* (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, The Muirn hunters). **Their orders: bring Caine back, dead or alive.**
- **He is the go-between who made that hire possible.** *A Nest family does not deal with the Muirn — the Nest's privateers raid them* (`factions-and-races/the-pirate-guild.md`, The Muirn). **A Vessk can**, *because the Vessk already hold a knife at the Muirn's throat* (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The family now).

## GM read *(offered, not set)*

**The Strays want Caine for the fire. Preet may want him for something else.** *A Shunka hunting the cursegiver line with a scrap of their cloth is exactly the threat the Vessk keep the family frightened of* (`factions-and-races/races-lizardkin.md`, The family now). **Doing the Strays' errand and stopping that search are the same job** — *and nobody in the Strays needs to know the second reason.*

## What comes next

- **The hire failed.** *No raiders came back, and four of them crew the ship they were sent after* (`characters/the-four-muirn.md`). **Preet will hear it** — *the four know who hired them, and the Strays' eyes are everywhere.*
- **The Strays and Lusk now owe Caine a debt they meant to collect in blood** — *and Code is Lusk's customer* (`campaign/chris.md`).
