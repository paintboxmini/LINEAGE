# Thess — GM Notes

*What the party cannot learn by talking to him.*

---

**What he believes, and has never said to anyone:** that the cost of reaching for a Seat and failing didn't get paid at the moment of failure — it's still accruing, the way any unpaid thing accrues, and every small penance is interest, not principal. He doesn't expect to ever pay down the principal. He has made a kind of peace with that, the way someone makes peace with weather.
