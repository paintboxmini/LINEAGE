# Steve (and Pip)

*Wren's son. Young adult. Easy to underestimate, easy to lose track of, and genuinely curious about what waits beneath the Promise.*

---

Steve is forgetful in the ordinary, inconvenient sense: attention slips, memory drops threads, and he can lose track of why he is standing somewhere or what he was about to agree to. It is the part of him people mistake for being unremarkable. He is not empty; he simply does not always keep hold of the thread long enough for other people to notice what is there.

He is Wren's son. For years Wren has delayed his first ordinary bath, using the language of care and readiness. Steve has grown curious about the Final Current in the space around that delay and has spent time at its edges, without yet having refused Kaine or committed himself to the descent.

Early in the Tide Pulls Back sequence, during the Tide Market stretch with Mirel, Steve makes a clumsy attempt to ask her out. It does not land cleanly. He loses the thread of the ask, realizes how foolish he looks, and pivots into a joke at his own expense. Mirel laughs for real. Something is developing between them; neither has named it.

Later, in front of part of the congregation and the party, Steve tells Wren that he intends to join a Final Current descent. That is where the long delay becomes public. His eventual refusal happens later, on the group platform night, and belongs to the players' action in that moment.

His ordinary bath and the Pip arc remain downstream. They are not collapsed into the Kaine sequence.
