# Steve And Pip — Gm Notes

---

## Tide Pulls Back — Steve's Position

Steve is Wren's son and a young adult. He is forgetful in a way that should be visible at the table: attention slips, memory drops threads, and he can lose track of why he is standing somewhere or what he was about to agree to. Do not play him as merely bland or forgettable. The forgetfulness is the load-bearing trait.

He has been curious about the Final Current and has spent time around its edges. At the start of Tide Pulls Back he has **not** refused Kaine. Do not play the refusal early.

During the Tide Market stretch with Mirel, the party sees Steve make a clumsy attempt to ask her out. The attempt does not land cleanly. He loses the thread, realizes it, and turns the failure into self-deprecating humor. Mirel laughs for real. Something is developing between them; neither has named it.

Later, Steve tells Wren in front of part of the congregation, with the party present, that he intends to join a Final Current descent. This is the public moment when the long delay around his ordinary bath is named. Wren is not the formal leader of the congregation and is not part of the Final Current. The household remains intact; Wren's resistance is protective, not a break in the family.

The later refusal happens on the group platform night, while Kaine is conducting the descent. It belongs to the live player action in that moment. Steve's earlier curiosity and public declaration should make the refusal meaningful without making it predetermined.

Steve's ordinary bath remains downstream of this sequence. Do not collapse the bath or Pip arc into Kaine's sequence.

## The Pip Arc

Pulleys fail → Steve is down too long → Pip forms on the way up → Steve becomes an accidental Messiah → the People of Promise gain an influx of followers drawn by something they can feel but not name → Pip finishes popping → the pull evaporates → everyone who upended their life for Steve is still there, looking at Steve, who is still just Steve.

**The party's choice:** intervene or don't.

- **Intervene** — warn people, try to separate Steve from the crowd, try to understand what Pip actually is. The fallout changes shape. It doesn't disappear.
- **Do nothing** — Pip runs his week. The pull fades. People who joined the Promise community because of Steve's draw are left with real questions about what happened to them. Social fallout within the community. **Any leftover Final Current interest shows up here and nowhere earlier** — Kaine's remnant may try to exploit it once Pip is already real and drawing a crowd, never before.

The ripples are emergent. They depend on what the party adds. Don't pre-write the resolution.
