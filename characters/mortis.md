# Mortis

Bartender, ex-adventurer. Underground Tavern.

A necromancer. Precise. Comfortable with death magic in a way most people never get to be, because most people never had a reason to get that good at it.

He and Giblets were partners for years, before either of them settled in the capital — adventuring work, the kind that gets people killed. Giblets died for it more than once, always buying Mortis time or covering his back, never asked to and never once regretting it. Mortis brought him back every time. He never left him behind.

They retired together. Mortis took the tavern. Giblets took the stall. Different corners of the same bazaar, still each other's closest thing to family.

He went into the mine once since. To pull Kess out who had gone in after her mother.

He pours Lily a drink without being asked every time she comes in. He does the same for Kess. Professional courtesy for people who have lost someone.
