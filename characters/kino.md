# Kino

*Every circle he draws is an answer to a question you haven't asked yet.*

**Cards:** `cards/kino.md`

*This is Kino grown — the young man he becomes after he leaves Vulture's Nest, not the fifteen-year-old running the Red Scarves on the docks (`locations/vultures-nest.md`). That version stays exactly as written; this is who he is once the docks are behind him. Stat block and deck are a starting point. Scale to match where he enters the story. Adjust Mind, HP, and card count before use.*

**Mind 5 / Body 3 / Soul 4 — HP 15**
**Creature Threat Rating:** 12

---

## Appearance

Older than the docks remember him — not by decades, just enough. Dress cleaner, blade real instead of borrowed. The circles he draws hang in the air a half-second after his hand moves, thin lines of light in whatever color he's called on, gone the instant they've done their work. He still ties a scarf the same proper way. Red, same as always.

---

## Behavior

Doesn't rush an opening — waits for the shape of one to become obvious, then takes it without hesitating twice. Tells you exactly what a circle will stop, in the color he draws it, and lets you attack it anyway if you want to find out for yourself. That confidence isn't new. Vulture's Nest just called it something else back when the stakes were bags of coin instead of blades.

Watches more than he explains. Rarely steps in ahead of what a moment actually calls for. Goes by Kino now, plainly — the alias belonged to a kid running docks, and he left both behind at the same time.

---

## Combat Identity

A fencer who wins the fight before it reaches him. EMBER CIRCLE, MIRROR CIRCLE, and THORN CIRCLE each answer one color completely — the first Red, Blue, or Green attack against an active circle simply fails, no card spent defending it — and each one also leaves a standing Resist, Ward, or Evade behind once it's up. Takes a full turn to draw each one, so the wall doesn't exist until he's spent real time building it, one color at a time, in full view of anyone paying attention.

Everyone can see exactly which colors are covered. That's the point — he's not hiding the trap, he's daring you to walk into it anyway.
