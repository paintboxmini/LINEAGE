# Vesper Thorn — Profile

---

Vesper has descended often enough that the change is visible. Stone patches have spread along the jaw, and one eye has gone animal in a way that no longer surprises anyone who knows the Final Current. The marks are not hidden. Vesper carries them as evidence rather than shame.

They believe the ordinary Promise is too slow. The bath changes a person, but the ordinary rhythm asks them to wait for what could be reached now. Repeated descent is not excess to Vesper. It is mercy: a willingness to let people become what the water is already trying to make them.

Vesper speaks for Kaine in smaller gatherings. The tone is calm, certain, and difficult to bait. They do not need to make ordinary Promise people look foolish in order to believe the Current is right. They can understand why someone is afraid and still believe fear is the thing the descent is meant to pass through.

On the night Kaine runs the group platform descent, Vesper is present and supportive. Steve is one of the people on the platform. Vesper does not need to be the loudest voice in the scene. Their certainty is strongest when it does not require volume.
