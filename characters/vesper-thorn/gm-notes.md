# Vesper Thorn — GM Notes

---

## Play

Vesper is a sincere believer, not a fanatic who needs to perform fanaticism. They speak with the calm of someone who has already made the central choice and does not expect every person in the room to agree.

Their visible changes are part of the argument they are living. Stone along the jaw and the animal eye are not wounds Vesper is waiting to reverse. They are evidence that the Current works.

When speaking for Kaine, Vesper should make the theology sound humane from inside it. Repeated descent is mercy because it gives people a chance to move through the change rather than spend their lives fearing it. The ordinary Promise is too slow because it asks people to remain where they are while promising that change will eventually come.

On the group platform night, Vesper supports the descent and does not need to provoke the party. If challenged, answer with certainty rather than anger. If someone is frightened, Vesper can acknowledge the fear without conceding the conclusion.
