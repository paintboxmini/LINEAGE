# Hess And Cob — Hess

The oldest living founder. Slight, slow-moving, still precise in small ways — the way she sets down a cup, the way she corrects a word in conversation. Was formidable once. Published work. Led the colony's early years with real conviction.

At some point she arrived at the answer. She understood what it meant. She did not say it.

The answer: they dropped the original goal of creating an equal, and replaced it with domestication.

She has been watching from her window ever since — the Old One's House, at the edge of the village (`places/canille.md`, Key Locations). She has a name for what the cubs actually are, the real answer to the original question, and she hasn't said it out loud in thirty years.

She avoids Cob. If asked why, she'd say she finds the young ones tiring. This is not true.

**If the party speaks with her:** warm, interested, asks good questions about where they've been. She will not bring up the cubs first. If the party does, she listens carefully and says very little. She does not lie. She also does not say the thing.
