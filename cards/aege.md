# Aege — Cards

Signature cards for Aege, the Carrion Guide. See `characters/aege.md`.

**These cards are unique to Aege. They cannot be awarded to players or transferred to other guides.**

---

**WATCHES FEET**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: If Defender is in Backline, apply Weak.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1.
Range: Both
*"She already knew which way you'd break."*

---

**KNOWN GROUND**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: You may change position. If you do, target ally may also change position.
Defensive Bonus: Change position. Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"There's always a way through, if you know to look for it."*

---

**WHERE IT'S GATHERING**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Scry 2 of Defender's deck.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1. If your HP is 6 or less, also gain Ward.
Range: Both
*"She already knows which way this goes. She never says so."*
