# Neshi — Cards

Signature cards for Neshi, Osha's cousin, who wants to bring her home. See `characters/neshi.md`.

**These cards are unique to Neshi.**

---

**WITHOUT LOOKING**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Deal +2 damage this attack. Gain Vulnerable.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Melee
*"She has never once checked how deep the water was before she went in."*

---

**OLD HIDING PLACES**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Gain Quick.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"Every place Osha ever hid, Neshi was the one who found her."*

---

**COME HOME**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: Target ally heals 4 HP.
Defense Effect: Target ally gains Evade.
Range: Both
*"Said the same way she has said it since they were children. It has always worked."*
