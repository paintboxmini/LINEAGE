# Duskwick — Signature Cards

---

**SNUFF**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: If this attack deals damage, target gains Blind until the end of their next turn.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Melee
*"The wick knows the difference between light and looking."*

---

**HALF-SEEN**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: This reveal doesn't count. For the rest of combat, effects that check your last-played color skip it.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Obscure until the start of your next turn.
Range: Both
*"You're not sure you saw it at all."*

---

**GUTTERING**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: If you are below half HP, this attack gains Deadly.
Defensive Bonus: If you are below half HP, gain Resist.
Range: Both
*"Brightest, right before the dark."*
