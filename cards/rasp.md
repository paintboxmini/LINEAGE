# Rasp — Cards

Signature cards for Rasp. See `characters/rasp.md`.

**These cards are unique to Rasp. They cannot be awarded to players or transferred to other poisoners.**

---

**OUT OF REACH**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Move to the Backline. Anchored — while the Passive is active, draw 1 at the start of each of your turns.
Defense Effect: Move to the Backline. Gain Evade.
Special Rule: Passive — while you are in the Backline, enemies cannot target you with attacks. Moving there with this card does not break it. Attacking, or moving again, does.
Range: Both
*"You were never actually aiming at her."*

---

**QUIET POISON**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Target takes 1 damage at the start of each of their turns until the end of combat.
Defense Effect: Attacker takes 1 damage at the start of each of their turns until the end of combat.
Range: Melee
*"Small enough to ignore. That's the point."*

---

**SEPTIC STRIKE**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: If the target is currently taking damage from QUIET POISON, deal +6 damage and end that effect immediately.
Defense Effect: Deal 2 damage to attacker.
Range: Melee
*"Once in a while, everything she planted comes due at once."*
