# Orin Vane — Signature Cards

Signature cards for Orin Vane, mage-sword fencer. Ward Glyphs and Mirror Glyphs are visually identical in the fiction — players cannot distinguish them until the Special Rule resolves. See `bestiary/orin-vane.md` for GM notes.

---

## Ward Glyphs

*When defending: if the attacker played the matching color, the attack is fully negated. No damage, no effects, no RPS outcome applies. Special Rule takes precedence over all tie resolution.*

---

**CRIMSON WARD**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Special Rule: Ward Glyph — When defending: if attacker played a Red card, this attack is fully negated.
Effect: Draw 1 card.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker discards 1 card at random.
Range: Both
*"The color names itself. The glyph listens."*

---

**AZURE WARD**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Special Rule: Ward Glyph — When defending: if attacker played a Blue card, this attack is fully negated.
Effect: Draw 1 card.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 2.
Range: Both
*"Ink knows ink."*

---

**VERDANT WARD**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Special Rule: Ward Glyph — When defending: if attacker played a Green card, this attack is fully negated.
Effect: Draw 1 card.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Resist.
Range: Both
*"The living glyph holds."*

---

## Mirror Glyphs

*Visually identical to Ward Glyphs. When defending: if the attacker played the matching color, their card's Effect and Defensive Bonus are negated. Damage resolves normally.*

---

**CRIMSON MIRROR**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Special Rule: False Glyph — When defending: if attacker played a Red card, negate their card's Effect and Defensive Bonus. Damage resolves normally.
Effect: Gain Evade.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Blind.
Range: Both
*"The shape is the same. The intent is not."*

---

**AZURE MIRROR**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Special Rule: False Glyph — When defending: if attacker played a Blue card, negate their card's Effect and Defensive Bonus. Damage resolves normally.
Effect: Scry 2.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker discards 1 card at random.
Range: Both
*"You read the glyph. You read it wrong."*

---

**VERDANT MIRROR**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Special Rule: False Glyph — When defending: if attacker played a Green card, negate their card's Effect and Defensive Bonus. Damage resolves normally.
Effect: All allies gain +1 to their next attack.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"Patience is also a shape."*

## Sword Cards

---

**LUNGE**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Move to Frontline. If target is Backline, deal +2 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Move to Backline.
Range: Melee
*"The distance closes whether you're ready or not."*

---

**SIGNATURE THRUST**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Special Rule: Can only be played if a Ward or Mirror Glyph has triggered this combat.
Effect: This attack cannot be negated by any Glyph effect.
Defensive Bonus: Counter Attack.
Range: Melee
*"The glyph was never the point."*

---

## Fencer Cards

---

**STEP ASIDE**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Move to any position. Attacker must attack you next round if able.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"He's not retreating. He's choosing the next angle."*

---

**FEINT**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Defender must reveal their hand.
Defensive Bonus: Before the next RPS resolution, the attacker must reveal their chosen card. You may then swap your chosen card with any card in your hand.
Range: Melee
*"The sword goes where the eyes don't."*

---

**INK THE AIR**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Scry 3. You may place any Ward or Mirror Glyph cards found on top of your deck in any order.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1.
Range: Both
*"One motion becomes two."*

---

**SURVEY**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Scry 2 of target's deck. If both cards share a color, deal +3 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1 of any deck.
Range: Ranged
*"The map was never about the territory."*
