# Orin Vane — Signature Cards

Signature cards for Orin Vane, mage-sword fencer. Attack Counter Glyphs and Defense Counter Glyphs are visually identical in the fiction. See `characters/orin-vane.md` for GM notes.

---

## Attack Counter Glyphs

*Effect sets a persistent color trap. The next time an enemy plays the matching color against Orin, that attack is negated before RPS resolves — no cards revealed, no damage, no effects. Expires on trigger.*

---

**CRIMSON WARD**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d2
Effect: Set a Red Attack Counter. Until triggered, the next Red card played against you is negated before RPS resolves.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker discards 1 card at random.
Range: Ranged
*"The color names itself. The glyph listens."*

---

**AZURE WARD**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Set a Blue Attack Counter. Until triggered, the next Blue card played against you is negated before RPS resolves.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 2.
Range: Ranged
*"Ink knows ink."*

---

**VERDANT WARD**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d2
Effect: Set a Green Attack Counter. Until triggered, the next Green card played against you is negated before RPS resolves.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Resist.
Range: Ranged
*"The living glyph holds."*

---

## Defense Counter Glyphs

*Visually identical to Attack Counter Glyphs. Defensive Bonus sets a persistent color trap. The next time an enemy defends with the matching color, that defense is negated before RPS resolves — no cards revealed, no effects. Expires on trigger.*

---

**CRIMSON MIRROR**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Gain Evade.
Defensive Bonus: Set a Red Defense Counter. Until triggered, the next Red card used as a defense against you is negated before RPS resolves.
Range: Ranged
*"The shape is the same. The intent is not."*

---

**AZURE MIRROR**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Scry 2.
Defensive Bonus: Set a Blue Defense Counter. Until triggered, the next Blue card used as a defense against you is negated before RPS resolves.
Range: Ranged
*"You read the glyph. You read it wrong."*

---

**VERDANT MIRROR**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: All allies gain +1 to their next attack.
Defensive Bonus: Set a Green Defense Counter. Until triggered, the next Green card used as a defense against you is negated before RPS resolves.
Range: Ranged
*"Patience is also a shape."*

---

## Sword Cards

---

**LUNGE**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Move to Frontline. If target is Backline, deal +2 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Move to Backline.
Range: Melee
*"The distance closes whether you're ready or not."*

---

**SIGNATURE THRUST**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d10
Special Rule: Can only be played if at least 1 glyph of each color is currently active.
Effect: Remove 2 active glyphs of your choice.
Defensive Bonus: Set 2 random Glyphs.
Range: Melee
*"The glyph was never the point."*

## Fencer Cards

---

**STEP ASIDE**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Move to any position. Attacker must attack you on their next turn if able.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"He's not retreating. He's choosing the next angle."*

---

**FEINT**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Defender must reveal their hand.
Defensive Bonus: Apply Predictable to the attacker.
Range: Melee
*"The sword goes where the eyes don't."*

---

**INK THE AIR**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Scry 3. You may place any Ward or Mirror Glyph cards found on top of your deck in any order.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1.
Range: Ranged
*"One motion becomes two."*

---

**SURVEY**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Scry 2 of target's deck. If both cards share a color, deal +3 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1 of any deck.
Range: Ranged
*"The map was never about the territory."*
