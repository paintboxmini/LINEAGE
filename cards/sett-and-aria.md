# Sett and Aria — Cards

Signature cards for Sett and Aria, married road surveyors of the Cartographers Guild. See `characters/sett-and-aria.md`.

**These cards are unique to the two of them.** Three each, one per colour.

---

**PLANT THE POLE**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Defender gains Rooted.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"The pole goes in where he says the road goes. So does everything else."*

---

**BIG TALK**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Defender gains Weak.
Defense Effect: Gain Deadly.
Range: Ranged
*"Half of it is true. He will not tell you which half, because he does not know."*

---

**WALK IT FIRST**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: Gain Quick.
Defense Effect: Target ally gains Evade.
Range: Both
*"Whatever is on this road, it meets him before it meets her."*

---

**FLASH PLATE**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Defender gains Blind.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Ranged
*"A light-map plate holds a picture. Tilted right, it holds the sun."*

---

**SMALLER TARGET**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Gain Evade.
Defense Effect: You may change position.
Range: Both
*"Nobody aims at the little one first. That is their mistake, and she has stopped being sorry about it."*

---

**KEEP WALKING**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: You and all allies heal 2.
Defense Effect: Gain Ward.
Range: Both
*"Said lightly, and meant all the way down."*
