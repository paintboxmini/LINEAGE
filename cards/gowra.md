# Gowra — Cards

Signature cards for the Gowra. See `bestiary/gowra.md`.

**These cards are unique to Gowras. They cannot be awarded to players.**

---

**THE ROAR**
BLUE — MIND
*Playable only from an enclosed position — a chamber, a collapsed bank, a cave, under a deck. On open ground this card is not in the deck.*
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Every enemy in this position gains Staggered.
Defense Effect: The attacker gains Staggered.
Range: Both
*"It is not the volume. It is that the sound has nowhere to go and neither do you."*

---

**UNDER THE BANK**
RED — BODY
*The opening move, and it only ever happens once per fight.*
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: The defender is taken. They and the Gowra both leave the current position and arrive together in its den — a dry chamber under the bank, a few seconds away, reachable on foot by anyone willing to go in after them. The defender keeps everything they are carrying and acts normally from there.
Defense Effect: The attacker is pulled halfway in with you and moves to Frontline.
Range: Melee
*"It did not bite him. It took hold and it went home, and the water closed, and that was the entire event."*

---

**TAIL BLADE**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Also deal Body damage to one other enemy in this position.
Defense Effect: Deal 4 damage to the attacker, unpreventable.
Range: Melee
*"Four feet of it came round behind him while he was watching the teeth."*

---

**TUSKWORK**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d10
Effect: If this attack wins, the ground here gives way — every combatant in this position moves to Frontline and cannot leave it until the end of their next turn.
Defense Effect: Gain Armour 2 for the rest of combat.
Range: Melee
*"The same tools it digs with. It has never thought of them as two different jobs."*
