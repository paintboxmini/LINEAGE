# Redjaw — Signature Cards

Signature cards for the redjaws of Elowen. See `bestiary/redjaw.md`. Tagged ELOWEN.

**One card per colour, and these three are the whole blank deck.** *The pack works as one animal: one drags a person down, and the next bite lands on somebody who cannot block it.*

---

**DRAG IT DOWN**
RED — BODY — ELOWEN
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Defender gains Staggered.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"It does not need to kill you. It needs you on the ground for the next one."*

---

**EYES IN THE TREES**
BLUE — MIND — ELOWEN
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Defender gains Vulnerable.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Ranged
*"You count four. There were always more than four."*

---

**CIRCLE THE CAMP**
GREEN — SOUL — ELOWEN
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: You may change position.
Defense Effect: Gain Quick.
Range: Both
*"They never come straight in. They go round, and round, and wait for the fire to get low."*
