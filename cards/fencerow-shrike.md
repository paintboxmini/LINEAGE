# Fencerow Shrike — Signature Cards

---

**IMPALING DIVE**
RED — BODY — BRIARWOODS
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Move to the Frontline. If you began this turn in the Backline, this attack deals +2 damage. You cannot defend until your next turn.
Defensive Bonus: Move to the Backline.
Range: Both
*"Not cruelty. Storage."*

---

**THORN LARDER**
BLUE — MIND — BRIARWOODS
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Exile 1 card from the defender's hand — impaled on the fence, out of the fight until the larder gives it back.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Melee
*"It keeps what it can't finish."*

---

**WATCHFUL PERCH**
GREEN — SOUL — BRIARWOODS
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Move to the Backline and gain Evade.
Defensive Bonus: Apply Initiative Shift +2 to yourself.
Range: Both
*"Still is not the same as gone."*
