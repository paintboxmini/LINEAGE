# The Bone Collector — Signature Cards

Signature cards for the Bone Collector. See `bestiary/bone-collector.md`. Tagged as WEALD — obtainable via the Oracle deck after encounters in Turnroot Weald.

---

**SILK THREAD MEASURE**
BLUE — MIND — WEALD
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Reveal Hand. Choose 1 revealed card; the defender discards it.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 2.
Range: Ranged
*"It measured the merchant twice. Once before, once after."*

---

**PRECISE REMOVAL**
RED — BODY — WEALD
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Exile 1 card of your choice from the defender's discard pile.
Defensive Bonus: Exile the attacker's card instead of sending it to the discard.
Range: Melee
*"The cuts were made after. That is the part that should worry you."*

---

**STITCHED CASE**
GREEN — SOUL — WEALD
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Gain Armour 1.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Thorns 1.
Range: Both
*"Everything on it belonged to someone who also thought they were leaving."*
