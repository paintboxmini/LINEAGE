# Vescal — Signature Cards

Signature cards for Vescal, censer-bearer of Trutinate. Frontline anchor and support. See `characters/vescal.md` for GM notes.

---

## Signature Move

---

**CENSURE**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: Attack all Frontline targets simultaneously. All targets hit gain Rooted 2 — they cannot change position for 2 turns.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Rooted 2.
Range: Melee
*"Be still. This has already been decided."*

---

## Support Cards

---

**SANCTUARY**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d2
Effect: Anchored — At the start of your turn, all allies heal 3 HP.
Defensive Bonus: Target ally heals 3 HP.
Range: Melee
*"Hold the ground. Let the smoke do the rest."*

---

**RESIST BLESSING**
GREEN — SOUL

GREEN — SOUL

## Control Cards

---

**SMOKE SCREEN**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Target enemy moves to Backline. Apply Blind to all Frontline enemies.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Blind.
Range: Melee
*"Visibility is a luxury."*

---

**BINDING RITE**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Target enemy gains Rooted. If they are already Rooted, shuffle 1 Wound into their deck.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Rooted.
Range: Melee
*"The temple still has jurisdiction here."*
