# Muirn Hunter — Signature Cards

Signature cards for the Muirn hunters who ride with Vaun. See `bestiary/muirn-hunter.md`. Tagged GHARIAL.

---

**INTO THE MUD**
RED — BODY — GHARIAL
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Defender gains Rooted.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"On land they are slow. Nobody fighting them is on land for long."*

---

**CAST THE NET**
BLUE — MIND — GHARIAL
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Apply Initiative Shift -1 to the defender.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Ranged
*"Not to catch you. To slow you down until someone else does."*

---

**CLOSE THE RING**
GREEN — SOUL — GHARIAL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: All allies in your position gain Deadly.
Defense Effect: You may change position.
Range: Both
*"Family hunts together. It is the only way they have ever hunted."*
