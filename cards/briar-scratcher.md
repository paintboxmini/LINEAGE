# Briar Scratcher — Cards

Signature cards for the Briar Scratcher. See `bestiary/briar-scratcher.md`.

---

**RAKING CUT**
RED — BODY — BRIARWATCH
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: On hit, add 1 Injury to the bottom of the defender's deck.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker adds 1 Injury to their own discard.
Range: Melee
*"The claws are small. The Injury is not."*

---

**SKITTER AWAY**
BLUE — MIND — BRIARWATCH
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: After attack, may reposition.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"Gone before the counter."*

---

**NIP AND TEAR**
GREEN — SOUL — BRIARWATCH
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: If target has an Injury in their deck, deal +2 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Heal 1.
Range: Melee
*"The cut remembers the first one."*
