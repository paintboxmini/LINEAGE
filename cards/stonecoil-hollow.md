# Stonecoil Signature Cards — Hollow

Signature cards for the Stonecoil. Tagged as HOLLOW — obtainable via the Oracle deck after encounters in the Hollow Below Briarwatch.

---

**COIL STRIKE**
RED — BODY — HOLLOW
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: If target is in the same position as you, deal +2 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Rooted.
Range: Melee
*"In the narrow dark, distance is a luxury."*

---

**PRESS THE COIL**
RED — BODY — HOLLOW
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: If this attack wins the reveal, apply Rooted to target.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"It doesn't chase. It waits for you to run out of stone."*

---

**COIL DRAG**
RED — BODY — HOLLOW
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Pull target to Frontline. If target is already in Frontline, deal +2 damage instead.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Rooted.
Range: Both
*"The grip comes before the bite."*

---

**VIBRATION LOCK**
BLUE — MIND — HOLLOW
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Scry 1 of target's deck. If the card matches the color they just played, apply Blind until start of their next turn.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1 of attacker's deck.
Range: Both
*"It has never seen anything. It doesn't need to."*

---

**STILL WATER**
BLUE — MIND — HOLLOW
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: If target changed positions last round, deal +3 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"The Borrowers learned this first: do not run."*

---

**READ THE STRIKE**
BLUE — MIND — HOLLOW
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: If target attacked this creature last round, deal +2 damage and Scry 1.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Ward.
Range: Both
*"The vibration of your last swing is still in the stone."*

---

**COIL PATIENCE**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Anchored — at the start of your next turn, heal 2 HP.
Defensive Bonus: Apply Rooted to attacker.
Range: Melee
*"It has been in this tunnel longer than the tunnel has had a name."*

---

**SHED SKIN**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d2
Effect: Exile 1 Wound from your deck. Gain Evade.
Defensive Bonus: Exile 1 card from your discard pile.
Range: Both
*"What the stone leaves behind, the coil does not miss."*

---

**DARK CORRIDOR**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Apply Blind to target until start of your next turn.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"There is no light here. That is not an accident."*
