# Stonecoil Signature Cards — Hollow

Signature cards for the Stonecoil. Tagged as HOLLOW — obtainable via the Oracle deck after encounters in the Hollow Below Briarwatch.

---

**DRAG**
RED — BODY — HOLLOW
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Pull defender to Frontline. If defender is already in Frontline, deal +2 damage instead.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Rooted.
Range: Both
*"The grip comes before the bite."*

---

**VIBRATION LOCK**
BLUE — MIND — HOLLOW
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Scry 1 of target's deck. If the card matches the color they just played, apply Blind.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1 of your own deck.
Range: Both
*"It has never seen anything. It doesn't need to."*

---

**SHED SKIN**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d2
Effect: Exile 1 Wound from your deck. Gain Evade.
Defensive Bonus: Exile 1 card from your discard pile.
Range: Both
*"What the stone leaves behind, the coil does not miss."*

---

**DARK CORRIDOR**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Apply Blind to defender.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"There is no light here. That is not an accident."*

---

**COIL LATCH**
RED — BODY — HOLLOW
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: If the target moved this turn, deal +2 damage.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Rooted.
Range: Melee
*"The unyielding clasp of stone restraint."*

---

**STILL GROUND**
BLUE — MIND — HOLLOW
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: If the target changed positions last round, deal +1 damage and Scry 1.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1 of your own deck.
Range: Both
*"Tethered thoughts in a shifting world."*

---

**PATIENCE OF STONE**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Anchored — At the start of each of your turns, heal 2 HP.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Advantage.
Range: Melee
*"The unshaken abide, while chaos churns."*
