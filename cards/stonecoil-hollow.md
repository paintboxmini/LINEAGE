# Stonecoil Signature Cards — Hollow

Signature cards for the Stonecoil. Tagged as HOLLOW — obtainable via the Oracle deck after encounters in the Hollow Below Briarwatch.

---

**DRAG**
RED — BODY — HOLLOW
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Pull defender to Frontline. If defender is already in Frontline, deal +2 damage instead.
Defensive Bonus: attacker gains Rooted.
Range: Both
*"The grip comes before the bite."*

---

**VIBRATION LOCK**
BLUE — MIND — HOLLOW
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Scry 1 of target's deck. If the card matches the color they just played, apply Blind 1.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1 of attacker's deck.
Range: Both
*"It has never seen anything. It doesn't need to."*

---

**SHED SKIN**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d2
Effect: Exile 1 Wound from your deck. Gain Evade.
Defensive Bonus: Exile 1 card from your discard pile.
Range: Both
*"What the stone leaves behind, the coil does not miss."*

---

**DARK CORRIDOR**
GREEN — SOUL — HOLLOW
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Apply Blind 1 to defender.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Resist 1.
Range: Melee
*"There is no light here. That is not an accident."*

🔴 COIL LATCH
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: If the target moved this turn, +2 damage
Defensive Bonus: The attacker gains Rooted 1
Range: Melee

🔵 STILL GROUND
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: If the target changed positions last round, +1 damage and Scry 1
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1 of the attacker’s deck
Range: Both

🟢 PATIENCE OF STONE
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Anchored — At the start of your next turn, heal 2 HP
Defensive Bonus: Gain Advantage on your next damage roll (roll twice, take the higher result)
Range: Melee
