# Harlock — Cards

Signature cards for Harlock, captain of the Arcadia. See `characters/harlock.md`.

**These cards are unique to Harlock. They cannot be awarded to players or transferred to other captains.**

---

**BOARDING ACTION**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: You and the defender both move to Frontline. Gain Thorns 2 for the rest of combat.
Defense Effect: The attacker moves to Frontline with you.
Range: Both
*"He does not come aboard to fight you. He comes aboard because it is his ship now."*

---

**THE PRICE**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Name the cost. The defender chooses: discard a card of your choosing from their hand, or take 4 unpreventable damage.
Defense Effect: The attacker chooses: their card is discarded with no Effect, or they take 3 unpreventable damage.
Range: Both
*"Either one settles it. I don't care which."*

---

**DEAD RECKONING**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Scry 2 on any one deck in the combat. Gain Quick.
Defense Effect: Scry 1 on any one deck.
Range: Both
*"No stars, no bank, no bottom. He brought her in anyway."*

---

**CUT THE LINE**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Exile one Wound, Exhaust, or status card from your hand or discard pile. Gain Evade.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade. If you are at Frontline, you may move to Backline for free.
Range: Both
*"Whatever it was, it is in the water now."*
