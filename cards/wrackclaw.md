# Wrackclaw — Cards

Signature cards for the Wrackclaw. See `bestiary/wrackclaw.md`.

**All three used to grant Evade on defence. Split 2026-09-22**, because a spined thing that clamps on and hauls is armoured rather than quick, and because three identical defences gave the creature no defensive decision and the party nothing to read. **Evade stayed on the one card that is literally about moving sideways.**

**Changed at the table, 2026-10-18** *(Drew)*: **SIDELONG SCUTTLE is Green, Both range, and moves it**; **CARRION PULL is Blue and Ranged — the pull is a sticky web it spits** (`bestiary/wrackclaw.md`, The web). *That is how a crab hits at range at all.*

---

**PINCH**
RED — BODY — SHORELINE
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Defender gains Rooted.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"It doesn't need to be fast. You're not going anywhere it can't follow."*

---

**SIDELONG SCUTTLE**
GREEN — SOUL — SHORELINE
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: You may change position.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"It feels you coming through the sand before it sees you."*

---

**CARRION PULL**
BLUE — MIND — SHORELINE
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: The second time this card succeeds against the same target, that
target goes into the water. Counted per target, for the whole fight.
Defense Effect: Gain Thorns 1.
Range: Ranged
*"Everything on this bank gets dragged back to the water eventually."*

*Was "Move target to backline" until 2026-09-22. The drag toward the water is real and it is already covered: the Frontline is wherever the fighting is and it moves when the fighting does (`rules/combat.md`, Positioning), so being hauled seaward while both of you stay Frontline is exactly what the rule describes. **Nothing on the table changes and nothing should.** What the card needed was a count, not a position — see `bestiary/wrackclaw.md`, Going into the water.*
