# Root Heart Signature Cards — Weald

Signature cards derived from the Root Heart's abilities. Tagged as WEALD — obtainable via the Oracle deck after the Root Heart encounter or at DM discretion.

---

**ROOT LASH**
RED — BODY — WEALD
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Pull target to the Frontline.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Rooted.
Range: Melee
*"The roots remember every path you walked. They reach before you decide."*

---

**FOREST MEMORY**
BLUE — MIND — WEALD
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Name a card color. If the target plays that color next round, heal 2 HP.
Defensive Bonus: Return 1 card from your discard pile to the top of your deck.
Range: Both
*"The Weald does not forget. It catalogues."*
