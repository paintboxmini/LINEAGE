# Blowback Beetle Signature Cards

Signature cards for the Blowback Beetle. See `bestiary/blowback-beetle.md`.

---

**BLOWBACK**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Push every enemy in the defender's position to the Backline. Deal 2 unpreventable damage to each.
Defense Effect: Push the attacker to the Backline.
Range: Ranged
*"A long drag of air, a beat of nothing, and then you are somewhere else."*

---

**GULP**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Gain Resist 2. Your next successful attack this combat deals +4 damage.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist 2.
Range: Both
*"Everyone hears it. That is not a design flaw, it is just how a bellows fills."*

---

**BRACE THE LEGS**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Anchored — your attacks deal +2 damage.
Defense Effect: You cannot be moved until your next turn.
Range: Melee
*"Whatever it does to you, it would do to itself. So it sets its feet."*
