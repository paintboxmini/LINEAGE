# Rootstalker Signature Cards — Weald

Signature cards for the Rootstalker. Tagged as WEALD — these cards represent the predator's symbiosis with the forest and may be made available to players who align with or harvest from the Weald.

---

**LIMB-SNAPPER**
RED — BODY — WEALD
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: If the target is in the Backline, deal +2 damage and pull them to the Frontline.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Thorns 2.
Range: Both
*"The stalker's reach is longer than the path you thought you took."*

---

**CAMOUFLAGE STRIKE**
BLUE — MIND — WEALD
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: If you are in the Backline, gain Evade.
Defensive Bonus: Move to the Backline; Scry 1.
Range: Ranged
*"It isn't that the creature hides; it's that the forest wants it seen too late."*

---

**THORN-BIND**
GREEN — SOUL — WEALD
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Defender gains Weak.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Rooted.
Range: Both
*"The Weald does not block your exit; it simply makes the price of movement too high."*

---

**SPLINTER-BURST**
RED — BODY — WEALD
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Pay 2 HP; deal half the total attack damage (rounded up) to another Frontline enemy.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Thorns 2.
Range: Melee
*"To strike the stalker is to invite its sharpest parts into your skin."*
