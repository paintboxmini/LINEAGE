# Wall-Reader Cards — The Coil

Signature cards for the Wall-Reader. Tagged as COIL — obtainable via the Oracle deck after encounters in The Coil.

---

**PRESS**
RED — BODY — COIL
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: If target is Frontline, deal +2 damage and apply Rooted.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Staggered.
Range: Melee
*"The wall does not move. Neither does it."*

---

**GRASPING REACH**
RED — BODY — COIL
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Pull target to Frontline.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Disadvantage.
Range: Both
*"There are more hands than you counted."*

---

**STONE GRIP**
RED — BODY — COIL
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: If target is Rooted, deal +d4 additional damage.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Thorns 2.
Range: Melee
*"It holds the way the stone holds — without effort, without end."*

---

**SURFACE READ**
BLUE — MIND — COIL
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: Scry 2 of target's deck.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1 of your own deck.
Range: Both
*"It has never used its eyes. The walls tell it everything it needs."*

---

**VIBRATION TRACE**
BLUE — MIND — COIL
Attack: Mind + d2
Effect: If target changed position since their last turn, deal +2 damage and apply Staggered.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"Movement is a confession."*

---

**WALL ECHO**
BLUE — MIND — COIL
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: Expose Blue — if the selected card is Blue, apply Blind.
Defensive Bonus: Scry 1.
Range: Both
*"A creature that cannot see has no patience for those who rely on it."*

---

**STEADY ADVANCE**
GREEN — SOUL — COIL
Attack: Soul + d2
Effect: Anchored — at the start of each of your turns, heal 1 HP.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"It has been walking this passage since before you were born."*

---

**MANY HANDS**
GREEN — SOUL — COIL
Attack: Soul + d2
Effect: Apply Rooted to target. If target is already Rooted, also apply Staggered.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Armour 1 until the start of your next turn.
Range: Melee
*"One would be enough. It does not use one."*

---

**WALL BOND**
GREEN — SOUL — COIL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Target gains Disadvantage.
Defensive Bonus: Attacker gains Rooted.
Range: Melee
*"You pushed against it. Now you belong to the wall."*
