# Wallows Slime — Signature Cards

Signature cards for the Wallows Slime. See `bestiary/wallows-slime.md`. Tagged as WALLOWS — obtainable via the Oracle deck after encounters in the Wallows.

---

**YOUR OWN HEARTBEAT**
BLUE — MIND — WALLOWS
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: The defender gains Rooted and heals 3 HP.
Defense Effect: The attacker gains Rooted and heals 3 HP.
Range: Both
*"Nothing in here is asking anything of you."*

---

**FOLLOWS WARMTH**
RED — BODY — WALLOWS
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Pull the defender into contact with you — you are both Frontline. If they were already in the Frontline with you, they gain Rooted.
Defense Effect: Pull the attacker into contact with you — you are both Frontline.
Range: Ranged
*"It does not lunge. It arrives."*

---

**ENVELOP**
GREEN — SOUL — WALLOWS
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: The defender gains Rooted and Staggered.
Defense Effect: The attacker gains Rooted.
Range: Melee
*"Held is not the same as hurt. It is only harder to explain afterward."*
