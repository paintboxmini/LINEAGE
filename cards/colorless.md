# Colorless Cards

This file holds cards that don't carry a fixed color or stat on their face — currently cards that resolve to one only at the reveal step, per their own text (the point of them, not an exception to the rule; because RPS and stat totals aren't known until reveal, they deliberately fall outside color-locked bans like Axiom that check at commitment time), and reserved as the future home for any status-effect-only card built without a color or stat of its own.

**How colorless resolves against a defense:** a colorless card auto-loses to any card with a real color — it never wins or ties against one. It only auto-loses when actually challenged that way, though: against no defense at all, or a defender who can't defend (Collapsed, Staggered), a colorless card still wins fully, same as anything else would in that situation. Two colorless cards facing each other tie.

**AFTERIMAGE**
COLORLESS
Attack: Mirrors the color and stat of whoever acted immediately before you + d4. If no one has acted yet, this stays genuinely colorless (no default) — just the flat d4, no stat bonus.
Effect: Defender gains Blind.
Defensive Bonus: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"Whatever they were becomes what I am."*

---

**FOLLOW-UP**
COLORLESS
Attack: Before RPS resolution, becomes a full copy of the card on top of the most recently discarded ally's discard pile — this card's own Attack/Effect/Defensive Bonus are entirely replaced by the copy. If no ally has discarded yet, this stays genuinely colorless (no default) and deals no damage of its own.
Effect: Replaced by the copied card's Effect.
Defensive Bonus: Replaced by the copied card's Defensive Bonus.
Range: Both
*"I only know how to follow."*

---

**BECOMING**
COLORLESS
Attack: Deals no damage on its own.
Effect: Choose a card in your hand. Permanently replace it with a card drawn from the Oracle pool. This card is exiled until the end of combat. After its 3rd use, permanently destroy it instead.
Defensive Bonus: Same as Effect.
Range: Both
*"Every shape I've worn, I've worn on purpose."*
