# Delve Roller — Cards

Signature cards for the Delve Roller. See `bestiary/delve-roller.md`.

*PATIENCE OF STONE and ROLLOUT were both promoted to core and now live in `cards/green-soul.md` and `cards/red-body.md` — Delve Roller's deck uses those core cards in their place. ROLLOUT gained a real mechanical addition on promotion: it returns to hand instead of discard after use (the Pokemon reference, made real) — unless its own reveal loses the RPS exchange. It was renamed PLANT on 2026-09-08: the old name promised movement on a card that pays you for standing still.*
