# Foulhaul — Cards

Signature cards for the Foulhaul and for the Grandfather. See `bestiary/foulhaul.md`.

**These cards are unique to Foulhauls. They cannot be awarded to players.**

---

**OVERBEAR**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d10
Effect: The defender is moved to Frontline with you and gains Rooted until the end of their next turn.
Defense Effect: Deal 3 damage to the attacker, unpreventable.
Range: Melee
*"It does not swing at you. It arrives where you are and you are underneath it."*

---

**BURDENED**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Gain Resist 1 for the rest of combat.
Defense Effect: Gain Armour 1.
Range: Melee
*"It did not put the other one down to deal with you."*

---

**THE SMELL OF IT**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Every enemy in this position gains 1 Weak.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"You stop being able to think about anything else, and then you stop being able to think."*

---

**THE LONG REACH**
RED — BODY
*The Grandfather only.*
Attack: Body + d10
Effect: None.
Range: Both
Defense Effect: Gain Resist 1.
*"The back rank was never out of range. Nobody had measured him."*

---

**WHAT IS OWED**
GREEN — SOUL
*The Grandfather only.*
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: If anything in this position is dead, downed, or no longer moving, he takes it — and does not attack again this round.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist 1.
*"Pneum has a word for this. The word is wrong and it has worked for two hundred years."*
