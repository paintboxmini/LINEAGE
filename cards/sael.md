# Sael — Cards

Signature cards for Sael, the Speaker of Pneum. See `characters/sael.md`.

**These cards are unique to Sael.**

---

**OLD SHAPES**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Defender gains Rooted.
Defense Effect: Gain Thorns 2.
Range: Melee
*"Whatever the congregation made of the rest, the Muirn is still under there."*

---

**ASKED PROPERLY**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Defender gains Weak.
Defense Effect: Attacker gains Weak.
Range: Both
*"Ask the right way and Sael answers. Ask any other way and you are answered anyway."*

---

**THE CHAIR BESIDE**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: Target ally heals 4 HP. Target ally gains Resist.
Defense Effect: Target ally gains Protect.
Range: Both
*"On Pneum it is the best seat on the island, and nobody has ever had to be asked to take it."*
