# Shockquill Signature Cards

Signature cards for the Shockquill. See `bestiary/shockquills.md`.

**These three run on Charge**, the counter the Shockquill's Static Build passive maintains. Charge is not a keyword and does not appear anywhere else — it lives in this creature's stat block and these three cards.

---

**TUMBLE**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: Gain 1 Charge. Move to any position.
Defense Effect: Gain 1 Charge.
Range: Both
*"It is not doing anything to you. It is just moving, and moving is what winds it."*

---

**RAISED QUILLS**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Gain Thorns 3.
Defense Effect: Gain Thorns 3 and Evade.
Range: Melee
*"Twice the animal, none of the weight."*

---

**FULL DISCHARGE**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Spend all Charge. Deal 2 unpreventable damage per Charge spent.
Defense Effect: Spend all Charge. Deal 2 unpreventable damage per Charge spent to the attacker.
Range: Melee
*"Four turns of walking around, arriving all at once."*
