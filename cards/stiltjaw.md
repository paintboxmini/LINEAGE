# Stiltjaw — Cards

Signature cards for the Stiltjaw. See `bestiary/stiltjaw.md`.

**A simple creature, run from blanks only** (`rules/gm-guide.md`, Running simple creatures from a blank deck): every Blue it plays is HIGH STEP, every Red is SNAPPING JAW, every Green is TAIL. *Its deck is fourteen blanks — 2 Blue / 8 Red / 4 Green — so it bites far more than it does anything else, which is right for it.*

---

**SNAPPING JAW**
RED — BODY — NEST
Attack: Body + d4
Effect: Defender gains Rooted.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
*"It has been waiting with its mouth open since before you arrived."*

---

**HIGH STEP**
BLUE — MIND — NEST
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: You may change position. Defender gains Vulnerable.
Defense Effect: You may change position.
Range: Both
*"Four legs like pilings, and the head above all of it, looking down into the weed."*

---

**TAIL**
GREEN — SOUL — NEST
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: Pull defender to frontline
Defense Effect: Deal 3 damage to the attacker, unpreventable.
Range: Both
*"It grabs with that end too."*
