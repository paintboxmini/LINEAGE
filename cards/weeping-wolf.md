# Weeping Wolf — Cards

Signature cards for the weeping wolf, the grown Ocellus. See `bestiary/weeping-wolf.md`.

**These cards are unique to the weeping wolf. They are not Oracle loot.**

---

**LAST THING YOU SEE**
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d8
Effect: Deal +2 damage this attack.
Defense Effect: Gain Thorns 2.
Range: Melee
*"Everybody it has killed noticed it eventually."*

---

**OUT OF MIND**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Defender makes a Hard (16) Body/Senses check. On a failure, they cannot attack you until they spend an Action finding you again.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
*"It did not go anywhere. You just stopped being able to keep it in your head."*

---

**PETRIFYING GAZE**
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d6
Effect: Defender gains Staggered.
Defense Effect: Attacker gains Rooted.
Range: Ranged
*"A hundred eyes, and for one moment every one of them is on you."*

---

**WEEPING VEIL**
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: All enemies in the defender's position gain Blind.
Defense Effect: Attacker gains Blind.
Range: Both
*"It whips its whole body round, and the tears come off it like rain off a shaken coat."*
