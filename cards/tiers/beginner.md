# Beginner Tier

The cards cleared for a first campaign. 84 are seated — the Oracle 63 and the expansion 21 — and 53 more are cleared and on the bench, 137 in all.

Cleared means somebody read the card with new players in mind and nothing stopped it — it is not a promise that the card is balanced forever. The screen these were cleared against, and the four bars it applies, are in `rules/early-campaign-cards.md`. Cards that failed it are in `middle.md`, not deleted.

Each colour is 28 in the seated sets, at 16/4/8 in Red, 8/16/4 in Blue and 4/8/16 in Green — the same 12/6/3 ratio in each colour's own range identity, doubled across the two sets.

## Eligible but unseated (53)

Beginner-legal, on the bench, no seat in a printed set. This is where a later set draws from first — the bench-first rule in `rules/cards.md` says a card that already exists has already been priced against its neighbours.

**Red (23).** ANCHOR · BLEED · BRACE · CLEAVE · DART · DIG IN · ENDURE · EXPEND · GORE · HAMMER · IRON GRIP · MAUL · OVERCOMMIT · OVERDRIVE · PLANT · PUSH · REPAY · REPEL · RHYTHM BREAK · SHOULDER · SKEWER · STAUNCH · WARSONG

**Blue (9).** CHAIN · DECODE · DISSECT · DRAIN · INTERCEPT · REDIRECT · SLIPSTREAM · TRACE · UNMAKE

**Green (21).** ABIDE · ALIGN · ATTUNE · AWAKEN · BOLSTER · CONSUME · DELAY · ENTREAT · HARMONIZE · HASTEN · PATIENCE OF STONE · RESONATE · SEED · SHELTER · STEAL · STIR · SUPPORT · TWIN STRIKE · UNIFY · WAIT · WITNESS

Three are worth naming. **INTERCEPT** *(Blue, Melee, d8)* was written on 2026-09-17 for the Blue melee shortage and is the one of the four that did not find a seat. **PUSH** *(Red, Melee, d8)* came off the Oracle 63 that day to make room for STRIKE — pure forced movement on both halves, and the deck covers that motion in TRAMPLE, PULL, REPEL and SHOULDER. **CONSUME** *(Green, Melee, d6)* is clean on every bar and was cut from the expansion for composition, not for anything wrong with it.

## No card here touches resolution

That is bar 1, and as of 2026-09-17 it is absolute. Six cards win or salvage a tie — ANTICIPATE, REBUTTAL, STAND, ADAPT, CALL and PUNISH — and all six are in `middle.md`. Two of them were seated in the Oracle 63 until that day.

The argument for keeping them had been that a tie advances nobody on its own, which is true of one card and not of a deck built from six. Worth remembering if a tie-winner is ever proposed for a starting deck again: the question is not what the card does, it is what four of them do together.
