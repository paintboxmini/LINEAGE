# Geography Overview

## Scale and Travel

Major hubs are roughly two days of travel apart. The Regency maintains the roads and halfway-inns between them.

## The Basin

The known world occupies a basin. Its boundaries are defined by:

- **The Unheld Ocean** to the north, west, and south — surrounding open water that does not obey held reality.
- **The Turnroot Weald** wrapping the eastern edge of the Ashfall Wastes — a sentient forest that insulates the basin from the War Engine beyond.

## Islands

Islands float in the surrounding Unheld Ocean. They are inhabited and trade-connected. A strong seafaring culture has developed from the necessity of reaching them.

Trade sailors who regularly cross the Unheld Ocean are highly valued. There are never enough of them.
