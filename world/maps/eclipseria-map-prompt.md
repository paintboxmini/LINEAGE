# Eclipseria — Reference Map Prompt

The prompt behind `eclipseria-reference-map.png`, kept here so it can be revised as canon changes rather than lost in chat history. **A living document — update it alongside place content changes, same discipline as `eclipseria-reference-map.md`.** When the image gets regenerated, use whatever version of this prompt is current, not the one that made the existing PNG.

---

> A hand-inked fantasy world map on aged, weathered vellum, dim sepia and faded ochre tones. Title banner at the top reads "ECLIPSERIA," subtitle "Known Lands." A single continent shaped like a rough triangle, ringed by a strange grey, static, mist-shrouded ocean labeled "The Unheld" — dead flat grey instead of blue, no horizon line, nothing sailing on it.
>
> A web of interconnected rivers laces the whole continent — a real network, tributaries splitting and rejoining, small ponds and lakes dotted throughout, following one large clockwise loop: a channel starting at the northern cliffs, running south along the eastern side, curving west across the southern stretch, then north again back up toward the cliffs. This web stays moderate in density across most of the continent — enough branching to read as a living network, not so much that the land dissolves into scattered islands. The loop cuts closest to the center at a walled capital city, "ECLIPSERIA," marked with a small capital-city icon (not a throne), sitting where the loop's inner channel runs shortest, fed by six roads — three spokes to the corners, three more connecting the corners into a triangle around the capital.
>
> At the continent's northern point, jagged pale quartz cliffs with a small cliffside town built into the stone, labeled "Glasslight Reach."
>
> To the southeast, where the loop reaches the coast, the web tightens dramatically: far denser branching than anywhere else on the map, many channels splitting, braiding, and rejoining around a cluster of small islands, pooling into a wide lake, all converging on a river-trade settlement labeled "Vulture's Nest" — visibly the most interconnected, web-like knot of waterways on the whole map, a clear step up in complexity from the rest of the network rather than the only place a web exists at all.
>
> To the southwest, a dense dark sentient forest labeled "Turnroot Weald," wrapping the eastern edge of a separate ash-grey blasted wasteland labeled "Ashfall Wastes" — the web here keeps its normal moderate branching, clearly calmer than the Vulture's Nest delta but not a single bare channel.
>
> Between Turnroot Weald and Vulture's Nest, a day's travel west of the Nest and cut a little way into the forest's own eastern edge, a small farmland settlement labeled "Briarwatch": red-dirt fields, waist-high fences, and a line of old watchtower ruins marking where the cleared farmland stops and the Weald begins.
>
> Separate from the mainland, out in the grey Unheld waters south of Vulture's Nest, an isolated jagged landmass glowing faint violet from a massive rift at its center, labeled "Abyssal Ruins."
>
> Hand-lettered compass rose, a small illustrated legend box with simple ink icons (capital city, settlement, road, river, lake, mountains, forest, ruin), a scale bar in an old surveyor's hand, and a small credit line reading "Surveyed by the Cartographers Guild" with an arbitrary stylized year. Small decorative flourish text near the compass: "No ship crosses the Unheld. No mast finds purchase. Only mist. Only stillness." Cartographer's ink linework, cross-hatched terrain, slightly uneven hand-lettering, warm candlelit parchment color grading, no modern typography.
