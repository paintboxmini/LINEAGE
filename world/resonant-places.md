# Resonant Places

*The place-equivalent of a Resonant Person or Resonant Item.*

---

A Resonant Place is what a place becomes when it aligns so closely with a Seat's domain that the domain begins to manifest through it. Like a Resonant Person or Resonant Item, it does not hold the Seat. It expresses one.

---

## How a Place Becomes Resonant

The same way a person or an object does: through history, not decree. What's lived, repeated, and left behind on a place over enough time can align it with a domain as surely as it aligns whoever walks through it.

Unlike a person or an item, a place can't act — only accumulate. Everything that becomes true of it is the residue of what was done there, again and again, long enough for the pattern to hold.

---

## Degrees of Alignment

Resonant Places pass through the same three-stage structure a Resonant Person or Resonant Item does (`world/resonant-people.md`, Degrees of Alignment; `world/resonant-items.md`, Degrees of Alignment). The first two stages share their names. The third doesn't — a place isn't a single body or a single object, it's an entire extent becoming continuous with the domain across all of it, not one vessel taking the domain on.

**Stage I — Resonance.** Ambient, unexplained, and minor. Something real is happening — a sound, a texture, a detail nobody in the place can account for — but the domain isn't doing anything through the place yet, only sitting close enough to leave a trace.

**Stage II — Alignment.** The domain begins expressing itself through the place directly — no longer a trace, an active behavior. The place isn't merely marked by the domain anymore; it does something because of it.

**Stage III — Hallowing (Archon).** A Resonant Place's ladder ends the same place the other two do: Archon (Drew, confirming directly — "it does" and "it means a location can fill a seat"). Reached not through biology, the way a person's ladder is, or through use, the way an item's is, but through the same accumulation that makes a place Resonant in the first place, carried far enough that the place stops merely expressing the domain and becomes its full, distributed bearer. When that happens, the Seat is Filled by the place itself, alongside — not instead of — whatever else has independently reached the same summit (`world/archons.md`, Multiple Bearers, One Seat). Neither Known Resonant Place, below, has reached it. What that would actually look like, for something that can't act the way a person or an item can, is still unbuilt — confirmed as real, not yet built as fiction.

---

## Price, at a Place's Scale

A place can't pay the way a person or an item does — it has no vitality to spend, nothing to degrade. What it has instead is an existing, already-built mechanic that does the same job: **the pressure track.** Failing to move through a Resonant Place the way its domain demands — a failed navigation, a violated custom — is failing to pay it, and the debt doesn't vanish, it accumulates: the place escalates against whoever's standing in it, exactly the way Price already works everywhere else in this cosmology (`world/the-regency.md`, Binding and Holding — debt "called in whole" instead of paid piecemeal). Holding at a level for real compliance is paying it down without clearing it. See `rules/places.md` for the applied mechanics.

## Known Resonant Places

**Glasslight Reach** *(candidate Song and Silence, Stage I — Resonance)* — the cliff fractures sing when wind passes through them, and nobody in the town knows why. Song and Silence is the closer fit among the Seats (`world/seats.md`, Song and Silence), but the alignment is unconfirmed — the same register Fermata's alignment sat in before it was confirmed. The resonance is real, and reads as ambient and unexplained rather than active — a trace, not a behavior — which is what puts it at Stage I rather than higher. Which domain it belongs to isn't settled yet.

**The Temple of the Rivers, Eclipseria** *(candidate Song and Silence, Stage I — Resonance)* — a reflective pool fed by a channel bled off the capital's own tidal intake, rising and falling on the same breathing rhythm as the rivers themselves (`world/geography-overview.md`). Song and Silence is the closer fit, unconfirmed the same way Glasslight's cliffs are — a third guess landing near the same edge, not proof of anything. The temple's own theology calls it something else entirely (`places/capital/capital.md`, The Temple District).

**Turnroot Weald** *(Seat unestablished, Stage II — Alignment)* — a forest whose sentience isn't metaphor, and whose pressure escalates the longer anyone stays inside it. Named here as a Resonant Place because its nature already behaves like one, and that behavior — the domain actively doing something through the place, not just marking it — is what puts it at Stage II rather than Stage I. Its Seat is unestablished — nothing in the current roster (`world/seats.md`) accounts for what it is, and this file isn't the place to manufacture an answer nobody's found yet.
