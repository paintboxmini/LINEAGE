# Resonant Places

*The place-equivalent of a Resonant Person or Resonant Item.*

---

A Resonant Place is what a place becomes when it aligns so closely with a Seat's domain that the domain begins to manifest through it. Like a Resonant Person or Resonant Item, it does not hold the Seat. It expresses one.

---

## How a Place Becomes Resonant

The same way a person or an object does: through history, not decree. What's lived, repeated, and left behind on a place over enough time can align it with a domain as surely as it aligns whoever walks through it.

Unlike a person or an item, a place can't act — only accumulate. Everything that becomes true of it is the residue of what was done there, again and again, long enough for the pattern to hold.

---

## Degrees of Alignment

Resonant Places pass through the same three-stage structure a Resonant Person or Resonant Item does. The first two stages share their names. The third doesn't — a place isn't a single body or a single object, it's an entire extent becoming continuous with the domain across all of it, not one vessel taking the domain on.

**Stage I — Resonance.** Ambient, unexplained, and minor. Something real is happening — a sound, a texture, a detail nobody in the place can account for — but the domain isn't doing anything through the place yet, only sitting close enough to leave a trace.

**Stage II — Alignment.** The domain begins expressing itself through the place directly — no longer a trace, an active behavior. The place isn't merely marked by the domain anymore; it does something because of it.

**Stage III — Hallowing (Archon).** A Resonant Place's ladder ends the same place the other two do. Reached not through biology, the way a person's ladder is, or through use, the way an item's is, but through the same accumulation that makes a place Resonant in the first place, carried far enough that the place stops merely expressing the domain and becomes its full, distributed bearer. When that happens, the Seat is Filled by the place itself, alongside — not instead of — whatever else has independently reached the same summit.

## Known Resonant Places

**Glasslight Reach** *(Song and Silence, Stage II — Alignment)* — the cliff fractures sing when wind passes through them.

**Turnroot Weald** *( , Stage II — Alignment)* — a forest whose sentience isn't metaphor, and whose pressure escalates the longer anyone stays inside it. Named here as a Resonant Place because its nature already behaves like one, and that behavior — the domain actively doing something through the place, not just marking it — is what puts it at Stage II rather than Stage I. Stage II is a ceiling here, not a waiting room: the forest carries the Thorne Throne, Change's own Embodied item, but full convergence needs its Archon holding still long enough for a place to catch up — and Elias never does.
