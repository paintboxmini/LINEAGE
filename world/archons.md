# Incarnation

The highest expression a person tied to metaphysical domain can reach. For a living being, that means its highest living expression — Stage III, Incarnation, the culmination of the Resonant Person ladder. A Resonant Item reaches the same culmination by its own, separate, path — its own Stage III, Embodiment. A Resonant Place, by a third path, its own Stage III, Hallowing.

It is what happens when something aligns so completely with a domain that it becomes its inevitable expression.

Incarnations function as distribution for their domain. Their free will decides the gradients. They do not command their domain. they aim it, and acting against it would mean acting against their own nature. Their presence affects the reality around them through what they embody.

## An Incarnation's Will Shapes Price

Act in the grain of an Archon's will, and the debt reality demands for reaching past the ordinary lightens. Act against it, and the debt deepens. The Archon does not simply permit or forbid — they tilt the whole economy of consequence toward what they are and away from what they are not.

## Multiple Bearers, One Seat

A Seat's three ladders don't compete with each other. A living being, a Resonant Item, and a Resonant Place can each independently reach Archon on the same Seat — all three Filled at once, if all three have actually walked their own ladder that far. A person-Archon doesn't displace an item-Archon; neither displaces a place that reached the same summit by its own accumulation.

Contested - two claimants on the same ladder, fighting for the one bearer-slot that ladder allows. Cross-ladder coexistence isn't a contest, because nothing is being taken from anyone — three separate paths can each reach the same summit without any of them being on the others' trail.

When all three converge, the Seat stops being only an abstract position and becomes a literal one. Each ladder's summit produces a different kind of physical role, and the three complete each other: the item becomes the embodiment of a domain itself. A Hallowed place is the space that seat is in. An Incarnated person is the one who wields it.

## Fading

Archons can fade. A domain whose practice dies in the world starves its expression: the Archon thins and recedes until the Seat stands empty.

Fading also means the domain's expression is weakened while the Archon is still present. The Seat does not simply stop functioning at the moment the bearer begins to fade. Its domain continues to exist, but the Archon's ability to distribute that domain through the world diminishes. The effects become weaker, thinner, or less fully expressed until the bearer recedes entirely and the Seat stands empty.

The Seat itself never fades. Seats are permanent structural features of existence; what fades is the bearer.
