# Duskwick — Field Notes

*A small night hunter at the edges of the light.*

**Cat-sized, low and long**, built for slipping through gaps. **Six thin legs** with soft pads, no claws — it makes no sound.

**Flat black fur that throws back no light at all**, and down its spine **a single glowing line, amber, like a banked coal.** *That line is the only part of it you see.*

**No eyes.** Where eyes should be there is only more fur.

**It waits where the light is failing**, and it does not chase.

# Duskwick — Measured


| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1 | 3 | 9 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Never Legible:** nobody reads what a Duskwick is carrying or planning. *It is permanently Obscure — no card needed.*

**Its cards** — it plays one for each colour it draws:

```

RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6

Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Melee
"The wick knows the difference between seeing and looking."
```
