# Ocellus — Field Notes

*The thing in the crate.*

**A soft, low animal about the size of a curled-up dog**, grey-brown and loose-skinned, with **no face worth the name.**

**Dozens of small eyes scattered over its back and flanks** — none of them in any pattern, none of them blinking together, **none of them looking at anything.**

**It barely moves.** Left alone it shifts a few feet in an hour and stops.

*It was in a crate in the middle of the market, and nobody had an opinion about it.*

# Ocellus — Measured

| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 | 4 | 15 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Unremarked:** while it is in a crowd, **nobody can attack it without first deliberately noticing it** — stopping and actually looking. Once you have noticed it, you have noticed it for good.

**Trait — Dulling:** everyone in the same position as an Ocellus takes a penalty on Senses and Reason checks. A second one does not add to it.

**Its cards** — it has none of its own; these are the three it plays, one for each colour it draws:
