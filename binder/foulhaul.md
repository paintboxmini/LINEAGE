# Foulhaul — Field Notes

*Riverbank scavengers you smell before you see.*

**The smell comes first** — carrion, standing water, and something sweeter underneath — **a full minute ahead of the animal.** *Nobody has ever been ambushed by one.*

**Seven feet standing**, though it rarely stands all the way up. **Long black hair over everything, the face included** — there is no face to find. The head sits straight on the shoulders.

**It is usually carrying something**, one arm hooked under a load, walking the bank like it has somewhere to be.

*Rivermen say they eat the dead.*

# Foulhaul — Measured


| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 2 | 19 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Reek:** every enemy in the same position as a Foulhaul always has 1 Weak. A second Foulhaul in the same position does not add another.

**Trait — Carries Off:** when a Foulhaul brings someone to Collapse, it does not finish them. **It picks them up and walks off with them**, without hurrying and without defending itself on the way.

**Its cards** — it has no Blue one of its own:

```
OVERBEAR
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d10
Effect: The defender is moved to Frontline with you and gains Rooted until the
end of their next turn.
Defense Effect: Deal 3 damage to the attacker, unpreventable.
Range: Melee
"It does not swing at you. It arrives where you are and you are underneath
it."
```

```
BURDENED
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Gain Resist 1 for the rest of combat.
Defense Effect: Gain Armour 1.
Range: Melee
"It did not put the other one down to deal with you."
```

```
THE SMELL OF IT
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d4
Effect: All enemies in your position gain 1 Weak.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Both
"You stop being able to think about anything else, and then you stop being
able to think."
```

# The Grandfather — Measured

*An old, enormous Foulhaul. What measuring him reveals.*

| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 8 | 4 | 39 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Old Hide:** Armour 2, permanently.

**Trait — Longer Than You Think:** he can attack the Backline from the Frontline.

**Trait — The Offering:** he does not attack first, and **he does not attack anyone who has just given him something.** Put a body in front of him — anything no longer moving — and he takes it and goes.

**His own cards:**

```
THE LONG REACH
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d10
Effect: None.
Range: Both
Defense Effect: Gain Resist 1.
"The back rank was never out of range. Nobody had measured him."
```

```
WHAT IS OWED
GREEN — SOUL
Attack: Soul + d6
Effect: If anything in this position is dead, downed, or no longer moving, he
takes it — and does not attack again this round.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist 1.
"Pneum has a word for this. The word is wrong and it has worked for two
hundred years."
```
