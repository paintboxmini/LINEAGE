# Bicolor Spider — Field Notes

*An orb-weaver of the high country.*

**A big orb-weaver**, the body about the size of a walnut.

**Two colours, split clean across the middle** — the front of the abdomen bright orange, the back deep blue. Not a pattern, not a fade. **A hard line.**

**It builds large, well-made webs** between rock and growing things, and comes out at dusk.
