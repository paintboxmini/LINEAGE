# High-Altitude Bat — Field Notes

*A cliff bat from above the fog line.*

**Wings about two feet across**, the membranes so fine they are nearly see-through at the edges. **Lean.**

**They roost in colonies in the cliff cavities**, high up where the air is thin and cold, and hunt insects at dusk.

**Disturb one and the whole roost wakes.**
