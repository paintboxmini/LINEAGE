# Gene-Thief Tardigrade — Field Notes

*A thing that learns you while it fights you.*

**The size of a large dog, translucent, on eight stubby legs.**

**Inside it, at the centre, is a shifting mass** — and it looks like everything it has ever eaten. *No two of them look the same; each one looks like where it was found.*

**It was watching what we did, and it changed.**

# Gene-Thief Tardigrade — Measured

| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 3 | 3 | 18 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Genetic Absorption:** it keeps track of each colour that hits it. **The first hit of a colour does full damage. The second gives it Resist against that colour. The third keeps the Resist and gives it a new ability** — Red: Thorns 2. Blue: Evade at the start of each of its turns. Green: heals 2 HP at the start of each of its turns.
