# Gollop — Field Notes

*Rock-throwers of the sandstone high country.*

**Built like a chimpanzee**, same size and same shoulders. **The coat is the exact brown of the cliffs** — still on a rock face, it is simply not there.

**A band of black fur across the eyes**, running all the way round the head. *If you can see the band, you have been close enough to be hit for a while.*

**Two bare red patches on each side of the neck.** Before trouble they swell bright and tight, and it makes the sound it is named for — **a wet, swallowing *gollop*.** *It is calling the others.*

**It throws rocks, and it does not miss much.** Close with one and **it has spurs on the backs of its legs.**

# Gollop — Measured


| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 3 | 2 | 16 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Reads the Odds:** when half the Gollops in a fight are Down, every one still standing leaves up the rock on its next turn instead of acting. They do not come back the same day.

**Its cards** — it plays one for each colour it draws:

```
CHOSEN STONE
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Deal +2 damage this attack.
Defense Effect: Gain Evade.
Range: Ranged
"It picked that one up before you came round the corner."
```

```
SENTRY'S CALL
BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
Effect: All allies gain Deadly.
Defense Effect: Gain Quick.
Range: Both
"It was never talking to you. You just happened to understand it."
```
