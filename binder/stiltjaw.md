# Stiltjaw — Field Notes

*A turtle that stands up out of the wrack.*

**A heavy, ridged shell like a stone with moss on it**, carried high on **four long, thin legs** — four or five feet of them, jointed like pilings. It does not swim so much as wade, and it can wade where a boat cannot.

**The jaws are far too big for it.** A hooked beak big enough to close around a man's chest. It holds its mouth open and waits.

**A long tail that grabs** — and whips.

**It hunts the wrack.** It stands above the weed, head high, looking down. *Where one has been working a bank, there are fewer Wrackclaws than there should be.*
