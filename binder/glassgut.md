# Glassgut — Field Notes

*The light on the bank that came toward us.*

**A river bottom-feeder the size of a sow** — broad, low and slow.

**It glows.** Its hide is full of ground glass and grit, **and the grit holds light** — a steady pale glow, the kind people on the river call a spooklight.

*It did not seem to know it was glowing. It came toward us anyway.*

# Glassgut — Measured

| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 5 | 2 | 24 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Bedded In:** while it has not moved since the start of its last turn, it has **Armour 2.**

**Trait — Carrying the Light:** it can never hide in darkness and can never be ambushed. **Both sides always know exactly where the other is.**
