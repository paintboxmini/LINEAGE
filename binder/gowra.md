# Gowra — Field Notes

*The one everybody has heard of and nobody believes in.*

**"A gowra story" means a lie.** Everybody in the Nest has heard of them. Nobody believes in them. *There is a man in the market selling gowra teeth at two coppers each.*

**What we saw:** four times the length of a man, most of it tail, **low on short bowed legs** with webbed, clawed feet. **Slate-grey plates. A ridge of blunt horns down the spine. Two tusks** curving up from the lower jaw, worn flat at the tips.

**The tail ends in a blade** — a wedge of the same plate, a foot across at the base.

**It came out of the water.** *Nobody at the Nest is going to believe us.*

# Gowra — Measured


| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 8 | 3 | 37 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Plated:** Armour 2, permanently.

**Trait — Undermines:** a bank a gowra has dug under is hollow, and gives way under enough weight.

**Trait — Takes One Under:** **its first move is to take somebody** — whoever is nearest the edge — and drag them under the bank to its den: a dry chamber full of air, a few seconds away, that anyone can follow on foot. **It does this once.**

**Trait — Knows Where It Is:** **it only roars in an enclosed space** — a chamber, a collapsed bank, a cave, under a deck. *On open ground it never does.*
