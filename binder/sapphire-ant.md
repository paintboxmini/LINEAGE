# Sapphire Ant — Field Notes

*A small blue ant of the dry high ground.*

**Half a centimetre long, iridescent blue**, and brighter in direct light.

**The soldiers are a deeper blue** than the workers.

**They live in rocky, mineral-rich ground** — outcrops, cliff edges, dry riverbeds — **and they leave you alone unless you disturb the mound.**
