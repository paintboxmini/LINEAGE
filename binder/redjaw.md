# Redjaw — Field Notes

*Wolves of the island woods, with red mouths.*

**Wolves, and ordinary ones to look at — except the mouth.** Every one of them has a jaw stained red to the throat. *Every redjaw looks like it has just fed.*

**They run in packs**, and they never come straight in. They go round first.

**The loggers' rules are about them**: work in crews, be out of the trees before dark, and never sleep in the woods.

# Redjaw — Measured


| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 2 | 15 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Last of the Pack:** when a redjaw is the only one of its pack still standing, it runs on its next turn instead of acting.

**Its cards** — it plays one for each colour it draws:

```
DRAG IT DOWN
RED — BODY
Attack: Body + d6
Effect: Defender gains Staggered.
Defense Effect: Gain Resist.
Range: Melee
"It does not need to kill you. It needs you on the ground for the next one."
```

```

BLUE — MIND
Attack: Mind + d4
```
