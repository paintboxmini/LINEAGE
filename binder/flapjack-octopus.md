# Flapjack Octopus — Field Notes

*A river ambusher that lies flat and disappears.*

**Pancake-flat at rest**, pressed against whatever it is lying on. **Eight short arms from the rim** of the body, not the middle.

**Its skin turns the colour of what is underneath it** — pale stone, dark silt, old crust. *When it stops moving, it is gone.*

**When it moves, it moves fast**, and it comes from below.

# Flapjack Octopus — Measured


| Mind | Body | Soul | HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 3 | 1 | 15 |

**Skills:** none. **Passives:** none.

**Trait — Flattened:** it can press itself flat against the nearest surface. While Flattened, the two traits below are active. *It stops being flat when it attacks.*

**Trait — Floor Mimic:** while it is still and Flattened, finding it takes a Hard (16) Mind/Reason check. Attacks against it while it is undetected have Weak, until it is found or it attacks.

**Trait — Self-Tenderizing:** while Flattened, it has Armour 1.

**Its cards** — it plays one for each colour it draws:

Effect: The defender makes a Normal (13) Body/Senses save or gains Staggered.
Ends Flatten.
Defense Effect: Gain Flatten.
Range: Melee
"It comes from below."
