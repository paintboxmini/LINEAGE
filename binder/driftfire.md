# Driftfire — Field Notes

*A stretch of river you cannot use.*

**Thumbnail-sized, see-through, and never alone.** Most of each one is **threads** — yards of them trailing from a body you could hide under a fingernail.

**They come in thousands**, and the water goes faintly cloudy with them. **At a distance it looks like fire on the river.** A dipped oar comes up coated.

**The threads burn**, and they hold on.

*There is nothing to fight. It drifts with the current, and it comes back round.*

# Driftfire — In the Water

*The rule for anyone who goes in.*

**The Thread Save.** Roll **one d6 + a stat** against **7**. *Any stat — if you can say why it helps.* Roll a fistful at once and count the failures.

**How many saves going in:**

| Where | Saves |
|---|---|
| The edge of a bloom | 5 |
| One push further in | 10 more |
| Further again | 20 more |

**Six failures and you are held.** Failures add up the whole time you are in the water, and do not reset.

**Holding still:** one save each turn you stay in the same place.

**Struggling, fighting or thrashing:** the 7 goes up by 1 — **and stays up** as long as you are in the water.

**Being held is not death.** It takes **days**, and the bloom keeps drifting with you in it. **Someone can come and get you** — and they take the same saves getting there.
