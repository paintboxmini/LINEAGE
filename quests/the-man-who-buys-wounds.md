# The Man Who Buys Wounds

*Standalone roadside NPC encounter, anywhere on the road to Briarwatch. Early tier. Non-violent by default; the teeth are in the terms.*

---

## Intent

Give the party their first *bargain* — relief now, price unspecified, seller polite. Teaches Wound economy by offering to solve it, and teaches players to read a deal the way they read a creature.

---

## Setup

A cart stopped neatly at the road's widest point, pointed neither direction in particular. A man in a wool coat too clean for the road, feeding nothing to a horse that doesn't look at him. The cart's bed is empty and scrubbed. It does not smell like animals. It doesn't smell like anything.

He introduces himself as Carosa and explains his trade: he buys culls, the Fenwool the farms can't keep. If anyone in the party is carrying wounds, he mentions — mildly, the way a man mentions weather — that he buys other culls too.

---

## The Offer

Carosa will remove one Wound in exchange for:

1. A token payment to the player of a few coins ("so it's a sale"), and
2. The player's name, written in his ledger, in their own hand.

That is the whole price. One purchase per customer. He does not press, upsell, or repeat the offer to a player who refuses.

---

## Approaches

- **Sell.** The Wound is destroyed — real, immediate, mechanical relief. The name goes in the ledger. Nothing else happens.
- **Refuse.** He nods, genuinely unbothered, and recommends the Roadhouse stew.
- **Probe.** Read (DC 13): Carcosa is not lying about anything, which is worse. Reason (DC 13): the ledger's earlier pages hold many names in many hands; a few are neatly crossed out.
- **Rob or threaten him.** He cooperates completely — hands over the coin, the cart, the ledger, all of it. The party has stolen a horse and an empty book from a man who watched them do it politely. He walks toward Briarwatch.
