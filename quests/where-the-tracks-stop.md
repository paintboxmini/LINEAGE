# Where the Tracks Stop

*Standalone road encounter, day 2 of the road to Briarwatch. Early tier. Combat-optional — a place, not a fight.*

---

## Intent

Teach the three Perception modes on a real problem, and teach the older lesson under them: the world is not obligated to explain itself. Understanding is optional. So is digging.

---

## Setup

Livestock tracks cross the road at intervals and don't always continue on the other side. Everyone local knows this. Nobody local stops.

At one crossing, the party has reason to stop — a dropped feed sack, a bleating sound, or simply the tracks themselves: a full set of sheep prints coming out of the bramble, crossing the packed dirt, and ending mid-road. Not scattering. Not doubling back. *Ending,* mid-stride, halfway across.

The soil past the last print is road dirt like all the rest. The Regency gravel is undisturbed.

---

## Perception (DC 13)

- **Observe:** the last prints are deeper at the toe — the animal was leaning forward, mid-step, unhurried. There are older track-ends here too, from different seasons, all stopping within the same arm-span of road.
- **Sense:** stand on the spot and the road smells like nothing — no rain, no bramble, no dung. An absence with edges.
- **Read:** the crossing does not feel violent. It feels *kept* — the way a door held open for you feels. The invitation is the unsettling part.

---

## Approaches (no forced outcome)

- **Walk on.** Nothing happens. The road is the Regency's and the road is safe. This is a complete and correct answer.
- **Dig.** An hour of it: soft earth, then limestone voice beneath — hollow when knocked. Each digger adds 1 Exhaust to their deck. The hole finds nothing. The ground has already closed its business here.
- **Bait it.** A tethered animal, goods, or a brave volunteer's boot on the last print. Hours pass; nothing takes bait that is *watched*. (Sense DC 13: the crossing knows the difference. It is not hungry. It is punctual.)
- **Stand on it.** A player who stands on the final print alone and unwatched for a full minute feels the ground shift its weight like something rolling over in sleep — and, GM's choice, may briefly hear it: hooves, many, walking away *underneath the road,* unhurried, in step.

---

## Outcomes

The crossing does not resolve. That is the encounter. What the party leaves with is comprehension-shaped dread and, if they stood on the spot, one true fact: the flocks that cross halfway are still walking somewhere below, and something down there keeps them in step.

**If it turns hot:** provoked digging near dusk can bring 2 Borrower Sentries (`quests/shifting-burrow.md`) up to repair the crossing — they will not pursue past the bramble line.

**Hooks:** the sinkhole at Briarwatch (`quests/hollow-below-briarwatch.md`) is a day up the road; the wound-buyer (`experimental/the-man-who-buys-wounds.md`) pays best for animals "the ground already picked."
