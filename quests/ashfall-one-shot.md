# The Ashfall Wastes — One-Shot

*Session 1 of 2. Continues in `quests/war-engine.md`.*

**Session Target:** 2 hours. Escalating environmental strain, resource tension, one major buried relic event.

For location overview, see `places/ashfall-wastes.md`.

---

## Core Concept

The land is not cursed. It is exhausted.

Everything that burned here burned too hot. Now the ash remembers heat.

This is not a place of introspection. It is a place of exposure. Turnroot is alive and routing. Ashfall is dead and unstable. Play that contrast.

---

## Environmental Rules

No memory bleed. No whispering madness. No symbolism pushing. The pressure here is physical.

- Ash reduces visibility after movement.
- Ash clings to lungs — Body saves on extended exertion.
- Ashstorms roll in unpredictably.
- Heat behaves incorrectly.
- Metal hums under the surface.
- Embers flare without warning.

---

## Exposure Track

Exposure increases when players push through storms, dig into relic sites, travel without shelter, or trigger dormant machinery. **Exposure never decreases during the one-shot.**

| Level | Name | State |
|-------|------|-------|
| 0 | Stinging Air | Ash in the eyes. Visibility reduced at distance. |
| 1 | Reduced Visibility | Navigation checks at +2 DC. Ranged attacks at disadvantage. |
| 2 | Ashstorm | All outdoor movement costs double. Body save DC 13 each scene or gain 1 Exhaust. |
| 3 | Engine Reactivation | Dormant war machines come online. Encounters escalate. |
| 4 | Catastrophic Ignition | Something buried ignites. The session clock is now a countdown. |

---

## War Relic Layer

Beneath the ash:

- Half-buried siege engines, some still faintly warm
- Melted armor fused into stone
- Charred banners preserved like fossils
- Buried construct cores with residual heat
- Cannons that fire once more if reactivated

The question is never "what does it mean?" It is always "is it still dangerous?"

## Tone

**Remove:** Obsidian statue guardians. Chest puzzles. Mural exposition. Psychological pressure.

**Keep:** Fossil towers. Engines under ash. Fires that burn cold. Embers that flare unpredictably.

---

## What It Pays

**~160 gold per character per session** — Tier 2 rates (`rules/equipment.md`, Pacing; multiply by party size), across this session and `quests/war-engine.md`.

Somebody sent them (see `quests/war-engine.md`, Core Concept), and whoever that is pays on delivery rather than up front, because Ashfall has a reputation and advances do not come back. The GM decides who — the Regency wanting the Wastes surveyed, a faction wanting the Engine quiet, or a private party who will not say why.

**Salvage is the other half.** The War Relic Layer is the real payday and is not the employer's business. Anything the party carries out of the ash is theirs to sell, and Ashfall relics price well above ordinary goods precisely because so few people come back with any.
