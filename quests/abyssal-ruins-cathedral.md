# The Cathedral — Abyssal Ruins

*Encounter location within the Abyssal Ruins.*

---

## The Structure

The cathedral's spires reach into a cloudy, yellowed sky. The entrance is a grand doorway adorned with carvings of divine figures and cosmic beings — all twisted and contorted in unsettling ways.

Inside: vast, labyrinthine. High ceilings. Passageways that seem to shift and change. Footsteps echo longer than they should. The air carries a persistent unease.

---

## Landmarks and Traps

**The Stained Glass Window**
A massive window depicting divine judgment. Glass cracked and distorted, casting shadows that shift across the walls. The scene seems to move — a story of a priest's descent playing out in fragmented light.

**Pressure Plates**
Concealed beneath ornate rugs and tapestries. Trigger falling debris or swinging pendulums. Require careful movement.

**The Twisted Statues**
Grotesque figures of angels and cherubs, contorted into nightmarish forms. Spaced throughout the corridors as markers — though of what, exactly, is unclear.

**The Candlelit Mosaic Chamber**
Flickering candlelight. At the center: an intricate mosaic of a serene garden. The contrast with the surroundings is unsettling. Something about the scene suggests impending doom beneath the surface calm.

**The Altar Room**
Dominated by a towering altar adorned with decaying flowers and symbols of devotion. Where the abomination waits.

---

## The Magical Door

Carved with a depiction of a lavish feast taken to excess — feasters gorging themselves, faces contorted with hunger and obsession. The figures' expressions blur the line between ecstasy and desperation. Fine lines in the carving seem to shift, almost in motion.

At the center: a massive platter holding a grotesque amalgamation — fragments of creatures, mundane and exotic, merged into something that transcends normal culinary logic.

**Unlocking mechanism:** The abomination's attacks cause tremors that reverberate through the cathedral. The door responds to these vibrations. Players must identify the pattern, synchronize their actions with the strongest tremors, and strike, cast, or move in the correct resonant way. Success triggers a series of melodic chimes and a shimmer of light across the door's surface.

---

## The Chamber Beyond

A floating crystal, suspended in mid-air. Soft, ethereal glow. Intricate patterns and symbols etched into its surface — shifting, never static.

The crystal is not an artifact to be taken. It is a stabilizing force. Removing or breaking it would cause catastrophic collapse of the passage the party crossed to reach this place.

This information is available through investigation. The crystal pulses in apparent awareness of the party's presence. What they do with that knowledge is their choice.

## The Gluttony Abomination

## What It Pays

This is a delve, and the ruins pay in what the ruins contain — a hoard, not a wage (`rules/equipment.md`, Pacing).

The Gluttony Abomination's cathedral is a lair with centuries of offerings in it.

**Treasure: 5d6 × 40 gold** (~700 gold average, range 200–1,200) — the accumulated offerings, split however the party agrees. For a four-person party that's roughly 175 gold each on average, in line with what the rest of this arc pays (`quests/war-engine.md`, ~160g/character at Tier 2) — a hair above, since nobody commissioned this one and the party has to dig it out of a Threat 17 boss's lair themselves rather than collect a fee.

Beyond the coin: centuries of devotional objects, most too degraded or too specific to a dead faith to carry real value at market. A GM looking for a place to drop a piece of found gear (`rules/equipment.md`, Pricing Consumables — "Found gear is off this curve entirely") has an obvious, thematically loaded spot to put one.
