# The War Engine

*Session 2 of the Ashfall arc. Can be run standalone. Inherits Pressure from `quests/ashfall-one-shot.md` if run in sequence.*

---

## Session Target

2 hours. Escalating internal instability. Mini-trials reduce Pressure. Firing is inevitable. Players influence trajectory only.

---

## Core Concept

They were sent to disable a war machine.

They expect a central core. An engine room. A kill switch.

Instead they discover: there is no engine room. The castle is the engine.

The corridors are conduits. The towers are targeting arrays. The battlements are stabilizers. The foundation is recoil dampening.

They aren't infiltrating a machine. They're inside the barrel.

---

## Tone

Not haunted. Not possessed. Not sentient. **Procedural.**

Everything that happens inside feels like old instructions executing. No malice. No anger. No ghosts. Just: sequence.

---

## Two-Session Continuity

| Track | Session 1 (Ashfall Traverse) | Session 2 (Engine Interior) |
|-------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Pressure (Firing Sequence) | Starts ticking. Never decreases. | Carries over. Mini-trials reduce it. |
| Exposure (Environmental) | Increases from storms, digging, heat. | Increases from failing hazards, moving slowly, triggering unstable sections. Decreases from completing mini-trials. |

Session 1 ends at approach. Players enter Session 2 already under countdown.

## The Reveal

Don't announce it. Let it build.

Players spend the first chamber assuming there's an engine room somewhere deeper. By the second or third chamber, the architecture stops making sense as a building and starts making sense as a mechanism. By the time they understand what they're standing in, they're already most of the way through the barrel.

---

## Interior Zones

### 1. The Intake Halls

Exterior ash is being drawn in through massive vents. It pours like waterfalls into rotating filtration drums.

**Hazard:** Reduced visibility. Grinding mechanisms. Falling debris.

**Mini Trial:** Clear a jammed intake to prevent a pressure spike. Or close a vent manually while the storm howls outside.

**Design:** Players realize ash is fuel. This thing runs on aftermath.

---

### 2. The Gear Cathedral

A vertical shaft filled with interlocking gears the size of houses. Bridges cross between them. Heat radiates from below.

**Hazard:** Shifting platforms. Gear timing. Falling risk.

**Mini Trial:** Manually align a stabilizer gear to slow targeting rotation. Or secure a collapsing support before a chain reaction begins.

**Design:** The structure is rotating internally. It's reorienting.

---

### 3. The Recoil Vaults

Massive pistons embedded in stone and steel. Walls scarred from previous firings.

**Hazard:** Pressure build-up. Steam bursts. Sudden seismic shifts.

**Mini Trial:** Vent excess pressure to avoid premature detonation. Reinforce blast shutters.

**Design:** If they're inside during firing, this chamber matters. They know what those scars mean now.

---

### 4. The Targeting Spire

Spiral ascent. Exterior partially open to the ashstorm. Gyroscopic rings align slowly with the distant horizon.

**Hazard:** Storm breach. High wind. Narrow walkways.

**Mini Trial:** Delay ring alignment. Slightly alter trajectory. Override locking pins.

**Design:** This is where the aperture first becomes visible. Through the storm, something in the distance begins to resolve.

---

### 5. The Tracking Chamber

No throne. No captain's chair. Just massive mechanical rails and a viewing aperture.

The Abyssal Ruins, framed in targeting rings.

Not glowing. Not active. Just there. Already broken. Already scarred. Already erased once.

And the targeting sigils snap into place on that exact location.

No exposition. No murals. No ancient voice explaining history.

Just: this machine has done this before.

**Final Mini Trial:** One vector shift. Limited window. Choose.

Then ignition sequence.

---

## Combat Inside the Engine

Choose one option based on table tone. Do not combine.

**Option 1 — Maintenance Constructs** *(best fit)*
Half-buried automata that activate when calibration begins. They don't roar or taunt. They restrain, pin, redirect, seal doors, force repositioning. Their goal isn't to kill — it's to keep the firing sequence clean. Body-heavy. Position manipulation. Rushdown. They push players into dangerous terrain.

**Option 2 — Ash-Hardened Warforms**
Corpses of soldiers fused with metal, partially reanimated by heat and ignition surge. Not undead. Not cursed. Residual kinetic energy reactivating nervous systems. Short-lived. Violent. Unstable. Collapse after a few turns. Reinforces: heat + ash = catalytic reanimation.

**Option 3 — Internal Security Protocol**
No enemies. The engine locks corridors. Combat is fighting while platforms rotate, pistons slam, and bridges retract. Low enemy count. High environment. The cleanest option for pure survival tension.

One real combat encounter is enough. Let it be short, sharp, dangerous, and position-based. Win this exchange while the engine finishes alignment.

## The Alignment Marshal

See `bestiary/alignment-marshal.md`.

### Reveal Structure

**Phase 1 — Evidence** *(do not show the Marshal)*

Show consequences:
- Doors bent inward, not outward
- Ash dragged across walls in arcs too high to be human
- Gear teeth missing — ripped, not broken
- Deep, rhythmic thuds somewhere below
- Blast shutters partially crushed from inside

No gore. No theatrics. Something large. Something industrial. Something moving through places it shouldn't.

**Phase 2 — Activation Trigger**

It activates because players interfere with a subsystem. They reroute steam, align a stabilizer, interrupt a sequence, damage something critical.

*System integrity compromised. Correction protocol engaged.*

No roar. No scream. Just: a massive locking mechanism disengages somewhere deep below. Then metal sliding against metal.

**Phase 3 — Active Pursuit**

The Marshal appears where it shouldn't. Uses vertical movement. Forces position changes. Breaks through sealed doors. Collapses bridges behind them. Shoves players toward unstable platforms.

Not a damage sponge. A positioning predator. It doesn't need high HP. It needs control.

---

## Firing Inevitability and Player Agency

The engine will fire. The Abyssal Ruins will be struck. That history is locked.

The angle is not.

**Final challenge — Tracking Chamber:**
- **Mind:** Calculate the vector shift
- **Body:** Physically force alignment
- **Soul:** Decide what they're willing to sacrifice

### If They Do Nothing
The beam clips the forest edge. Roots vaporize along the Turnroot Weald's western boundary. Pressure echoes through the canopy. Something old wakes.

### If They Adjust the Shot
Turnroot is preserved. The beam hits deeper into the Ruins. Something buried doesn't stay buried.

### Player-Earned Framings

**If they stay inside:** Shockwave through corridors. Ash blast tearing through breaches. Emergency bulkheads slamming shut.

**If they reach the Tracking Chamber:** They watch the barrel align. See the Abyssal Ruins framed in targeting rings. Feel the heat spike. Then light.

**If they try to outrun the structure:** They see it from the ashstorm — a beam or projectile carving the sky. Sound arriving seconds later.

---

## Ending

Don't add anything after the shot.

The aperture still smoking. Ash hangs like it forgot how to fall. A single piece of slag drops from the aperture rim. Ticks against stone.

Far below, something resets.

*Thud.*

*…Thud.*

*…Thud.*

Not aggressive. Not triumphant. Routine.

If the Marshal lives, it doesn't look at them. It recalibrates. One leg stutters. A prism flickers. Then it folds slightly smaller than it should be able to and pulls itself into a crawlspace meant for something much simpler.

Metal scraping on metal. Gradual. Unhurried.

No one announces victory. No one declares failure.

The weapon fired. The world absorbed it. And something inside the engine continues, as though the event was merely a scheduled function.
