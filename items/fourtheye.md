# FourthEye

*The drug the whole Nest thread is about. The chain runs harvest → transport → sale: `bestiary/ocellus.md`, `factions-and-races/the-cellar-custodians.md`, `places/capital/underground-bazaar.md`.*

A thumbnail of grey-iridescent grit, taken under the tongue. It is the film an Ocellus weeps, scraped off, dried and refined several times over. It has almost no taste, and people who have taken it will tell you the absence is the taste — that it is like putting nothing in your mouth on purpose.

---

## What It Does

**You see somewhere else, and what you see is true.** Not a memory, not a symbol, not a prophecy. A place, as it is right now, while you are watching it. Four or five minutes, and it is clearer than sight — there is nothing to do in a vision but look, so you look properly, which most people have never done in their lives.

**You do not pick the place.** This is the part everyone tries to argue with and nobody has beaten. **The vision goes where attention isn't** — it lands on the nearest place to you that no one is currently paying any mind to. A corridor between rounds. A hold at anchor. A counting-room after the lamps are out. The four seconds in a packed street where every head happened to be turned the other way. Take it in the middle of a market and you will not see the market; you will see the room above it.

**Which follows from what it's made of.** The film is unspent *noticing*, taken in traces off everyone who walked past a crate (`bestiary/ocellus.md`). Concentrated and swallowed, it does the only thing it knows how to do — it goes looking. It has nobody's particular business to attend to, so it goes where nobody's attention is.

Two eyes for what's in front of you and a third one for whatever the mystics are selling. The fourth is the one that looks where no one is. That is street etymology, not doctrine, and the people selling it did not name it.

---

## Why People Take It Twice

**The vision is not the addiction. The come-down is.**

For as long as it is in you, everything is worth registering. Every detail of the unwatched room arrives at full weight, and the faculty doing that work is running hotter than it has ever run, because it is somebody else's faculty on top of your own.

Then it passes, and the room you are actually standing in goes flat. Nothing in front of you is worth the work of looking at. Habitual users describe the sober world as *furniture* — still there, still functional, not worth a second glance. They are not describing a mood. They are describing a real and measurable loss of the exact thing the animal eats.

So they take another one. Not to see anywhere in particular. To have the world be interesting again for four minutes.

**A long user stops witnessing.** They miss the obvious: the argument in front of them, the fire two doors down, the face of the man who has robbed them twice. They are not stupid and they are not absent. The part of them that registers what is around them has been spent somewhere else, and it does not come back on its own.

## Why the Regency Calls It a Pandemic

It crosses all three hard lines — too addictive, too destructive, no cut for the council (`places/vultures-nest.md`) — but the third line is the one that gets it *noticed* and the second is the one that actually frightens them.

**A district of users is a district that does not witness.** Crimes go unreported because nobody registered them. Fires burn for an hour before anyone mentions it. Wardens take statements from forty people who were standing right there and get forty shrugs. Every instrument of government that Eclipseria has ever built assumes that a street full of people is a street full of eyes, and FourthEye quietly repeals that assumption one customer at a time.

**The other half of the problem is the evidence.** A user has been in a locked room. Ask them what they saw and you have a true report from somewhere nobody is supposed to be able to look. Users therefore know things, at random, that they have no business knowing, and cannot say why, and cannot do it again on request. That makes them useful to exactly the wrong people and extremely dangerous to the people selling to them.

Erubeth is at the far end of this thread, working backward from the bazaar (`characters/erubeth.md`, `places/vultures-nest.md`). He knows the shape of the chain. He does not have the animal yet.

## Using It At The Table

**Out of combat — the vision.** The taker names nothing and steers nothing. The GM describes one place, real and current, that no one present is watching, and picks it from somewhere the party's situation makes it matter: the room they are about to enter, the room they just left, the cabin of the ship they are on, the cellar under the building they are standing in. The player gets a clean true look at it and no explanation of why that place. One dose, one vision, no reroll.

Be generous with the detail and stingy with the frame. The tension in the item is that it always tells the truth and never answers the question.

**In combat — the flash.** Using a dose is an action: **Scry 3** on any one deck in the fight, including an enemy's (`rules/card-glossary.md`). You have looked where they were not looking. Then take a **FOURTHEYE** curse card into your deck.

**The curse.** FOURTHEYE is a Status — Curse card (`rules/card-glossary.md`). Like every curse it sits outside the deck-size cap (`rules/character-creation.md`), and like every curse it comes off by story, not by resting. It is not a straight penalty, and that is the point of it — at the table the card will occasionally be the best thing that happens to you that turn, which is a more honest model of a drug than a card that only ever hurts.

---

## Price

**~40 gold a dose** in the Underground Bazaar, which buys one Scry 3 and a permanent curse — a bad trade at consumable rates (`rules/equipment.md`, Pricing Consumables) and priced by scarcity rather than utility. The people paying it are not paying for the combat use.

Outside the bazaar there is no price, because there is no supply. Everything that exists comes up the same chain.

## Not Yet Set

- **Whether a user can ever learn to steer it.** Currently: no, and everyone who has tried has failed. Whether a long enough user, or the right ritual, or proximity to a live Ocellus changes that is open and is a good escalation if the campaign wants one.
- **Whether the dulling is permanent.** Same open question as the animal's Dulling trait (`bestiary/ocellus.md`), and it should have the same answer.
- **What the Regency does when someone points out how useful it is.** A drug that reads unwatched rooms is exactly what a council of spymasters would want, and the official position is that it is banned three times over. Nobody in the repo has put that to the council yet.
- **Who at the Nest is running the animals.** Erubeth's whole reason for being there (`places/vultures-nest.md`).
