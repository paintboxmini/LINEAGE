# Items — No Fixed Source

General use items without a fixed location source. Sources and availability left to DM discretion.

---

**TERRORMITE CAPSULE**
*Consumable*
*Price: ~25 gold*
Gain Armour 1 and your attacks deal +1d6 damage.
End of combat: Shuffle 2 Wound cards into your deck.

---

**ECHO SHELL**
*Consumable*
*Price: ~30 gold*
Repeat your last action this turn — replay the card that produced it directly from your discard pile, as a fresh attack. Only works if that card hasn't already been shuffled back into your deck.

---

**BLOOD PHIAL**
*Consumable*
*Price: ~30 gold*
When you attack, add the Effect of the target's own most recently played card to your attack, on top of your own card's Effect.

---

**IMPRINT SIGIL**
*Consumable*
*Price: ~30 gold*
Target follows one simple command you give them, for their next turn only.

---

**UNIVERSAL PIN**
*Consumable — Does not require an action to use.*
*Price: ~30 gold (priced above the flat consumable rate — genuinely free-action, worth about a point on its own; `rules/equipment.md`, Pricing Consumables)*
Fix a target in place — they cannot change position — until the start of their next turn.

---

**PHASE DRAUGHT**
*Consumable*
*Price: ~30 gold*
Take your turn as normal. At the end of it, you return to the position and state you were in at the start of the turn — the turn's outcome for everyone else stands; only your own position and state snap back.
