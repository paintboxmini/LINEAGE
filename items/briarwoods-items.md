# Briarwoods — Items

Consumables and equipment found in or around the Briarwoods region. Sources noted per item.

---

**BARBED WRAP**
*Consumable — Strips of wire-wrapped leather*
*Source: Roadhouse barracks (see `locations/roadhouse.md`)*
Duration: 1 battle.
Effect: When you are targeted by an attack, deal 1 damage to the attacker.

---

**CARRION FEATHER**
*Consumable — Single dark feather, tied with road-worn cord*
*Source: Given by Aege upon delivering the party to Briarwatch*
Use (1): Negate a forced reposition effect that would affect you.

---

**SPLIT WEDGE**
*Consumable — Iron wedge, worn grip, field tool*
*Source: Briarwatch farmer reward (after resolving the Hollow Below)*
Use (1): Gain Anchored — +2 damage this turn.
