# Accessories

*The Accessory slot — two per character (`rules/character-creation.md`, Equipment). Every one names a **Skill tag** and grants +1, +2 or +3 to it, and nothing here does anything in a fight.*

**Pricing is the ordinary equipment ladder** (`rules/equipment.md`, Weapon and Armor Tiers): **Tier 1 ~200 gold, Tier 2 ~800, Tier 3 ~2,000.** A Perception-mode accessory costs one tier above its number, because a mode is broader than a trained action.

**This list is not meant to be complete and never will be.** It is a starting shelf and a set of worked examples. Anything on it can be reskinned to a different tag in ten seconds — that is the point of the category.

---

## Tier 1 — ~200 gold

**GRIPWAX BLOCK** — *Climbing +1*
A knob of amber-coloured wax in a tin, worked into the palms. Smells strongly of pine and faintly of something that was in the pine.
*Where: Harwick Sundries, Vulture's Nest; general markets.*

**THE HONEST COAT** — *Negotiation +1*
Plain, well-made, unfashionable by a decade. It does nothing but make its wearer look like somebody who is not trying to sell you anything, which turns out to be most of the job.
*Where: Elara's Marketplace, capital.*

**TALLOW-LINE SPOOL** — *Lockpicking +1*
Waxed cord and three bent wires on a ring, wound so nothing rattles. Somebody's working set, sold on.
*Where: Underground Bazaar, capital — for a card or a secret, not coin (`places/capital/underground-bazaar.md`).*

**DROVER'S WHISTLE** — *Animal Handling +1*
Carved bone, two notes, both of them low. Animals that have never heard it before still stop.
*Where: Briarwatch; Weald families.*

**RIVERMAN'S KNOTBOOK** — *Sailing +1*
Twenty pages of knots tied into the pages themselves rather than drawn. Half of them are for things that no longer exist.
*Where: Vulture's Nest docks.*

**SMOKED LENS** — *Forgery +1*
A palm-sized disc of dark glass on a thong. Holds a document at the right angle and shows where the ink went down twice.
*Where: Willem's stall, Underground Bazaar; capital scriveners.*

**WEIGHTED HEM** — *Stealth +1*
Lead shot sewn into the bottom of a cloak so it stops moving when you do. The single most useful thing about it is that it does not billow.
*Where: general markets; commonly made rather than bought.*

**SALT-CURED GLOVES** — *Fishing +1*
Stiff, unpleasant, and the last pair of gloves you will ever need on a line.
*Where: Vulture's Nest; any river village.*

## Tier 2 — ~800 gold

**THE LISTENING RING** — *Negotiation +2*
A plain band that is very slightly too warm. The wearer finds themselves waiting half a beat longer before answering, and half a beat is most of it.
*Where: Brother Alden, capital (`items/capital-items.md`) — he will want to know what it is for.*

**QUARTZ EYE** — *Reason +1 (Perception mode)*
A sliver of Glasslight quartz in a wire frame, worn over one eye. Holds the colour of the last thing it was pointed at for a second or two after you look away, which is long enough to compare (`places/glasslight-reach.md`).
*Where: Cartographers' Guild; Glasslight Reach.*

**PITCHDARK CLOAK** — *Stealth +2*
Weighted hem, hood that sits properly, and a dye that does not read as black so much as absent. Made by somebody who worked nights for a living.
*Where: Underground Bazaar — priced in secrets.*

**FALSE-BOTTOM CASE** — *Forgery +2*
A scrivener's travelling case with the tools in it and a second set under the tools. The second set is better.
*Where: capital, discreetly.*

**COLDHAND PICKS** — *Lockpicking +2*
Six picks of a grey metal that does not warm to the touch, in a roll of oiled cloth. Nobody sells these openly.
*Where: Underground Bazaar; the Cellar Custodians know where more are.*

**THE LONG TETHER** — *Animal Handling +2*
Braided from something that is not quite rope. An animal on the end of it does not pull, and nobody has ever been able to say why.
*Where: Turnroot Weald families; not sold so much as given.*

## Tier 3 — ~2,000 gold

**SENSE OF THE ROOM** — *Read +2 (Perception mode)*
A thin chain worn under the collar, warm against the throat. It does not tell the wearer anything. It simply makes it very hard to stop noticing.
*Where: Brother Alden, capital, and he will ask twice whether you want it.*

**THE PATIENT STEP** — *Stealth +3*
Boots resoled in layered felt and something else, by a maker nobody can name. They do not make you quiet. They make you slow in exactly the way that turns out to be quiet.
*Where: found, generally. Not stocked anywhere.*

**TRUE-EDGE RULE** — *Forgery +3*
A brass straightedge with no markings on it at all. Laid against a document, the hand knows where the line should have been.
*Where: capital, through people who do not advertise.*

## Building More

**Pick a tag first, then a number, then a thing.** In that order. The tag is the whole design; the object is decoration and should take thirty seconds.

**Good tags are things a person trains at** — a trade, a craft, a specific physical act, a way of dealing with people. If the tag needs a sentence to explain, it is too broad and the accessory is mispriced.

**Reskin freely.** Every item above is a tag and a price with a paragraph attached. Swap the paragraph for something local and the item is new — a Weald version of the Pitchdark Cloak is moss-dyed and smells of wet bark, and is the same accessory.
