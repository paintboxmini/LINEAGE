## Temple of the Forge (Harlan & Sera Vetch)

The working counter, not the shrine. Harlan Vetch is the master smith — weapons and the high rack. Sera Vetch fits armor, and holds a piece until the party agrees who wears it. Tier 1 arms and plate, priced to the Session 5 curve; two Tier 2 pieces on display for whoever's further along.

### Temple Edge
*Price: ~200 gold (Tier 1 weapon, `rules/equipment.md`)*

**Equipment — Weapon (Tier 1). Effect:** +1 attack damage.

## Marketplace — Nessa Coil (Crossroads Sundries)

Regional consumables at capital markup, plus general-use capsules and draughts when the river brings them.

**Regional resale** (source files keep the lower price; Nessa charges the capital column here): Luminova Leaves/Powder (`items/hollow-and-weald-items.md`), Echothorn Seed, Sap Vial (`items/turnroot-weald-items.md`), Nest dock goods (`items/vultures-nest-items.md`), Barbed Wrap (`items/briarwatch-items.md`).

**General consumables when stocked** (no fixed source, `rules/items.md`): Universal Pin, Terrormite Capsule, Echo Shell, Blood Phial, Imprint Sigil, Phase Draught.

## Marketplace — Brother Alden (Resonant Goods)

Seat-adjacent objects and the rare Artifact. Formerly Temple of Knowledge; still keeps their archive for secrets paid in kind.

### Mile-Marker Ring
*Price: ~60 gold — carried*

**Use:** Once per combat, your Move Position action may include a Rushdown.

---

### Ledger Weight
*Price: ~45 gold — carried*

**Use:** Once per combat, force the defender to re-reveal their chosen card — costs them a discard.

---

### The Quiet Index
*Price: ~400 gold + a true secret archived — Artifact, Knowledge*

**Use:** Start of combat, Scry 1. Once per session, read the true intent behind something written or carved.

---

### Cord of the Held
*Price: ~250 gold + a real vow — Artifact, Love/Binding*

**Use:** Once per combat, prevent an ally's Collapse — they remain at 1 HP instead. Inert until a long rest.
