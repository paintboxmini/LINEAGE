# Hollow and Weald — Items

Items from the Briarwatch Hollow and the Turnroot Weald region. Hollow items sourced from Borrower goodwill. Weald items harvestable in the field.

---

**LUMINOVA LEAVES**
*Consumable — Translucent, bioluminescent*
*Source: harvestable in the field, Turnroot Weald (Luminova Clearing)*
*Price: ~15 gold*
Use: Heal 4 HP to self or one ally (Use an Item — costs your Action). The leaf goes dark when spent.

Can be dried and ground into **Luminova Powder** (`items/turnroot-weald-items.md`) for increased potency — a real crafting step, not a duplicate item. The raw leaf is weaker but immediate; the powder trades a processing step for roughly double the healing.

---

**CLAY BOWL TREMOR DETECTOR**
*Consumable — Hollow clay bowl, Borrower-made*
*Price: ~15 gold*
Use: Set it down before an encounter begins. The party cannot be surprised (no ambush) for that encounter. Spent whether or not anything was actually lurking.

---

**MOVING-STONE MAP**
*Consumable — Clay-marked leather, Borrower cartography*
*Price: ~30 gold*
Use: Consult it before rolling initiative. For the next combat only, the party acts before all Stonecoils present, regardless of the initiative roll. Spent after that one combat.
