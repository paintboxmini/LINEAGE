# Washed Ashore — Items

Everything here is found, not bought — there's no vendor on this stretch of coast and the party starts with no gold to spend anyway. Prices are given for reference only (what these would run at a real market later, per `rules/equipment.md`'s pricing curve), not because anyone's selling.

---

## Hullback's Den

Available once Hullback (`bestiary/hullback.md`) is killed, driven off, or abandons the fight — see that file, Encounter Setup. Years of salvage from whatever this stretch of coast has wrecked, picked over by something that never had a reason to throw anything back.

**WRECK-IRON CUTTER**
*Weapon — a boarding blade, pitted with rust but the edge still holds*
*Source: Hullback's den*
*Reference price: ~200 gold (Tier 1)*
A Tier 1 weapon (`rules/equipment.md`): the first successful attack against an enemy each combat inflicts Weak.

---

**BRINE-SET WRAP**
*Consumable — a length of cloth cured in salt and some tide-plant resin nobody in the party can name*
*Source: Hullback's den*
*Reference price: ~15 gold*
Use (1): Heal 4 HP.

---

**A double handful of old coin**
Waterlogged, a few different mints, none of it recent. **10 gold**, split however the party agrees — the first money any of them have held since waking up on this beach.

---

## The Open Tideline

Available without a fight — a GM call on how long a search takes and what the party's actually looking for. Mostly texture; nothing here needs a roll unless the table wants one.

- Driftwood, rope-ends, a scattering of shells — nothing usable as gear (`rules/character-creation.md`'s starting clothes and weapons already cover that ground, no mechanical stacking on top of it).
- A single boot, empty, half-buried — not matched to anyone in the party. Whoever it belonged to isn't explained here.
- Tide pools between the rocks hold nothing mechanical on their own — a place to look, not a guaranteed find. If a GM wants a second small consumable somewhere on this coast without routing it through Hullback, this is the natural spot to put one.
