# Vulture's Nest — Items

Items found at, traded through, or native to Vulture's Nest. Sources noted per item.

## Found

**THE FILIGREE DAGGER**
*Weapon, Tier 1 — an enchanted dagger worked in gold filigree*
*Source: Lusk's payment to the Muirn mercenaries sent after Caine; the party took it off them (`campaign/session-4-log.md`, The Muirn hunters; Drew, 2026-11-17)*
*Value: ~200 gold, found rather than bought (`rules/equipment.md`, Pricing Consumables — found gear is off the curve)*
+1 attack damage.

*It came off Lusk's counter, which is where stolen things go to become merchandise* (`places/vultures-nest.md`, The Strays, and Lusk). **Anyone who knows Lusk's stock may know it on sight** — *and it is proof of who paid for the hunt.* **Who in the party carries it is not set.**

## Dock Equipment

**DOCKHOOK LINE**
*Consumable — Weighted hook on heavy rope*
*Source: Vulture's Nest market or dockside traders*
*Price: ~10 gold*
Use (1): Pull yourself or a target enemy to Frontline.

---

**LOW LANTERN**
*Consumable — Squat iron lantern, smoked glass*
*Source: Vulture's Nest market*
*Price: ~10 gold*
Use (1): Gain Obscure for 1 combat — enemies cannot look at or manipulate your hand or deck, and nothing reveals your stats (does not prevent status cards being added to it).

*Obscure is defined in `rules/card-glossary.md` since 2026-10-09 — the day something else needed it, as this note always said it should be. It now also hides your stats from anything that would reveal them.*
