# Fog Basin — Items

Items native to the Fog Basin outside Eclipsera. Primary source: Pell. For location context, see `locations/fog-basin.md`.

---

## Artifacts

### Fog Goggles
*Artifact — Carved lens, smoked quartz, leather wrap*
*Source: Pell — price varies per customer (a memory, a secret, a name)*

While equipped: never Blind from fog or Fogburst effects. See clearly through obscurement.

**However:** add FOGLUST to your deck.

---

## Status Cards

### FOGLUST
*Colorless — Status — Curse*
When drawn: Gain Blind until end of your turn.
*Removal: Story dependent.*
*"The fog remembered somewhere beautiful. You went with it."*

## Pell's Lanterns

Pell carries lanterns that remember paths. Each holds a captured piece of fog memory — a moment the basin recorded. He never explains them fully. He rummages in the coat and says something like:

> *"Nah, not that one. That one remembers a drowning. Ah. Here we are. This one remembers a road."*

Whether he made them or found them already glowing in the basin is not something he confirms.

| Lantern | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Lantern of Returning | The fog cannot lead you astray while lit. Misdirection Trap has no effect. |
| Lantern of the First Path | Shows the way someone originally walked through an area. DC 12 Mind to read correctly. |
| Lantern of the Unlost | Echoes gather near it and repeat useful fragments of speech. |
| Lantern of Quiet Wings | Fogcallers will not initiate combat while it burns. |

*All lanterns are single-use. They burn until the party exits the basin or a significant event extinguishes them.*

*Price varies. Pell decides.*
