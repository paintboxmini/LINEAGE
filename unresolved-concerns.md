# Unresolved Concerns

A scannable index of everything currently open that will eventually need a decision or a fix — the things that otherwise sit buried in `memory.md`'s prose (Recently Shipped entries, Standing Reasoning, Pending Propagation) until someone rereads the whole file. **This is an index, not a third home**: each entry is a line or two plus a pointer to where the full trail lives. Add a line when a concern gets flagged; delete the line when it's resolved (`memory.md` keeps the history — this file only ever shows the present).

**What does NOT belong here**: deliberately open questions. This repo keeps quiet doubts and unresolved world-hooks on purpose (`CLAUDE.md`: "the open questions are on purpose") — who bears the Storm Seat, what the Ferryman reads, what's at the Coil's center. Those are design, not debt. This file is for actual debt: flagged issues, known imbalances, deferred decisions that shouldn't be forgotten.

Also not here: **Pending Propagation** (operational staleness — print sheets, sim sync) stays in `memory.md` where the Sync workflow already looks for it. Entries below may point there, but the ledger itself doesn't move.

## Canon & World

- **Two different mechanisms are stated for the Unheld pressing in, and the In-Between is built on one of them.** `mythology/creation-myth-the-three-cuts.md` and `rules/the-summons.md` both say *"Where any Cut weakened, the Unheld pressed close."* `world/eclipseria-overview.md` says *"Where the Seats weaken, the Unheld presses in."* Cuts and Seats are different structures — the Cuts are the three founding wounds, Seats are domains with bearers. Both may be true, but nothing says so, and `world/the-in-between.md` is built entirely on the Cuts version (Drew's own framing: "a place where the 3 Cuts are thin"). **Authority 3.** Surfaced by the 2026-08-02 world audit.
- **Does reaching Stage III make you an Archon?** `mythology/echoes.md` heads the stage **"Incarnation (Archon)"** and calls it *"the highest stable form of Echo."* `world/seats-archons-echoes.md` describes the same stage as "Incarnation," adds *"This is not godhood. It is necessity,"* and never equates it with Archonhood — while separately defining an Archon as *"the highest biological expression of a Seat's domain."* The two may be saying the same thing, but only one names the endpoint, and it changes what a Stage II Echo like Aege is on track to become. **Authority 3.** Surfaced by the 2026-08-02 world audit.
- **The Echo stages and the Seat States are each written out in two files.** `mythology/echoes.md` + `world/seats-archons-echoes.md` for the stages; `mythology/seats.md` + the same world file for the states. They had already drifted — the world file was missing the **Fading** state entirely while its own Archon section described fading and cited the other file. Patched 2026-08-02 and `mythology/seats.md` marked canonical, but the duplication itself is the hazard and will drift again. Consolidating is a structural call. *Trail: memory.md, the world audit entry.*
- **The Underground Bazaar has zero mechanical items** — a real gap, nothing built yet, and Drew's own flag for a genuine future deep-design pass on the location: buying/selling mechanics are meant to be a highlight there, not an afterthought. (How pricing would work — memory/secret/debt economy, not gold — is open design, not debt itself; don't assume standard gold pricing transfers when this gets built.) *Trail: `rules/items.md`, Underground Bazaar section.*
