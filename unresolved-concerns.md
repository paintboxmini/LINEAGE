## Mechanics & Balance

- **Even-matchup PvP mirrors are still more lethal than the 1-death-per-20-sessions target — and the "does it come down to a 1v1" question is now checked directly, not assumed.** Ran 5000 fresh mirror battles (frost×3 vs frost×3) instrumented to log the winning side's still-active headcount at the finish: 51% end with exactly 1 winner still standing, 46% with 2, only 3% a clean sweep — so yes, roughly half the time it really does narrow to a near-1v1 finish, confirming the intuition rather than refuting it. But `living()` (`team_engine.py`) — the thing that actually ends the battle — checks "not collapsed," not "not dead": the fight stops the instant a whole side is collapsed, and death (`is_dead`) is a separate, rarer follow-up that needs a second hit landing on an already-collapsed target before that check fires. That's the real reason the death-occurred rate (41–48%) sits well under the ~100% collapse rate the losing side always hits — collapsing is unavoidable for whoever loses, dying isn't. Real levers for lowering death specifically, not discussed yet: the follow-up-hit odds themselves (`FINISH_DOWNED_CHANCE`), how fast revival can reach a downed ally before a second hit lands, or the death threshold's hit count. *Trail: memory.md, the 2026-07-24 entry with the real numbers; Standing Reasoning (the team-targeting rework entry).*
- **The Patient Host's kit-vs-stats question is answered; what a scaled-party test actually needs is narrower than originally flagged.** REGISTERED reworked to actually express Mind 8 — win rate barely moved (78.0%→77.9%), confirming an identity fix, not a power one. Corrected 2026-07-24: gear progression itself already exists (`rules/equipment.md`'s 3-tier Weapon/Armor budget system) — this was mischaracterized as a flat "no progression system" gap. What's actually still open, per Drew: a pacing layer for *when* a party should have access to which tier of gear (and where consumable items, priced in `items.md` but with no leveling curve, fit against that) — parked by Drew ("let the host run his inn in peace, for now"). *Trail: memory.md, Pending Propagation (the parked entry) and the 2026-07-23 REGISTERED rework entry.*
- **Frost/Steele are a comparison anchor that erodes with every card change, not a deck-quality baseline.** Corrected 2026-07-24: Drew built Crimson and Sky as real player decks too (not in the sim roster yet), so this was never about AI-built decks being weaker — it's that Frost/Steele were hand-built once as a stable reference point, and every card tweak this session (dice, effects, wording) quietly erodes how stable that reference still is, meaning periodic reference-deck rebuilds are just a normal maintenance cost, not a one-time flaw. *Trail: memory.md, the 2026-07-23 6:36 AM entry and the 2026-07-24 correction.*
- **RETALIATE/OVERCOMMIT/SEED's self-deferred Deadly — deliberately left alone in the latest card pass, with a fallback already chosen.** Drew, direct: left them out on purpose; the dice/range balancing pass (already deferred as lower priority all session) is the first lever, and if that alone doesn't close the gap, add another Deadly stack. Not an open design question anymore, just queued behind the dice/range pass. *Trail: memory.md, the 2026-07-23 11:15 PM entry and the 2026-07-24 correction.*
