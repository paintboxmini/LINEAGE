# Unresolved Concerns

A scannable index of everything currently open that will eventually need a decision or a fix — the things that otherwise sit buried in `memory.md`'s prose (Active Reasoning, Pending Propagation) or `archives/key-design-decisions.md`'s work-log trail until someone rereads the whole file. **This is an index, not a third home**: each entry is a line or two plus a pointer to where the full trail lives. Add a line when a concern gets flagged; delete the line when it's resolved (`memory.md` keeps the history — this file only ever shows the present).

**What does NOT belong here**: deliberately open questions. This repo keeps quiet doubts and unresolved world-hooks on purpose (`CLAUDE.md`: "the open questions are on purpose") — who bears the Storm Seat, what the Ferryman reads, what's at the Coil's center. Those are design, not debt. This file is for actual debt: flagged issues, known imbalances, deferred decisions that shouldn't be forgotten.

Also not here: **Pending Propagation** (operational staleness — print sheets, sim sync) stays in `memory.md` where the Sync workflow already looks for it. Entries below may point there, but the ledger itself doesn't move.

Also not here, same reason as the deliberately-open questions above: **Active Pending Threads** (`memory.md`) — possible future connections noticed while doing other work that don't require answering them. If it doesn't need an answer for current work to tie into the repo correctly, it's a thread, not debt; it lives there, not here.

## Mechanics & Balance

**`rules/card-glossary.md`'s per-keyword counts need a real mechanical recount.** The header numbers are a stated snapshot. On 2026-08-03 I wrote a recount script while adding seven cards and it disagreed with the header on **16 keywords, in both directions** — including decreases, which adding cards cannot cause. That means my counting rule differs from whatever produced the header, and I could not tell which was right. I updated only the six keywords the seven new cards demonstrably touch (Initiative Shift +2; Weak, Staggered, Resist, Evade, Thorns +1 each) and left the rest alone rather than overwrite live numbers from an unvalidated regex. Someone should settle the counting rule once and recount properly. `verify.py`'s `check_glossary_count` only checks the block total, not the per-keyword numbers. Trail: `memory.md`, the 2026-08-03 Oracle 12/6/3 entry.

**No `verify.py` check catches item-price duplication drift, the same shape `check_hp_formula` already catches for the HP formula.** Found 2026-08-07 when Grok's review of the capital vendor pack flagged `rules/items.md`'s Sap Vial summary bullet still showing ~60 gold — the price the 2026-08-03 price-coherence pass corrected to ~25 in `items/turnroot-weald-items.md`, the actual source file. The summary bullet was never updated and had been silently wrong since. `check_hp_formula` proves the pattern already works for a formula restated across seven files; the same technique (extract a number, compare against the source-of-truth file, fail on mismatch) could catch this for item prices too, but nobody's built it. Trail: `memory.md`, the 2026-08-07 Sap Vial entry.

**`verify.py`'s `check_decks` silently skips any bestiary deck line that isn't in the exact `**Deck (N — B Blue / R Red / G Green):** NAME *(color)*` format.** `bestiary/briar-scratcher.md`'s deck line is written as prose ("3 signature + 1 core to fill: ...") instead — it has never matched the regex, so its per-color counts and card names have never actually been machine-validated, and the check reports clean either way (a non-match looks identical to "checked, found nothing wrong"). Found 2026-08-06 while adding Wrackclaw and Hullback, whose first draft copied Briar Scratcher's prose style and got the same silent pass — caught only because the reported deck count didn't move after adding two new files, then fixed by rewriting to the strict format. Briar Scratcher itself left as-is; either reformat its deck line to the checkable style, or make the check flag prose-style lines instead of silently ignoring them. Trail: `memory.md`, the 2026-08-06 Wrackclaw/Hullback entry.

**Senshi's Bone Collector pricing is 10× off from his own published rates, and it's a real inconsistency, not an inference to silently fix.** Senshi offers 35 *silver* for a Bone Collector specimen while his own published rates run 35–40 *gold* for far safer creatures — same buyer, same numeral, hardest job paying least. Either a unit slip or deliberate lowballing; a price is canon and not mine to change without Drew's call. Trail: `archives/key-design-decisions.md`, Active Reasoning archive sweep, "Gold pacing built."

**Obscure/Reveal Hand's "unmodeled, same footing" justification holds only conditionally — needs re-checking if a policy brain ever gains real hand-aware logic.** The standing justification for leaving CIPHER GLYPH's Obscure and BREAK/READ's Reveal Hand unmodeled is that the sim AI already has full internal visibility regardless of what any card says — true only as long as no policy brain actually reads an opponent's hand contents to decide its own action. Confirmed clean as of 2026-08-03 (grepped every `.hand` access across `policies.py`, `team_policies.py`, `content.py` — no policy consults `foe.hand`/`target.hand` before deciding a move). Re-run this same check the next time a policy brain's logic changes. Trail: `archives/key-design-decisions.md`, Active Reasoning archive sweep, "Obscure and Reveal Hand."

**Balance concerns on far-off content have nowhere to be measured against yet — a real, standing category of debt, not a closed methodology.** A balance flag on distant bestiary content (a boss, a pack-scaling card) can't be tested until a party's fiction actually reaches it — the referent is missing, so the analysis is unfalsifiable today. Drew: *"anything that caught my eye balance wise has nothing to measure up against until the real players get closer to them in fiction first."* Doesn't excuse red-team's non-balance catches (dead cards, unbounded loops, rules ambiguities) — those are wrong at any tier. Trail: `archives/key-design-decisions.md`, "Balance findings on far-off content."

**Threat-level-style enemy targeting is explicitly the wrong model — a real, named, unbuilt design direction.** Drew: *"traditional threat levels make enemy targeting too predetermined... real combat is partially about manipulating the opponent's perception... real enemies don't just understand that, they make use of it themselves."* What's actually built (instinct-driven retaliation targeting) is a narrow honest piece of this, not the whole idea. The bigger direction — enemies that can be deceived, and that attempt deception themselves — is real and standing but not decided or scoped. Don't build toward it without Drew. Trail: `archives/key-design-decisions.md`, Active Reasoning archive sweep, "A real design direction named, not built."

## Content & Tone

**Voice-pass initiative — named locations NPCs still reading as pure function.** 2026-08-07's repo-wide scan (`agent-tools/finding-a-voice.md`'s first real use) found the gap concentrated in commercial/institutional layers — stalls, council seats, tavern staff get built location-first and never circle back for interiority. Three clusters shipped same day: Vulture's Nest (Dess Harwick, Salla, Harlow, Bean), the capital vendor layer (Nessa Coil, Brother Alden, Sera Vetch, Brannok "Sparks," Big Marcus), and the borderline group with existing partial texture (Jonas Widdlepen, Marta, Lyra Moon-Singer, Vesper, Sister Calda, Willem, Bella) — 16 people total, zero pre-existing facts altered. That scan's list is now exhausted; everything left unaddressed on it (Weck, Moth, Steve, Cole, Lady Isabella, King Bradley) is thin by design, not by gap, and `finding-a-voice.md` says explicitly not to touch those. **Still genuinely open:** nine of the ten Temple District temples (`places/capital/capital.md`) have no named clergy at all — not thin, just empty, a location gap rather than a voice gap, but worth surfacing here so it doesn't get mistaken for "done" — and any named person added to the repo going forward should get this same pass rather than waiting for a second full sweep. Trail: `memory.md`, the 2026-08-07 voice-pass entries.

**Name/Price/Distance backlog — every existing NPC, place, and item is now retroactively "incomplete" against the new applied treatment.** 2026-08-10: `rules/people.md`, `rules/places.md`, and `rules/equipment.md` formalized all three Cuts as explicit design steps per subject — Name (what something is, an actual name, traits, history), Price, and Distance. Drew, naming the cost directly: *"I'm aware I'm creating a large amount of backlog work by having to go through an explicitly fill out name, price, and distance for people/places/items."* Nothing in the repo has been retroactively audited against this yet — most existing content predates the formalization and was built under looser, non-explicit versions of some or all three. Not urgent, not blocking, and explicitly not something to attack unprompted — this is a large-scale pass, not a quick fix — but real, acknowledged debt rather than a design choice to leave things as they are. Trail: `memory.md`, 2026-08-10 (the rules/people.md and rules/places.md creation, and this session's Naming-formalization entries).

**Jackrabbits and Briar Scratchers aren't integrated into the Day 1 road-encounter toolkit — undecided whether they should be.** `quests/washed-ashore.md`'s The Walk built out a real toolkit for the beach-to-Roadhouse crossing (terrain-bleed beats, Skeinwing/Stonecoil sightings, a Trisect+Wrackclaw combat) — Jackrabbits and Briar Scratchers remain general options available to a GM but never written into that toolkit specifically. Needs a decision: fold them in, or leave them as ambient GM discretion on purpose. Trail: `archives/key-design-decisions.md`, "Road encounters."

**`items/consumables.md`'s five oldest entries are stubs, not thin flavor: Echo Shell, Blood Phial, Imprint Sigil, Universal Pin, Phase Draught.** Each has a name, a type tag, and a price — no flavor line, and no effect text even in their own "full entry" file; the actual mechanics only exist one level removed, in `rules/items.md`'s summary bullets. Unlike Harwick Sundries' mystery counter (`places/vultures-nest.md`), which is unbuilt on purpose and says so, nothing marks these five as deliberately incomplete — they just read that way, and the names themselves ("Universal Pin," "Imprint Sigil") are generic enough to fail the alignment-checker's own "could this exist in any TTRPG" test. Flagged 2026-08-07 in conversation, not fixed — Drew wants them held until development surfaces where each actually belongs (a source, an ecology, a reason it exists) rather than given flavor text cold.
