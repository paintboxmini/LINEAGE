# Platform Night

**Pressure:** Kaine's public ceremony turns Steve's choice into an immediate player decision.

Kaine leads a group descent on a platform lower and farther out than the ordinary single-body Last Bath. The group form is the Final Current's inversion of the ordinary rite: several people descend together rather than one body entering alone.

Vesper Thorn, Rook, and Lirren are present with Kaine. Other Final Current members stand with them. Open combat is therefore an expensive choice rather than a clean solution. Rook is the practical barrier; Vesper is settled certainty; Lirren is eager encouragement.

Sael is with the ordinary congregation and is not on this platform.

Steve is on the platform. Wren and Mirel are present. The ropes begin to lower.

Wren's face carries the emotional weight of the moment. He has already admitted that he bound Steve's future once, without Steve's consent, and does not know whether any eventual cost belongs to him or to Steve. The declaration may press here, but do not resolve its price automatically.

**Player decision point:** Steve's refusal, if it happens, is tied to what the players do in this moment. Do not pre-resolve it in the scene file. The descent, refusal, intervention, and any consequence remain open to play.
