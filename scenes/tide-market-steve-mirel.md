# Tide Market — Steve and Mirel

**Pressure:** A small, ordinary moment becomes visible to the party.

Steve, a young adult and Wren's son, makes a clumsy attempt to ask Mirel out. He loses the thread of the attempt, realizes it, and pivots into self-deprecating humor.

Mirel laughs for real. She does not rescue him from the embarrassment, but she does not mock him either. Something is developing between them. Neither has named it.

Later, when Mirel mentions Steve to the party, she describes him only as curious about the Final Current. She has no reason to describe a refusal that has not happened. A careful listener can hear that her interest in him is more personal than the wording requires.

**Player decision point:** The party may notice, tease, encourage, ignore, or simply observe. Do not force the relationship forward.
