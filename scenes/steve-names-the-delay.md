# Steve Names the Delay

**Pressure:** A private parental decision becomes public.

In an open gallery or fracture, Steve tells Wren, in front of part of the congregation and the party, that he intends to join a Final Current descent.

The statement names the years-long delay around his first ordinary bath. Wren has been protecting Steve from the change the Unheld brings, using the language of care and readiness. Steve is now choosing whether that protection is still his to accept.

Wren does not need to argue the point publicly. The wound is visible beneath the careful refusal: the accusation that protection is control would hurt because Wren believes the protection is real.

**Player decision point:** The party can speak to Steve, Wren, or the congregation, but the choice to join the descent remains Steve's.
