# The Gilded Tusk

*The most prominent tavern in Eclipsera. Where policy drinks.*

---

## Entry

The door opens against its own weight.

Warm air rolls out — not tavern-smoke alone, but layered: frankincense first, sweet and resinous and expensive, then fat hitting iron, then ale.

Inside, the space dips downward — three shallow marble steps into a sunken central floor. The mosaic beneath your boots is old imperial work: geometric laurels and boars, cracked and stained but still deliberate. This place remembers being grand.

White marble pillars ring the pit. Above them, dark oak beams cross like ribs. Iron chandeliers hang on chains, each candle steady but imperfect, casting long shadows that make the scholars at the tables look more severe than they probably are.

The sound isn't laughter. It's debate. Low, persistent, layered with ceramic mugs striking wood.

Behind the bar, Big Marcus doesn't look up immediately. He polishes a pewter mug with a rag that has seen better days. When he does look up, his eyes sweep the party once — measuring purse, measuring trouble.

"You here to argue," he says evenly, "or to drink?"

From the back kitchen: the sharp clang of iron and a burst of steam. Senshi's voice rises: *"If you won't eat it because it has six legs, you don't deserve dessert!"*

Above the hearth, the boar skull watches. One tusk plain bone. One gilded. Hairline crack at the base — repaired, not replaced.

---

## Architecture

Roman foundation: sunken central floor, mosaic tiles, white marble pillars. Medieval overlay: dark oak ceiling beams, small leaded-glass windows. Hanging lanterns shaped like tusks — bone and brass. The gold here is accent, not excess.

At the back: a staircase curling upward. A door beside it. Closed.

---

## Atmosphere

- **Scent:** Frankincense from the Temple District, sizzling street meat from the Marketplace, ale.
- **Sound:** Low scholarly argument. Merchants and officials. Not rowdy. Not rustic. Controlled.
- **Clientele:** This is where power relaxes. Wealth-adjacent, politically aware, proud of its history.

## Key NPCs

**"Big" Marcus** *(barkeep, human)*
Barrel-chested. Leather apron over a stained silk tunic. Prosthetic brass ear — a gift from an enchanted artisan. Knows every rumor about the Regency Council. Treats the party well as long as they have coin. Enforces one rule: **No Magic in the Pit.**

**Senshi** *(cook)*
Eccentric. Constantly pushing locals to try unfamiliar food. Gets personally offended when people refuse on the basis of legs. Menu pending — see note below.

**Lyra Moon-Singer** *(entertainer, half-elf)*
Plays a lyre. Uses minor illusion to manifest small spirit dancers on the tabletops while she performs.

**Sitrump the Scribe** *(regular, gnome)*
Always at the same corner table, surrounded by scrolls from the Library of Arcane Knowledge. Obsessed with unmasking Lord Archibald (The Pig) and Lady Eveline (The Elephant). Extremely paranoid.

> *"Lower your voice! Do you want the Pig to hear? He has ears in the walls, I tell you. Actual ears. I've seen the masonry twitch when Lady Eveline walks by..."*

Hook: Sitrump found a scroll suggesting the late King Bradley didn't die in the North — see `locations/abyssal-ruins.md` (Ghostly Lead).

## The Menu

**The Golden Pendragon** *(drink of the house)*
Heavy spiced mead in a pewter mug. Rumored to be the same recipe King Bradley drank before he went to war. Strong enough to make a Bear stagger and a Fox tell the truth.

**The "Citadel" Platter**
Roasted boar haunch with Roman-style garum and thick trenchers of bread.

**Honeyed Locusts**
A delicacy from the Unheld Sea islands. Crunchy, sweet. Popular with the Prince's guards.

## The Masked Toast

Every night at midnight, Marcus rings a brass bell. All patrons raise a glass to the Regency Council. Most locals do it with a sarcastic smirk or crossed fingers. Refusing earns a thumbs-down from the local toughs — a Roman tradition Marcus kept alive. The party's reaction will be noted.
