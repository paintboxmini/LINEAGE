# The Abyssal Trench

*Sub-area of the Abyssal Ruins. Also called The Trench.*

A depression in the ruins floor — twenty feet below the surrounding level. Accessible via cracked stone ramps on the north side or a straight drop from the plaza edge above. The violet haze that clings to the rest of the ruins pools here, thicker and lower, underlaid by a faint bioluminescent glow from the organisms living in it.

The floor is not safe to walk across without attention. The walls are colonized. The air smells like salt and age and something organic that has no name in any living language.

---

## Navigation

The floor of The Trench is covered in Death-Ball Sponge colonies — calcified growths that blend with the pale stone. Moving through without triggering one requires watching where you step.

**Floor check:** DC 13 Mind/Observe to spot a colony before entering it. Failure triggers the sponge — see `bestiary/death-ball-sponge.md` for mechanics.

The safe paths through the colonies are narrow and irregular. The Flapjack Octopus has mapped them. It lies flat along those paths.

---

## Inhabitants

**Flapjack Octopus**
Ambush predators that lie flat between sponge colonies. A party careful enough to navigate the floor may step over one without ever detecting it. Found in the middle section of The Trench, between the entry ramps and the deepest point.

See `bestiary/flapjack-octopus.md`.

---

**Elder Tower Creatures**
At the deepest point — three to five Tower Creatures growing from the floor. Fifteen to twenty-five feet tall. Ancient. Non-hostile. Pores along the body occasionally produce harvestable tubes.

The towers pulse with a slow, regular sound. When the sound stops, something has disturbed them.

See `bestiary/elder-tower-creature.md`.

---

## Atmosphere

- **Light:** Bioluminescent haze from below. Blue-green. Enough to navigate by, not enough to see the far end.
- **Sound:** A low periodic pulse from the Tower Creatures. Regular, like a slow heartbeat. The Trench is wrong when it stops.
- **Floor:** Pale and irregular. Safe paths are narrow. The sponge colonies look like nothing until you're on them.
- **Air:** Salt and something old. Not the smell of the ruins above — something underneath it.
