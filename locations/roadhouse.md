# The Roadhouse

A government inn on the main road between Vulture's Nest and Briarwatch. It exists because the road requires it to.

---

## The Place

Stone walls. Two floors. A crown seal above the door, faded but legible. The sign doesn't have a name — just a black rectangle with a candle painted on it.

Inside: six rooms, a common area with four tables, a hearth that runs through the night. Everything is functional. Nothing is charming. The food is fine because the contract says it has to be.

A register sits on the counter by the door. Guests sign in. This is not optional. Nobody explains where the records go.

---

## The Keeper

Runs the place alongside the posted guards. Middle-aged, efficient, no interest in conversation. Knows the road conditions, will share them if asked directly. Won't volunteer anything.

Does not remember faces. Does remember if someone refused to sign the register.

---

## The Guards

Two Regency soldiers on rotation. Not elite — road assignment is not a prestigious post. They know it.

One handles the register and the door. Checks it when the party arrives, checks it again in the morning. Doesn't explain why. The other keeps to the barracks section at the back of the building or sits near the hearth with a drink they're not quite finishing.

They won't start trouble. They will finish it. If the party is cooperative and unremarkable, the guards are furniture.

**The Barracks** — a back section closed off from the guest rooms. Two bunks, a weapon rack, a small table with a candle stub and a log of road incidents. Guests don't go back there. The door is never locked.

*Contents for curious parties:*

- Weapon rack: two short swords, a crossbow, a quiver of bolts, a shield with a worn crown seal. Standard issue. Nothing remarkable.
- Under one bunk: a wooden chest, unlocked. A few coins (roll d6+2 silver), a spare uniform folded neatly, a whetstone.
- On the table: a folded posting order with an official wax seal.

**The Posting Order** — standard Regency language. Assignment to Roadhouse waypoint, indefinite duration. Register compliance, cargo documentation. Report irregularities to *Quartermaster Voss, Eclipsera South Gate.*

Below the printed text, added in smaller handwriting and different ink:

*"— anything from the docks that isn't in the manifest."*

Nobody signed it.

---

## The Traveler Going the Other Way

There is usually one. Coming from the Briarwatch direction, heading toward the Nest. Tired. Road-worn. Unremarkable.

If the party asks where they've been: brief answer, nothing useful. If the party asks about Briarwatch: a pause. Then: *"Ground's soft up there near the fork. Watch your step."*

That's all they'll say. They finish their meal and turn in early.

---

## Rest

Short rest available. Wounds reduce by 1. Decks reset. The beds work.

This is the last clean rest before Briarwatch.

---

## Tone

Nothing is wrong here. That is not reassuring.
