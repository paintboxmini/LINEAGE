# The Briarwoods

A stretch of dense, low-canopy woodland on the northern side of Vulture's Nest, running up and west to surround the Roadhouse and Briarwatch. It is not a destination. It is the space between destinations.

---

## The Place

Thick undergrowth. Bramble walls that force the road into corridors. The canopy is patchy enough that overcast sky is usually visible overhead, which doesn't help much.

The ground shifts between packed road dirt and soft earth that remembers recent rain. The further northeast you travel, the softer it gets. This is because of the limestone. It does not improve.

The road through the Briarwoods is maintained by the Regency in the same way the Roadhouse is maintained — because it has to be, not because anyone particularly cares about it.

---

## Travel

Day 1: Vulture's Nest → Roadhouse. Road is clear. The Briarwoods close in about two hours out of the Nest and don't open up again until the Roadhouse clearing.

Day 2: Roadhouse → Briarwatch fork. Thicker brush. The road narrows. Livestock tracks cross it at intervals and don't always continue on the other side.

---

## Creatures

**Briarwatch Jackrabbit** — territorial at the woodland edges and the Watchtower Ruins near Briarwatch. Fast, defensive of nesting hollows. See `bestiary/briarwatch-jackrabbit.md`.

**Briar Scratcher** — found deeper in the undergrowth, drawn to road noise and movement. Present on the longer route between the Roadhouse and the Briarwatch fork. See `bestiary/briar-scratcher.md`.

---

## Tone

Nothing in the Briarwoods announces itself. Things that live here are used to the road traffic. They've learned when it's worth coming out for.
