# The Adventurers' Hall

*The public desk for the freelancer branch of Lord Cedric's military (`locations/capital/capital.md`, The Inner Precinct). Squat, square, plainly military — thick walls, narrow windows, a small drill yard at the side, a single reinforced door under a plain iron sign. A registered party is an irregular unit under the Bear's authority, not a member of some separate guild. Most parties will meet the city through this counter before they meet anyone on the council directly.*

---

## The Board

A long contract board behind a scarred registration counter, weapon racks kept locked. Postings are slate or paper, pinned and re-pinned, dated by a clerk who does not editorialize. Taking one down means signing for it at the counter — name, party size, declared intent — same discipline as the Roadhouse's own register (`locations/roadhouse.md`). Nobody explains where those records go here either.

Reward on any of these is negotiated at the counter against the standard session pacing (`rules/equipment.md`, Pacing) — the postings below don't carry a fixed price; the clerk sets one against what a job's actually worth once a party asks.

---

### Apnea — Wellness Census, Hospice Islands

> *"Regency census overdue on three hospice islands, southern lake chain. State inspectors do not travel. Confirm residency rolls current, report any irregularity. Standard fee, travel included."*

Dry, bureaucratic, and completely genuine — the Regency really does run a periodic census on hospice islands, and Apnea's really is overdue. What the clerk doesn't know: there's a second, unrelated reason a party might end up asking after a specific resident there, routed privately through Lily at the Underground Bazaar rather than this board (`locations/apnea.md`, The hook). The census job gets a party to the island. What they find there is the island's business, not the Regency's.

---

### Canille — Correspondence Lapse, Research Colony

> *"Founding-era research colony, lake northeast of Vulture's Nest, has not filed its periodic report in longer than administratively comfortable. Confirm the colony is extant and functioning. Discretion appreciated — old charters are touchy about oversight."*

A real gap in the paperwork, filed by a clerk who has no idea what's actually being administered out there. `locations/canille.md` — nothing about the cubs, the masks, or the Founding Hall needs to be in the posting. The posting is a paperwork lapse. What a party finds once they arrive is theirs to sit with.

---

### Clayhollow — By Letter Only

*(This one doesn't get pinned to the board — it arrives already sealed, in the village's own hand, and the clerk pins the envelope itself rather than a summary.)*

> *State your needs in writing. Three days.*
> *A household's golem has returned from repair carrying a token that matches no family here. We do not know how to ask this without asking it aloud. We are asking it in writing instead.*

Clayhollow communicates the way Clayhollow communicates (`locations/clayhollow.md`, Cultural Rules) — even hiring outside help. The actual hook is the file's own "The golem that came back wrong." A party that takes this contract should expect the entire job to run on paper and silence; showing up and talking normally is itself a violation of the only rule that matters there.

---

### Pneum — Quarantine Hold, Tideward Compact

> *"Vessel held at Vulture's Nest pending Compact review — unregistered communicable material, origin unconfirmed. Owner in custody, uncooperative. Compact wants the origin traced before the vessel's released or destroyed."*

Already fully official — the barge is real, the quarantine is real, Corvel is really being held (`locations/vultures-nest.md`, `characters/corvel.md`). Following the origin leads to Pneum and the People of Promise (`locations/pneum.md`). The posting undersells what's actually out there on purpose; the Compact doesn't know yet either.

---

### Veldmire — Stalled Shipment, Trade Dispute

> *"Caravan contracted to Veldmire has been held at the village edge eleven days pending an internal ruling. Cargo perishable. Client wants either the shipment released or a formal refusal on record — the caravan cannot wait indefinitely."*

Mundane commerce, dropped into the middle of "what Veldmire decides next" (`locations/veldmire.md`, Open Hooks) — a party arriving to collect on a contract gets pulled straight into the dusk ritual whether they came for that or not. The Regency cares about the shipment. What actually decides it is a stone catching the light.

---

### Weatherheart Vale — Wellness Concern, Escalating

> *"Persistent adverse weather reported at Weatherheart Vale beyond seasonal norm — multiple independent traveler reports. Village has not requested aid. Assess and report; intervene only if invited."*

The Regency doesn't know the weather is the village, but the posting's own caution ("intervene only if invited") accidentally gets the fiction right. This is the file's own built encounter (`locations/weatherheart-vale.md`, The Boy Who Won't Calm) — a party that reports back honestly will have opinions about what "seasonal norm" means once they've stood in it.

---

### The Abyssal Trench — Naturalist Collection, Standing Order

> *"Standing purchase order, Gilded Tusk kitchens: Death-Ball Sponge (intact), Flapjack Octopus (whole), Elder Tower Creature tissue (harvested live, non-lethal only). Payment per specimen on delivery to the Tusk, not the Hall."*

Lower stakes than the ruins proper, and genuinely recurring — this one's less a quest than a standing gig a party can pick up again later. Full harvest table and DCs already exist (`locations/abyssal-trench.md`, Harvest Summary). The "non-lethal only" line on the Tower Creatures is the Tusk's own condition, not the Regency's; ignoring it is a choice with its own consequences, not a rules violation.

## Word of Mouth — Not on the Board

Some jobs never make it to a posting, because there's nothing an official notice could actually say. These circulate at the Gilded Tusk, in the Marketplace, at the Roadhouse — never signed for, never dated, no clerk's record. A party hears about them, or doesn't.

**Havenrise.** *"Trader's second son went east past the cliffs eight months back, hasn't sent word since. Family's asking around, not paying much, just wants to know."* `locations/havenrise.md` is genuinely blank past this — the posting can exist; the destination, for now, can't be developed further than "he went that way."

**Roaat.** *"Anyone still doing runs out to Roaat? Nobody's confirmed a delivery in a season. Just want to know if the road's still good."* Same shape as Havenrise — a real gap in trade contact, not yet a real place (`locations/roaat.md`).

**RavenHold — unnamed, unmapped.** *"Third one this year. Traveler comes back from the Vulture's Nest–Glasslight road not quite right — quiet, won't talk about the missing days, swears it was only an afternoon. Families want to know what's out there. Nobody's found where 'there' is."* This is `world/geography-overview.md`'s edge cases finally getting a face — the job is investigating disappearances near a road, not a destination a party can be sent to, because RavenHold refuses to be found on purpose (`locations/ravenhold.md`). A party could chase this for a long time before the valley decides to let them in — if it ever does.

**The Mother of Many — Underground Bazaar.** *"Oversized specimen, vendor stall, east row. Management would very much like it handled before the offspring mature. Ask for the gremlin with the ledger."* Already written as a real, playable fight with its own clock (`locations/capital/underground-bazaar.md`, The Mother of Many) — just Bazaar business, not Regency business, so it stays off the official board even though it's as concrete a job as anything on it.
